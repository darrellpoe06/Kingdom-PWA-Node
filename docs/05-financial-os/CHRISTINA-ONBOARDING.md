# Christina · PoeTech Family OS Onboarding

**Goal:** Get Christina using the system to reduce her current work load, in the order that produces the most relief per minute spent learning.

Each week is **~30 minutes total** unless noted. Nothing here is mandatory — skip anything that doesn't fit. The system is a tool for her, not another job.

---

## Week 1 — Big Picture only (15 min · do this first)

**Why first:** Single screen. No data entry. Shows where the family stands right now without her doing anything. This is the "is anything on fire?" view.

**What to do:**
1. Open the app, land on **Big Picture**.
2. Look at the **Action Queue** at the top. Anything urgent shows up here (tenant late, broken HVAC, overdue bills).
3. Look at the **Family Capacity** meter. Green = healthy, amber = tight, rust = over-committed.
4. Look at the **three hero numbers** — net cash flow, consumer debt freedom date, rentals owned-free date.

**Outcome:** She knows what's happening across the household and businesses in 30 seconds, every morning, without touching anything else.

**Indicator it's working:** Christina can answer "is there anything I need to deal with this morning?" by glancing at the Action Queue.

---

## Week 2 — Books → Accounts (15 min · cash visibility)

**Why next:** Removes the "do we have enough cash to pay X?" anxiety. She sees the spendable balance at a glance.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Books → Accounts**.
2. The big **All Accounts · Total Cash** number at the top is what's actually spendable. Credit cards live separately below — they don't drag down the cash number anymore.
3. Look at the **30 / 60 / 90-Day Forecast** below. Compare the prior-90-day actuals (left) to the forward projection (right) to gut-check the next 3 months.
4. If she wants to update any account balance, tap **Edit** on the row, change the number, save. No scroll jump.

**Outcome:** "How much cash do we actually have right now?" — answered in 2 seconds, any time.

**Indicator:** She stops asking Darrell "did the rent come in yet?" and just looks.

---

## Week 3 — Real Estate (20 min · rental rhythm)

**Why now:** Property management is the biggest source of irregular stress (late tenants, repair calls, vendor schedules). The Real Estate tab consolidates all 11 doors with the **Tenant Not Paying** card automatically appearing when a property's status is `late` — three one-tap buttons (Change / Incident / Project) open it as the right kind of issue.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Real Estate**. The 11 Poe Properties doors are already loaded.
2. For 1508 Holly Hill (currently `late`), look at the rust **Tenant Not Paying** card. The default urgency button (Incident · 3-day resolution window) is usually right.
3. For any property, tap **Records** to see maintenance log + conversation log + lease details. Add a quick conversation note if she's talked to a tenant about anything — date, who, summary, optional notes.
4. **Don't worry about** filling in every property field on day one. Lease, equipment, room-by-room work — those are nice-to-have, not required.

**Outcome:** She has one place to log "Tracy called about the leaking faucet" instead of remembering it. The system reminds her when items go stale.

**Indicator:** A week from now, she can open the property card and see what was discussed last week without trying to remember.

---

## Week 4 — Calendar reminders (10 min · don't-forget mode)

**Why now:** Browser notifications mean tax deadlines, license renewals, recurring obligations all surface without anyone having to remember.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Books → Calendar**.
2. First time: the browser asks for notification permission — accept. (One-time.)
3. Scan the **Recurring Obligations** list (IL LLC fees, vehicle reg, State Farm, MSW license, CEU, etc.).
4. Add an event for any one-off Christina needs reminded about — e.g., "Christina's CEU course due 2026-11-01."

**Outcome:** Browser pings her ahead of every deadline. No more "oh wait, was that due?"

**Indicator:** She gets a notification she's grateful for in the first 2 weeks.

---

## Week 5+ — Practice tab (when she's ready · saves her ~5 hrs/mo)

**Why later, not earlier:** Practice Operations tab tracks pre-intake inquiries (people who called/emailed but haven't booked yet). It replaces whatever sticky-note / Acuity-only system she uses now. **No PHI.** Acuity stays the system of record once they're scheduled.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Practice**. The 8 sample inquiries demo what it looks like.
2. Delete the samples once she's seen them.
3. For each NEW phone call or contact form submission, add an inquiry: name, phone, source (referral / Facebook / church / etc.), interest, best time to call, notes.
4. Move them through the status workflow (New → Contacted → Scheduled in Acuity → Active client).

**Outcome:** "Who do I still need to call back?" → one tab, sorted by oldest. No more lost leads.

**Indicator:** Her conversion rate climbs because she stops dropping balls.

**Time saved:** ~5 hrs/mo of "wait, who did I forget to follow up with."

---

## Months 2+ — PoeTech (only if/when she wants involvement)

Christina doesn't need to touch the PoeTech tabs (Dev/Ops, Projects scope tool, etc.) unless she wants to. Those are Darrell's lane. The system separates concerns so she sees only what she needs.

If she ever does want a look — the **Action Queue** on Big Picture already surfaces any PoeTech item that needs her attention (e.g., a project Darrell flags as needing her input), so she doesn't have to go hunting.

---

## What Christina should expect

- **First 30 days:** Mostly looking. The system shows what's already there. No new work.
- **First 60 days:** Logging conversations and adding events become habit. ~5 min/day total.
- **First 90 days:** Notifications start saving her from missed deadlines. She probably stops using sticky notes for property issues.
- **Beyond:** It compounds. Every conversation logged today is searchable next month. Every tenant pattern becomes visible. Every projection becomes more accurate.

## What it should NEVER feel like

- A second job. If something feels burdensome, skip it — the system should be optional in every direction.
- A surveillance tool. Everything is local-first; nothing leaves her device until she explicitly chooses.
- A learning curve. If something isn't obvious, the system is wrong, not Christina. Tap the **💬 Feedback** button (header AND floating bottom-right) and tell us what's confusing. We fix it. That's literally the design.

## Where to find help

- **In-app:** 💬 Feedback button (header). Tier preview in header. Tooltips on most controls (hover).
- **In the repo:** `/docs/00-foundations/GLOSSARY.md` (terms), `/docs/00-foundations/KPIS.md` (what each number means).
- **From Darrell:** "What does this button do?" any time. The system should never require expertise she doesn't have.
