## Where the teaching landed

**A judgment you WATCHED land on somebody else was written for you — and
whether it teaches you is settled by whether the cost ever reached you.**

The lead money item closed on three statements and then explained them in a
fourth: everyone in that trade knows what borrowing against a position can do;
one of them was destroyed in public over the summer where all of them watched;
borrowing afterward reached a record; and the loss had stayed with the side
holding the position rather than the side that lent. They saw it, they know it,
they did it again larger — because it never reached the ones watching.

Scripture runs that experiment four times and reports the failure each time
(Jeremiah 3:7-8; Ezekiel 23:11; Daniel 5:18-22; Zephaniah 3:6-7), prices the
cheap road in a verse almost nobody reads for what it says (Proverbs 19:25;
Proverbs 21:11), and names the mechanism, which is comfort (Jeremiah 48:11;
Psalms 55:19; Amos 6:1-6). The close is the cleanest control case in the
Bible: two men saw the same cross from the same distance, and the one who was
saved refused to watch from safety — *"seeing thou art in the same
condemnation"* (Luke 23:40).
