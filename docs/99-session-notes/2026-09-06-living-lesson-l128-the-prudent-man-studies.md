# L128 — The Prudent Man Studies: Systematic Analysis, the Ways That Protect, and Seeing Him While Blind

**Date:** 2026-09-06

## What was spoken

1. A prudent man studies and does not get into trouble, because he is systematically
   analyzing what Yahweh said to us humans — study to show yourself what He has
   hidden, so you don't fail to see the same thing His children studied to see.
2. Actions are protected by the Ways of Yahweh; they speak louder because you know
   you would have done something differently if it wasn't for His voice — not
   audible — recognised the way he understood 2Pac without ever meeting him,
   because of shared similarities. Now he sees Yahweh by His Ways, while blind.
3. He is changing my ways to be like His, because they are higher level ways than
   what I have defaulted.
4. I need faith in Him to lead and guide me, but He has to keep my attention.
5. We abiding in His temple — my actual heart or subconscious. Amazing integration.
6. Words abiding.
7. Noticing the difference between abiding on and off — nuance, thin line, spirit
   vs flesh, soul umpire, and the decision-making is you.
8. Seek ye first; abide in me; the secret place of the Most High, the shadow of
   the Almighty — what does it, and what is the deterministic doing?
9. Carnal mind vs spiritual.
10. Can't see, not won't see.
11. Willing and obedient; not willing and not obedient, then it doesn't work,
    because of the ways — deterministic.

## The find

The first two looked like separate lessons. They are not. **Hosea 14:9** carries
both words in one sentence — *"Who is wise, and he shall understand these things?
**prudent**, and he shall know them? for the **ways of the LORD** are right, and the
just shall walk in them: but the transgressors shall fall therein."* That verse is
the anchor, and it is what made this one lesson instead of two.

The causal chain the eleven messages describe, in order:

> **study systematically** → the words **abide** (John 15:7) → in the **heart**, which
> is the **temple** (1 Corinthians 3:16) → which is the layer that runs the
> **defaults** → so Yahweh **changes the defaults** (Ezekiel 36:27, "cause you to
> walk") → so the **actions are protected** (Psalms 91:11, "in all thy ways") →
> and the proof is **retrospective**, with no sound required (Genesis 28:16).

That chain is why "Words abiding" is the hinge movement of the lesson: it is the
link that makes the studying become the protecting.

## Guard rails carried in the lesson

- A sense of being led is **tested** against a fixed outside standard (Psalms 18:30),
  because the instrument is compromised (Jeremiah 17:9).
- Protection is **not** an untouched life — *"Though he fall"* is inside the verse
  (Psalms 37:24), and both houses in Matthew 7:24-27 got identical weather.
- The same ways cut **both** directions — Hosea 14:9 ends *"but the transgressors
  shall fall therein."*
- The 2Pac analogy is carried as **recognition by resemblance only**, with no claim
  about the artist in either direction, and fenced immediately by Isaiah 55:8-9.
- `can't see` is taught as **cannot** (Romans 8:7; 1 Corinthians 2:14) — which turns
  contempt into mercy — while keeping the condition **curable**, not fated
  (2 Corinthians 3:16).
