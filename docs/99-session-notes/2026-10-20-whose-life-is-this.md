# Whose life is this? 88 stories say "a true story" and name nobody

**2026-10-20 · for Darrell · answering the re-review DR-0550 left for today**

DR-0550 asked for the lessons built from your lived accounts to be swept for other
checkable details — days, counts, names, places — and for each one to be put in front
of you rather than in front of a gate. This is that list, and the sweep found something
before it got to the days and counts.

## What the app is telling a reader right now

A story marked `kind: 'testimony'` is headed **"A true story, lived — [title] · [source]"**
and closes with **"A true story: this happened, told with [source]'s consent."** When no
source is written down, the page still makes the claim anyway — it just says *the
person's* consent instead of a name.

There are **93** of these stories. **5** carry a source — all 5 yours, in Living Lessons.
The other **88** carry none. 88 of them sit two to a lesson across 11 courses, one
parable and one "true story" from each course's first commit.

**52 are in your voice** — "I", "we", "my father". **36 report someone unnamed** — "a man who had
lent four thousand pounds to a friend for a van said…".

`PARABLE-AND-TESTIMONY-METHOD.md` says a true story "Requires: it actually happened (no
embellishment), explicit consent, privacy / minor protection" and that "What a real person
actually said or lived is a testimony with its source, or it stays out." So each of the 88
is one of three things, and only you can say which:

1. **Yours** — then it needs your name on it, and the details below need your eye.
2. **Someone else's, told to you** — then it needs them named or properly anonymised, and
   their consent recorded.
3. **Written to teach, not lived** — then it is a parable, and the "true story" label comes
   off. No shame in that: Jesus taught by them (Matthew 13:34). It just must not wear the
   other label.

You can answer a whole course at once if the answer is the same for every story in it.

I have not changed one word of any story. A gate now refuses a NEW one without a source,
pinned at these 88 so the number can only fall.
