## The two decisions, made

### DECISION 1 — Reinstate POE TECH, LLC. Do not form a new entity.

The pack framed this as a genuine fork. It is, and it resolves in favour of
reinstatement once the pieces are laid side by side:

- **The 2016 priority date is the most valuable single fact in the whole IP
  estate**, and a new entity forfeits it outright. Other users of the PoeTech
  name were found in the clearance pass (California, Maryland); 2016 is what
  may put this entity ahead of them. A new LLC starts in 2026, behind everyone.
- **Continuity of the instruments we actually hold.** The EIN, the executed
  operating agreement, and the §1.03 disregarded-entity treatment all attach to
  *this* entity. Reinstating keeps them; starting clean means re-papering all
  three.
- **The name is already in commercial use in Illinois public works.** A
  contractor is addressing bid invitations to "Poe Tech LLC" this month. That
  is recognition a new entity would have to rebuild.
- **The "inherits ten years of unknown history" worry is thin here.** Single
  member, disregarded for tax, no employees surfaced, no litigation surfaced,
  no creditors surfaced. The known history is an unfiled annual report.

**The honest argument against, stated rather than buried:** if Illinois
reinstatement does NOT relate back to 2024-03-08, there is an ~18-month window
in which the entity did not exist while the mark was used personally. That is
a real question for counsel — **but it argues FOR reinstatement, not against.**
A gap that must be explained still beats a new entity with no claim to 2016 at
all.

### DECISION 2 — Appoint a commercial registered agent service. Not Darrell personally, not new counsel.

Three options existed. This one wins on the evidence of what actually broke:

- **It is the direct structural fix to the root cause.** The failure was
  notices arriving at an office with nobody in it. A service scans and forwards
  to a monitored inbox. The failure mode is designed out rather than re-pointed.
- **Darrell travels, and this repository has already paid for that once.** The
  2026-06-06 runaway-automation incident happened while he was away. An agent
  obligation requiring physical presence at an Illinois address during business
  hours is the same class of single-point-of-failure.
- **It keeps the home address off the public record.** Appointing himself puts
  a residential address into a permanently public state database.
- **New counsel is the arrangement that just failed.** It fails identically the
  next time an attorney retires, moves, or closes a practice without handover.

**Selection criteria, so the choice is not brand-shopping:** registered to do
business in Illinois; Illinois street address; same-day scan-and-forward to
email; automatic annual-report reminders; and a published escalation path when
service of process arrives. Any provider meeting all five is acceptable — the
criteria are the decision, not the vendor name.

## The filing order — fixed, and the order matters

**The agent change cannot be filed first as its own transaction.** Verified at
ilsos.gov on 2026-09-19: the stand-alone electronic agent-change application is
open only to companies **in good standing**, and this one is not. This is a
state-imposed sequence, not a process preference.

| # | Step | Form / route | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Engage the agent service, get its Illinois street address and its consent | vendor | Darrell (payment) |
| 2 | Call ILSOS Business Services and have them compute the exact delinquency | phone — see script | Claude-drafted, Darrell dials |
| 3 | File reinstatement **with the agent change inside it** | **LLC-35.40/45.65** + **LLC-1.36/1.37** | filed together |
| 4 | File every delinquent annual report in the same packet | **LLC-50.1**, one per year | same packet |
| 5 | Pay reinstatement fee + all delinquent report fees + all penalties | with the packet | Darrell |
| 6 | Confirm status returns to good standing, then order certified Articles | ILSOS | Claude can verify online |
| 7 | Only then execute the IP assignment | attorney pack Schedule A | after step 6 |

**Step 7 is the whole point.** Every one of the 25 register rows fails the
"Owned" test until step 6 clears.
