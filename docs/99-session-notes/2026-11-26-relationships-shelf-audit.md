**Method:** every one of the 996 lessons mounted in Learn on 2026-11-26 (`learnInventory()`), read by its title, big idea, the opening of its lesson body and its section headings, never by keyword; six readers in parallel, each over one sixth of the catalog, each re-reading its sixth when the criteria widened; then a coordinator pass over every close call. Excluded lessons are not listed one by one; the near-misses (lessons that touch relating but whose subject is something else) are, each with its reason.

**Result:** 303 lessons on the shelf (2 new lessons at the top, then six groups), 149 near-misses. The shelf was 35 (DR-1686); every one of those 35 is still on it. A test (`app/src/__tests__/relationships-shelf-audit.test.js`) fails the build when a shelf id is missing from the tables below or carries no reason, or a lesson listed here is not on the shelf.

**Not read in full:** the audit read each lesson's opening (about 900 characters of the body) plus its headings, not every word of every lesson. A lesson whose relational teaching sits only deep in its body may be a missed inclusion; Darrell or a reader can name it and it is added with its reason. `re-review: 2026-12-26`.
