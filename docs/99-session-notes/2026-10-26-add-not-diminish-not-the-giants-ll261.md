**Why one lesson and not two.** This was the hardest judgment in the intake. Two sendings arriving hours apart on the same passage, pulling opposite ways, is the raw material for exactly the both-sides piece this platform exists to remove. DR-0098 forbids it — and names Genesis 6 "sons of God" as its own worked example. So the phrase is taught from the Word's own usage (Job 1:6; Job 38:7), and **both** sendings are held to one measure that was already in the text: "Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish ought from it" (Deuteronomy 4:2). One prohibition, two directions, which are precisely the two failures that arrived. The first adds; the second diminishes. Jesus names the single root of both: "Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God" (Matthew 22:29). The dispute is named in order to educate past it; the reader is never asked to vote. Worth noting: the two sendings actually **agree** that the "sons of God" are heavenly beings. Their real quarrel is about how far a claim may be carried, which is the lesson's subject.
