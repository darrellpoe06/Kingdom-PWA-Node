## The shape

1. The Word testified first — Luke 1:1-4 (its purpose clause is *certainty*),
   John 21:24, John 19:35, 2 Peter 1:16, 1 John 1:1, Acts 1:3,
   1 Corinthians 15:6, Acts 26:26.
2. He put it in writing, on purpose — Habakkuk 2:2, Job 19:23-24, John 20:31,
   John 21:25, Deuteronomy 6:6.
3. The promise that needs no artifact — Matthew 24:35, Isaiah 40:8,
   1 Peter 1:24-25, Psalms 119:89, Psalms 12:6-7, Jeremiah 1:12, Psalms 138:2.
4. A scrap the size of a card — P52, established and contested, separated.
5. The names they could not have guessed — Matthew 10:2-4, Luke 1:5,
   Deuteronomy 19:15.
6. Where the famous writers missed — Luke 3:1-2, Matthew 27:2, Luke 2:1-2,
   Acts 18:12.
7. Even the mockers testify — 1 Corinthians 1:23, John 20:25, Matthew 27:37,
   John 19:19-20, John 20:28, Hebrews 1:8, John 1:29, Philippians 2:10-11.
8. Evidence has a ceiling, and He said where it is — Luke 16:31, John 20:29,
   Hebrews 11:1, Hebrews 11:3, 1 Corinthians 2:14, Romans 1:20,
   Colossians 1:17.
9. Searching it out is not doubt — Acts 17:11, 1 Thessalonians 5:21,
   1 Peter 3:15, Proverbs 18:17, Proverbs 25:2, 2 Timothy 2:15,
   2 Timothy 3:16, John 17:17.
10. The practice — Deuteronomy 6:7, Proverbs 27:17, Revelation 12:11,
    John 3:16, closing on John 20:31.

Two parables. **The Receipt in the Coat Pocket** (light): the receipt did not
make the story true; it took the excuse out of a grandson's hand — Luke 1:4.

the shelf as a place to stand while he decided not to kneel — Luke 16:31.

## What was verified, and what was refused

The DR-0192 pass ran **before** first ship, by independent live research. Three
tiers, and the third is the one that shaped the lesson. Established: the Rylands
papyrus 457 facts, the 1961 Caesarea discovery, **prefect** on the stone against
Tacitus's **procurator** (and the Gospels' general word, right either way), the
Palatine graffito at c. AD 200, Bauckham against Tal Ilan's lexicon. Contested
and named narrowly: every P52 date rests on palaeography alone — Roberts, then
Orsini and Clarysse's c. AD 125-175, then Nongbri arguing the precision is
unsupportable, his view not universally accepted and the second-century
placement still standard.

**Refused, because it could not be verified:** the lecture's **260 sheep** for
Codex Sinaiticus (the project's own description says primarily calf skin),
**Damaris and Lydia attested nowhere** and the **10%** unique-names statistic,
the January day for P1, and "1,900 years old" for a c. AD 200 graffito. None of
them appears anywhere in the module, and the lesson's own gate fails if any
comes back.

One doctrinally charged item was not carried as the source carried it: the
lecture names a group in order to rebut a stake-not-a-cross claim, and this
lesson teaches the same ground from the Word alone — the plural nails
(John 20:25), the notice over His head (Matthew 27:37), the title in three
languages (John 19:19-20) — and names no group. DR-0098 is satisfied by
teaching the text, not by prosecuting anybody.
