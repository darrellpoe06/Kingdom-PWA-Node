## The answer, in eleven parts

1. **Who Judah is, and the timeline the Word carries.** Praise (Genesis 29:35); man, tribe, kingdom, people; the split (1 Kings 12:20), Assyria (2 Kings 17:6, 17:18), Babylon (2 Kings 25:21), the return (Ezra 1:1-5), *"salvation is of the Jews"* (John 4:22), Rome foretold (Luke 21:6, 21:20, 21:24). Dates stated plainly: about 931, 722, 586, 538 BC; AD 70.
2. **Scattered, never lost.** Amos 9:9; Deuteronomy 30:4; Luke 2:36 (Anna of Asher); James 1:1; Acts 26:7; Revelation 7:4; 2 Timothy 2:19. The lost-tribes debate named to teach past it: Ezra 2:62; 1 Timothy 1:4; Titus 3:9; Deuteronomy 29:29 — no modern nation named a tribe.
3. **The Way.** Acts 9:2; 19:9; 19:23; 22:4; 24:22; defined by Acts 24:14 — believing all things written; the name is His (John 14:6); Christians later (Acts 11:26).
4. **A tribe from every nation — the precedent inside Judah.** Caleb the Kenezite counted of Judah (Numbers 13:6; 32:12; Joshua 14:14); Ruth and Rahab (Ruth 1:16; Matthew 1:5); Exodus 12:48-49; Isaiah 56:3; Psalms 87:6; Esther 8:17. The Word's shape: grafted in among them (Romans 11:17), fellowcitizens (Ephesians 2:19), Abraham's seed (Galatians 3:29), a holy nation (1 Peter 2:9-10), one fold (John 10:16), kings and priests out of every nation (Revelation 5:9-10); Revelation 7:4 beside 7:9.
5. **Joined, never replacing.** Romans 11:1, 11:18, 11:20-21, 11:23-24, 11:28-29.
6. **Whom to listen to.** One Voice — Deuteronomy 18:15, 18-19; Matthew 17:5; Acts 3:22-23; Hebrews 1:1-2; John 10:27. One test — Isaiah 8:20; Acts 17:11; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 1:8; Matthew 7:16. Judah's honour kept — Romans 3:1-2; 1:16; Zechariah 8:23 with its condition. The two untils — Luke 21:24; Romans 11:25-26; the gathering promised.
7. **Psyops, and known by love.** The parallels are the Word's method (Ecclesiastes 1:9; 1 Corinthians 10:11). The war named (Ephesians 6:12), the lie's father (John 8:44), the disguise (2 Corinthians 11:14-15), the reach (Revelation 12:9), the inversion (Isaiah 5:20), the cost (2 Thessalonians 2:10-11); identity forged (Revelation 2:9; 3:9 read to its end — He exposes, not we). The news or the Word (Isaiah 8:12-13; Psalms 118:9; John 17:17; Proverbs 14:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21); reasoning from known history is the Word's discipline (Luke 1:3-4; Proverbs 18:13, 18:17; Deuteronomy 19:15). Their Ways (Matthew 7:20; 1 John 3:10; Galatians 5:22-23; John 7:24; 1 Samuel 16:7); their suffering (Deuteronomy 28:37, 28:65; Amos 3:2; Jeremiah 30:11; Isaiah 53:3; John 15:18-20; Matthew 10:22; 2 Timothy 3:12; Revelation 12:17 — suffering alone proves nothing). Known by love: John 13:35; 1 John 3:14; 4:7-8; Romans 12:9 (without dissimulation); 2 Corinthians 10:5.
8. **More than conquerors.** Romans 8:36 before 8:37; Psalms 44:22; 120:7; James 4:1; Matthew 26:52; Matthew 5:44; Romans 12:19; Revelation 12:11. The condition: Proverbs 16:7; Deuteronomy 28:1, 28:7; 2 Chronicles 7:14. The raising His: Psalms 33:16; 146:3; 75:6-7; Daniel 2:21; Psalms 113:7-8; 147:6; John 11:25; Hosea 6:2; Revelation 6:9-11; Luke 18:7-8. The undermining named: Psalms 94:20; Isaiah 10:1-2; Habakkuk 1:4; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Acts 5:29; Psalms 37:7-11.

Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; Luke 10:7; James 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 22:13; Proverbs 22:22-23; Jacob under Laban (Genesis 31:7, 31:9, 31:42); Colossians 3:23-24; 1 Peter 2:18-20; Galatians 6:9; James 5:7, 5:11; Proverbs 16:8; Matthew 6:33.
10. **Which country; Tubman; a means of making money; but if not.** The measure, not a flag (Psalms 2:1-2; Proverbs 14:34; Psalms 33:12; 9:17; Jeremiah 18:7-10; Daniel 4:17); the laws the Word judges (Exodus 21:16; 1 Timothy 1:10; Deuteronomy 23:15-16).
