## The Word first, and what it is doing

The hour opens on **Psalm 75:6-7**. Three compass directions are named and the fourth is left out,
because the answer was never a direction: *God is the judge: he putteth down one, and setteth up
another.* Promotion is a verdict handed down, not a prize won. **Luke 22:24** then supplies the
scene — a strife among the disciples, at the supper, after the cup, over which of them should be
*accounted* the greatest. A league table is that same strife with a ticker attached.

The eight movements run: promotion handed down (Psalm 75, Daniel 2:21, Daniel 4:17,
1 Samuel 2:7-8) · the ranking quarrel and its downward measure (Luke 22:24-27, Mark 10:43-45,

**1 Samuel 13:19-22 is the find of the hour.** The reshoring item is, in Scripture's own terms, a

make them swords or spears*. The part a modern reader misses is that the consequence reached the
FARM before the army: Israel walked down to the Philistines to sharpen a share, a coulter, an axe
and a mattock, and paid for it. On the day of battle two men in the whole army had a weapon.

## Where this gate is narrower than its name (DR-1467, stated rather than smoothed)

**The book list is DERIVED, not typed.** DR-1467's second measured example was a verse sweep whose
book list omitted Revelation while Revelation 3:7 stood quoted in all five reader places. This
file builds its book list from the module's own parenthetical references, so it cannot omit a book
the lesson cites, and there is a proven-to-catch case that drops Psalms from the list and watches
the psalm quotations orphan.

**But it is still narrower than its name in one way, and that is said here rather than smoothed:**
a quotation whose parenthetical reference is malformed in a way the reference regex does not match
would never reach the derived book list. So the guarantee is carried by the wider check — *every
double-quoted span in every string of the module must be Scripture* — which reads `allStrings(M)`
and not only the five reader places. The derived list is a convenience over that, not a substitute.

Two more things this gate does not claim: the whole-course contiguity claim belongs to the catalog
gates, not to one lesson (DR-1146); and nothing here measures the deployed page — CI's own clone
and the deploy run on the merge SHA are the witnesses for that.
