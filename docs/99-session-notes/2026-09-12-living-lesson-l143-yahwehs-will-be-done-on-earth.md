## The spine

His economy is legislation with mechanisms, not a mood: a seventh-year release, no usury from a brother, restitution at principal plus a fifth, gleaning edges, the open hand, a fiftieth-year return. And it carries a guarantee no credential offers — `Save when there shall be no poor among you`. That is why Darrell calls it the highest-level documentation, and why treating a human framework as its peer is a category error rather than humility.

Then the turn that makes it a lesson rather than an indictment, which is his and which the tests pin: the wealth **was produced**, therefore capacity was never the missing variable. What was taken was the return. So it is producible, and the present position is better in every input than the conditions under which it was first demonstrated. Haggai answers it directly.
