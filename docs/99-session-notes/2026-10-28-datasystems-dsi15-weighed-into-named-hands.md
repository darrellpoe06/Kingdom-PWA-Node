## What the request was

One word. The newsletter's lead item concerns knowing what hardware and software an organisation actually owns, and the forgotten machines still answering on the network. Everything else in that edition is advertising or an unrelated story, and none of it is taught.

## Why this module and not another

The course had fourteen modules teaching a volunteer to **operate** every surface — the data layer, the loops, the NAS, the GPU node, the wall, the service day, roles, onboarding — and had never asked the prior question: *how does this house know what it actually has?* That is the gap the forward filled.

## How the spine was chosen

By counting, not by theme. Every `anchor.ref` across the whole catalog was collected and searched before a verse was picked, because the obvious readings of this subject are exactly the ones other weeks already carry:

- **Numbers 1** (numbering the people) — taken, twice (`Numbers 1:3`, `Numbers 1:46`).
- **John 10:3** (the shepherd who calls his own sheep by name) — taken.
- **2 Samuel 24** (David numbering Israel) — taken.
- **Luke 16:2** (give an account of thy stewardship) — taken.
- **Proverbs 27:23** (know the condition of thy flocks) — taken by dsi3, in this same course.

Measured at **zero** occurrences and therefore free: **Ezra 8**, **Numbers 4**, **Exodus 38**, **Luke 15:8**. The lesson stands on Ezra 8 — weighed out into named hands, watched on the road, weighed again on arrival, written down — which is the completest chain of custody in the Word and happens to answer the newsletter's own plainest diagnosis, *an accounting or systems error where things are not reconciled*.

## The reality-trace, run first

The lesson sends the reader at a surface that genuinely exists and teaches what the code actually does, not a drawn version of it:

- `app/src/lib/inventory.js` — `onHandFor` derives on-hand from an append-only ledger; `MOVEMENT_KINDS` = in / out / adjust / transfer-out / transfer-in; `itemHistoryFromMovements` reads one item's history.
- `app/src/lib/help-content.js`, the `inventory` entry — the live help registry says in its own words that counts are run *to reconcile reality against the ledger*, and that *inventory you cannot trust is worse than none*.

The lesson teaches that where shelf and ledger disagree the **shelf governs**, and the correction is itself a recorded movement — which is Ezra at both ends of the road.

## The two stories

- **The Second Weighing** (parable, light) — four radio microphones lent across town, serial numbers read aloud and signed for, three returned, the fourth found in nineteen minutes because the sheet said four. *Ezra 8:34*.
