## The find: the moving goalpost is named in the Torah, and it is not a metaphor

> **"Thou shalt not have in thy bag divers weights, a great and a small."** (Deuteronomy 25:13)

A merchant carried two stones — the heavy one when buying from you, the light one when selling to you. Same scale, same manner, **a different stone depending on who stood across the counter**. The Torah closes the indoor loophole in the next verse (25:14), states the requirement positively and ties it to length of days in the land (25:15), and the verdict is repeated until it can't be softened: Proverbs 20:10, 20:23, 11:1. Leviticus 19:35 puts **judgment first**, then the tools — this is a heart that keeps two standards, not a commerce regulation.

So a question that raises its threshold the instant it is met **is** divers weights. Naming it does something for the reader: *you were not failing a test.*

## The three moves the lesson makes

1. **Name it** — and note you don't owe a rigged question an answer. Jesus read intent (Matthew 22:18; Luke 20:23) and once simply declined (Matthew 21:27). Proverbs 26:4-5 are held **together** and deliberately not resolved into a rule.
2. **Render the honour owed** — Romans 13:7 lists honour with *tribute and custom*, i.e. taxes. A debt, not a courtesy. First installment: **get the name right** (the voice transcript had Matzilica, Daniel Hill, Patricia Beth).
3. **Tell it exactly right** — because the same verse forbids the padded weight as well as the light one (Proverbs 11:1).

## The just weight applied to our own side

This is the half a later edit is most likely to soften, so it's gated hardest:

- Latimer **improved the filament**; he did not invent the bulb — and the improvement is what made electric light infrastructure rather than a curiosity.
- Dr. Daniel Hale Williams repaired the **pericardium** in 1893 and the patient lived; calling it the first open-heart surgery overstates it — and he needs no help, having founded **Provident Hospital** in 1891 because Black physicians and nurses were shut out.
- The widely repeated claim that Dr. Shirley Ann Jackson's research produced caller ID / call waiting is **not well documented**; her actual record (first Black woman with an MIT doctorate, chaired the NRC, president of RPI) needs no story.
- The doorknob attribution rests on thin documentation; the gas mask, traffic signal, dry-cleaning patent and ironing-board patent are solid.
