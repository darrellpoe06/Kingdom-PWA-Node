## The one open doctrinal point

Darrell offered, as his belief, that the spirit Yahweh gave us has always existed, and asked that it be Word first. The Word says the spirit is breathed in by Him (Genesis 2:7), is in man (Job 32:8), and returns to Him who gave it (Ecclesiastes 12:7); it does not say it pre-existed. Per DR-0098 (where the Word is reticent, stay with it), L345 teaches what is written and says the Word goes no further. His words are kept in the definition as his. Raised for his word.
