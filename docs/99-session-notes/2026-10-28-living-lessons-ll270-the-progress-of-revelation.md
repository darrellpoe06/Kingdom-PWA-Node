## What his word became

A Living Lesson that takes the chart to the Word. The chart's own heading is Hebrews 1:1 (sundry times, divers manners). The Word records the writing being commanded from Moses (Exodus 17:14) to John (Revelation 1:11), dates itself under named kings to the day (Haggai 1:1), and supplies two rules that settle most of the chart from the text: a book is no older than its last event (2 Kings 25:27; 2 Chronicles 36:22 and Ezra 1:1; Ruth 4:22; Proverbs 25:1) and no younger than its first reader (Daniel 9:2; Luke 20:42; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 3:16). The chart's blanks (Job, Hebrews, Samuel, Kings, Esther, the two question marks) are kept, because the Word keeps them (Deuteronomy 29:29). The close is what did not move in fifteen centuries (Psalms 119:89; Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 24:35).
