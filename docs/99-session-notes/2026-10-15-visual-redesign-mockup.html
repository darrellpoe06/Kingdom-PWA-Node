The Heritage Redesign

The same application, every room intact, wearing a new finish: forest green navigation, navy selection, ivory rooms, gold and terracotta trim. Below are the proposed home surface and one internal page (Big Picture), each at desktop and phone width. Every screen is drawn from records the app already holds; rows marked sample come from the app's own family-of-4 working scenario, never from the live database.

1 · The palette, measured

These are the exact values that will ship as the new Heritage theme. Every text-on-surface pairing was measured against WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1) with the repository's own contrast math. Gold is a fill and a rule, never body text on a light ground; where gold must read as text it darkens to the gold-text value. True red is reserved for the Blood of Jesus (DR-0099), so "overdue" wears terracotta.

2 · Home, desktop

Sidebar replaces the scrolling tab row at laptop width and holds every one of the 34 sections, grouped. The welcome banner is page content, not chrome, so the text-size rules that cap the header are untouched. The six navigation cards, Today's Focus, Quick Actions and the unread count are the Life Hub's existing real-record tiles (lib/life-hub.js, lib/daily-focus.js). Recent Messages reads the Messages threads; Recent Activity reads the records log and the build stamp.

4 · What stays exactly as it is

Every section and sub-tab

  • 34 top-level sections, all their second-row tabs (Books ×12, Church ×20, TLC ×8, Create ×8) and ~60 in-page tab rows keep their ids and their order.
  • Nothing is merged or removed. Grouping in the sidebar and in More is presentation only.

Accessibility

  • Text size A to A44, read-aloud, voice, the five existing color themes and the comfort bar all stay, gathered under Settings and still one tap away in the bottom bar.
  • Chrome never grows with the text size; the words do (DR-0438, DR-1011).

Data and logic

  • No schema, formula, permission, integration, authentication or payment change. Components are restyled; libraries and records are untouched.
  • Nothing on a surface shows a number that is not read from a record.

Color theology

  • True red stays reserved for the Blood of Jesus (DR-0099). Overdue and alert states wear terracotta and amber.
  • The existing themes (Cream, Snow, Glacier, Sapphire, Rose, Midnight) remain choices; Heritage becomes the first-run default.
