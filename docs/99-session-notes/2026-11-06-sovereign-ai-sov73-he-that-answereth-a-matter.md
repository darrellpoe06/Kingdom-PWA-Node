## The three tiers, claim by claim (DR-0100)

**DOCUMENTED** — measured here, stated plainly: the forward and its hour and its one word; the letter's own text; the six probe results above. Also documented *about the publication*: it printed its own cautions beside its own headline, which is more than most do.

**ATTRIBUTED** — the publication's figure carried as the publication's figure, never on our authority: 83% / 36% / 22% on hard journal cases; 80% / 47% on more typical emergency cases; 304 hard cases in the set; a cost at roughly half to a third with fewer tests; and the letter's own note that most of the gain came from the models underneath, one scoring 78.9% alone with the orchestrating layer adding about four points.

**UNCHECKED** — named narrowly, never smeared over the subject: every one of those figures, since nothing opened; whether one living patient was helped, which the letter reports the company itself calls still to be tested; whether the comparison was like for like, since the letter says the physicians were primary care doctors and general internists working **alone**; and the reported financing, which the letter itself hedges with *reportedly*.

**OVER-REACH, corrected by the Word while the true data under it stands** — the *medical superintelligence* framing the letter reports an executive using. The lesson refuses the **word** (Job 38:3; 1 Corinthians 13:12) and says in the same breath that a faster searcher is a good and real thing, that it is not a superior mind, and that **the true result underneath the inflated word is untouched by our refusing the word**. That last clause is the whole of DR-0100's third tier, and it is the clause that keeps the refusal from becoming the under-claiming failure the same record forbids.

## The Word, and how the spine was chosen

Word first (DR-0127 / DR-0282). The anchor is **Proverbs 18:13**, read for what it actually does: it never mentions the answer being wrong, so the fault is on the **SEQUENCE**. That one observation is why a benchmark score cannot settle the question it is being used to settle, and everything else hangs off it.

Eight movements: the verdict on answering early (Proverbs 18:13; 15:28; 18:15; James 1:19; Proverbs 20:5) · the One who asks though He knows (Genesis 3:9; 18:21; Job 38:3; Mark 10:51; John 5:6; John 2:25) · the changed test (Proverbs 25:2; 18:17; Jeremiah 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:12) · the counsel of many and its named failure (Proverbs 11:14; 15:22; 24:6; 1 Kings 22:6-8) · the cost of asking (Luke 14:28; Proverbs 24:3-4) · a score is not a healing (Deuteronomy 19:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 14:15; Proverbs 27:2) · the physician honored and the healing Yahweh's (Matthew 9:12; Colossians 4:14; Exodus 15:26; 2 Chronicles 16:12) · the other number (Proverbs 27:1). Then the standing Godhead confession and growth measure.

**The hinge of the second movement is John 2:25 standing beside Mark 10:51.** The text says flatly that He *needed not that any should testify of man*, and two gospels later He is still asking a blind man what he wants. So the asking is **not** a gap-filler; it is **honor**. That is the sentence that turns the week from a technique into a character, and it is the reason the lesson can say plainly that a system asking to raise its score is doing a mechanically similar thing for an unrelated reason.

**The fourth movement reads the counsel promise exactly.** Proverbs promises the multitude **SAFETY** and **ESTABLISHMENT** three times and never promises **TRUTH** — and then 1 Kings 22 shows the failure in its most exact form: four hundred agreeing, assembled by the man who already knew what he wanted to hear, with the one dissenting voice known by name, locally available, and excluded *for being unwelcome rather than for being wrong*. The tell the lesson hands the reader is therefore not whether a panel argues but **whether anybody in it can lose** — which is the question to put to a multi-agent panel whose own publication says most of the gain came from the models beneath it.
