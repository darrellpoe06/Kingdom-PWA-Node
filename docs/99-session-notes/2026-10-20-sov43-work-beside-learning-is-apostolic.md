## What his word became

Week 43, in ten movements for the adult and four bands written for their readers, every verse filled from `app/public/bible/kjv` by `scripts/sov43-fill.mjs`:

1. **The Word-first algorithm, five steps, in order** — the verse as written; its context with the sense given; across the Word; rightly divided; only then the thing in front of you. The diagnosis the page hands over: *most arguments among believers are two people who ran step five first.*
2. **Context is King, run on the verse he was reading** — 15:1 sets the clock, 15:3 draws the circle (the covenant brother; the foreigner may still be asked, so no universal cancellation), 15:9-11 name the sin the release tempts, 15:15 is the floor: *redemption remembered on a calendar.* Context did not soften the verse; it gave it its weight.
3. **The release across the Word** — a servant freed in the seventh year, the fiftieth year, the release kept after the exile, taken back and avenged in Jeremiah 34, promised in a Person in Isaiah 61, read aloud in Nazareth with *"This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears,"* prayed in *"forgive us our debts,"* told as the king and the unpayable debt, done at the cross (Colossians 2:14), done by Paul's own hand for Onesimus. It is called the LORD's release because **He is the Releaser.**
4. **"Apostolic" explained** — a sent one; the pattern the sent ones received from Him, lived before the churches, taught, and left to be followed. The test: received from Him, lived, left to be followed. If yes, not ours to experiment with; ours to walk in.
5. **Work beside learning is that pattern** — the carpenter of Nazareth, fishermen called from their nets, Paul's tents with the hours and the reason named, *"to make ourselves an ensample unto you to follow us."* An experiment is tried to find out whether it works; an apostolic pattern is received because it already did (Jeremiah 6:16). **And the limit Paul named** (1 Corinthians 9:14-18; Luke 10:7): not "no paid teachers," not a verdict on the sick, the widow, or the apprentice. Context is King on that verse too.
