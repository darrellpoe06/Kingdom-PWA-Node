## The teaching

Nine movements, Word first, no figure from the send standing above the frame:

1. **The agreement is not the last step.** Amos 3:3; Psalms 133:1; Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 (the second man is there for the fall, not for the company); Proverbs 15:22; 11:14.
2. **The maker and the counter, in one passage, by name.** Exodus 38:21-22, 38:24 — *after the shekel of the sanctuary*, one agreed unit, because a figure signifies nothing until both sides measure in the same thing.
3. **The only recorded restraint on procurement in Scripture.** Exodus 36:5-7 — and the **craftsmen** raised it, because the office nearest the material sees the over-buy first. Ask the crew, not the committee.
4. **One chest, two hands on it.** 2 Kings 12:9-11 and 2 Chronicles 24:11 — then the reversal at 2 Kings 12:15: *they reckoned not with the men… for they dealt faithfully*. Control at the gate buys trust downstream; a frightened house audits everybody a little and nobody properly.
5. **Honest in the sight of men.** 2 Corinthians 8:20-21 — Paul built a control nobody had asked him for, so an accusation would be impossible rather than merely untrue.
6. **A numbers complaint, answered with an org chart.** Acts 6:1-5 — character stated *before* the appointment.
7. **One person in both seats.** Genesis 41:33-35, 41:48-49 — *until he left numbering*. The plan worked; the Word still recorded the hour the count stopped. Set the stop-counting line before you reach it.
8. **The Resources, and the body.** Proverbs 24:3-4 (Wisdom builds, Understanding establishes, Knowledge fills — capitalised per DR-0530), 27:23-24, 13:10; 1 Corinthians 12:21; Philippians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 13:2.
9. **Whose money it is.** Haggai 2:8; 1 Chronicles 29:14-16; Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:19; and the One the account is rendered to — John 1:29, John 3:16, Hebrews 1:8, Colossians 1:17.

Two parables carry it in story: *The Two Ledgers* (two men, each improving the only number he was judged by, and the house poorer for it) and *The Chest With Two Hands On It* (one page, one shared number, forty minutes a month). The week's work is bounded: the two names, the two numbers, the two hands.

## Where my own gate was narrower than its name

It was, and its own first run said so — in the opposite direction from the usual failure. The leak check matched `the six steps are`, and so fired on the child band's **refusal**: *"we do not say what the six steps are. We do not know."* That is the very sentence the file exists to require. A gate that reports the refusal as the leak trains a reader to delete the honest sentence, which is worse than having no gate. The pattern now matches an **enumeration** only (`step one`, `the third step`), both halves are proven-to-catch, and the refusal is asserted positively and separately in every reader place.
