# 2026-09-06 — The Name in the corpus: LORD (Yahweh) vs Lord (Adonai), corrected with two witnesses

**Lane:** `claude/yahweh-actions-by-century-exjvvv` → PR → auto-merge.
**Closes:** the "corpus question" left open this morning (`2026-09-06-kjv-case-paid-down-and-the-corpus-question.md`, re-review 2026-09-20) — closed the same day, because the second witness turned out to be fetchable from here.

## What was wrong, stated plainly (DR-0100 tier 1)
The in-repo KJV (`app/public/bible/kjv`, fetched verbatim from aruljohn/Bible-kjv) mis-rendered the Name in **111 verses**. The case is not decoration: the KJV prints small-caps **LORD** for the Tetragrammaton (Yahweh), **Lord** for Adonai, and **Lord GOD** for Adonai Yahweh. So:

- Genesis 15:2 printed "LORD God" where the KJV reads "Lord GOD".
- Exodus 33:9 printed "the Lord talked with Moses" — hiding the Name.
- Psalm 3:1 printed "Lord, how are they increased" — the Name lowercased.
- The New Testament printed LORD in **29** verses (Acts 15:11 "the LORD Jesus Christ", John 20:28 "My LORD and my God") where the KJV prints it in **five**: Psalm 110:1 quoted at Matthew 22:44 / Mark 12:36 / Luke 20:42 / Acts 2:34, and the title at Revelation 19:16 — and it had **lost** the Name at Acts 2:34.

Readers saw this text in the in-app Bible. It was the upstream source's rendering, faithfully ingested — not an ingest defect, and not the adversary-name lowercasing of PR #1397, which is Darrell's directive and stands untouched.

## How it was settled — fetched, never remembered (DR-0076)
1. **A second public-domain KJV** (thiagobodruk/bible, `en_kjv.json`, reachable through the proxy) compared verse by verse: 289 case-only differences, of which 191 are the deliberate adversary lowercasing and **98 are the Name**.
2. **The WEB already on disk as an oracle.** The World English Bible prints "Yahweh" exactly where the Hebrew has the Name and "Lord" for Adonai. Mapped to a sequence (Y/L) per verse, it arbitrates the Old Testament deterministically: of 59 disputed OT verses it vindicated the second source in **51** and ours in **0**; 8 it could not decide (it phrases them differently), and those stay uncorrected and named.
3. **The NT rule** (LORD only inside OT quotations of the Name) plus the second source for the 24 strays and Acts 2:34.
4. A second sweep took every remaining OT verse where the corpus disagreed with the oracle **and** the second source agreed with the oracle — 38 more, every one with two witnesses.

## What changed
- `scripts/kjv-name-case-corrections.mjs` + `.json` — a **manifest** of 111 verses with before/after text; case-only over the tokens LORD/Lord/GOD/God; idempotent; re-applicable after any re-ingest (`node scripts/kjv-name-case-corrections.mjs`). Fields: `undecided` (8), `webPhrasesDifferently` (10 where both KJV witnesses agree with the corpus).
- `app/public/bible/kjv/*.json` — 28 books, 111 verses, letter case only. Also Proverbs 22:1's printer small-caps ("A GOOD name" → "A good name").
- The lessons and the by-century verse table followed the corpus (23 quotations re-cased by the same case-only fixer; Job 28:28 "the fear of the Lord" in L91's level prose; `yahweh-by-century-verses.json` regenerated by its own script).

## The gate (`kjv-corpus-name-case.test.js`, 8 tests)
Every manifest correction is in place and changed only case; the NT prints LORD in exactly the five verses; the OT agrees with the WEB oracle on every comparable verse (5,870) except the five named where both KJV witnesses side with the corpus — a new disagreement fails, a vanished one fails (the list must tell the truth); the meaning-bearing cases read right; proven-to-catch with Exodus 33:9's pre-correction text; the adversary stays low. `scripture-provenance`: `kjv-case` **0** (the last 9 were the corpus's, not the lessons').
