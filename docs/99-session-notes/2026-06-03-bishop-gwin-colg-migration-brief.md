# For Bishop Gwin — The Church of the Living God + PoeTech

*A brief from Darrell Poe. No timeline pressure; this is yours to govern.*

---

**What PoeTech is.** PoeTech is a sovereign family-and-church financial stewardship platform I have been building — a tool that helps a household, and a congregation, see their money the way Scripture asks us to steward it: with understanding, with order, and with the soul first. From the beginning I have built it with The Church of the Living God in mind as the named first congregation it serves. Our church is not an afterthought or a test market; it is the proof that this can bless the people we love.

**What is already true on the COLG site.** TheChurchOfTheLivingGod.com is live and healthy on its current host (Turbify). It already carries your online giving link, your pastoral content, the Sunday Worship time (11 AM), Wednesday Bible Study (1 PM and 6 PM), and the 312 E. Bradley Ave. address. PoeTech does not replace any of that. It works *with* what you already have.

**What PoeTech offers COLG.** (a) A **free Church module** for the congregation — part of what is free for everyone, forever. (b) **Free PoeTech+ for life** for the first 100 COLG families, as our founding Loved Ones cohort. (c) A **Sermon-to-Content pipeline** that turns your sermons into an audio archive, searchable transcripts, sharable clips, and study guides — evangelism content, multiplied, with your approval on every piece. (d) **Sovereign data residency** — the platform runs on our family's own server here, not a vendor's cloud, so the congregation's information stays the congregation's. (e) A **10% tithe surface** configured to your teaching, integrated with the giving link you already use — never a new platform imposed on the church.

**What PoeTech asks of COLG — time and partnership, nothing more.** (a) Your blessing to serve COLG as our first community and to name the church publicly in that "first community served" language. (b) About 30 minutes to record one introductory sermon, so we can pilot the Sermon-to-Content pipeline. (c) Your input, when you are willing, on how the Church study assistant draws on Scripture and tradition — we hold the Word first and the Body of Christ as one, and your shepherd's judgment shapes that. (d) The opportunity, when you are ready, to introduce PoeTech to the congregation at a Sunday or Bible Study.

**What stays under your authority.** Nothing on COLG's surfaces changes without your blessing. The giving link stays yours. The Sunday Worship time stays yours. The pastoral content stays yours. PoeTech serves COLG; COLG remains COLG.

**A word from Scripture.** The Word reaches the poor through congregations willing to send it — *"And how are they to hear without someone preaching?"* (**ESV — Romans 10:14**). And the order matters: *"Beloved, I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health, as it goes well with your soul"* (**ESV — 3 John 1:2**) — soul prosperity first, and the rest follows. The Lord Himself sat down and watched who gave, and saw the widow's two copper coins as more than all the rest: *"she out of her poverty has put in everything she had, all she had to live on"* (**ESV — Mark 12:41-44**). PoeTech simply makes those stewardship realities visible with honest data, so the calling COLG already carries is honored where the congregation can see it.

**Next step.** Bishop, when you are ready to look — I will walk you through the platform myself. No timeline pressure; this is yours to govern.
