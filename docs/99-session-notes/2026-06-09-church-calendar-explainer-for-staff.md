# A Better Way to Share the Church Calendar — for Our Staff & Leadership

*A short, plain explainer. No tech background needed. — The Church of the Living God (The Love Corner)*

---

## What we're changing

Right now, when the calendar changes, someone makes a **picture of it** and posts that picture. That works — but a picture can only do so much. We're moving to a simple calendar **you type into once**, and the app takes care of putting it everywhere else.

**You fill in one form. The system does the rest.** That's the whole idea.

---

## How it works for you

When something is happening — a service, a Bible study, a revival, a special program — you (or whoever handles the calendar) open the church app and **add it on a simple form**:

- What is it? (e.g., "Sunday Worship")
- When? Pick the date and time.
- Does it repeat? Just tap **"every Sunday"** or **"2nd Monday each month"** or **"one time only."**
- Anything else people should know? (optional)

Tap save. **That's it.** You never touch anything technical — no settings, no "feeds," no formats. If you can fill out a form on your phone, you can run the whole calendar.

Even easier: when events come up in meeting notes, the app can **suggest** them for you, and you just tap **"Approve."**

---

## Why this is better than posting a picture

Everything below happens **automatically**, the moment you save — you don't do any of it:

- **It updates everywhere at once.** Change a time once, and the website, the app, and everyone's reminders all change together. No re-making and re-posting a picture every time something moves.
- **People can search it.** "When's the next revival?" — they can find it instead of scrolling through old images.
- **It sends reminders.** The app can gently remind people before a service or event. A picture can't do that.
- **It's accessible.** People using a screen reader, or reading on a small phone, can actually read it. A picture of text leaves those folks out.
- **Visitors always see the *current* schedule.** No more wondering if the picture they're looking at is from last month.
- **It feeds the app on its own.** The calendar you type becomes the calendar the whole system uses — automatically.
- **Anyone who wants it on their own phone calendar can have it.** If a member likes to see church events in their Google or Apple calendar, the app gives them that — and *you never have to set it up or understand it.* The system makes it for them.

## What you do vs. what the system does

| You do | The system does (automatically) |
|---|---|
| Fill in a simple form (or tap "Approve") | Show it on the church website |
| Pick "repeats every Sunday," etc. | Show it in the app |
| Save | Send reminders |
| | Keep visitors' view current |
| | Make it work on phones + for screen readers |
| | Offer it to anyone who wants it in their own calendar |

**Your whole job is the form. Everything in the right-hand column is handled for you.**

---

## Why we're doing this

The picture-of-the-calendar habit started for a good reason: the other tools felt complicated and out of reach. **This is built so it doesn't feel that way.** It's one friendly form, on a phone or computer, and the hard parts are carried by the system — not by you.

The goal is simple: **less work for our staff, and a calendar that actually serves our people and our visitors well.** The schedule is part of how we welcome people and care for them — it should be easy for us and clear for them.
