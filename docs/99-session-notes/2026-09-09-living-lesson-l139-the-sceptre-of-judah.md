## The answer

The Word says yes to all three, and it names the who.

1. **The verse and its frame.** *"The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be."* (Genesis 49:10) Jacob's heading over the whole blessing: *"that which shall befall you in the last days."* (Genesis 49:1) So *until the end* is the text's own frame. Every line of 49:8-12 is picked up later (the brethren bowing, the lion, the sceptre, the gathering, the colt, the garments in the blood of grapes).
2. **Who Judah is** — four ways the Word uses the name: the man named praise (Genesis 29:35), who sold Joseph (Genesis 37:27) and later stood surety and offered himself *"instead of the lad"* (Genesis 43:9; 44:33); the tribe that went first (Judges 1:2; Numbers 2:9; Psalms 78:68; 1 Chronicles 5:2); the kingdom that kept David's house (1 Kings 12:20); the people (*"salvation is of the Jews"*, John 4:22).
3. **Judah has the sceptre — by choice and by oath.** 1 Chronicles 28:4; Psalms 60:7 (*"Judah is my lawgiver"*); Numbers 24:17; fixed to David for ever (2 Samuel 7:16; 2 Chronicles 13:5; Psalms 89:34-36; Jeremiah 33:20-21); kept through the worst kings for David's sake (2 Kings 8:19).
4. **Until Shiloh — who Shiloh is.** The Word does not translate the name; it defines the Person by the clause the name carries (*"unto him shall the gathering of the people be"*) and by Ezekiel 21:27 on the same frame (*"until he come whose right it is; and I will give it him"*). Then it points: Revelation 5:5, Hebrews 7:14, Matthew 1:2-3, Micah 5:2, Zechariah 9:9 (the colt of 49:11), Revelation 19:13 (the garment of 49:11), Luke 1:32-33. Shiloh is Jesus; the title on the cross was the sceptre displayed (John 19:19).
5. **Until means arrival, not expiry** — Darrell's instinct is exact. Psalms 110:1 is the tutor; the sceptre stays: Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:14; Revelation 11:15; 19:16; 22:16. That is why Judah still matters to the end.
6. **Humans will listen to Judah** — the Word's own words: Isaiah 11:10; 2:3; 42:4; 49:6; Zechariah 8:23; John 18:37. And Judah the people remains in the ending: Jeremiah 23:6; Zechariah 12:7; Revelation 7:5 (sealed first); 21:12; Ezekiel 37:24; Romans 11:26.
7. **The Lion is the Lamb** (Revelation 5:5-6, 5:9) beside Genesis 44:33; the King of Judah is a priest only by a changed law (Hebrews 7:12-17).

8. **Added the same day, from six more lines Darrell spoke — the devil does not want the sceptre to rise; the Lawgiver who only wants His Ways; lawgivers, a whole tribe that would change the world; a certain percentage of leaders moves the whole; African Americans influence the world through the god of this world, but the Law is the opposite and the civil rights movement is studied worldwide; media and music are tools we do not own, stars undermining our own children.** The war on the sceptre by name: Genesis 3:15; Exodus 1:22; 2 Kings 11:1-3; Esther 3:6; Matthew 2:16; Revelation 12:4-5 (the rod of iron is the sceptre), the counterfeit refused (Luke 4:6-8), Psalms 2:2, 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:8; Hebrews 2:14; Colossians 2:15; Revelation 12:12; Psalms 2:9; Revelation 19:15. One Lawgiver (Isaiah 33:22; James 4:12) who wants His Ways walked, for our good (Deuteronomy 10:12-13; Jeremiah 7:23; Psalms 119:1; 128:1; Isaiah 55:9; John 14:15; Luke 6:46; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 5:3; Psalms 40:8). A whole tribe that CARRIES His law, never authors it (Psalms 60:7; Exodus 19:5-6; Deuteronomy 4:6-8; Psalms 78:5-7; Jeremiah 31:33; Isaiah 51:7; 2 Corinthians 3:2-3; Numbers 11:29; Matthew 28:19-20; 5:13-16; Psalms 119:46; Acts 17:6; Isaiah 2:3; Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27). The percentage: the Word gives cases, not a percent (Genesis 18:32; Jeremiah 5:1; Ezekiel 22:30; Judges 7:3, 7:7; 1 Samuel 14:6; Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Matthew 13:31-33; Zechariah 4:10; Isaiah 1:9; Romans 11:5; Exodus 18:21; Numbers 11:16-17; 2 Timothy 2:2; Acts 1:15; 2:41; Proverbs 29:2; 11:11) — the leverage is alignment, never the percentage. The god of this world versus the Law (2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 2:15-17) with the civil rights movement as established history (churches, pastors, Exodus 5:1, Amos 5:24, Isaiah 40:4, Matthew 5:44, Romans 12:21; the 1964 and 1965 Acts; studied worldwide) — Deuteronomy 4:6 in living memory; no leader quoted. Media and music as tools (1 Samuel 16:23; 2 Chronicles 20:21-22; Psalms 150:3-4; Daniel 3:5, 3:7; Isaiah 5:12; Ezekiel 33:32), not ours yet His (Nehemiah 5:5; Proverbs 22:7; Lamentations 5:2; Deuteronomy 28:32; Psalms 24:1; Haggai 2:8; Leviticus 25:23), stars by the Word's definition (Daniel 12:3 versus Jude 1:13; John 12:43; 5:44; Matthew 20:25-27), the children guarded (Matthew 18:6; Psalms 127:3; Ephesians 6:4; Proverbs 22:6; Malachi 4:6).

**Where the Word stops:** no derivation of the name Shiloh is added; the town Shiloh (Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 7:12) is a different use — Genesis 49:10 says the gathering is unto *him*.
