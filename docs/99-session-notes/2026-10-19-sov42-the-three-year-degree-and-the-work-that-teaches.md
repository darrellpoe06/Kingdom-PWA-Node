## The Word, senior to the argument

- **Forming is bought with use over time**, and that is a mechanism rather than a
  sentiment: Hebrews 5:14, beside Luke 2:52, Hebrews 5:12-13, Isaiah 28:10,
  1 Corinthians 13:11, Proverbs 22:6 and Luke 6:40.
- **The three-year degree is obeying Luke 14:28-30 and counting the wrong
  number.** A degree carries two costs, money and forming; only one is printed.
- **The counter-witness the Word itself gives, which the page may not hide:**
  Daniel 1:4-5, 1:8, 1:17, 1:20 — a three-year curriculum, by name, blessed. So
  nobody leaves claiming Scripture forbids a short degree. The text names why it
  worked, and the length is not among the reasons: a purposed heart and knowledge
  given. Shortness is not sin; length is not virtue.
- **Work beside learning:** Acts 18:3, 1 Thessalonians 2:9, 2 Thessalonians
  3:8-10, and older still Exodus 31:3-5 and 35:30-35 — the craft a Spirit-given
  filling in **Wisdom, Understanding and Knowledge**, arriving with a commission
  to teach it onward so the skill outlives the craftsman. Genesis 2:15,
  Ephesians 4:28, Colossians 3:23, 2 Timothy 2:15.
- **The debt:** Proverbs 22:7, Romans 13:8, Deuteronomy 15:1-2 — His remedy is a
  scheduled release, not an amputated education.
- **Wages:** Deuteronomy 24:14-15, Jeremiah 22:13, James 5:4, 1 Timothy 5:18,
  Luke 10:7 — stated unsoftened; then the limit, Proverbs 18:13, Proverbs 18:17,
  Deuteronomy 19:15.
- **Margins and weights:** Leviticus 19:9-10, Deuteronomy 24:19-21,
  Proverbs 13:23; Matthew 25:15, Luke 19:17, Luke 16:10 against Proverbs 11:1
  and Proverbs 20:10.
- **The stranger:** Leviticus 19:33-34, Exodus 22:21, Deuteronomy 10:18-19 —
  taught, not staged, with the party frame refused in both directions and the
  Word's own reticence named.
- **Retention is feeding:** Ezekiel 34:2-4, John 21:15-17, 1 Peter 5:2,
  Proverbs 27:23, Proverbs 9:9.
- **The close:** Ecclesiastes 1:9, Proverbs 25:2, 1 Thessalonians 5:21,
  Proverbs 14:15, 1 Corinthians 13:1, Philippians 1:10, Proverbs 24:3-4,
  Proverbs 4:7, Proverbs 23:23, Jeremiah 6:16 — then Galatians 5:22,
  Hebrews 3:13 and Deuteronomy 6:7 for how growth is measured here, and
  John 1:29, John 3:16, Hebrews 1:8 for the finished work no curriculum can add
  to. Psalms 19:14 closes it.
