## The answer

The question has a fear in its first half and the answer in its second, and the Word speaks to both.

1. **He is not withholding.** *"If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him."* (James 1:5). Liberally; without a lecture; to all. A giver so described is not holding perception in reserve pending a trust review (Proverbs 2:3-6; Jeremiah 33:3).
2. **Perception is grown, not issued.** *"those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil."* (Hebrews 5:14). Exercised, by use. A muscle handed over full-grown would not be a muscle. A dawn, not a switch (Proverbs 4:18; Isaiah 28:10).
3. **Darrell's half is right, and the Word states it as a sequence:** *"If any man will do his will, he shall know"* (John 7:17); *"a good understanding have all they that do his commandments"* (Psalms 111:10); Psalms 119:100; Romans 12:2; John 14:21; Psalms 25:14. DO, then KNOW. But the *why* changes how it feels: alignment is not a toll He collects before trusting us; **alignment is the exercise** the senses are grown by. Not the toll — the road.
4. **The tests are the training, not a trust exam.** *"to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart"* (Deuteronomy 8:2) beside *"a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart"* (Hebrews 4:12) and *"now I know that thou fearest God"* (Genesis 22:12): the test makes visible what He already knows. What a test produces is the very perception asked for — Hebrews 12:11 (*exercised thereby*), James 1:3-4, Job 23:10 — and the motive is stated: *"but he for our profit"* (Hebrews 12:10).
5. **Why it seems to come after:** increase follows use (Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Matthew 13:12).
6. **What He keeps and what He gave.** Deuteronomy 29:29 (the revealed things are ours *that we may do*); Isaiah 55:9; 1 Corinthians 13:12 — and the faculty itself is a Person already given: John 16:13, John 14:26, 1 Corinthians 2:14, Ephesians 1:17-18, John 15:15. Guidance in its order: trust first, direction after (Proverbs 3:5-6; Psalms 32:8). Where a lack is real the Word says *rejected*, not withheld (Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 1:7; Matthew 11:25). Solomon asked and was given (1 Kings 3:9, 3:12).
7. **Diligence is evaluated by Yahweh — and the diligence He evaluates is diligence in His Word** (folded in the same day). He *"is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him."* (Hebrews 11:6); the diligent *"shall stand before kings"* (Proverbs 22:29; 12:24; 13:4; 2 Peter 1:10; Hebrews 6:11-12) — outcomes attached, the slack hand not graded like the diligent one (Proverbs 10:4). The measure is HIS perspective (Isaiah 55:8), and haste without knowledge is sin (Proverbs 19:2), so the diligence He rewards aims at His Word first: *"Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman... rightly dividing the word of truth."* (2 Timothy 2:15; Proverbs 2:4-6 — the seeking ours, the giving His). *We must be educated* is the Word's own diagnosis: Hosea 4:6; Matthew 22:29 (*"Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures"*); commanded and modelled — Deuteronomy 6:7, Ezra 7:10 (SEEK, DO, TEACH), Acts 17:11, Nehemiah 8:8, Ephesians 4:14; the perspective is available — 1 Corinthians 2:16. One guard: 2 Timothy 3:7 / James 1:22 — learning that never becomes doing is the counterfeit; the loop lands where the lesson began, *by reason of use*.
