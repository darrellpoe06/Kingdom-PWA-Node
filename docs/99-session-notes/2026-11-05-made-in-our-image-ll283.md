## 1. What arrived, and why it was not a thin forward

The send is a CEO's letter announcing a printed quarterly special issue titled **"Made in Our Image"**, plus the issue's own promotional descriptions of four of its features, one third-party advertisement, and a membership pitch. It would be easy to read it as a thin forward. It is not, for one reason: the letter's author describes, in his own words and as a virtue, the exact posture two of this repository's binding rules exist to remove.

Three sentences carry it:

- *"Somewhere along the way, however, discussions like that become harder to find. The world is no less full of new ideas to explore, but we talk about them less often. And when we do, the goal is too often confirmation rather than consideration."*
- *"I hope you read it, argue with it, and then argue about it with someone else. Maybe at the dinner table."*
- and the lead feature: *"THE BIG SPLIT — 10 perspectives that divide the AI world"*, described as *"10 competing philosophies into a single primer that explores what's at stake if we embrace or reject each."*

That is **DR-0098** ("we teach the Word and show how it works; we do not platform debate — never a ratings-style here-are-three-views-you-decide") and **DR-0100** ("established fact is stated plainly; false skepticism is not discernment") described from the inside by a man who believes they are a service to the reader. He is not a villain in this lesson and is not treated as one. He loves ideas, says so, and is honest about selling a magazine. The lesson's quarrel is with a frame, not a person.

And the title is the lesson. **"Made in Our Image"** is one word out of position from Genesis 1:26-27. Reverse that sentence and you have not escalated the claim about humanity; you have deleted the foundation it was resting on.

## 2. The one thing this lesson could most easily have gotten wrong

Reproducing what it teaches about. The obvious, natural, journalistically respectable way to build this lesson is to lay out the ten perspectives, weigh them, and let the reader choose. That would have been the failure — the lesson would have become an eleventh entry in the market it is describing.

DR-0098's own words set the line: *"We may NAME a debate — to educate past it, by the Word ... never as a ratings-style 'here are three views, you decide' that leaves the Word un-taught."* So the lesson names that the split exists, quotes the issue's own description of it, says plainly why we will not reproduce it, and then teaches past it from Scripture's own naming of the posture.

That refusal is not left to an author's good intentions. It is gated. `enumeratedPositions()` in the lesson's test counts numbered position markers and camp labels in every reader text, requires fewer than two markers and zero camps, and is proven against a planted enumeration (`1) The accelerationist view says build fast. 2) The doomer view says stop.`) which it catches on both counts while the real text scores zero on both.

## 3. The hinge, which is the part worth keeping

The letter says the problem is *"confirmation rather than consideration."* The diagnosis is correct. It is also ancient — and the Word reached for that exact word first, in the very passage about making a thing in our own image.

Isaiah 44 does not dismiss the idol-maker in a line; it spends eleven verses on him, because the argument is the sequence. The craftsman is skilled (44:13). He fetches the timber himself (44:14). Half of it becomes dinner and heat — *"Aha, I am warm, I have seen the fire"* (44:16) — and the other half of the same tree, on the same afternoon, he finishes and kneels to: *"Deliver me; for thou art my god"* (44:17). One tree, one man, one day. No stranger deceived him. His own competence did.

Then the indictment, and it is not what a modern reader expects:

> *"And none considereth in his heart, neither is there knowledge nor understanding..."* (Isaiah 44:19)

**Not nobody argued. Nobody considered.** And the close is a question a man owes himself rather than the room: *"Is there not a lie in my right hand?"* (Isaiah 44:20).

So the letter and the Word agree on the diagnosis and part company entirely on the cure. The letter's cure is more argument, with more people, at more dinner tables. The Word's cure is one man looking down at what is in his own hand and telling the truth about it. Consideration is an interior act; it cannot be argued into existence and it cannot be delegated to a panel. That distinction is pinned in the lesson and in all four bands.

No environment change was proposed for it: the letter is the primary document for everything the lesson actually teaches, so the blocked hosts bound two peripheral facts and nothing load-bearing.

**What the checking CHANGED — two things.**

1. *The print cadence.* The letter calls this "this quarter's special issue." The publisher's own description of its publishing programme — reached only as a search result and never read on the page — says the themed issue is monthly and **every third one** is printed and mailed to members, which is why a quarterly print issue exists at all. That is a real finding and it is carried in all four long bands, attributed to the publisher and never asserted on our own authority.

So **"CEO, Big Think Media" rests on the letter's own signature line alone and was not independently confirmed.** The lesson says that, in those terms. `re-review: 2026-11-12`.

Under-claiming is also a failure (DR-0100 §1), so what the message establishes about itself is stated plainly rather than hedged: the letter exists, is dated, is signed, and says what it says.

## 7. The 12 red cases the gate found in my own first draft

Worth recording, because the gate did its job against its own author. The first draft passed 50 of 62 and failed 12. Nine of those were one defect: **the child band had been simplified past the point of honesty.** It carried the teaching but had dropped the trust-but-verify note, the blocked hosts, the "not independent" admission, the creative-director limit, the membership-pitch naming, and the writer's name. A child is a reader place; DR-0190 §5 and DR-1324 do not have a junior tier. The note was written into the child band in child register and the band grew from 1517 to 1947 authored words.

Two more were content: the child band's Habakkuk quote was clipped to `"a teacher of lies"` where the pinned span is `"the molten image, and a teacher of lies"`, and its close read "never have life" where the lesson's own line is "never carry life". Both fixed in the text.

Two were defects in the gate itself, not the lesson, and were fixed in the gate: a regex written `/has (not been|gone un)check/` could never match "has not been checked" because the alternation ate the space, and the enumeration proof used `[^.]{0,120}` as a bridge across text that contained a full stop, so the planted enumeration it was meant to catch slipped through. A gate that cannot match the thing it describes is the anti-theater failure DR-0076 §3 names, caught here by running it rather than by reading it.
