## What the Word supplies that the article does not reach

The article's example is three words long — Alice trusts Bob, Bob trusts Alice.
Scripture settled order first, and as instruction with its reason stated:
*"Let all things be done decently and in order."* (1 Corinthians 14:40) beside
*"For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace, as in all churches of
the saints."* (1 Corinthians 14:33). It is **assigned**, not emergent — *"But
now hath God set the members every one of them in the body, as it hath pleased
him."* (1 Corinthians 12:18) — and **enumerated** where it must be
unmistakable: first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers
(1 Corinthians 12:28), and *"every man in his own order: Christ the
firstfruits"* (1 Corinthians 15:23).

**Then the centre, and it is DR-0098 caught in the act.** Matthew 4:6 quotes
the Psalms accurately and removes the clause *"to keep thee in all thy ways"*
from Psalms 91:11, splicing verse 12 over the seam so the cut does not show.
Every surviving word is genuine. The lie was built by **rearrangement alone** —
nothing invented, something relocated — and it turned a promise of Yahweh's
keeping into an invitation to cast himself down. Jesus answers neither by
calling the verse false nor with an argument of His own: *"It is written
again."* (Matthew 4:7) More of the Word, restoring the broken arrangement.

The remaining movements: the compass set upon the deep (Proverbs 8:27) and why
two coordinates are needed when one looks sufficient; the repeating ordinances
a man measured but did not invent (Genesis 8:22, Genesis 1:14, Jeremiah 33:25,
Job 38:33, Romans 1:20); line upon line as the shape of teaching itself
(Isaiah 28:10, Luke 1:3, Acts 11:4, 2 Timothy 2:15); and the frame — He is not
first in the sequence, He is before it (Colossians 1:17, Hebrews 11:3).
