## The part that fits cleanly (green light — it's ALREADY the sanctioned model)

Ideas (1) and the *compliant* form of (2) are not new policy — they are the
product expression of a decision Darrell already closed on 2026-06-03:
**Community-free is funded by vetted aligned-brand partners — open-source
tools, data-sovereignty HARDWARE, and family-stewardship services PoeTech
genuinely recommends — public-radio-underwriting style: limited slots,
8-criterion vetting, full disclosure, NO behavioral targeting, never selling
data** (memory: project_community_free_funded_by_aligned_brand_sponsorship;
About.jsx + landing strip, commit 884f75a).

A hardware-advisory surface is the natural home for that: it recommends the
right-sized NAS / storage / IoT device for the user's ACTUAL need (property
count, photo volume, family size — exactly the no-backup-user problem from
the photo-sovereignty note), and the underwriting/affiliate revenue from the
data-sovereignty hardware partner is the already-approved funding mechanic.
This also solves durability for the no-NAS user: the app helps them get the
storage they own, and routes setup to the 1099 team as a paid engagement.

## The part to STOP on (premise conflict — Darrell's to govern)

**"Ad money" as literally an advertising model / ad network is forbidden by a
binding foundation.** DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT-NOT-EXTRACTION.md lists, as
architectural commitments that NEVER ship: "no advertising model," "no
engagement optimization," and the anti-patterns "surveillance disguised as
service" and "dark UX." The aligned-brand decision itself says "NO behavioral
targeting." And we just spent two sessions shipping the photo-sovereignty
promise — "never sold, never mined, never used to train, there is no such
pipeline here." A behavioral ad network would directly break that promise and
the moat it protects. You cannot tell a user "no ad pipeline touches your
photos" and run an ad network in the same app.

**The distinction that keeps the mission intact:**
- ALLOWED — *underwriting / affiliate on products the family genuinely uses
  and recommends*, disclosed openly, recommendation driven by the user's need
  (not by who pays most). Public-radio model. This is real revenue and it is
  already sanctioned.
- FORBIDDEN — *an advertising model*: monetizing user attention or data,
  behavioral targeting, ad inventory inside the app, pay-for-placement that
  overrides need-based recommendation. This is the extractive model PoeTech
  exists to be the opposite of.

**Recommendation (Claude advises):** adopt the hardware-advisory feature
under the existing aligned-brand/underwriting model; explicitly do NOT build
an ad network. If a recommendation ever carries an affiliate/underwriting
relationship, disclose it on that exact surface, and never let the payment
change what gets recommended for the user's need. This is DR-worthy — it
extends the 2026-06-03 funding decision to hardware procurement + affiliate
mechanics. (DR creation deferred: another session owns the DR ledger —
project_dr_ledger_consolidation_state — so flag, don't author here.)
