## The statute, verified

Read against the regulation itself, not the summary: [34 CFR 675.41 (Cornell LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/34/675.41); [GPO eCFR PDF](https://www.gpo.gov/fdsys/pkg/CFR-2012-title34-vol3/pdf/CFR-2012-title34-vol3-sec675-41.pdf); [federal-regs.com](https://federal-regs.com/title/34/part-675/675.41/). Statute: 20 U.S.C. § 1087-58 (HEA § 448).

A "work college" is an institution that:
1. is a public or private nonprofit, **four-year, degree-granting** institution with a commitment to community service;
2. has operated a **comprehensive work-learning-service program for at least two years** before applying;
3. requires **resident students, including at least one-half of all students enrolled full-time**, to participate — at least **five hours a week, or 80 hours per period of enrollment** (summer excluded; approved study abroad or externship excepted);
4. gives participants the chance to contribute to their education and to the community's welfare.

Federal funds under Subpart C are matched **dollar-for-dollar** by the institution. Darrell's summary matches the regulation on every point above.

## Constraints, stated plainly (DR-0100)

A Work College is a *designation of an accredited, Title IV-eligible, four-year college with resident students*. PoeTech is an app; The Church of the Living God is a church. Neither grants degrees, neither is accredited, neither has a campus. Reaching the designation means, in order: a separate nonprofit college entity; state authorization; regional accreditation (years); Title IV eligibility (FISAP, annual audits, FERPA, Clery); cash to match federal dollars 1:1; **then** two years of the program; **then** the application. A 5-to-10-year institutional question, not a feature of the app.

## Opportunities, as they actually stand

1. **The two-year track record is the asset either path needs, and the app is the right ledger for it.** What the regulation really requires is a *record*: mandatory hours, named supervisors, regular evaluations that become part of the permanent record, consequences tied to standing. PoeTech already holds the pieces — learner records, Poe Properties door work orders, church media and choir duties, the SME pipeline. A work-learning-service record (hours, supervisor, evaluation, on the learner's transcript, exportable) is buildable in the app the day the program is named.
2. **College prep is the honest designation today:** a work-learning-service program for the family's and COLG's youth, with records that travel, and partnership or articulation with an existing work college (Berea, College of the Ozarks, Alice Lloyd, Blackburn, Warren Wilson, Sterling, Paul Quinn, Bethany Global, Kuyper) rather than becoming one.
3. **It is the Word's own pattern**, and it was the lesson on his screen the same hour: Luke 2:52; Colossians 3:23; 2 Thessalonians 3:10; Proverbs 22:6 — in the week's own words, *"a forming with a clock in it, and the clock cannot be bought out."*

The one thing only Darrell can give is the program's shape for COLG youth — who works, where, under whom, and what the record says on a transcript.
