## What the lesson does

The spine is method before content. The first misrepresentation of Yahweh in Scripture is not a lie but an edit, and the exhibit is provable from the corpus rather than asserted: the adversary quotes Psalm 91 to Jesus accurately and drops the clause `to keep thee in all thy ways`, which is the clause that rules out jumping. Jesus answers by restoring the page, not by denying the verse. Peter's diagnosis names both of Darrell's causes in one verse — `unlearned and unstable` who `wrest` the scriptures — so incompetence and craft are held together rather than chosen between.

Each of the four planks then gets its true half conceded fully and first, then its whole page. The fourth is the heaviest and is stated without a hedge: Augusta 1845, the 1838 sale of 272 people from four Maryland plantations to fund a college, St. George's 1787, the 1915 Klan under a former preacher, and the 1807 Bible printed with the deliverance from Egypt and all the Psalms removed. Then the Word out-charges the objection — man-stealing as a capital crime, `menstealers` among the lawless, `I never knew you`, and Yahweh declaring Himself against those who `use their tongues, and say, He saith`.

It closes where Darrell closed: religious leadership used the government against the Son and He predicted the pattern; the body is reachable and the soul is not; the fourth man in the fire; and the last word is an invitation rather than a proof — `O taste and see that the LORD is good`.

Darrell's own reading is the teaching: our laughter is often grief wearing a mask, and the laugh of Heaven is the laugh of One who already sees the end of a thing.
