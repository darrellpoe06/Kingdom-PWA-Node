## The spine

One word, son, for three relationships the Word names: made, begotten, adopted. The angels are sons by making (Job 1:6, Job 38:7; Genesis 6 read through Job; Ephesians 3:15; Adam too, Luke 3:38). The Son is Son by begetting, and Hebrews 1 says plainly what was never said to an angel (1:5, 1:6, 1:8, 1:13); He is the Maker of the angels (Colossians 1:16). People are sons by adoption because the Son took the seed of Abraham and not the nature of angels (Hebrews 2:16, 2:10-17), through receiving Him and being led by His Spirit (John 1:12, Romans 8:14-17, Galatians 4:4-7). The angels serve the heirs and refuse worship (Hebrews 1:14, Revelation 22:9); the family before Adam was the Father, the eternal Son, the Spirit and the angels (John 1:1, Job 38:7); one house with two kinds of children stands at Mount Sion (Hebrews 12:22-23, Luke 20:36). The Word is left silent on the angels' day and number (Deuteronomy 29:29).
