  risks what He entrusted (Genesis 6-7 — fetch verbatim).
- **Noah sat and waited for the full instruction; Jonah ran to handle it his
  own way.** Favor is an invitation to be faithful to a difficult mission
  exactly as commanded. Proximity over distant hearing: far enough away, you
  fill the gaps with your own methods — and the gap-filling corrupts the work.

  and sets an internal precedent. A leader's "fall" is not a slip but the
  fruit of practice made perfect. Rituals (washing hands, shoes off on holy
  ground) strip worldly residue so the carnal never tracks into the holy.
- **The I-doctrine** — self-promotion, control, manufacturing outcomes — turns
  holy assignment into carnal manipulation; "aftermarket parts" hand access to
  other authorities and disconnect the work from Yahweh's maintenance.
- **Genetically-modified doctrine:** mixing human opinion into the Word alters
  the taste buds of those discipled until pure truth is indigestible. The
  heart as a SAFE CONTAINER: purified of ego, trauma, and the idol of past
  experience; surrendered on the how/when/who.
