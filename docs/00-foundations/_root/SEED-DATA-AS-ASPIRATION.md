# Seed Data as Aspiration

Every Claude session reads this before generating, editing, or sanitizing any seed, sample, or demo data.

## The principle

**The starter state of the app is not a placeholder. It is the first impression of what success looks like when this system is doing its work.**

Darrell's words: *"We need good seed data nothing with our information and make sure it's a good start to life and some amazing success that they can see as seed data impacts dopamine and the desire to use it to grow."*

What this means concretely:

- The seed must contain **no real personal information** about the Poe family. No real entity names, no real account fragments, no real addresses, no real salary numbers. Privacy first.
- The seed must show **a thriving family stewardship picture**. The user lands in a state that looks like the destination they want to reach. Steady income, growing buffer fund, debt being chipped down, tithe consistent, savings accumulating, retirement on track. Stewardship working.
- The seed must trigger **desire to use the system to get there**. The dopamine hit isn't from gamification; it's from seeing what's possible when the four questions (what/when/why/how) are answered every day. The viewer thinks "I want my books to look like this."
- The seed must include **one small attention-needing item** so the viewer sees what the system DOES for them. Not a problem; an example of the kind of nudge the system provides. ("Groceries trending 8% over budget — consider switching one weekly run to Aldi.") The system isn't passive ledger; it's an active guide.

## What this is NOT

This is not gaming the user. This is not unrealistic financial fantasy. The thriving family in the seed is achievable for a normal-income household practicing the disciplines this app embodies. The aspiration is honest.

It is also not perfect — perfect is unrelatable. The seed family has debt (being chipped down), has at least one over-budget month somewhere visible, has a buffer fund still growing (not maxed out). It's the picture of a family two years into stewardship discipline, not a family that's already arrived.

## Design contract for seed data

Every seed dataset (the public PWA seed, every demo persona, every sample for a stakeholder lens) must answer YES to all four questions:

1. **Privacy: does this contain any real personal information about the Poe family, or about any real person we know?** If yes, redesign.
2. **Aspiration: does the financial picture look like one a viewer would WANT for their family?** If no, the numbers need work.
3. **Relatability: is the income level + life stage achievable for the audience this seed is for?** If no, scale it down.
4. **Active guidance: is at least one moment of "the system tells you something useful" visible in the seed state?** If no, add one — the seed needs to demonstrate the app's voice, not just its ledger.

## Specific design directives

### Income shape

Show moderate, sustainable, stewardship-discipline income. Suggested ranges by audience:

- Family of 4 (the universal default): ~$7,500-$10,000/mo gross combined, two-earner. Achievable for most middle-class American households.
- Separated co-parents: each household $3,500-$5,000/mo gross. Lower but viable.
- Solo professional: $8,000-$15,000/mo gross practice revenue, owner-draw to personal.
- Landlord: day-job $5,500/mo gross + rental net $1,500-$3,500/mo. Real-but-not-aspirational.

### Buffer fund

Always show a buffer fund **in progress**. Target $2,000-$5,000 depending on audience; current balance 60-80% of target. Growing visibly month over month from a recurring transfer. The viewer sees the system's most important early discipline working.

### Debt picture

Show debt on the way DOWN, not eliminated. Two debts max in the snowball:

- One small consumer debt (~$1,500-$3,000 credit card) close to payoff
- One larger amortizing debt (~$8,000-$12,000 auto or student loan) being chipped on a steady minimum + a small extra

Avoid showing a debt-free family — that's the destination, not the journey. The viewer relates to "I'm in this with you, working it down."

### Tithe / charitable giving

Always show consistent charitable giving as a recurring outflow. $150-$250/month. This is faith-rooted product DNA per THE-WAY foundation — the family's first-fruits practice is normative, not optional.

### Income vs outflow

Net cash flow positive but modest — $400-$1,200/mo. Enough to feel possible, not so much it feels unattainable. The buffer-fund contribution should be visible in this margin.

### Active guidance moment

Build in one (and only one) visible "the system is helping me right now" surface in the seed:

- A category trending over budget that hasn't broken anything yet
- An upcoming bill the user will be reminded about
- A rental property approaching capex (if the seed is a landlord)
- A specialist referral suggestion (if the seed is a personal household; "your buffer fund is well-funded — a fee-only financial planner could help you think about next steps")

The user sees the system's voice as a partner, not a passive recorder.
