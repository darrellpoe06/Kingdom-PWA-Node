# Founder's Confession

> Spoken by the founder. Lightly cleaned for grammar and flow at his request; substance, voice, and conviction unchanged. Attributed. Dated. Treated as upstream of every design decision in this codebase.

---

**Author:** Darrell Poe
**Date recorded:** 2026-05-18
**Context:** Spoken to the system mid-build, while reviewing the day's work on the SKOS / PoeTech Family OS. The founder explicitly chose to put this on the record so that anyone reading the codebase — engineer, customer, family member, skeptic, seeker — meets the heart behind the work, not just the architecture. ## The confession

> This is how I worship Yahweh: action-based love with no strings attached. He loved me first; I'm just reciprocating, which is my reasonable service. He is helping, holding, and lifting me on eagles' wings. I'm just clay watching an eternal, invisible Yahweh set a table before my enemies and also show me, in real time, how to love people who take more than they give at times. No one needed to tell Jesus about man — He already knew man. He knew He would be killed, knew it was necessary, and then did it. Amazing.
>
> So since my flesh is weak, I figured I would build something that keeps me as close to His will as possible — a blind human in the earth, fighting the good fight of Faith, longing to be Home finally. Not yet. But when I see His Face. Suffer with Yahweh, His Way, and reign with Him eternally. The world is so crazy someone has to judge this place — and He already said He was. Patience. Faith. Belief. Steadfastness. Honor. Truth. Die Once Correctly. Live forever with the King as a king. His Story, not mine.
>
> And I am saying all of this on purpose, because that is how I truly feel, why I feel it, and what I believe. Why not say so? I believe the King prefers that I give this information, because He cares — and it may influence someone to follow the King of kings. I do, and I take Him seriously. He is the Shepherd of souls. Most humans do not know that. And even some who claim to know Him do not use His Word the way I do, and it shows up in the outcomes and in the thinking patterns. I needed to study to change my thinking patterns — and the Word did it best.
>
> Let Yahweh be true and every man a lie — even me. Who else in this world should I listen to more than the One who made us? Big words with no love mean nothing. I also fear Yahweh, but I love Him more, and He has never hurt me. He has always allowed the hurt and expected me to have self-control, while the people who were undermining on purpose at times — and oblivious at other times — still did not know what they were doing according to Yahweh's Will. This helps me keep His Will in mind, so my soul abides in His Kingdom now and through death — just like Jesus did. He was loyal to His Father. Through the first death we must do the same: take the heat and move on from this dimension into the next — from 3rd to 4th, seen to unseen — which is more real than the 3rd dimension according to the biblical scriptures.

## Why this document exists

Every codebase eventually grows past its founder. New contributors join. SMEs review. Customers read the public docs. Future-Darrell forgets what present-Darrell knew. When that day comes, **this file is the answer to the question** *"why does this thing exist at all?"*

The other foundations describe what the system **does** and how it **behaves**. This one describes the heart it grew out of.

If a future design decision conflicts with what is written above, the design decision is wrong — not because the founder's preference outranks the user's, but because the system is built as **stewardship under the King**, and any feature that drifts from that commission is no longer the same system.

## How this document binds the work

When designing, reviewing, or shipping, ask:

1. **Would this feel like worship on a tired day?** If using the feature on a hard morning would feel like one more obligation rather than an act of stewardship, redesign.
2. **Does this keep the user close to His will or distract from it?** Notifications, dashboards, and prompts should reduce noise, not generate it.
3. **Whose story is this telling?** The system, the founder, and the user are all clay. The protagonist is the King. Surfaces should reflect that.
4. **Does this honor the values named?** Patience. Faith. Belief. Steadfastness. Honor. Truth. Diligence to die once correctly. If a feature trades any of these for speed-to-market or feature-count, it does not ship in that form.
5. **Could a seeker meet the King through this?** The founder explicitly chose to put his confession on the record because *"it may influence someone to follow the King of kings."* The system is not a hidden Christian project; the foundations are open, the scripture is named, and the work is offered as one steward's reasonable service. Public-facing copy, marketing pages, and onboarding should not bury this. They should not be preachy either. They should be honest in the same way the founder was honest — *"why not say so?"*

The 5th filter does not mean every screen quotes scripture. It means **the work itself is the witness**: features that reduce real stress, numbers that tell the truth, designs that honor the dignity of the user, code that doesn't ship until it's right. The Word changed the founder's thinking patterns; the system is one downstream artifact of that change; a customer who never reads the foundations should still feel the difference. The foundations are there for the ones who want to know *why*.
