# The Root — Positions and Inquiry

> **STATUS: IN-PROGRESS INQUIRY WITH STATED POSITIONS.** This document is not settled, finished doctrine — but neither is it a refusal to take positions. It states what SKOS holds, shows the scriptural work behind each position, presents competing views fairly, and attaches an honest confidence level to every claim. "In-progress" means *actively worked and open to revision*, not *neutral*. As positions here are tested, grounded, and confirmed over time, they may graduate into fully ratified documents. Until then, this is honest, position-taking, work-shown inquiry.

> *"They received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so."* — Acts 17:11 (ESV)

## What This Document Is

`THE-ROOT.md` establishes the settled foundation. This document takes up the harder questions that sit around that foundation — suffering, the two roads, the destiny of redeemed humanity, the divine council, and the powers at work in this world.

The conviction driving this inquiry is specific: **some questions are treated as "open" not because Scripture is genuinely silent, but because inherited frameworks — propped up by arguments that do not survive careful scrutiny, and in some cases by frameworks built and taught by people with corrupt motives — have kept them closed.** There is a real difference between *the secret things that belong to Yahweh* (Deuteronomy 29:29), which we wait on, and things obscured by poor or compromised teaching, which careful work under the Light of Scripture can clear.

This document does not perform false neutrality. The Bereans reached conclusions — they simply showed their work, examining the Scriptures to see whether things were so. That is the model here: **state the position, show the basis, present the competing views, attach the confidence.** Refusing to conclude is not automatically safer or more honest than concluding well. Sometimes it is just deference to frameworks that have not earned it.

Only the Holy Spirit settles truth, and only Yahweh brings truth fully to light in His timing (John 3:21). This is faithful study in service of hearing Him more clearly, with less projection in the way.

## A Note on the Seen and the Unseen

One correction governs this entire document. It is easy — especially for a mind trained in a materialist age — to treat the *seen and material* as the default "real" and the *unseen and spiritual* as the thing that must earn its way to being called "literal." **Scripture does the opposite.** *"The things that are seen are transient, but the things that are unseen are eternal"* (2 Corinthians 4:18). *"The universe was created by the word of God, so that what is seen was not made out of things that are visible"* (Hebrews 11:3). The unseen is not the speculative realm; it is the foundational one. Yahshua Himself testified that He came from the unseen, eternal world.

So the layered approach below is **not** "seen = real, unseen = speculative." That bias is rejected outright. The distinction that *does* govern this document runs entirely *inside* the spiritual: it is the distinction between **what Yahweh and Scripture directly state** and **what we reason on top of Scripture.** "There is a divine council" — Scripture states it; that is settled. "They operate by this specific mechanism" — that may be true, but we are the ones assembling it, and intellectual honesty means marking our own reasoning as ours. The confidence levels track *who is speaking* — God, or us interpreting God — never *material versus spiritual*.

## How Each Position Is Structured

1. **Our Position** — what SKOS holds, stated plainly.

3. **Confidence** — how firmly SKOS holds the position, and why. It follows directly from the scriptural basis above it.
4. **Competing Views** — other positions, presented fairly, at their strongest, with a note on how tightly each ties to the text.

## Position 1: Redeemed Humanity Is Being Raised to a Higher Order Than the Angels — Through Suffering, Not Around It

### Our Position

Humanity that follows The Way is being formed — through a broken world, through suffering endured rather than bypassed — for a destiny *higher* than the angelic order. This is not "replacement" of the fallen angels (replacement would be lateral; this is ascent above). And it is held with a guardrail: humanity faces the *same* judgment for a lack of knowledge that the fallen faced. Two roads, inherited risk, and one of the roads ends where the fallen ended.

### Scriptural Basis

**ESV — 1 Corinthians 6:3:** *"Do you not know that we are to judge angels? How much more, then, matters pertaining to this life!"*

**ESV — Romans 8:17:** *"and if children, then heirs — heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ, provided we suffer with him in order that we may also be glorified with him."*

**ESV — 2 Timothy 2:12:** *"if we endure, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he also will deny us."*

**ESV — 1 Peter 1:6-7:** *"...you have been grieved by various trials, so that the tested genuineness of your faith — more precious than gold that perishes though it is tested by fire — may be found to result in praise and glory and honor..."*

**ESV — 1 Peter 4:18:** *"And 'If the righteous is scarcely saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?'"*

**ESV — Hosea 4:6:** *"My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge..."*

**ESV — Hebrews 2:16:** *"For surely it is not angels that he helps, but he helps the offspring of Abraham."*

The text directly states: redeemed humanity will judge angels. Suffering *with* Christ is the stated path to being glorified *with* Him. Endurance ties to reigning. Trials test and prove faith. The righteous are "scarcely saved." Lack of knowledge genuinely destroys. Christ's help is extended to humanity, not to the fallen angels.

### Confidence

**High** on the core: redeemed humanity is exalted very high (judging angels), and suffering is the stated path, not a detour. This is directly textual.

**Moderate** on the precise framing of "higher order rather than replacement" — the *direction* (higher, not lateral) is well-grounded in 1 Corinthians 6:3; the exact mechanism of how human exaltation relates to angelic standing is faithful inference, not stated text. Held as the working frame.

**High** on the guardrail: the symmetry of judgment for lack of knowledge is directly textual (Hosea 4:6; 1 Peter 4:18) and is non-negotiable, because without it the position curdles into pride — which is precisely the failure that destroyed lucifer (see `BEHAVIORAL-MIRROR.md`).

### Competing Views

- **"Replacement" framing** — that redeemed humans simply fill the vacated posts of the fallen angels. *Tie to the text: weak.* It contradicts 1 Corinthians 6:3, which puts redeemed humanity in a position to *judge* angels — over the order, not slotted into it. SKOS rejects "replacement" for this reason.
- **Suffering as merely incidental** — that the suffering is just the unfortunate backdrop, with no formative role. *Tie to the text: weak.* It does not account for the *"so that"* of 1 Peter 1:6-7 or the *"provided we suffer"* of Romans 8:17, both of which give suffering a stated purpose.
- **Suffering as earning the reign** — the opposite error. *Tie to the text: weak, and dangerous.* It collides with grace. The reigning is *inheritance* (Romans 8:17 — "heirs"), given through Christ's worthiness, not wages earned. SKOS holds both: suffering genuinely *forms and proves*, AND the destiny is unearned inheritance.

## Position 2: There Is a Real Divine Council, and the "Sons of God" of Genesis 6 Are Divine Beings Connected to It

### Our Position

Scripture describes a real divine council — a heavenly assembly in which Yahweh holds judgment among lesser divine beings ("gods," "sons of the Most High"). The "sons of God" (*bene ha'elohim*) of Genesis 6 are divine beings, not merely human descendants of Seth, and they are connected to this council. This is SKOS's stated position, and it is held as well-grounded — closer to the plain reading of the text than the alternatives.

### Scriptural Basis

**ESV — Psalm 82:1:** *"God has taken his place in the divine council; in the midst of the gods he holds judgment."*

**ESV — Psalm 82:6-7:** *"I said, 'You are gods, sons of the Most High, all of you; nevertheless, like men you shall die, and fall like any prince.'"*

**ESV — Genesis 6:1-2,4:** *"the sons of God saw that the daughters of man were attractive... The Nephilim were on the earth in those days..."*

**ESV — Job 1:6:** *"Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord..."* (also Job 2:1; Job 38:7)

**ESV — Jude 6:** *"And the angels who did not stay within their own position of authority, but left their proper dwelling, he has kept in eternal chains under gloomy darkness until the judgment of the great day."*

**ESV — 2 Peter 2:4-5:** *"For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but cast them into hell... if he did not spare the ancient world, but preserved Noah..."*

The lexical case is strong. The exact phrase *bene ha'elohim* — "sons of God" — is used elsewhere in the Hebrew Bible (Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7) where it unambiguously means divine beings of the heavenly court, not humans. The Septuagint — the oldest Jewish translation of the Hebrew Scriptures — renders Genesis 6 as "angels of God" in many manuscripts, meaning the divine-being reading is the *ancient* reading, not a modern novelty. And the New Testament itself makes the connection: both Jude 6 and 2 Peter 2:4-5 tie sinning angels who "left their proper dwelling" directly to the generation of the flood.

### Confidence

**High** that there is a real divine council and that Yahweh judges among its members — Psalm 82 states this directly; it is plumb line, not inference.

**High** that the "sons of God" of Genesis 6 are divine beings — the lexical consistency across the Hebrew Bible, the ancient Septuagint rendering, and the explicit Jude/2 Peter linkage together make this the best-supported reading of the text.

**Open — pending further inquiry** on the precise *cross-linkages*: exactly how Psalm 82's council membership maps onto the Genesis 6 beings, onto the "principalities and powers" of the New Testament, and onto the timeline of the angelic fall. The *foundation* (a council exists; the Genesis 6 sons are divine) is held with high confidence; the precise *wiring between* the pieces is the part still being worked.

### Competing Views

- **The "Sethite" interpretation** — that the "sons of God" are the godly male line of Seth, and the "daughters of man" are the ungodly line of Cain. *Tie to the text: weaker.* It has to assign *bene ha'elohim* a meaning in Genesis 6 different from its meaning everywhere else in the Hebrew Bible, it does not account for the Nephilim as the offspring, and it does not fit the Jude/2 Peter linkage of angelic sin to the flood. It became prominent later in church history, in part as discomfort with the divine-being reading grew. SKOS does not hold it.
- **Pure myth/allegory** — that Genesis 6 is non-historical mythological residue. *Tie to the text: weak by the standard of this document*, which treats the unseen realm as Scripture treats it: real, not allegory.

## Position 3: The Fallen Operate Against Humanity From the Unseen Realm — With the Specific Mechanism Held as Working Model

### Our Position

The fallen powers are real, they are actively hostile to humanity, and the human struggle is genuinely *against* them, not merely against flesh and blood. They had illicit offspring with human women (the Nephilim), and angels who sinned were bound for it. SKOS holds this as its position. The *further* model — concerning what became of the Nephilim offspring, and the precise mechanism by which the fallen now operate against humans — is held as a **working model** at lower confidence, and part of it is explicitly **pending the SKOS canon decision** (see below).

### Scriptural Basis

**ESV — Ephesians 6:12:** *"For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places."*

**ESV — John 10:10:** *"The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy. I came that they may have life and have it abundantly."*

**ESV — Genesis 6:4:** *"The Nephilim were on the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God came in to the daughters of man and they bore children to them."*

**ESV — Jude 6** and **2 Peter 2:4** (above) — angels who sinned were bound.

Directly textual: the powers are real, the struggle against them is real, the thief's purpose is to kill/steal/destroy, the illicit union and offspring happened, and sinning angels were bound.

### Confidence

**High** that the fallen powers are real and actively hostile, and that the human struggle is genuinely against them — Ephesians 6:12 and John 10:10 are direct.

**High** that the illicit union and Nephilim offspring occurred, and that sinning angels were bound — Genesis 6:4, Jude 6, 2 Peter 2:4.

**Moderate / pending canon** on the dead-Nephilim-become-demons mechanism — explicitly from Enoch, pending the formal SKOS canon decision in `THE-CANON.md`.

**Low / held as metaphor** on the dimensional framing and the precise operational mechanics.

**Not claimed** — the exact timing of any change in the fallen's access to the physical realm.

### Competing Views and the Canon Question

The claim that **the disembodied spirits of the dead Nephilim became the roaming demons that now operate against humanity** is the classic explanation found explicitly in **1 Enoch** (chapters 15-16). It is *not* stated outright in the 66-book Protestant canon. The 66-book canon offers circumstantial hints (Yahshua casts out spirits that seek embodiment; the language of "unclean spirits"), but the specific *mechanism* — that demons are precisely the dead Nephilim — comes from Enoch.

**This claim's confidence is therefore downstream of a decision SKOS has not yet formally made: what is the SKOS canon?** The architect has indicated SKOS works from an 81-book canon (the Ethiopian Tewahedo broader tradition, which includes 1 Enoch). That canon decision is being taken up properly — through research, human spiritual counsel, and the leading of the Holy Spirit — in a forthcoming process and will be recorded in `THE-CANON.md`. Until that decision is formally on the record:

- If SKOS formally ratifies the 81-book canon including Enoch, this claim moves toward **stated position**.
- Until then, it is held as **working model, drawn substantially from 1 Enoch, with suggestive but not explicit support in the 66-book canon.**

The "4th-dimensional" language for the unseen realm is held as **useful metaphor** for the real operational asymmetry between the unseen and seen — not as a cosmological measurement claim. The unseen realm is real (this document's governing note establishes that); "4th-dimensional" is borrowed vocabulary to describe it, not a claim Scripture itself makes in those terms.

The precise timing — *when* the fallen lost or changed their access to the physical realm — is **not known** and not claimed. What is held: the illicit union happened, offspring resulted, sinning angels were bound. The exact chronology of access is left open.

## On Inherited Frameworks, Suppression, and Why Re-Examination Is Owed

A word on *why* this document exists in position-taking form rather than as deferential "open questions."

Some of what the broader church has treated as "fringe" was treated that way not because the text was against it, but because of *who was teaching it* and *what the dominant culture had already decided to credit.* This is not a small footnote. It is documented history.

The divine-council and related material was taught by **Stephen Darby** (Stephen Darby Ministries) before the more recent academic articulation made it broadly acceptable — and it was met with suspicion by parts of the body. **Dr. Frederick K. C. Price** publicly confronted racism within the body of Christ and was likewise not respected by much of the larger body when he did so. When **Michael Heiser**, with academic credentials in Hebrew Bible and ancient Semitic languages, articulated the divine-council framework, much of the body moved it from "fringe" to "respected" — though the *content* had not changed. What changed was the messenger's credentials and, in at least one case, the willingness of the dominant culture to hear a Black preacher.

The principle this yields — and it is itself a discernment teaching — is: **credentials are not the test, and dismissal is not the test. The text, carefully handled under the Light of Scripture, is the test.** This cuts in *both* directions, which is what makes it sound rather than a grievance:

- It indicts **inherited inertia and corrupt motive** — "fringe" has often meant "said by someone the dominant culture had already decided not to credit." And the history is worse than mere inertia in places: frameworks for interpreting Scripture were, in documented fact, bent to justify the enslavement of human beings made in Yahweh's image. The largest American slaveholding denominational body was a church body; the Southern Baptist Convention was formed in 1845 specifically so that slaveholders could serve as missionaries, formally apologized for that history in 1995, and still exists. A church culture that got *that* catastrophically wrong about human beings cannot be assumed reliable about what it dismissed elsewhere. Re-examination under Scripture is therefore *owed* — not optional.
- It also keeps the **guardrail** — the fix is *not* "therefore the dismissed voice is always right." Being dismissed is not proof of being correct, just as having credentials is not proof of being correct. Every claim still goes to the text, carefully handled. That is *why* this document states positions *with their scriptural basis and an honest confidence level* — so a claim can be weighed on its scriptural ground regardless of who carries it, and so that breaking a bad framework does not become an excuse to build another one.

Secular scholarship has documented this same pattern outside the domain of Scripture. The historian Chancellor Williams (*The Destruction of Black Civilization*, 1971) argued for a systematic erasure of African historical achievement. Cheikh Anta Diop and Théophile Obenga, through linguistic, cultural, and anthropological evidence, forced mainstream scholarship — at the 1974 UNESCO symposium in Cairo and after — to formally engage the African origins of Egyptian civilization rather than dismiss them. Their domains were history and anthropology, not theology; but the pattern they documented is the same one this document identifies in Scripture interpretation: *the record gets shaped by power, and the shaping has to be consciously corrected for.* A church that cannot acknowledge this — when scholars and communities already know it — loses credibility precisely with the people who can see it most clearly. (The discipline of this document applies to this point as well: the strong, well-documented core — that the historical record was shaped by power, and that mainstream scholarship was forced to engage rather than dismiss — is what is claimed here; not every specific contested methodological claim within that body of work.)

A further observation worth recording: inherited religious vocabulary sometimes *obscures* this material rather than clarifying it. Much religious language has been worn smooth by repetition into a *devotional* register, while this material is fundamentally *operational* — it describes mechanisms, cause and effect, a real conflict with real tactics. "Spiritual warfare" as a worn phrase lands softer than a precise description of hostile powers operating from the unseen realm. SKOS, in this document and generally, prefers language with the edges left on — and prefers the rigor of careful study, under the Light of Scripture, that brings truth into the open as Yahweh wills it.

## Why This Document Stays In-Progress

This material is biblically serious. None of it is fringe in the dismissive sense. But it remains in this position-and-inquiry document, not yet fully ratified, for a disciplined reason: **the same energy that breaks a bad framework can build a new bad framework, if a position is held more tightly than its scriptural basis warrants.** The structure — position, basis, competing views, confidence — is the protection. It lets each position be stated boldly where the text is strong, and held more lightly where the work is more inference, with the difference always visible.

This is not waiting on inherited perspectives to catch up. It is building carefully enough that the work *holds* when it is tested — because it will be tested, and it should survive the test. The Southern Baptist Convention's founding is itself the cautionary example: bad frameworks, confidently held, do catastrophic harm. The discipline of this document is how SKOS refuses to become that — while still refusing the opposite error of saying nothing at all.

As positions here are tested, grounded, and confirmed, they may graduate into fully ratified Root documents.
