## The patterns (design inputs)

### 1. Inside-out sequence: identity before mechanics

Open the Financial System for Families from **identity**, not from spreadsheets: God owns it, the family stewards it (the ownership-vs-stewardship frame). Right financial behavior flows out of who the steward is, not the reverse. This matches `MIND-OF-CHRIST.md` (identity-grounding) and `ANXIETY-CLARITY-PRINCIPLE.md` (the scared family needs the why before the how).

**Surface implication:** onboarding leads with the steward's identity and the Source's ownership; the ledger and categories come second.

### 2. The Five Divine Purposes of Money as a budgeting lens

A dignified, faith-grounded category set for the family budget, re-derived from Scripture per our standard:

1. Advancing God's Kingdom
2. Supporting one's family
3. Fulfilling personal calling
4. Helping those in need
5. Enjoying God's blessings

A proposal to sit alongside (or replace) generic needs/wants/savings categories. **This is a product-identity choice reserved for Darrell** -- held here as a proposal, not a commitment.

### 3. The three-step arc as a clarity device

A simple, finite, sequenced plan reduces financial anxiety (`ANXIETY-CLARITY-PRINCIPLE.md`):

1. Get the heart and identity right (ownership vs. stewardship).
2. Get free of debt with a simple, disciplined plan. (Home: the Books > Debts surface.)
3. Build and steward surplus toward the five purposes. (Home: the buffer-fund / savings surfaces.)

Honest framing for the guidance copy: "the steps are simple, but following them requires hard work" -- faith-expressed-in-works, no get-rich-quick promise. This matches `SEED-DATA-AS-ASPIRATION.md` (a thriving steward, not a lottery winner).

### 4. Church-as-economic-engine framing for the Church Module

A church that teaches financial stewardship as part of discipleship -- not a separate secular track. The Church Module can carry a financial-literacy teaching track generalized from COLG's needs (`COMMUNITY-FIRST-MISSION.md` Commitment 6, train-the-community).

## Guardrails (binding)

- **Doctrine flows from the Worldview + Scripture, never from an external teacher.** Cite our source; corroborate, do not canonize.
- **Discern prosperity-adjacency.** PoeTech's posture is stewardship-and-sufficiency under The Way -- never wealth-as-proof-of-favor. Reject the prosperity-gospel distortion AND the poverty-equals-holiness distortion.
- **Discern tithing positions against the Worldview**, not against any single teacher.
- **No upsell.** The Financial System for Families stays free (`project-what-is-actually-free`). Empowerment message yes; extractive monetization model no (`DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT-NOT-EXTRACTION.md`).
- **Religion AND Relationship.** Backbone and warmth together (`EXCELLENCE-STANDARD.md`).
