# The Root

> *"For no one can lay a foundation other than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ."* — 1 Corinthians 3:11 (ESV)

## What This Document Is

The Root is the foundation tier of SKOS — the load-bearing confessions every other document, module, and practice grows from. It lives in `docs/00-foundations/_root/`.

This document defines what The Root *is*: not a layer of abstract principles, but the Rock Himself, and what it means that a human being is built on Him. It is the most foundational document in the SKOS architecture after the meta-frame established in `THE-WAY.md`.

## The Root Is a Person

The Root is not a concept. The Root is Christ.

**ESV — Revelation 5:5:** *"behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered..."*

**ESV — Revelation 22:16:** *"I am the root and the descendant of David, the bright morning star."*

**ESV — Isaiah 11:10:** *"In that day the root of Jesse, who shall stand as a signal for the peoples — of him shall the nations inquire, and his resting place shall be glorious."*

Christ calls Himself the Root. He is also named the Rock (1 Corinthians 10:4), the Chief Cornerstone (Ephesians 2:20), the Rock of our salvation (Psalm 95:1), the one foundation that can be laid (1 Corinthians 3:11). The Root tier of SKOS carries that name as a confession: everything SKOS is rests on Him, or it does not stand.

**ESV — Matthew 7:24-25:** *"Everyone then who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock. And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house, but it did not fall, because it had been founded on the rock."*

**KJV — Matthew 7:24-25:** *"Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock."*

The rain, the floods, the winds come to every house. What determines whether a house stands is not whether storms come — it is what the house was founded on.

## The Human as Sovereign Temple

Here is the foundational claim of this document: **the human being is the Sovereign temple of Yahweh.** Not a metaphor borrowed for effect — the direct teaching of Scripture.

**ESV — 1 Corinthians 3:16:** *"Do you not know that you are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells in you?"*

**ESV — 1 Corinthians 6:19-20:** *"Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body."*

**ESV — 2 Corinthians 6:16:** *"For we are the temple of the living God; as God said, 'I will make my dwelling among them and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.'"*

This reframes everything SKOS does. SKOS is named an *operating system* because the human it serves is a kind of system — a temple meant to house the presence of Yahweh, with a mind, a body, and a spirit designed to run in connection with Him. The mind, body, and spirit of a human connected to Yahweh — the Godhead — is the highest technology integration that exists. It was Yahweh's original intent: He walked with humanity in the garden in the cool of the day (Genesis 3:8), and the end of the story is the same picture — *"the dwelling place of God is with man"* (Revelation 21:3). Original intent and final destination are one image. SKOS exists to serve the restoration of that integration.

But a temple can run on corrupted inputs. A temple can be defiled, neglected, or filled with the wrong things. Which is why the next two truths matter.

## Repentance Is Framework-Correction

The first word Yahshua preached was *repent*.

**ESV — Matthew 4:17:** *"From that time Jesus began to preach, saying, 'Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.'"*

The Greek word is *metanoia* (G3341) — literally a *change of mind*, a change of the framework through which everything is processed. Repentance is not merely feeling sorry. It is the correction of the operating framework itself, so that the temple runs on true inputs instead of false ones.

**ESV — Romans 12:2:** *"Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect."*

**KJV — Romans 12:2:** *"And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God."*

> Greek: *metanoia* (G3341) — *meta* (change) + *noia* (mind). The mind turned, the framework corrected.

This is why Yahshua taught repentance first. If the temple's framework is corrupted — if the data flowing through the mind is false — then nothing downstream runs right. Correct the framework, and the temple can begin to function as designed. Repentance is the framework-correction command, and it is ongoing, not one-time.

## Carnal Mind or Spiritual Mind — A Choice

The temple, framework-corrected, still faces a daily choice. Scripture names two settings the mind can run on.

**ESV — Romans 8:6:** *"For to set the mind on the flesh is death, but to set the mind on the Spirit is life and peace."*

**KJV — Romans 8:6:** *"For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace."*

**ESV — Galatians 5:16-17:** *"But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh. For the desires of the flesh are against the Spirit, and the desires of the Spirit are against the flesh, for these are opposed to each other..."*

We choose the carnal mind or the spiritual mind. Same person, two possible settings, and the setting chosen produces the actions that follow — the bad ways and the good ways both trace back to which mind was set. The Holy Spirit always chooses life. The choice the human makes is whether to set the mind where the Spirit already is.

## The New Spirit Is Capacity — Abiding Is the Skill

Here is a distinction that matters greatly, and it is easy to miss.

**ESV — Ezekiel 36:26-27:** *"And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you. And I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. And I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes and be careful to obey my rules."*

The new spirit is *given*. It is the installed capacity — the equipment that makes spiritual life possible at all. But capacity is not the same as competence. Having the new spirit does not mean automatically operating in it. There is a skill to be learned, and Scripture names it: **abiding.**

**ESV — John 15:4-5:** *"Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit by itself, unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in me. I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing."*

**KJV — John 15:5:** *"I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing."*

The new spirit gives the branch its life-connection to the Vine. Abiding is the learned, practiced skill of *staying* in that connection — of setting the mind on the Spirit, repeatedly, as a developed competence rather than a one-time event. SKOS exists, in large part, to help people *learn that skill*. Capacity is given by Yahweh. Competence is trained. SKOS is training.

## Abiding Is Worth It — The Incentive

Scripture does not merely command abiding. It makes abiding *desirable*. It shows the one who abides what the abiding produces, so that the thirst to learn the skill is itself awakened.

**ESV — Psalm 1:3:** *"He is like a tree planted by streams of water that yields its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither. In all that he does, he prospers."*

**ESV — Psalm 23:2-3:** *"He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside still waters. He restores my soul."*

**ESV — Isaiah 40:31:** *"but they who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles; they shall run and not be weary; they shall walk and not faint."*

**KJV — Isaiah 40:31:** *"But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint."*

The one who abides is the tree by the streams — fed, fruitful, unwithered. Led to lie down by still waters in green pastures, the soul *restored*. Given strength that mounts up on eagles' wings, faster and higher than striving in our own might can reach. There is a real and good reason to learn this skill: the abiding life is the life that is fed, restored, and carried. Where the soul lies down shapes what the soul becomes — and the still waters reshape what striving never could.

> **On the Word reshaping the whole temple.** It would be too small a claim to say "Scripture speaks of the soul; neuroscience speaks of the brain; the two merely rhyme." Scripture says more than that. *"For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart"* (Hebrews 4:12, ESV). The Word operates on **both** registers at once — soul *and* spirit, joints *and* marrow, the immaterial *and* the material — as one act, down to the thoughts. Scripture is not merely *compatible* with the idea that Yahweh's Word physically reshapes the temple; it *claims* it. The renewing of the mind (Romans 12:2) and the Word piercing to joints and marrow (Hebrews 4:12) together assert that His Word works on the whole integrated system. Where modern observation of neural change lines up with this, it is *watching a mechanism* of what Scripture already declared — it is observation, not the source. The plumb line is Hebrews 4:12 and Romans 12:2. The science observes; Scripture declares.

## Forgiveness — Release the Debt, Keep the Wisdom

The abiding life produces something the carnal mind cannot: the capacity to forgive completely while still remembering clearly.

**ESV — Matthew 6:12:** *"and forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors."*

**ESV — Colossians 3:13:** *"bearing with one another and, if one has a complaint against another, forgiving each other; as the Lord has forgiven you, so you also must forgive."*

**ESV — Micah 7:19:** *"He will again have compassion on us; he will tread our iniquities underfoot. You will cast all our sins into the depths of the sea."*

**ESV — Matthew 5:44:** *"But I say to you, Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you."*

Forgiveness is full and without asterisk. The *debt* — the claim the offense held on the heart — is released, cast into the sea of forgetfulness. But forgiveness is not amnesia. It does not mean erasing the event, because Yahweh Himself instructs us to learn from suffering. So three things happen at once, and only the third makes the first two survivable:

1. **Release the debt** — it goes into the sea; it is no longer held in the heart.
2. **Keep the wisdom** — the lesson the suffering taught remains; the scar informs.
3. **Love the enemy anyway** — the actual command, not a suggestion.

Joseph is the model: *"you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good"* (Genesis 50:20). Full forgiveness, clear memory, redeemed meaning — all at once. He could name precisely what his brothers did *and* he wept over them in love. That combination — clear memory held in the same hand as genuine love — is not a natural human capacity. The flesh can do one or the other: love by pretending the harm away, or remember by nursing the grudge. It cannot do both. Only the new spirit, abiding, gives the capacity to hold clear memory and genuine love together. This is the hardest rep of the abiding skill — and therefore the clearest proof that it is the Spirit's work and not our own. We forgive for Him, because He did it for His Father.

## The Only Way

This document, and the whole SKOS architecture, rests finally on a claim Yahshua made about Himself.

**ESV — John 14:6:** *"Jesus said to him, 'I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.'"*

**ESV — Acts 4:12:** *"And there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."*

The Way is the only Way. This is not SKOS being narrow — it is SKOS confessing the claim Yahshua made. And the only Way is not a method, a technique, or a system. The only Way is a Person. SKOS is named "The Way" precisely to keep this confession at the center. Every tool SKOS builds, every module, every practice, exists to help families walk with the Person who *is* the Way — never to substitute for Him.

## Why The Root Holds

The Root holds because the Root is Christ, and Christ has already conquered (Revelation 5:5). The storms come to every house. What was founded on the Rock does not fall — not because the storms are gentle, but because the foundation is Him.

A house built on The Root can endure rain, flood, and wind. That endurance is not the absence of suffering — `THE-ROOT-POSITIONS-AND-INQUIRY.md` takes up the harder questions of suffering, the two roads, and what it means that humanity is being formed through a broken world. But the foundation question is settled here: the house stands because of what it is built on.

## Religion AND Relationship in The Root

**Religion-side:** The Root is structure — the load-bearing confessions, the disciplined framework, the non-negotiable foundation. It does not drift with mood or fashion. It is the rock under the house.

**Relationship-side:** The Root is a Person — Christ Himself, walking with humanity, long-suffering toward backsliding people, restoring souls beside still waters. The foundation is not a doctrine to be filed. It is a Someone to be abided in.

Both.
