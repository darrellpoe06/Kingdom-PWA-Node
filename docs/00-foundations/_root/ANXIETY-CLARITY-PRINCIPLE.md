# Anxiety-Clarity Principle

**Declared by Darrell, 2026-05-28.** This document captures binding product DNA for PoeTech Family OS. It joins THE-WAY, MIND-OF-CHRIST, EXCELLENCE-STANDARD, and SCRIPTURE-REFERENCE-STANDARD as a senior foundation that governs all generated content and design decisions.

## The principle

**Anxiety is informational at root, not emotional.** People aren't anxious because they're weak; they're anxious because they don't know what to do. Provide clarity on what to do, when to do it, why it matters, and how to actually carry it out — and the anxiety lifts. Not because the situation changed, but because the fog did.

Therefore: **every surface of this system answers four questions for the person standing in front of it.**

- **What do I do?** The next concrete action, named in the user's own language.
- **When do I do it?** Today, this week, by the 1st — with the deadline shown plain.
- **Why does it matter?** The reason this matters to the people in their care — not abstract optimization, but real consequence.
- **How do I actually do it?** Step-by-step, in a way someone scared can still follow. Assistance and guidance present and patient, even at risk of being "almost too much."

## The theological frame

**Yahweh has our back and front.** The work is protected. The agent operating in this system does so under the conviction that the family doing the work is doing what the Father gave them to do. We build with confidence, not reservation. Not arrogance — confidence. The difference is that arrogance trusts the builder; confidence trusts the One who called them to build.

**His Will be done.** When a design choice is in doubt, the test is: does this remove anxiety from a family that doesn't know what to do? If yes, ship it. If no — even if elegant, even if clever — set it down. The Will being done is the family standing in clarity, not the developer admiring the architecture.

## Design implications

Every view in the app should be auditable against four questions:

1. **Does this view answer "what should I do right now?"** If a user lands on this view and walks away still unsure of the next action, the view has not done its job.
2. **Is the timing surfaced?** Date, deadline, days-until — never implicit.
3. **Is the consequence visible?** The "why this matters" reasoning is one tap away, never buried in a help doc.
4. **Is the how broken down small?** Steps a frightened or tired person can still follow. No "just import the OFX file" — show them where Chase puts the export, walk them through the file picker, confirm the success.

When a view fails any of these, it's not WCAG that's broken — it's the anxiety-clarity contract. The view is producing the very anxiety the system exists to lift.

## Guidance-richness comfort level

Darrell, 2026-05-28: "Almost too much but we'll see."

The system errs toward MORE guidance, not less. Where conventional UX wisdom says "don't tell users what to do, let them explore" — the conventional UX wisdom is wrong for this audience. Families in financial pressure are not "exploring." They are looking for someone to tell them, with authority and compassion, what to do.

The acceptable failure mode is "too much hand-holding for a power user." The unacceptable failure mode is "not enough direction for a scared parent." We optimize for the parent.

## The "almost too much" check

When a new feature is proposed, the question is not "is this too directive?" The question is: "if a family in real pressure landed here today, would this give them what they need to take the next step, OR would it leave them still wondering what to do?" If they'd still wonder, add direction. If they'd act, ship.

## Surfaces this principle should reshape

**Big Picture dashboard:** today shows three hero metrics. Should also surface "today's clarity" — the single most important action this household should take in the next 24 hours. With deadline. With reason. With one-tap "do it now" if it's an in-app action.

**Books → Accounts:** today shows balances. Should also surface "reconcile gap" guidance per account when bank Δ exceeds threshold — "your manual ledger and the bank disagree by $X — here's what to do about it."

**Books → Tx → Needs attention:** today shows count + filter. Should surface a workflow companion: "Here's the queue. Tap Accept on rows that match what you remember. Tap Noise on fee reversals. We're walking through this together." Not a wall of rows; a guided pass.

**Demo welcome modal:** the public-facing sample already begins this work — "Here's what providing for the people in your care looks like with the books open." Stewardship framing over feature listing. The pattern carries through.

**Vacation runbook:** already structured as "what's running / what could break / what to do if X" — the runbook IS the anxiety-clarity contract for a traveling owner.
