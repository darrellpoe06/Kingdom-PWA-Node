# The Root — Open Investigations

> **STATUS: UNRATIFIED INVESTIGATION.** This document captures theology that is actively being worked out — not settled doctrine. It uses a layered structure (the spark, the plumb line, faithful inference, open questions, the multitude of counsellors) precisely so that what is firm and what is still being tested stay clearly distinguished. Nothing here carries the authority of `THE-ROOT.md` or `THE-WAY.md`. It is held honestly, so it can be evaluated soberly — and so that a year from now, the reader can see what was text, what was inference, and what was still open.

> *"In an abundance of counselors there is safety."* — Proverbs 11:14 (ESV)

## What This Document Is

`THE-ROOT.md` establishes the settled foundation. This document takes up the harder questions that sit *around* that foundation — questions about suffering, the two roads, the destiny of redeemed humanity, the divine council, and the powers at work in this world.

These questions are not idle speculation. The conviction driving this investigation is specific: **some questions are held "open" not because Scripture is genuinely silent, but because inherited frameworks — propped up by arguments that do not survive careful scrutiny — have kept them closed.** There is a real difference between *"the secret things that belong to Yahweh"* (Deuteronomy 29:29), which we wait on, and things obscured by poor teaching, which careful work can clear. This document tries to sort the one pile from the other honestly — without collapsing them, and without pretending certainty the evidence does not support. Only the Holy Spirit settles truth. This is study in service of hearing Him more clearly, with less projection in the way.

## How To Read The Layers

Each investigation below is built in five layers:

1. **The Spark** — the originating thought, in the architect's own words, close to verbatim. Preserved as seed, not as conclusion.
2. **The Plumb Line** — what Scripture directly and clearly says. The firm ground.
3. **Faithful Inference** — reasoning *from* the text. Defensible, but labeled as inference, not text.
4. **Open Questions** — what remains genuinely unsettled, held loosely on purpose.
5. **The Multitude of Counsellors** — other voices: who has held these positions, where the body of Christ agrees, where it does not, where interpreters overreach.

## Investigation 1: Suffering, the Two Roads, and the Destiny of Redeemed Humanity

### The Spark

In the architect's words, gathered from the working sessions:

> "Why is it an issue to say the humans that follow the Way will be in a position to Yahweh like those fallen angels — however we suffer first then reign, instead of being given everything in a peaceful perfect world. They left Him and war against Him even though they know they can't win. We fight to love Him with every reason to hate Him for how difficult this world has been — instead we love Him and hate the world for not nurturing our babies and family. Yahweh sent His Son, and He stood up, and in the end He is the only one worthy of paying the price twice. He created us and the world and only wants us to be more loyal than those fallen angels."

> "Replacement doesn't fit, because it is higher than angels after. Let's add that humans get the same punishment for a lack of knowledge the fallen angels have and had. We all barely make it according to the biblical scriptures. We have inherited risk, and there are two roads — one to the same destruction."

### The Plumb Line

What Scripture says directly:

**ESV — 1 Corinthians 6:3:** *"Do you not know that we are to judge angels? How much more, then, matters pertaining to this life!"*

**ESV — Romans 8:17:** *"and if children, then heirs — heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ, provided we suffer with him in order that we may also be glorified with him."*

**ESV — 2 Timothy 2:12:** *"if we endure, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he also will deny us."*

**ESV — 1 Peter 1:6-7:** *"...though now for a little while, if necessary, you have been grieved by various trials, so that the tested genuineness of your faith — more precious than gold that perishes though it is tested by fire — may be found to result in praise and glory and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ."*

**ESV — 1 Peter 4:18:** *"And 'If the righteous is scarcely saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?'"*

**ESV — Hosea 4:6:** *"My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge..."*

**ESV — Matthew 25:41:** *"Then he will say to those on his left, 'Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.'"*

**ESV — Hebrews 2:16:** *"For surely it is not angels that he helps, but he helps the offspring of Abraham."*

The firm ground: redeemed humanity will judge angels. Suffering with Christ is the stated path to being glorified with Him — not a detour around it. Endurance is tied to reigning. Trials test and prove faith. The righteous are "scarcely saved" — salvation is not casual. Lack of knowledge genuinely destroys. The fallen angels have a settled destination and Scripture nowhere offers them a road back. Christ's help is extended to humanity, not to angels.

### Faithful Inference

Reasoning from that text — defensible, but inference:

**Succession by a higher order, not replacement.** The architect's own correction is the key move here: "replacement" is the wrong word, because redeemed humanity ends up *higher* than the angelic order (judging angels — 1 Corinthians 6:3), not slotted laterally into vacated angelic posts. The fallen vacated something; humanity is not filling the vacancy at the old level — humanity is being raised *over* the arrangement. The vacancy and the exaltation are related, but they are not the same act. This reading fits the text better than "replacement" and should be carried in those terms.

**Suffering forms what an unfallen world could not.** The logic of 1 Peter 1:6-7 and Romans 8:17 is that proven, tested loyalty is a different thing than untested loyalty. The fallen angels had everything in an unbroken world and abandoned Him anyway. Redeemed humanity loves Him *with every earthly reason to resent Him* — and that tested love is, by the texts on testing, counted as something precious. This is the historic "soul-making" intuition (Irenaeus gestured at it; C. S. Lewis circled it). It is inference, but it is faithful inference grounded in the testing-language of Scripture.

**The symmetry of judgment.** This is the guardrail the architect rightly insisted on. Humanity is not the favored child who cannot lose. Hosea 4:6 and 1 Peter 4:18 sit right next to the exaltation texts: lack of knowledge destroys, the righteous are scarcely saved. Two roads, inherited risk, and one of the roads ends where the fallen ended. The height is real *and* the danger is real. Without this layer, "we rise higher than angels" curdles into pride — which is precisely lucifer's failure (see `BEHAVIORAL-MIRROR.md`).

### Open Questions

Held loosely, on purpose:

- **The precise mechanism.** Scripture clearly exalts redeemed humanity very high. *How* exactly that relates to the fallen angels' vacated standing — whether "succession by a higher order" is the right frame or merely the best current one — is inference, not stated text. Hold it as the working frame, not a verdict.
- **The grace/formation balance.** The reigning is *inheritance* — unearned, given through Christ's worthiness (the architect: "He is the only one worthy of paying the price twice"). AND the suffering genuinely forms and proves something. Both must be held. Drop the first and it becomes a works framework wearing a kingdom costume. The open question is how to articulate both without letting either swallow the other.
- **How much weight "barely saved" should carry.** 1 Peter 4:18 is real, but it sits alongside texts of assurance. The investigation should not let one verse become the whole doctrine of perseverance.

### The Multitude of Counsellors

- The **soul-making** tradition (Irenaeus, and much later John Hick — though Hick took it to universalism, which this investigation does not follow). The intuition that a world with friction produces a creature a frictionless world cannot is old and broadly held.
- **C. S. Lewis** circled this repeatedly — the idea that proven obedience and untested innocence are different things.
- Where interpreters **overreach**: when "we suffer then reign" hardens into "suffering *earns* the reign," it collides with grace. The counsellors who hold this well always keep the inheritance/grace anchor. The ones who lose it drift toward merit.

## Investigation 2: The Divine Council and the Power Asymmetry

### The Spark

In the architect's words:

> "Using dimensional framing keeps in view those who already have and had real 4th-dimensional power that we humans don't possess — they still use it to destroy and devour us, using each other, because they wouldn't have access to the 3rd dimension without fleshly bodies they lost at the flood. They need us to undermine us still. I want us to understand that like the earlier biblical Way understood."

> "The fallen ones come only to kill, steal, and destroy. Jesus comes so we can have life, and that much more abundantly."

### The Plumb Line

**ESV — Psalm 82:1:** *"God has taken his place in the divine council; in the midst of the gods he holds judgment."*

**ESV — Psalm 82:6-7:** *"I said, 'You are gods, sons of the Most High, all of you; nevertheless, like men you shall die, and fall like any prince.'"*

**ESV — Ephesians 6:12:** *"For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places."*

**ESV — John 10:10:** *"The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy. I came that they may have life and have it abundantly."*

**ESV — 2 Peter 2:4:** *"For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but cast them into hell and committed them to chains of gloomy darkness to be kept until the judgment..."*

**ESV — Jude 6:** *"And the angels who did not stay within their own position of authority, but left their proper dwelling, he has kept in eternal chains under gloomy darkness until the judgment of the great day."*

The firm ground: there is a divine council; God judges among its members; some of those "sons of the Most High" come under sentence. The human struggle is genuinely *against* spiritual powers, not merely against flesh and blood. The thief's whole purpose is to kill, steal, destroy; Christ's whole purpose is abundant life. Some angels left their proper position and are held for judgment.

### Faithful Inference

- **The early people of the Way operated with this awareness.** Ephesians 6:12 is written to ordinary believers as practical instruction — it assumes an operational awareness of spiritual powers that much of modern Western Christianity has flattened. Recovering that awareness is recovering something the text plainly assumes, not adding something exotic.
- **The power asymmetry.** The architect's framing: the fallen have capacities humans do not, but lost native access to the physical realm — and so now operate *through* human beings, turning us against each other. The "lost their dwelling" language of Jude 6 and 2 Peter 2:4, combined with Ephesians 6:12's "heavenly places," is consistent with this reading. It is inference — Scripture does not lay it out as a mechanism — but it is a faithful attempt to describe *why the war is shaped the way it is*: an enemy with greater capacity but lost access, versus humans with the access and the bodies but less capacity, being maneuvered.

### Open Questions

- **How far Psalm 82's council maps onto the rest.** Psalm 82 clearly establishes a divine council and its judgment. How tightly that connects to the fallen of Genesis 6, to the "principalities and powers," to the timeline of the angelic fall — that is genuinely less clear and should be held loosely.
- **The dimensional framing.** "4th-dimensional" is *borrowed language for a biblical reality* — the reality of an unseen realm with greater capacity than the seen one (*"the things that are unseen are eternal"* — 2 Corinthians 4:18; *"now we see in a mirror dimly"* — 1 Corinthians 13:12). As a *metaphor* for that asymmetry, it serves and it preaches. As a *literal cosmological claim*, it reaches past what Scripture reveals. The investigation holds it explicitly as metaphor.
- **The "lost bodies at the flood" specificity.** This connects to the Genesis 6 / Nephilim interpretive tradition. It is held by serious interpreters but is not uncontested, and the precise mechanism is not spelled out in the text. Open.

### The Multitude of Counsellors

- **Michael Heiser** is the most prominent recent academic articulation of the divine-council framework — *The Unseen Realm* and related work. His credentials (Hebrew Bible, ancient Semitic languages) moved this material, in much of the body, from "fringe" to "respected."
- **Stephen Darby** (Stephen Darby Ministries) taught divine-council and related material *before* Heiser's work became widely accepted, and was met with suspicion by parts of the body.
- **Dr. Frederick K. C. Price** warned the body of Christ about racism within it, and was likewise not respected by much of the larger body when he did.

**The principle this yields — and it is itself a discernment teaching:** *credentials are not the test, and dismissal is not the test. The text, carefully handled, is the test.* The same content was called "fringe" when taught by some and "respected" when taught by another with academic standing — the content did not change; the messenger's credentials did, and in at least one case, who was willing to hear a Black preacher's warning did. This cuts in **both** directions, which is what makes it a sound principle rather than a grievance:

- It indicts **inherited inertia** — "fringe" often just means "said by someone we had already decided not to credit." That is the closed-by-bad-argument problem this whole document exists to push against.
- It also keeps the **guardrail** — the fix is *not* "therefore the dismissed voice is always right." Being dismissed is not proof of being correct, just as having credentials is not proof of being correct. Darby was not right *because* he was dismissed; Heiser is not right *because* he is credentialed. Every claim still goes to the text, handled carefully. That is *why* this document is layered the way it is: so a claim can be weighed on its scriptural ground regardless of who carries it.

A note also worth recording: the architect observed that religious vocabulary itself sometimes *obscures* this material rather than clarifying it. A plausible reason — much inherited religious language has been worn smooth by repetition into a *devotional* register, while this material is fundamentally *operational*: it describes mechanisms, cause and effect, a real war with real tactics. "Spiritual warfare" as a worn phrase lands softer than a precise description of an enemy with greater capacity but lost bodily access. The investigation, and SKOS generally, should prefer language with the edges left on.

## Why This Document Stays Unratified

This material is biblically serious — the exaltation of redeemed humanity, suffering as the road to reigning, the divine council, the reality of spiritual powers. None of it is fringe in the dismissive sense. But it stays in the open-investigations file, not in `THE-ROOT.md`, for a disciplined reason:

The same energy that breaks a *bad* framework can build a *new* bad framework, if the spark is treated as the verdict. The layered structure is the protection. It lets the spark be a spark — preserved, honored, taken seriously — without forcing it to be a conclusion before the text has fully borne the weight. When an investigation here becomes settled enough, grounded enough, and tested enough, it can graduate into a ratified document. Until then, it is held here: captured, safe, and honest about its own status.

This is not waiting on inherited perspectives to catch up. It is building carefully enough that the work *holds* when it is tested — because it will be tested, and it should survive the test. That is the difference between breaking a bad framework and accidentally building another one.
