> *"They received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so."* — Acts 17:11 (ESV)

## The Idea in One Line

A living discussion layer attached to SKOS's living documents — so that understanding of the text is sharpened organically, by the whole community, anytime and anywhere, rather than only when one body convenes.

## Why This Fits SKOS

The Bereans "examined the Scriptures daily." The vision of SKOS is that the King's character becomes *clearer* through engagement, not more distant. Several SKOS documents — the canon study batches, `THE-ROOT-POSITIONS-AND-INQUIRY.md` — are explicitly **living**: dated, open to revision, meant to be revisited as understanding grows. A discussion layer is the natural engine for that growth. It gives the living documents a way to keep breathing: a continuous, organic channel for perspectives that are not yet in the documents to be raised, weighed, and — where they carry real substance — folded in.

The instinct behind it is sound: understanding should not be gated behind one body meeting at one time. The whole community, at whatever time is convenient to each person, anywhere, can contribute to sharpening how the text is understood.

## What the Feature Would Do

- **A discussion section attached to living documents** — open to the community, anyone, anywhere, anytime. A person can add a perspective, raise a question, or surface a consideration that the document does not yet address.
- **An engagement signal that flags where clarification is owed** — whatever is most discussed indicates where the community most needs the document sharpened. "Most-engaged" is a *focus signal*: it tells the system where to direct the next round of clarifying work.
- **A feedback loop into the living documents** — discussion feeds back as *input*. Substantive threads sharpen the documents, raise new questions, and send genuine substance to the appropriate spiritual counsel. The discussion layer is a **feeder**, not a **decider**.
- **A boundary marker on every document** — so a reader always knows which layer they are in: discussion that *sharpens understanding*, versus ratified positions and the canon boundary that are *settled by accountable spiritual counsel under the Holy Spirit*.

## The Hard Line — Why It Must Hold

The discussion layer can *inform and sharpen* understanding of the text. It cannot *settle the canon*, and it cannot *ratify doctrine*. This line is not a limitation bolted on — it is the thing that keeps the feature faithful.

The precise distinction:

- **Sharpening understanding of a text** → the whole community, organic, anytime. This is the discussion layer doing its proper work.
- **Deciding the boundary of the canon, or ratifying a doctrinal position** → belongs to accountable spiritual counsel — pastor, elders, mature believers who can be held responsible before Yahweh — under the leading of the Holy Spirit, recorded in the appropriate ratified document (e.g., `THE-CANON.md`).

Why the line holds: **"most discussed" tells you where the energy is — it does not tell you what is true.** A loud thread is not a verdict. Volume is not discernment. If "most discussed becomes what we clarify" were ever allowed to slide into "most discussed becomes what we conclude," the canon and the doctrine would be set by whoever shows up and posts most — which is a different thing entirely from the King's character tested by accountable people. This is the same principle the canon study runs on, drawn from Galatians 1:8: **rank does not authenticate the message** — and a high comment count is just rank in another form. The message goes to the standard: Yahweh's Way and character as revealed in the 66.

So the discussion layer is the **Berean engine** of the system — daily, eager examination of the Scriptures by the whole community — and not a vote. It surfaces, it sharpens, it feeds. It does not rule.

## How It Relates to the Canon Process

The canon questions are finalized through the established process: research support, the architect's vetting, then the body's vetting under the Holy Spirit — producing `THE-CANON.md` and the living canon study documents. Once those living documents exist, the discussion layer attaches to them and does its work: surfacing perspectives not yet in the documents, flagging where clarification is most needed, feeding substance back. The canon study documents are *living* precisely so this can happen — but "living" means *open to disciplined revision through accountable process*, not *open to revision by thread volume*.

## Open Questions for When This Is Built

- What is the actual mechanism by which a substantive discussion thread gets reviewed and, where warranted, folded into a living document? Who reviews, and on what cadence?
- How is the engagement signal measured without it becoming a popularity metric that distorts what gets attention?
- How does the boundary marker render in the actual interface, so the distinction between "discussion layer" and "ratified position" is unmistakable to every reader?
- How is the discussion layer moderated so it stays a Berean engine — eager, honest examination — and does not degrade into contention? (Relevant: the SKOS standards already drafted on vocabulary, tone, and the Test for generated output.)
- What is the relationship between the discussion layer and the human spiritual counsel — how does genuine substance get routed to them without the layer either bypassing them or burdening them?
