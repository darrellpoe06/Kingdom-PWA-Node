# The Devouring Patterns Library

Built on 1 Peter 5:8 (the adversary roams seeking whom he may devour). A catalog of biblical evil-patterns (Cain, Babel, Balaam, Saul, Pharisee, Judas, Diotrephes, Jezebel, Babylon, Sodom) with their diagnostic markers and historical/contemporary manifestations. A pattern-recognition tool, never a labeling system for declaring specific persons or groups outside fellowship.
