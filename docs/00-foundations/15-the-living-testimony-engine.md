# The Living Testimony Engine

Built on James 2:17 (faith without works is dead) and 3 John 1:2 (that your soul may prosper and you may be in health). The integration layer that runs across all twelve modules: captures faith-expressed-in-works, documents Yahweh's specific movement in the user's life, surfaces fruit-of-the-Spirit emergence, and builds the multi-generational testimony arc.
