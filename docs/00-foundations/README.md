# 00 — Foundations

The fifteen foundational documents, in order. Each is currently a placeholder.

1. [Grace and Mercy Standard](01-grace-and-mercy-standard.md) — The unbreakable design constraint: the system never gatekeeps, never declares users outside fellowship over secondary issues, never withdraws grace based on behavior.
2. [Falling and Rising](02-falling-and-rising.md) — The primary user-journey loop: fall, rise, gain understanding, fall again, rise again — growth measured in understanding, not behavior.
3. [Table Setting Practice](03-table-setting-practice.md) — Trauma healing through nervous-system regulation in the presence of triggers; the Spirit's peace as the mechanism for feasting while the threat is still active.
4. [The Understanding Layer](04-the-understanding-layer.md) — Captures the understanding the Holy Spirit gives the user across every experience into a personal, cross-referenced, inheritable corpus.
5. [Right Judgment and No Condemnation](05-right-judgment-and-no-condemnation.md) — The four-section diagnostic structure required of every output: DATA → TRUTH → IDENTITY → INVITATION.
6. [Three Realm Alignment](06-three-realm-alignment.md) — Body/mind/spirit as an operational diagnostic for finding the bottleneck realm — with caveats, never absolutized.
7. [Neuroplasticity and the Word](07-neuroplasticity-and-the-word.md) — The physical substrate of biblical transformation; metanoia as changing your mind, and the Spirit-given self-control forfeited at the Fall.
8. [The Holy Spirit as Leader](08-the-holy-spirit-as-leader.md) — The current third-dimensional operational executive of the Trinity; the system points to the Spirit and never substitutes for His work.
9. [The Council Chamber](09-the-council-chamber.md) — A perspective-processing space to think through hard situations against Scripture before engaging other humans.
10. [Kings Not Slaves](10-kings-not-slaves.md) — The formation of sovereign humans under Christ's lordship — kings encountering other kings, even when the other party doesn't yet know it.
11. [Seeing Evil Rightly](11-seeing-evil-rightly.md) — Internal self-examination structurally required before external pattern recognition; the architectural firewall against pride and paranoia.
12. [The Devouring Patterns Library](12-the-devouring-patterns-library.md) — A catalog of biblical evil-patterns with diagnostic markers — a recognition tool, never a labeling system for declaring persons outside fellowship.
13. [The lucifer Signature](13-the-lucifer-signature.md) — Self-examination prompts watching for the self-exaltation pattern in users with real gifts, including the system's founder.
14. [Naming Conventions](14-naming-conventions.md) — Typographic theology: God always capitalized, including pronouns; the adversary never capitalized as a proper name.
15. [The Living Testimony Engine](15-the-living-testimony-engine.md) — The integration layer that captures faith-expressed-in-works and builds the multi-generational testimony arc.
