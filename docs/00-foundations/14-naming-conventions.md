# Naming Conventions

Typographic theology. Yahweh, Jesus, the Holy Spirit, the Father, the Son are always capitalized, including pronoun references (He, His, Him when referring to God). Pronouns referring to the adversary (he, his, him) are never capitalized. The adversary — lucifer, satan, the devil, the dragon, the adversary, the accuser, the deceiver, baal and the false gods of his kingdom (added 2026-07-03, declared by Darrell) — is never capitalized as a proper name. One honest edge: Scripture quoted VERBATIM (KJV public domain, per DR-0076 fetch-don't-paraphrase) keeps its source capitalization inside the quotation marks — the Word is not edited; everywhere OUTSIDE a verbatim quote, the lowercase rule governs. he is a defeated, lame, loser figure and gets no honor.
