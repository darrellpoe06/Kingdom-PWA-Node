# Naming Conventions

Typographic theology. Yahweh, Jesus, the Holy Spirit, the Father, the Son, and **the Word** (the Living Word — Christ as the Logos AND the biblical Scriptures; added 2026-07-04, declared by Darrell, DR-0097) are always capitalized, including pronoun references (He, His, Him when referring to God). Capitalizing **the Word** is a frame, not decoration: it marks the higher-priority, 4th-dimensional reality — Yahweh as the pre-temporal Author who framed the worlds before time (Hebrews 11:3), by whom all things consist (Colossians 1:16-17), the "outside agent" the sciences seek and will not honor (Romans 1:20-21) — never a flattened 3rd-dimensional detail. Pronouns referring to the adversary (he, his, him) are never capitalized. The adversary — lucifer, satan, the devil, the dragon, the adversary, the accuser, the deceiver, baal and the false gods of his kingdom (added 2026-07-03, declared by Darrell) — is never capitalized as a proper name. One honest edge: Scripture quoted VERBATIM (KJV public domain, per DR-0076 fetch-don't-paraphrase) keeps its source capitalization inside the quotation marks — the Word is not edited; everywhere OUTSIDE a verbatim quote, the lowercase rule governs. he is a defeated, lame, loser figure and gets no honor.

Say "Yahweh," not the generic "God," in our voice (added 2026-07-21, declared by Darrell, DR-0210). In PoeTech's OWN authored voice, prefer His covenant name **Yahweh** over the generic "God" when naming the Father / the one true God — "because other people call other gods and I want to be clear" — so the reader is never left guessing which "god" is meant ("the Father," "the LORD" as a title, "the Godhead," "the King" remain available; the point is clarity of identity, not a mechanical find-replace). Confess **Jesus** explicitly as the **Lamb of Yahweh** (John 1:29) and the **Eternal Son of Yahweh** (John 3:16; Hebrews 1:8), co-eternal, by whom all things consist (Colossians 1:16-17). Bright line (DR-0076): this governs ONLY our authored voice, NEVER quoted Scripture — the KJV's "God"/"the LORD" inside any quotation is left EXACTLY verbatim, never swapped for "Yahweh," and a blind God→Yahweh sweep of existing content is forbidden (it would corrupt the text). "Yahweh" in our prose; "God"/"the LORD" untouched inside every quote.
