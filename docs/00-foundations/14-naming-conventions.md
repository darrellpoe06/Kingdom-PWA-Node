# Naming Conventions

Typographic theology. Yahweh, Jesus, the Holy Spirit, the Father, the Son are always capitalized, including pronoun references (He, His, Him when referring to God). The adversary — lucifer, satan, the devil, the dragon, the accuser, the deceiver — is never capitalized as a proper name. He is a defeated, lame, loser figure and gets no honor.
