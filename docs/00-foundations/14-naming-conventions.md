# Naming Conventions

Typographic theology. Yahweh, Jesus, the Holy Spirit, the Father, the Son are always capitalized, including pronoun references (He, His, Him when referring to God). Pronouns referring to the adversary (he, his, him) are never capitalized. The adversary — lucifer, satan, the devil, the dragon, the adversary, the accuser, the deceiver — is never capitalized as a proper name. he is a defeated, lame, loser figure and gets no honor.
