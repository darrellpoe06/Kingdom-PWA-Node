# Artwork batch 1: the 18 images that unlock every Kids Zone screen, and the 8 Jonah pages

Generate each from the approved character reference (`guides-reference.png`). The full collection and rules are in `2026-11-13-character-artwork-brief.md`; this is the exact order and spec for the first batch. File names are exact and case-sensitive; drop the files in `app\public\lovecorner\kids\`.

## Rules that apply to every image

- **Same two children, always.** The girl: about nine, deep warm brown skin, long box braids past the shoulders parted at the crown with a big red satin bow on top, small gold hoop earrings, bright wide eyes, open dimpled smile, red hoodie with white-and-black striped sleeve cuffs. The boy: about ten, warm brown skin a shade lighter than hers, short curly twists on top with a clean fade at the sides, warm easy smile, black hoodie with red lining and red drawstrings over a white T-shirt. Keep the bow, the hoops, the hoodies, the hairlines, the eye shapes and the smiles identical in every image; the ages never change.
- **Same look as the reference:** high-quality 3D animated feature-film render, soft warm studio lighting, gentle depth of field, expressive eyes, natural smiles, rich skin with subsurface warmth, clean clothing detail. Not flat vector, not clip art, not photoreal, never a different age.
- **Christ-centered, true to the text.** No magic, spells, wands, glowing runes, fairies, genies, potions, wishes, superpowers, occult signs. Nothing scary. Bible scenes show what the verse says, and miracles are shown plainly as the acts of Yahweh.
- **Three file kinds.** CUTOUT: the child alone on a fully transparent background, PNG with alpha, no shadow on the ground, no backdrop. HEADER: a 3:2 scene with the child and the activity's props, PNG or JPEG, opaque. SCENE: a 16:9 illustration, PNG or JPEG, opaque. Sizes below are minimums; larger is fine. The face stays sharp at 2× phone scale.
- **Where the app crops.** Headers are shown about 120 × 76 px on a tile, so the child's face sits in the upper half and the right two-thirds of the frame, nothing important in the far left or the bottom edge. The hero is shown full width at about 390 × 150 px on a phone, so keep both faces inside the middle band and leave the left third calm for a line of text.
