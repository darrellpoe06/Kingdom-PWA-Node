About the Institute

An educational ministry of The Church of the Living God. The Institute teaches the Word, trains those who serve, and prepares leaders for the house. Credentials are religious certificates of completion. The Institute does not claim accreditation, state authorization, or degrees.

The Love Corner homepage, redesigned

A phone-first mockup of the Church of the Living God app. Nothing in the existing app has been changed. Everything on the screen maps to a tab, a record or a schedule the app already has.

The emblem is the real one. It is the church seal the app already ships (lovecorner-icon-512.png). The purple and gold in this mockup were measured from it: the L is purple, the cross is gold, the heart is red. Red stays on the heart only.
Bishop Gwin's photograph is not in the repository and was not attached to this session. The slot is honest and empty. Use the loader above to preview the real photo in place; it never leaves your browser. No face has been generated.
The hero changes with the real schedule. Sunday Worship 11:00 AM, Bible Study Wednesday 1:00 PM and 6:00 PM (Champaign time), from the church record the app already uses. Inside a service window the button reads Live now. Outside it, Latest message.
Upcoming shows the honest state. The two announced events in the church record (the July Assembly, the August Gospel Fest) have passed, so the list shows the fallback and the weekly services, never an invented event.
The Institute has its own featured card and its own landing screen. Tap the card on the phone (or open #institute) to see the landing page. Only the four courses that already exist in the Learn tab are shown as available. Programs, enrollment and certificates are marked Planned. No accreditation or degree is claimed anywhere.

What the More button holds

All twenty existing tabs, grouped, none removed or merged. Signed-in and staff-only tabs keep their gates. Tap More on the phone to see it.

Who this is for

  • A child finds the Youth Zone in one tap from the home screen.
  • A senior finds Watch, Give, Prayer, and the text-size and read-aloud controls without scrolling through menus.
  • A first-time visitor sees the Bishop, the service time and an invitation before anything else.
