# DR-0529 — The school's subject map, and the gap nobody was tracking

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
- **Type:** governance
- **Relates to:** DR-0432 (Learn is a school, departments derived from the catalog), DR-0433 (Mathematics slice 1), DR-0500 (the Real Estate department opens), DR-0515 (eight of twenty-two), DR-0075 (perpetual improvement — a justified non-improvement needs a why and a re-review date), DR-0076 (measure, do not claim)

## What Darrell saw

He opened the course picker on his phone — **42 courses, 644 lessons** — scrolled the department list, and asked:

> *"We are neglecting the main subjects courses with other courses and lessons or filling them after or in a process I'm not aware of?"*

He was right, and the answer to the second half is **no**. There was no process. There was no plan of record for the school's subjects at all.

## The measurement

Taken from the live catalog, 2026-09-19:

| Department | Courses | Lessons |
|---|---|---|
| The Word & The Way | 7 | 243 |
| **Real Estate** | **12** | **96** |
| Serve the House | 5 | 46 |
| A.I. The Way | 3 | 38 |
| Kingdom Life & Stewardship | 4 | 28 |
| Project Management | 2 | 20 |
| Mathematics | 1 | 8 |
| Business | 1 | 8 |
| Development | 1 | 8 |

**Real Estate had become the second-largest department in the school**, behind only the Word. And these subjects had **nothing**: English, Science, History, Geography, music and art as subjects. The only reading content in the catalog was six pre-K lessons inside Little Learners. Mathematics shipped as "slice 1" under DR-0433 and slice 2 was never built or scheduled.

## The real failure, named

Darrell's standing instruction was *"Real-estate first then keep closing the gap!!!!!!!"* — and the agent executed the first half literally for twelve courses without ever surfacing what it was doing to the **shape** of the school. That is the failure this record exists to fix. A lopsided outcome accumulating under a standing instruction is exactly the thing the agent is supposed to put in front of the Governor before it reaches this size (DR-0075; the surface-premise-conflicts memory). It did not.

**The proportion of the Word department is not the problem.** 243 lessons on the Word is the spine and it is right. The problem is a school of 644 lessons with no English and no Science.

## His direction

> *"Development.... Business.... Project Management.... Mathematics could also be more Mathematics... English and Science from the Way.... Word first"*

Read against his own screenshot, the first four names are him walking the department list on his screen — Project Management, Mathematics, Business, Development — naming each as thin. Then the two that do not exist, with their design rule attached, and then the governing rule.

**The binding design directive is `from the Way`, and `Word first`.** English and Science are NOT secular syllabi with verses decorating them. The Word is the corpus the skill is learned on. For Science this pairs with the 4th-dimensional frame already in Layer 0 — Yahweh as the Author who framed the worlds (Hebrews 11:3; Colossians 1:16-17) — and with DR-0100, which forbids both ideological over-reach and the under-claiming of established fact.

## Music and Art are not a missing subject — they are Serve the House

Darrell, in the same exchange, correcting the list before it hardened:

> *"Music and Art... can be the sound board and choir aspect of Music.... learning a sound board... etc... all so people can see working on the church body on Sunday and you can gain all these skills working on the church physical systems and skilling up... while praising Yahweh...."*

This is a structural correction and it is better than what the measurement above proposed. **Music and Art do not become an abstract theory syllabus.** They route through **Serve the House**, which already holds sound-board (8), broadcast (9), infrastructure (10), word-out (5) and datasystems (14) — and the missing piece there is a **Music / choir course**, taught the same way the sound board already is.

**The principle underneath it is the important part, and it is now binding on how practical subjects get taught in this school: THE CHURCH'S OWN PHYSICAL SYSTEMS ARE THE CLASSROOM.** A person learns the desk, the cameras, the network, the voice, by working them on a Sunday, in front of the body, on real equipment carrying a real service that must not fail. Three things happen at once and none of them is a simulation:

- the skill is genuinely acquired, on the real kit, with real consequences
- the house is genuinely served, and the congregation can **see** who is serving it
- and it is all done **while praising Yahweh** — the skilling-up and the worship are not two activities competing for a Sunday, they are one activity

That last point is why this belongs in a governance record rather than a course plan. It answers the question of where practical education happens in this house: **not in a classroom beside the church, but in the church, on the systems the church actually runs.** Serve the House is an apprenticeship, and its courses should be written that way — as training for a real seat somebody will occupy this Sunday.

## The subject map — the plan of record that was missing

1. **English, from the Way** — does not exist. Task #70.
2. **Science, from the Way** — does not exist. Task #71.
3. **Mathematics** — past slice 1. Task #72.
4. **Development, Business, Project Management** — deepened, without overlapping their existing arcs. Task #73.
5. **Music, through Serve the House** — a choir and music course beside sound-board, taught as a real Sunday seat. Task #74.
6. **Real Estate** — twelve of twenty-two built. Ten remain, and they now queue **behind** the items above rather than ahead of them. Course thirteen (land-development) is drafted to three of eight lessons and is **parked in scratch, not abandoned**.

**History and Geography are deliberately NOT on this list yet.** They were named in the measurement as holes and Darrell did not pick them up when he gave his direction, so they are not being built on an assumption. They stay recorded here as measured gaps with no owner. **re-review: 2026-10-19**, with the rest of this map.

## One rule this changes

The zero-verse-collision discipline used across the Real Estate department is a **department-level** pin, and it must stay that way. Measuring the English candidates found 24 of 33 already cited — almost all in sovereign-ai, living-lessons or broadcast. **Catalog-wide verse exclusivity would make a Word-first English course impossible**, because the Word is the corpus the subject is taught on. English and Science cite freely across departments and **name the overlaps in their headers**, exactly as courses eleven and twelve named theirs. The pin stays verse-level-zero *within* a department.

## Re-review

The subject map is re-read whenever a department passes **four courses**, to check the shape of the school before the next one is built rather than after twelve. **re-review: 2026-10-19.**
