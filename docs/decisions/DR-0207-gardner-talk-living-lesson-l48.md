## Context

Darrell fed a **Chris Gardner** conversation (author of "The Pursuit of Happyness," at
the First Bank of the United States) across many segments, 2026-07-21 — the American
Dream gone global, the AI-accelerated arena ("competitors not even human"), the "A-game"
(excellence over "adequate/sufficient/good enough"), "sign the front of the check"
(ownership), personal integrity revealed in crisis, passion/purpose (transformative not
transactional), "scar tissue" (failures survived), long-term investing in what you
understand, and steering youth into trades over college debt (his stated goal: help
create 1,000 new self-made owners). Per DR-0089 / DR-0168 it is captured-and-shipped into
the right surface: the singular self-paced **Living Lessons** series (the lane already
used for other harvested talks — L33 impact, L35 sovereign+small, L38 tactics→essence).

## The decision

Added **L48 — "World-Class as Unto the Lord — Excellence, Ownership, and Scar Tissue in a
Global Age"** to `LIVING_LESSONS_MODULES` (weeks 47 → 48; the `weeks === MODULES.length`
gate). It harvests the talk Word-first through five movements:

1. **Excellence** — refuse "adequate": "do it with thy might" (Ecclesiastes 9:10);
   diligence stands "before kings" (Proverbs 22:29). Gardner's "internal validation" is
   **upgraded** to the Audience of One: "as to the Lord, and not unto men... ye serve the
   Lord Christ" (Colossians 3:23-24) — excellence that frees, not exhausts.
2. **Ownership** — "sign the front" = "the head, and not the tail" (Deuteronomy 28:13),
   increase "by labour" (Proverbs 13:11) — **guarded**: wealth is God-given "to establish
   his covenant" (Deuteronomy 8:18); "cannot serve God and mammon" (Matthew 6:24);
   "labour not to be rich" (Proverbs 23:4); the aim is to GIVE (Ephesians 4:28).
3. **What a leader is made of** — integrity proven in crisis (Proverbs 11:3; Job 27:5;
   "faithful in that which is least", Luke 16:10); purpose that is transformative not
   transactional (Philippians 2:4); and scar tissue (Proverbs 24:16; Romans 5:3-4).
4. **The long view** — "counteth the cost" (Luke 14:28); gather "by labour" over
   trend-chasing (Proverbs 13:11).
5. **A trade over debt** — "the borrower is servant to the lender" (Proverbs 22:7); "work
   with your own hands... lack of nothing" (1 Thessalonians 4:11-12).

Full module at the L42–L47 bar (bigIdea, inApp, 8 benefits, child/teen/senior levels,
