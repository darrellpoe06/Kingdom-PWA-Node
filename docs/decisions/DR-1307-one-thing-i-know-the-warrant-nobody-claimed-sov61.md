## Decision

**The lesson is built on the contrast and the one demand, and says both in every reader text.** `sov61-one-thing-i-know`, titled *One thing I know - the warrant nobody claimed, and the foothold you have to bring yourself*.

**Yahweh's own order of operations is read first, before any machine is discussed**, because the order is the teaching. He does not open with a method; He opens with His identity and then with the outcome: *"Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure"* (Isaiah 46:10). **The END from the BEGINNING** — the outcome named before anybody is handed a tool, and what stands at the finish is His counsel rather than the route. Then the half a technologist invariably skips: having declared the end, Yahweh **deliberately fixed something immovable** for us to lay hold of, and He owed that to nobody. He bound Himself anyway, *"That by two immutable things, in which it was impossible for God to lie, we might have a strong consolation"* (Hebrews 6:18), and the figure He chooses is a sailor's — *"Which hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and stedfast"* (Hebrews 6:19). **An anchor is neither the ship nor the voyage.** It is the one thing that does not move while everything else does.

**Twelve movements, every one on free ground:** Yahweh's order and the anchor He fixed (Isaiah 46:9-10; Hebrews 6:17-19); what arrived and how far from the event it stands, with both set-aside items named; the arrangement that worked, and what was actually carrying it; the inventory that comes before any multiplying — *"tell me, what hast thou in the house?"* (2 Kings 4:2), where she reports nothing and in the same breath **names** the one pot she has; the instrument nobody warranted, and the maker's own silence; *"which indeed appear beautiful outward"* (Matthew 23:27), where the woe falls not on the whitewash — which was there so nobody would touch the tomb by accident — but on reading the appearance as a report on the inside; its sharper sibling, where the method is executed **perfectly** and the point is omitted, *"strain at a gnat, and swallow a camel"* (Matthew 23:24), which is checking the format and not the sum; the failure case with its reason in four words, *"for they knew them not"* (2 Kings 4:39), a helpful man's lapful of an unidentified herb going into **the common pot** and *"there is death in the pot"* (2 Kings 4:40) until somebody who knew what to bring cast in meal (2 Kings 4:41); the fixed point a whole council could not move, *"one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see"* (John 9:25), with his parents naming honestly what they did not know (John 9:20-21); the fault located where nobody expects it, *"but now ye say, We see; therefore your sin remaineth"* (John 9:41); the guard a household was told about and never saw sound, closed with a father who brought his own child and said both halves out loud, *"Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief"* (Mark 9:24); and the practice, *"called the name of it Ebenezer"* (1 Samuel 7:12) and Jacob's *"Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not"* (Genesis 28:16) before he set the stone up for a pillar (Genesis 28:18).

**The sentence the lesson will not let pass is that a shape is a warrant, and the refusal is located precisely.** A machine with no warrant on its arithmetic stands exactly where the blind man stood, and **there is no guilt in that** — it cannot know and it has not claimed to. The trouble arrives when the FORM claims what the maker declined to: the slider, the clean total, the field that recalculates, all of it saying **we see** on behalf of something that never said so. And the guilt comes to rest wherever that claim is accepted and passed on, which means **on the steward**. The reason a believer can carry a fixed point at all is confessed in the lesson and in every band: the first one was given rather than earned, by **Jesus, the Lamb of Yahweh and the Eternal Son of Yahweh** (DR-0210), the anchor that entered within the veil.

**The three actions the week ends on are checkable:** before the next piece of work goes to a machine, write down the **one fact already known for certain** and the outside thing the answer will be laid against; refuse to read a shape as a warrant — ask of every tidy output **who claimed the numbers were right**, and say *nobody* out loud when that is the answer; and keep a **written stone**, one page of what this house has actually verified, each entry dated and with its method, kept where the work is done rather than where records are filed.

**DR-0100's three tiers are held apart in every band, and the gate requires it.** The established half is conceded in plain words before any correction: the convenience really is considerable, polish really was evidence once, and the senior band states the principle outright — **under-claiming a real thing is as much a failure as over-claiming a false one.** The children's-safety finding is stated **as plainly as it was reported**, with the admission that we did not read the report.
