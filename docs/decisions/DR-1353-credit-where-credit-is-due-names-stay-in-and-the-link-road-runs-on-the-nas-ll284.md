## Decision

1. **Credit where credit is due, as a gate.** `spoken-lessons-name-darrell.test.js`: a lesson whose own provenance says Darrell spoke it carries no bare *the teacher* / *the man who gave this lesson* in any string and names Darrell in the lesson and every band; found from the lesson's own words, never a list; proven to catch on the exact phrasing that shipped. A lesson built from someone else's words names that person as the material names them (DR-0719, DR-0711), and where the route gives no name, the lesson says so and the Research tab is where the name is found, not invented. **Names are never taken out, good or evil; what they said is written, researched and put in; the fruit is the data; assessment is not condemnation; evil seen is set aside and handed to the law; ignoring it is not an option.** The lesson teaches it; the gate enforces the part a gate can.
2. **Living Lesson 284** (`ll284-credit-where-credit-is-due`), four bands, two stories, eight quiz questions, a facilitator guide, gated by `living-lessons-ll284-credit-where-credit-is-due.test.js`; dated 2026-11-06, the day it was spoken and built. The order test's newest-day pin moves to 2026-11-06.
3. **A reader length, as a gate** (`new-lessons-keep-a-reader-length.test.js`): from ll284 on, the adult lesson is 1,500 to 3,500 words and the child band at most 2,000.
4. **A link Darrell sends is fetched on the NAS with no runner in the road.** `load-transcripts.py` reads the Research tab's rows (`agent_inbox` tagged `research`) for YouTube ids, newest first, ahead of the sermon backlog, and writes the transcript to `video_transcripts` exactly as a sermon's; four proven-to-catch tests. The five links are filed as the app files them (five `research` rows, `create:lesson`, 2026-11-06 14:06 UTC), so the first NAS cycle after this merge picks them up. The runner's roads keep their place as support and witness: `source-transcript-nas.yml` gains `more_video_ids` so a batch is one run, and the dead TS_AUTHKEY is a value only Darrell can mint (DR-1159 road 3), handed as plain steps and a paste.
5. **The request is the trigger (recorded, next build).** Darrell: *"the request becomes valuable trigger."* The lesson builder already listens on the NAS database and starts when a row lands (DR-0669). The Research tab's insert must ring that same bell so a sent link is fetched at once, not on the next clock. `re-review: 2026-11-08` — wire the research row to the builder's listener and retire the clock for sent links; the clock stays for the backlog.
