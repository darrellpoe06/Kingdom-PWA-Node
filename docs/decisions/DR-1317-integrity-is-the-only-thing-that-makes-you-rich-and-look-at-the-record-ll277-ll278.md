## Context

**The first teaching, rendered for meaning from his words (DR-0331):** integrity is the number one thing that matters. Lifted up or in a low position, no one should be able to guilt-trip you or gas your head up with information, because if you have integrity you know what you did, what you did not do, and why. It comes from an internal personal relationship with Yahweh, letting Him lead and guide and wanting surety from Him; you cannot listen even to yourself; integrity comes from hearing His inner voice, and His Word settles what you have inside you, so studying to show yourself approved is very important. Nothing matters except Christ and Him crucified: Jesus specifically chose His path and humbled Himself to a death that was embarrassing and undermining, with a focus of mind, and stayed dedicated. Integrity is more important than winning; you do not win without it, and even if you win, you lost, because when you pass He assesses why and how you did what you did, what you did behind closed doors and how it showed up. He had been talking with a young woman at work about people who bully behind closed doors and run manipulative, chatty strategies: have integrity about your work, execute it so the outcomes are quality-filled, measurable and objective, be excellent, and even a hater will have to fall back and look at the pathway, the pipeline, the outcome; if they do not, Yahweh is using them to sharpen you for another space and to raise you. What you are left with is you being you. Only Yahweh is worthy; what you do for Christ is all that lasts and what He did for us is all that matters. Do not lose your integrity trying to get back at someone. The King's relationship with you is the richest you can be; money cannot make you rich; integrity can; riches are sustainability; He supplies what is needed; and you cannot sustain a lie or undermining ways, only quality-filled relationships, decisions and ways.

**The second teaching, spoken minutes later, rendered for meaning:** unhealthy environments come from bad leadership without integrity that takes credit it does not have, that only has a certain amount of time, and from the people who protect such actors. He looks at it as development: Yahweh is using these people and situations to give him skill for handling them in future. The pressure is only pressure if the perception is wrong; refuse the narratives and there is no stress, you will be laughing, the joy of the Lord is your strength. With all the data and context, speaking truth with transparency, clarity, documented reasons and timelines, what can words say? Look at the record and he will be quiet while you see excellence. Critique the record and he will adjust and humble himself; throw things at the wall that are not true and he need say nothing. If the organisation lets a narrative outweigh the record, that is the problem, not him, and Yahweh is using it to make him want His will more in the earth and to be a better leader than the ones over him, fuelling better environments for souls, not revenge. Speak truth to power; let not your good be evil spoken of; let your yea be yea and your nay be nay; enjoy your day; expect people to talk, they project what they believe, not the truth. Focus on family first, then growth, development, skill, networking and rapport outside your space. Yahweh will use your gift to make room, at this company or a new one; He supplies, He opens and closes doors, He is the Shepherd of our souls; let Him do His job. It is His job to get Himself glory through him, not his; he does not glory in anything; he exists, lives, appreciates, studies the Word, lets it come through him, closes the gaps between what he does and what it says, and lets the chips fall. Yahweh has the rest, promise.

**The shape.** Two words, one morning, the second taking the first one step further (what if the leadership is the one without integrity?). As in DR-1294, the first teaching is L277 with a closing bridge, and the second is its own full lesson, L278, which opens by naming L277 and why it follows. The two titles share no opening words and the one-teaching-one-lesson guard (DR-1185) reads them as two lessons, which they are.
