## DR-0100 tiers applied (verified by live web search and direct fetch 2026-09-16)

- **Tier 1 — documented, stated plainly, source and date named.**
  - **RAG:** Lewis and coauthors, *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks*, **NeurIPS 2020** (arXiv 2005.11401) — a pre-trained parametric memory plus a non-parametric memory that is a dense vector index accessed by a neural retriever. **Search-verified 2026-09-16** against the publisher listing and the dblp record; `arxiv.org`, `proceedings.neurips.cc`, `huggingface.co` and `ai.meta.com` were all **egress-blocked** from this session and the lesson says so.
  - **Chunking:** the precision-versus-context tradeoff and the ~10–20% overlap convention — search-verified 2026-09-16.
  - **Embeddings:** query and documents must come from compatible encoders or the vectors are not comparable (different models occupy different spaces) — search-verified 2026-09-16; the article's own corollary that equal dimensionality is not compatibility is quoted.
  - **Metrics:** Milvus documentation, **fetched 2026-09-16** — "After normalization, the inner product equals cosine similarity."; pgvector exposes L2, inner-product and cosine as distinct operators (fetched 2026-09-16).
  - **Flat index exactness:** pgvector README, **fetched 2026-09-16** — "By default, pgvector performs exact nearest neighbor search, which provides perfect recall."; FAISS wiki, **fetched 2026-09-16** — "The only index that can guarantee exact results is the IndexFlatL2 or IndexFlatIP." O(n) explained as work in proportion to the vector count, and *exact* explained as a property of the numerical search only.
  - **IVF / nprobe:** FAISS wiki, fetched 2026-09-16 — clustering at build time, "at query time, a set of `nprobe` inverted lists is selected", search time roughly proportional to nprobe; pgvector's `lists` and `ivfflat.probes` (default 1).
  - **HNSW:** Malkov & Yashunin, *Efficient and robust approximate nearest neighbor search using Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs*, **IEEE TPAMI 42(4):824-836** (arXiv 1603.09320) — incrementally built multi-layer proximity graphs; search-verified 2026-09-16 (arxiv blocked). Parameters documented: FAISS — "the number of neighbors used in the graph", "the depth of exploration at add time", "the depth of exploration of the search"; pgvector — `m` "the max number of connections per layer" (default 16), `ef_construction` "the size of the dynamic candidate list for constructing the graph" (default 64), `hnsw.ef_search` "the size of the dynamic candidate list for search" (default 40). Those defaults are the **documentary proof** of the article's line that `ef_search` "isn't the number of results returned."
  - **Filtering:** pgvector, fetched 2026-09-16 — with approximate indexes filtering is applied *after* the index is scanned, and iterative index scans exist precisely because a selective filter can starve a post-filtered batch; the article's graph complication quoted verbatim.
  - **Updates:** changed text needs new embeddings; a metadata-only change can often skip re-embedding when the metadata was not part of the embedded text (article, quoted); stable identifiers plus explicit active-version rules make the swap predictable.
  - **Reranking / hybrid:** cross-encoder reranking scores the query and passage jointly rather than comparing two independent embeddings, and measured benchmarks place hybrid retrieval followed by reranking above single-stage dense or lexical retrieval — search-verified 2026-09-16.
  - **Calibration:** no similarity value guarantees relevance, the score-to-relevance relationship is non-linear, and thresholds are calibrated per corpus against human judgment — search-verified 2026-09-16.
- **Tier 2 — genuinely open, flagged narrowly.** Which index wins for a given workload (the article: no fixed collection size forces the move off flat search; the FAISS guidance is conditioned on memory); the right chunk size for a particular corpus; where a similarity threshold should sit; whether published hybrid-plus-rerank gains transfer to corpora unlike the benchmark sets. The lesson states explicitly **what is NOT open** — every mechanism in Tier 1, and the fact that a similarity score is not a probability of correctness.
- **Tier 3 — over-reach the Word corrects while the data stands.** (1) *The nearest match IS the truth* — the ranking is real and exact search really is exact under its metric; the leap from nearest to true is the error, refused by the article and refused harder by Proverbs 18:13, 18:17, 14:15, 1 Thessalonians 5:21, 1 John 4:1. (2) *A retrieval system can stand in for the Teacher* — John 16:13; 1 John 2:27; 1 Corinthians 2:14. (3) *More retrieval must mean more finding* — Amos 8:12 is endless searching with nothing found, and it is a famine of hearing, not of indexing. In every case the measured data underneath is left standing.

## The Word settles what the debate cannot

The anchor is the invitation — "Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you:" (Matthew 7:7) — and the standard for how a text may be cut: "Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth." (2 Timothy 2:15). The industry's words — chunk, embed, rank, filter, rerank, version — are the Word's *rightly dividing*, *comparing spiritual things with spiritual*, *he that is first in his own cause seemeth just*, *a word spoken in due season*, *two or three witnesses*, and *things new and old*; and the Word goes past all of them with a warning the pipeline diagram cannot carry: the very first attack on record was a true sentence cut from its condition, and the answer to it was "It is written again." The fruit: a learner leaves able to keep a condition with its rule, to read a passage in its paragraph, to treat a top hit as a candidate and require a second independent witness, to speak a true word in its season, to hold the administration/text distinction without confusion, to say plainly when the evidence holds no answer — and to know that the needle Heaven searches for is a person, and that the Searcher searches the searcher.
