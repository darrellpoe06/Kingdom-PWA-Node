# DR-0389 — Remember is a verb: His knowing, His withholding, and what the sea takes

- **Date:** 2026-09-13
- **Status:** accepted
- **Tier:** B (teaching content, COLG-facing; doctrinally sensitive)
- **Type:** word
- **Lesson:** L148 · `ll148-remember-is-a-verb-...`

## What was asked

Darrell, 2026-09-13, spoken — and asked **honestly rather than rhetorically**, which is why it gets a lesson rather than an answer:

> "You have to hate Love to actually go to hell, there is no other way according to the Word, Yahweh will desire us until death at least... not sure after because of the sea of forgetfulness so we forget those who are lost... however I'm not sure He can block His own knowledge and understanding can he according to the biblical scriptures? I know He can ignore... or not tell you something until He wants to tell you. Word first lesson."

## The decision: split the question, then answer each half from the text

**Two different questions were wearing one coat, and the Word answers them in opposite directions.** Separating them is most of the work.

1. **CAN HE STOP KNOWING?** The Word never says so and says the reverse plainly: *"his understanding is infinite"*, *"knoweth all things"*, *"all things are naked and opened unto the eyes of him with whom we have to do"*, *"Declaring the end from the beginning."* **Infinite has no edge to fall off, and no verse anywhere shows Him deleting a fact or claiming He could.**
2. **CAN HE DECLINE TO TELL?** Constantly, openly, and He says so: *"The secret things belong unto the LORD our God"*; *"shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end"*; *"Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not"*; *"It is not for you to know the times or the seasons."* And He opens on His own clock rather than never: *"he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets."* **SEALED IS NOT DESTROYED** — that sentence is the hinge, and Darrell's second instinct was exactly right.
3. **THE VERSE THAT RAISED IT RESOLVES ON A HEBREW HABIT, SHOWN RATHER THAN ASSERTED.** In Scripture **remember is an action word before it is a memory word**, and the lesson proves it from four places where the verb is followed immediately by an act: *"And God remembered Noah... and God made a wind to pass over the earth"*; *"God remembered Abraham, and sent Lot out of the midst of the overthrow"*; *"the LORD remembered her"*, and Hannah conceives; and the thief — *"Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom"* — who is plainly asking to be **acted for**. So *"their sins and their iniquities will I remember no more"* is **a verdict, not amnesia** — and that is the stronger reading, because what is forgotten can be recalled and what is judged is finished.
4. **THE SEA TAKES SINS, NOT PEOPLE — and the famous phrase is not in the Word at all.** *"thou wilt cast all their sins into the depths of the sea."* The sister promises keep the identical shape (transgressions blotted out; transgressions removed). The object is always the record against you, never the person. **This precision is load-bearing:** if the sea swallowed people, heaven would run on managed ignorance and the original worry would be fair. Because it takes the charge sheet, the worry dissolves.
5. **THE FIRST CLAIM WAS RIGHT, AND WISDOM SAYS IT IN SO MANY WORDS.** *"all they that hate me love death."* Light was not withheld — *"men loved darkness rather than light"* — and what is refused is named precisely: *"they received not the love of the truth"*, the **love** of it, not the argument. Then *"how often would I have gathered thy children together... and ye would not!"* Nobody in that sentence is kept out. Against His own oath: *"I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked... for why will ye die?"* And the window is named with **WHILE** doing the work: *"Seek ye the LORD while he may be found."*

## Two disciplines this lesson had to hold, and did

- **THE HARD VERSE IS READ, NOT AVOIDED.** *"But of that day and that hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels which are in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father."* The lesson reads it, states that it stands, and **stops where the text stops** rather than inventing machinery to smooth it. A lesson that drops its hardest verse is managing the reader rather than teaching them (DR-0098's "where the Word is reticent, stay with what it says").
- **WHERE THE WORD IS SILENT, THE LESSON SAYS SO.** Whether the redeemed forget particular **people** is *not stated in Scripture*. What is stated is the outcome — *"the former shall not be remembered, nor come into mind"*, *"God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes"*. **He promises the absence of grief without publishing the mechanism, and supplying one would be fabricating comfort** (DR-0076 §8). Fabricated comfort is still fabrication even when it is kind. The test pins this as an **absence** as well as a presence, because a well-meaning future edit would "improve" the lesson by adding exactly that comfort.
