## Context

Week 42 (DR-1115) said two of his phrases in a sentence each, inside a lesson built from a news digest: that work beside learning is apostolic, and that Yahweh's remedy for debt is a scheduled release with His name on it. He read that week to the release and asked for the thing the digest lesson could not be: the details, worked by a Word-first algorithm run across the Word; the word *apostolic* explained rather than used; and the governing rule stated and demonstrated — context is King.

## Decision

Week 43 of Sovereign A.I. takes his five phrases as its whole subject, in his order, and works each one the way the Word explains it:

1. **The Word-first algorithm, stated so anybody can run it** — five steps, in order: the verse as written (Psalms 119:160); its context with the sense given (Nehemiah 8:8); across the Word, the Word explaining the Word (Isaiah 28:10; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:20-21; Luke 24:27, 44-45); rightly divided (2 Timothy 2:15); and only then the thing in front of you (Acts 17:11; John 5:39). The diagnosis the page hands over: most arguments among believers are two people who ran step five first.
2. **Context is King, run live on the verse he was reading** — Deuteronomy 15:2 with 15:1 (the clock), 15:3 (the circle: the covenant brother; the foreigner may still be asked, so the release is no universal cancellation), 15:4-5, 15:9-11 (the sin the release tempts) and 15:15 (the floor of the chapter: redemption remembered on a calendar).
3. **The release run across the Word** — Exodus 21:2; Leviticus 25:10; Nehemiah 10:31; Jeremiah 34:8-17; Isaiah 61:1-2; Luke 4:18-21; Matthew 6:12; Matthew 18:27-33; Colossians 2:14; Philemon 18-19. It is called the LORD's release because He is the Releaser; every release His people made was a copy of His. The Lamb of Yahweh, the Eternal Son (John 1:29; John 3:16; Hebrews 1:8).
4. **"Apostolic" explained before it is used** — a sent one (John 13:16; Luke 6:13; Hebrews 3:1; John 20:21); the pattern the sent ones received, lived and taught (Acts 2:42; Ephesians 2:20; Galatians 1:11-12; 1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9). The test: received from Him, lived, left to be followed.
5. **Work beside learning is that pattern, not an experiment** — Mark 6:3; Luke 2:52; Matthew 4:18-19; Acts 18:3; Acts 20:33-35; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:7-10; 1 Thessalonians 4:11-12; Ephesians 4:28; the old paths (Jeremiah 6:16; Ecclesiastes 1:9). **With the limit Paul himself named**, so the pattern is never a club: 1 Corinthians 9:14-18; Matthew 10:10; Luke 10:7; 1 Timothy 5:18 — not "no paid teachers", not a verdict on the sick, the widow or the apprentice.
6. **The three tiers** (DR-0100): ESTABLISHED is the text; OPEN, narrowly, is any statute for a loan book or a payroll, which the Word is not; OVER-REACH is corrected in both directions by 15:3 and 9:14.

## Impact

Unresolved, his word would have stayed a paragraph inside a digest lesson, and "apostolic" would have gone on being used in this house without being explained — the exact both-sides-by-omission that leaves a reader to guess. Resolved, every reader of the course now holds a five-step method that produces conclusions he can reproduce, run in front of him on the verse it came from; the release is taught as His, with the circle (15:3) and the floor (15:15) that keep it from being read as a slogan; and work beside learning is taught as the sent ones' pattern with Paul's own limit beside it, so it can never be turned into a club against the sick, the widow or the apprentice.
