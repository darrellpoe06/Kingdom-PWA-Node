## Context

An engineering newsletter explains how a ~3-billion-parameter language model was made to run on a telephone: shrink the weights, keep the working notes, share one base with small per-task adapters, and send the hard requests to a sealed cloud. His one word over it was the ask. The article is the occasion only, and the subject it happens to describe — a model made small enough to live in a hand, serving without the cloud — is a thing Yahweh settled before there was a device to settle it upon.

## What was measured

- **The Word is the ground, and it is not a borrowed metaphor.** Deuteronomy 30:11-14 frames His own word in the language of DISTANCE and rules out two distances by name — not in heaven, not beyond the sea — then gives the reason the whole hour turns on: *that thou mayest do it.* A word you must send for cannot be obeyed at the hour obedience is asked. The gate asserts the lesson reaches that frame before it reports any figure, and that no figure appears above it.
- **EVERY primary host refused.** `machinelearning.apple.com` and its mirror, `arxiv.org` and every publisher host returned DNS failure or a 403 at this session's network boundary; the proxy reported `selective: false`, and `get_environment` (the first step toward asking for one read-only host) was denied by the user, so the work was not widened. **No figure here rests on a primary-source read by this build, and all five reader places say so.** `re-review: 2026-11-26`.
- **An independent search road was walked on each load-bearing number, and it CHANGED three.** (1) The 37.5 per cent is, in Apple's own framing, the SHARE OF LAYERS in the second block reusing the first block's keys and values — exactly what the article's own 5:3 depth split makes three eighths of — not a separately published memory saving. (2) The 6 GB and 1.39 GB figures are arithmetic and were re-derived, not repeated. (3) Two things the digest dropped were added back, because under-claiming is also a failure (DR-0100): Apple's own statement that 3.5 bits per weight costs no significant quality, and that the working notes themselves are held at 8 bits.
- **One claim checked out STRONGER than its single sentence,** so it is stated plainly: Private Cloud Compute now runs beyond Apple's own data centres, on Google Cloud machines with NVIDIA processors, requirements unchanged, with a published append-only ledger of that hardware.
- **Attributed, never asserted:** the name *palettization* and the pruning method behind the sparse model, both secondary-only. **Not checked at all:** the Neural Engine performance claims, any benchmark, whether the cloud boundary holds in practice. The publication's own disclaimer is passed on.

## Decision

1. **The Word opens the hour, the article does not.** The gate fails the build if any engineering figure appears before Deuteronomy 30:11-14, and every band carries the reason He gives.
2. **Two bright lines are required in EVERY band, because both comparisons are easy to cheapen.** A base model is swapped out next year by the men who made it, and other foundation can no man lay (1 Corinthians 3:11); Jesus is the Lamb of Yahweh and the Eternal Son. And a cache is a faint shadow of a memorial, which is a faint shadow of the Comforter — a Person sent by the Father in the Son's name (John 14:26), not a buffer.
3. **The blocked host is named to the reader, in all five places.** A road nobody could walk never reads as a verified claim.
4. **Every verse is filled from `app/public/bible/kjv`** by a generator, never typed; the gate re-derives it from disk. `anchor.ref` names every verse quoted and nothing it does not, so Search it out cannot link to a verse the hour never taught.
