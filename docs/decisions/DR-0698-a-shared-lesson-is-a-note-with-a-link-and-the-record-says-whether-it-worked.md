# DR-0698 — A shared lesson is a note with a link, and the record says whether the link worked

- **Status:** accepted
- **Tier:** B (schema: two new tables and three functions, RLS on; a public, anon-callable write path). Not Tier C: no money, no timer, no automation, no new external publication.
- **Type:** surface + schema
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Scope:** `app/src/lib/lesson-share.js` (new: the message, the summary, the token); `app/src/lib/lesson-share-record.js` (new: record a share, capture and report an open, read the ledger); `app/src/lib/lesson-links.js` (`lessonSharePayload`, `sectionSharePayload`, `shareLink`); `app/src/lib/tlc-lesson-links.js` (`tlcLessonSharePayload`); `app/src/components/ChurchLearn.jsx` (Share carries a token and is recorded; Copy lesson and Paper & print ask a signed-out reader for an account; the linked lesson reports ok or failed; Learn → My shares); `app/src/components/PracticeLearn.jsx` and `TlcPublicDoor.jsx` (TLC shares recorded, TLC opens reported); `app/src/components/EternalAlgorithmsStudy.jsx` (its Share recorded); `app/src/components/DownloadNeedsAccount.jsx` (new); `app/src/components/LessonShareLedger.jsx` (new); `app/src/components/AdminConsole.jsx` (Admin → Lesson shares); `app/src/main.jsx` (the boot capture); `app/src/poe-financial-mvp-v28.jsx` (one prop on the existing line: `signedIn={!!authSession}`); `infra/supabase/migrations-auto/0244-a-shared-lesson-link-keeps-its-record.sql` (new); `scripts/lesson-share-ci-smoke.sql` (new); `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (the `lesson-shares` leg, part of the required check); `scripts/system-flow-registry.mjs` (two nodes); tests below.

- **Grounds:** DR-0290 (the link is the door: church routes open to a signed-out visitor, `isPublicChurchRoute`), DR-0296 (the shell routes the link, or the lesson never opens), DR-0349 (a TLC lesson served by link), DR-0286 (links to the exact lesson), DR-0237 / migration 0237 (`is_lesson_governor()`), DR-0376 / migration 0216 (the anon-callable, validated, flood-braked SECURITY DEFINER seam this copies).

## Context

Darrell, 2026-09-30:

> "Also the shares should be a link with a short clarification of how to read the lessons like push play button to hear etc at the end and a note with title and a short summary of the lesson... make sense... not the full lesson... they can download it from the app however they would need an account no account needed for just reading it ect... also keep record of who does what send links etc... so we know they work and don't etc..."

Four asks: the share is a short note (title, summary, link) ending with how to read it; the link reads with no account; downloading needs an account; and a record of who shared what and whether each link worked.

## What was measured

**SHOULD.** DR-0290 §1: a shared church link opens for anyone, no login. DR-0349 §2–3: a TLC lesson link is served to a visitor as a taste. Neither record says anything about what the share text is beyond "short on purpose" (the comment above `lessonSharePayload`), and nothing documented a record of shares or opens: undocumented intent, itself a finding (DR-0219).

**ARE (before this change, on `origin/main` at 9faac105).**

- *What a lesson share sent.* `app/src/lib/lesson-links.js:176-183` `lessonSharePayload`: `title` = the lesson title; `text` = the lesson's **whole** `bigIdea` plus "— {course}, The Love Corner"; `url` passed separately to `navigator.share`. Measured across the mounted catalog: 44 courses, 601 lessons; a Living Lessons `bigIdea` runs to 951 characters (ll5), so "short" was not true. The link went in a separate field, so the receiving app placed it; there was no how-to at all. The same shape in `tlc-lesson-links.js:115-121` (TLC) and at `EternalAlgorithmsStudy.jsx:485` (which reuses `lessonSharePayload`). The clipboard fallback (`shareLink`, `lesson-links.js:270`) copied the link alone.
- *Call sites.* `ChurchLearn.jsx:2406-2413` (payload at `:2409`; Share on every lesson card, every course in Learn), `:2601` and `:2835` (Share this part), `PracticeLearn.jsx:688-694` (payload at `:691`, TLC lesson), `EternalAlgorithmsStudy.jsx:482-490`.
- *A public, no-account route* already exists and is reused: `?view=church&sub=learn&course=<key>&lesson=<id>` passes `isPublicChurchRoute()` (`lib/access-gate.js:70`) and routes to Learn through `initialChurchView()` (DR-0296); TLC links open on `/tlc/app/?tlc=1&course=…&lesson=…` (DR-0349). ▶ Play on the lesson card is not gated by sign-in (`ChurchLearn.jsx` "▶ PLAY THIS ONE").
- *Download / export.* Per lesson: "Copy lesson" (`ChurchLearn.jsx:2414-2419`, label at `:2415`, the full text via `lessonCopyBlock`). Per course: Paper & print → Copy markdown / Download .md / Print (`ChurchLearn.jsx:3121-3129`, heading at `:3123`, `downloadCurriculum` at `:1963`). **None required an account.** TLC has no lesson download.
- *A record.* None. `grep -rn "lesson_share" app/src infra` returned nothing; a share left no trace, and an open of a shared link was not observable anywhere.
- *Where the data lives.* The app reads the NAS Supabase since REPOINT-ARMED (DR-0310). Migrations under `infra/supabase/migrations-auto/` are applied by `db-migrate.yml` on merge: hosted first, then the sovereign database over the tailnet (`scripts/sovereign-replay-over-tailnet.sh`, DR-0317); `scripts/live-sql.sh` is the read path to that database.

**GAPS.** (1) The share sent the whole big idea, not a short note, with no how-to. (2) Downloads were open to anyone. (3) No record of shares; no way to know whether a link worked.

## Impact

A long share reads as the lesson, so the recipient has no reason to open the link, and the receiving app truncates it mid-sentence (the comment above `lessonSharePayload` names that hazard). Without a how-to, a recipient does not know the page reads aloud or that no account is needed. Without a record, a broken link is found only when someone says so; DR-0296 is the precedent: the link was broken while every gate was green.

## Decision

1. **The share is a note, in Darrell's order.** `lessonShareMessage` (`lib/lesson-share.js`): the title; the course and the house on the next line; a summary of **one or two sentences** of the lesson's own `bigIdea` (else `summary`, `inApp`, then the lesson's opening), cut at a sentence boundary by `sentences()` (not fooled by "A.I.", "U.S." or "1 Cor."), at most 300 characters, a single over-long first sentence cut at a word with "…"; the link; and **last** the how-to. Church: *"How to read it: open the link, no account needed. Press ▶ Play to hear it read aloud. Downloading the lesson needs a free account in the app."* TLC promises only what its visitor page does: *"How to read it: open the link, no account needed. It opens on the lesson, free to read."* (read aloud is off for TLC guests and there is no TLC download, so neither is promised). The copy does not name Him, so the Yahweh rule is not engaged. Section shares keep their section text (Darrell's 2026-08-15 directive) and end with the same how-to.
2. **The link rides inside the text.** `shareLink` passes `navigator.share({ title, text })` without `url` when the text already carries the link, so the receiving app cannot print it twice or put it after the how-to; the clipboard fallback copies the whole note. Payloads whose text has no link (the whole-course share) keep the old shape.
3. **Every share carries a token.** A 12-character token is made on the device inside the tap (the sheet must open inside the gesture, so no network round-trip first) and put on the link as `s=`. The existing public route is reused; nothing new is opened.
4. **Downloads ask for an account; reading and ▶ Play never do.** The shell passes `signedIn={!!authSession}` to Learn. Signed out, "Copy lesson" and Paper & print's Copy markdown / Download .md / Print are replaced by `DownloadNeedsAccount`: "Downloading needs a free account. Reading and ▶ Play never do." and a button that opens the existing `AuthModal` (sign in or create an account) over the page.
5. **The record (migration 0244).**
   - `lesson_shares(token PK, shared_by uuid, door, kind, course_key, lesson_id, lesson_title, method 'native'|'copy', url, created_at)`.
   - `lesson_share_opens(id, token → lesson_shares, outcome 'ok'|'failed', reason ≤120, opened_at)`. **No column can hold who opened it**, and the smoke fails if one is added.
   - RLS on both. `lesson_shares_read`: `shared_by = auth.uid() OR is_lesson_governor()`. `lesson_share_opens_read`: `is_lesson_governor()` only. Table privileges revoked from `PUBLIC, anon, authenticated`; `SELECT` granted back to `authenticated` only. No write policy: writes go only through the functions.
   - `lesson_share_record(p_token, p_payload)`: SECURITY DEFINER, `anon` + `authenticated`. `shared_by` is forced to `auth.uid()` (never read from the payload); validates the token shape, door, kind, method, course and lesson lengths, and that the URL is http(s) **and carries its own token**; `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` so a token can never be re-pointed or re-owned. Flood brake: 60 shares per signed-in person per 10 minutes, 120 signed-out shares per minute in total.
   - `lesson_share_open(p_token, p_outcome, p_reason)`: SECURITY DEFINER, `anon` + `authenticated`. Counts only a token that was really shared (anything else is a quiet `false`); the reason is reduced to `[a-z0-9 :._/-]`, so it cannot carry free text or a person's details. Flood brake: 30 opens per link per minute, 600 in total per minute.
   - `lesson_share_ledger(p_all)`: `authenticated` only; the sharer's own shares with `opens / ok_opens / failed_opens / last_opened_at / last_failure`; `p_all` is honoured for the Governor alone, who also sees who sent each (their account email; "a signed-out reader" when none).
   - No `instance_id`: a lesson is public reading and a share names only a course key and a lesson id, never a member or a client; so the tenancy and grant guards (instance-scoped tables) do not apply. The walls above are the RLS policies and grants. DR-0060's guards stay green and untouched.
6. **An open is watched from boot to the lesson on screen.** `main.jsx` calls `captureShareOpen(window.location.search)` before the history seed rewrites the URL. The surface that serves the lesson reports: Learn waits for `#learn-lesson-<id>` in the DOM and reports `ok`, or `failed` with "course not found: …", "lesson not found: …" or "lesson card did not render"; TLC waits for `#tlc-lesson-<id>`, and the TLC door reports a stale link at once. **If no surface reports within 30 seconds, a watchdog records `failed` "lesson not on screen within 30s"**: the DR-0296 class, where the lesson surface never mounts, is recorded rather than silent. One open is one row (the first report wins); a reload of the same tab after it reported is not a second open (sessionStorage).
7. **In the app.** Learn → **My shares** (a section chip, signed in): the person's own shares with opened / worked / failed. Admin → **Lesson shares**: every share, who sent it, and the counts, read live from `lesson_share_ledger(true)`. Both are `LessonShareLedger.jsx`; an unreadable record says so and shows nothing rather than a guess.
8. **CI.** A new `lesson-shares` leg in `ci.yml` (part of the required `app — lint + vitest` aggregation) applies 0244 twice on a throwaway postgres:16 and runs `scripts/lesson-share-ci-smoke.sql`. The flow is registered in `scripts/system-flow-registry.mjs` (`lesson-share` → `lesson-share-ledger`).

## Limits, stated

- The live proof is the first real share from a phone and its row in `lesson_shares`, then the recipient's open in `lesson_share_opens`. After merge, the migration's presence on the database the app reads is proven by query (the delivery report names it). `re-review: 2026-10-07`: read the ledger in Admin → Lesson shares after a week of real shares; any `failed` reason is work (DR-0621).
- The watchdog cannot record an open whose tab is closed before it reports (a phone locked in the first 30 seconds): that open is not recorded rather than recorded wrongly. Named, not hidden.
- TLC's visitor page has no ▶ Play (read aloud is off for guests there, `TlcPublicDoor.jsx`), so its how-to does not promise one. Whether a TLC guest should hear lessons read aloud is Christina's and Darrell's call. `re-review: 2026-10-14`.
