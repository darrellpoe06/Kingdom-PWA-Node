## Context

The first build of this course (DR-0590, 2026-09-23) passed every gate the repository had and was still wrong in the way that matters most here: its dates and its quotations about the 1619 Project — the historians' letter, the editor's reply, the fact-checker, the editor's note, the Pulitzer, the book — were written from the agent's training memory and only the 18th- and 19th-century primary records were probed on a runner. The voices gate (DR-0580) proved the words it checked; it could not check what had never been given a source. Two bands (teen, senior) carried the lesson; a child got nothing authored, which is the "watering down" he named. The witnesses — the people slavery happened to — were absent. The built world (why an interstate bends, who drew the red lines) was absent. And the project's people were never judged the way the Word judges a matter, by the testimony of people positioned to know.

He also pasted a Gemini overview of the project and asked whether it was better. The honest answer, recorded here so it is not lost: a cited overview is a doorway, not a record; our standard is the fetched page, and the first build had failed that standard once. The proof is this rebuild, not a comparison of summaries.

## Impact

The course now does what he asked in the order he asked it: the witnesses speak, the project's people are judged on their process by the people inside it, the facts are tested against the record with the claim quoted first, the correction is honored and the quiet edit is named, the city is read from the government's own pages, and a child gets the whole finding in words a child can read. Nothing in it rests on memory; every voice and every dated entry is probed on every push by `history-voices-witness`, so a source that changes or vanishes turns the build red rather than the lesson stale. The cost is a course that is honest about its reach: three records it could not fetch are named as not fetched, and the reader is told what would move them.

## Decision

1. The course ships rebuilt in place under the same catalog key; the first build is replaced, not kept beside it.
2. **Nothing unfetched.** Every claim about the project's people is their own words from the page where they said them; every date carries a probed source; a record the runner could not reach is named and not quoted. This is the standing rule for every History and Business course from here (it is what DR-0580 meant, made enforceable by the timeline-source gate).
3. **A witness of the time in every lesson** — someone who was there, in his own words — is a pinned requirement of this course and the template for the Business course's fix (the next DR).
4. **Four bands from the first commit** for any course whose subject a child must comprehend; "watering down" is a shorter finding, and the answer is the whole finding at a lower grade, not less of it.
5. **The order is by sense, with the reason recorded in the module** so the next session does not re-sort it by date.
