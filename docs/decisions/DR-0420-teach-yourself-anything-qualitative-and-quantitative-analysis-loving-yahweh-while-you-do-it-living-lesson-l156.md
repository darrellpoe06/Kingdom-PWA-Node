## The word, as spoken

Darrell, 2026-09-15: *"We might as well have a lesson on how to make yourself learn using qualitative and quantitative analysis and then picking any content you want so that way you can teach yourself about whatever you want and be competent in that while also loving your God, your Yahweh."* And, with the Little Learners directive the same night: *"the Lord is to be praised — we want to make sure everybody's competent and that's the most important piece."*

## What the Word supplies

The purpose of learning and where it begins (Proverbs 1:2-7); the method as condition and promise (Proverbs 2:1-6; James 1:5); qualitative analysis as the Word's own first act — naming before ruling (Genesis 1:28), Solomon's taxonomy (1 Kings 4:29-33), the creatures as teachers (Job 12:7-8), the search for the reason of things (Ecclesiastes 7:25); quantitative analysis as the Word's habit — the ark (Genesis 6:15), the sea (1 Kings 7:23), the census (Numbers 1:46), He who weighs and measures (Job 28:25; Isaiah 40:12) — bound to honest measurement as righteousness (Leviticus 19:35-36; Proverbs 11:1; Deuteronomy 25:15) and to counting before building (Luke 14:28-31); the synthesis in Nehemiah's night reconnaissance (Nehemiah 2:13-15) and Daniel's excellence in a foreign curriculum (Daniel 1:17-20); the test (1 Thessalonians 5:21; Acts 17:11; Proverbs 14:15; 27:17; 15:22); any field admissible (Proverbs 25:2; Psalms 111:2; 19:1-2) with Bezaleel as the Word's engineer (Exodus 31:3-5); the center that keeps study from weariness and pride (Ecclesiastes 12:12-13; Colossians 3:23; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Colossians 2:3); competence honoured (Proverbs 22:29; 2 Timothy 2:15; 24:3-4; 21:5; Luke 2:52); the pace (Isaiah 28:9-10; Psalms 1:2-3; Proverbs 9:9; John 8:32).

The lesson gives the learner two four-question sets (what is it, what kind, what is it like and unlike, what is it for; how many, how much, how big or long or often, compared to what) and a six-step loop (pick, classify, quantify, compare, test, hold, repeat), every step footed on a verse.

## Boundaries

- The lesson teaches a method of study; it does not certify competence in any field. Competence is proven in the field itself (Proverbs 22:29 names the proof: the work before kings), and no line in the lesson claims otherwise.
- Every verse is quoted whole; the two four-question sets and the six-step loop are the author's summary of what the verses show, and the lesson says so where it introduces them.
- Little Learners (Darrell's same-night directive) is a separate build with its own record; this lesson is the adult and youth statement of the same aim — "everybody's competent."
