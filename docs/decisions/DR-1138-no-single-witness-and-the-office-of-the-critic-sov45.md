## Impact

**What this buys.** The course gains the one question the four nearest weeks never asked, and the answer is the Word's own courtroom rather than an engineering fashion that happens to agree with it: the two-witness law, the proverb that opens the reviewer's office, the command to make diligent inquisition rather than accept a summary, Yahweh's own practice of written records, Jethro's chart, and the open correction of a king, an apostle and a gifted preacher. **A catalog-level claim living inside three lesson tests, left for its one owner.** `sovereign-ai-sov41-verses.test.js`, `-sov42-` and `-sov43-` each assert that the *whole course* is a contiguous `1..max`. The fix — moving contiguity back to its owner, `sovereign-ai-class.test.js`, which has `HELD_IN_FLIGHT` for exactly this — **ships once, from the week 46 branch (DR-1139)**, so those three files are deliberately untouched here; three branches editing the same three files is the collision, not the cure. This lesson's own test does not repeat the mistake: it asserts the weeks **ascend, are unique, and contain 45**, and says in a comment why whole-catalog contiguity is not its business — the pin a lesson's own test holds must be true of that lesson forever, not of the catalog on the day it was written.

**The ordering cost, which is real and is not hidden.** This branch is based on main at 43 weeks while week 44 is written in parallel, so the catalog's contiguity gate (`sovereign-ai-class.test.js`) sees a hole at 44. ## Decision

Week 45 of Sovereign A.I. teaches **the structure of testimony**, Word first, in twelve movements, with all four age bands authored:

1. **What arrived, and what this house can and cannot vouch for** — the forward, its one word, the limit stated *before* any teaching, and the four weeks this one differs from, named.
2. **A sentence is not a safeguard** — the article's own ceiling (*a prompt can describe the steps and cannot guarantee the model performs them*) set beside Ezekiel 33:31-32, Luke 6:46 and James 1:22. A hearer is not a liar, which is what makes him hard to catch.
3. **No single witness** — Deuteronomy 19:15 with 17:6, Matthew 18:16, 2 Corinthians 13:1, John 8:17, and the point the page refuses to let slide: the law never calls the one witness a liar. Begin by refusing yourself (Proverbs 18:13).
4. **The office of the critic** — Proverbs 18:17 (*seemeth* just, until somebody searches him), 16:2, 21:2, Jeremiah 17:9-10, 1 Corinthians 4:3-4, 2 Corinthians 10:12 (*measuring themselves by themselves* — an Expert scoring its own finding, nineteen centuries early), Proverbs 27:2, Galatians 6:3.
5. **Let the judge see the evidence** — Deuteronomy 13:14 (*enquire, make search, ask diligently*), 19:18 (*diligent inquisition*), Job 29:16, Proverbs 25:2, Acts 17:11, 1 Thessalonians 5:21, Proverbs 14:15.
6. **The record that remembers, and a journal is not a verdict** — Exodus 17:14, Malachi 3:16, Habakkuk 2:2, Deuteronomy 17:18-19, Joshua 4:6-7, Esther 6:1-3, Psalms 103:14.
7. **Small matters below, hard causes above** — Exodus 18:17-18, 21-22, 26; Acts 6:2-4 (they protected the office, not their comfort); Proverbs 11:14, 15:22, 24:6, where *established* is the two-witness law's own word.
8. **The one case that paid for the whole architecture** — the Expert that assessed exposed credentials as SECURE and the Critic that caught it, answered by 2 Samuel 12:7, Galatians 2:11, 14, Acts 18:26, Proverbs 27:5-6, 15:31, Psalms 141:5.
9. **The last witness is not one we made** — Slack's own admission that the Critic is a model whose score is a signal and not a fact, answered by Isaiah 8:20, James 4:12, Jeremiah 17:5, Psalms 118:8, Hebrews 4:12-13, and cited back to sov23.
10. **The specimen that spoke into the lane** — arriving text is **material to be tried, never an errand to be run**; Genesis 3:1-5 and Matthew 4:6-10; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 1:8. And the honest note that *this* newsletter carried no instruction aimed at a reading agent, with the discipline kept anyway, *because a rule that only holds while the text is friendly is not a rule, it is a mood*.
11. **The three tiers** — established, open (named narrowly, including both figures), over-reach corrected in **both** directions: a committee of models is not a trustworthy judge, and "machines err, therefore no machine may work" is not His answer either.
12. **The close** — the structure taken home, the Resources named (Proverbs 24:3-4) with Love senior (1 Corinthians 13:2), and the One who needed no second witness and stood to be examined anyway: the Lamb of Yahweh, the Eternal Son (John 1:29; John 3:16; Hebrews 1:8).

**No verse was typed.** `scripts/sov45-fill.mjs` holds only `[[Book C:V]]` placeholders and fills each from `app/public/bible/kjv` at build time; `anchor.ref` is derived from the same placeholders, so the reference list and the quotations cannot disagree (DR-0734 in both directions). The corpus on disk already reads `satan` in lower case at Matthew 4:10, so the typography and the DR-0076 bright line agree without a verse being touched — the lesson's own test pins that fact rather than assuming it.
