## The structural finds, all from the Word rather than from a man

1. **Leviticus 25:9 schedules the jubilee trumpet on the day of atonement.** Evans argues the social half cannot be inaugurated without the atonement, and he is right — but the reason to receive it is the verse, not his standing. The order the churches have argued over for a century is legislated: sin dealt with by blood, then the land moves.
2. **Ephesians 6:9 sits four verses under Ephesians 6:5.** The passage retained in the 1807 edition carries its own undoing in the same chapter: the master is told he has a Master and is forbidden to threaten.
3. **Leviticus 19:15 forbids both tilts in one verse** — "thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honor the person of the mighty". It bars favouring the poor as firmly as favouring the mighty, which is the guard that stops this lesson being carried as any faction's banner. It is required in **every band**, gated.
4. **Matthew 23:23 refuses the either-or in His own mouth** — "these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone".
5. **Forgiveness and jubilee are one word.** Evans defines forgiveness as releasing a debt; that is not his metaphor but Jesus' vocabulary (Matthew 6:12; Matthew 18:27), and Yahweh had already scheduled debt release every seventh year as "the LORD's release" (Deuteronomy 15:2). That is the weld between the justice half and the unity half of the episode.

## The bright line held on His stop

Jesus read to Luke 4:19 and stopped one clause into Isaiah 61:2, leaving "and the day of vengeance of our God" unread. Treating that as cherry-picking would teach the exact opposite of this lesson and would accuse Him of the sin it names. **He handed the scroll back whole** (Luke 4:20) — nothing removed, the remaining clause reachable within the hour. He declared which clause had arrived, being the man the clause was about. An edit removes the clause so the reader can never reach it. Authority over the calendar and theft from a reader are opposites that look alike only in summary. Gated: every band must carry the handed-back-whole detail.

## What is stated flat and what is attributed

Per DR-0100 tier 1, the established facts are stated without hedging: the 1807 *Select Parts of the Holy Bible for the Use of the Negro Slaves in the British West-India Islands* is a real object (a Fisk University copy exhibited by the Museum of the Bible), carrying 282 chapters against the King James Bible's 1,189, with the exodus among what was cut and Ephesians 6:5 among what was kept; the trade was manstealing under Exodus 21:16 and Deuteronomy 24:7 and is listed as "menstealers" at 1 Timothy 1:10; churches endorsed slavery, with Evans naming the economic motive. Softening documented evil into "some say" would be its own failure of truth.

Per DR-0076 §8, **Evans's reading of the Civil War as Yahweh's judgment on American injustice is reported as his theological reading and never asserted as ours.** The text does not hand us a verdict on a particular war, and we will not put one in Yahweh's mouth to make a point land harder. The lesson says so out loud, in its own section, and the gate forbids the claim appearing in this house's unattributed voice.
