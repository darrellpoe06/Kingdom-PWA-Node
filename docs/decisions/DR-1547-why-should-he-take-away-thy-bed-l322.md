## Context

A lender's routine monthly email ended with an offer to roll a loan and other
balances, like credit cards, into one line of credit against the home: one
payment, fewer due dates, more room. Its own asterisked disclosure said the
debts become secured by the home, the home could be lost, total interest may
rise, and the rate is variable to a ceiling. Personal money mail: taught
generically. No name, lender, account, amount, rate or date appears anywhere.

## Decision

1. **Word first, the fence at the center.** Yahweh kept lenders off the coat,
   the millstone and the threshold (Exodus 22:26-27; Deuteronomy 24:6, 10-11);
   consolidating unsecured debt into home equity moves it inside that fence by
   the household's own signature (Proverbs 22:26-27; 6:2).
2. **Stated plainly (DR-0100):** the disclosure's four facts are how such
   products work. The worked numbers are labelled illustrations.
3. **Read the small print first** (Proverbs 14:15; 22:3), **count the whole**
   (Luke 14:28; 21:5), **pay down, do not move** (2 Kings 4:1-7; Romans 13:8).
4. **No shame, no advice.** The way out is offered (Proverbs 6:3; Hebrews 13:5);
   the card says teaching, not financial, tax or legal advice.
