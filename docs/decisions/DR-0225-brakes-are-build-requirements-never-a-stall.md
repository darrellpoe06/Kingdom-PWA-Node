## Spec-conformance finding (DR-0219, run 2026-07-23)

**SHOULD:** The three-brakes rule (CLAUDE.md, 2026-06-08) governs what ships **active** — "No
autonomous, timer-driven ... automation ships `active` without ALL THREE of these brakes." It
says ship it **inactive**, brakes in. Nowhere does it say "do not build," and nowhere does it
say "ask before designing." DR-0111 says stopping to ask about directed work "USURPS the
user's authority." DR-0103 says "the gate is the brake; `hold` is the governor's hand."

**WAS (the gap):** The agent converted an activation constraint into a *conversation*
constraint — treating Tier C as "schedule a meeting with Darrell" instead of "carry the proof."
It repeated that stalled framing verbatim across turns (stale-status repetition compounding it).

## Decision — the true intention, recorded as binding

1. **The three brakes are ENGINEERING DELIVERABLES, never a permission conversation.**
   When directed work falls in the timer-driven/automation class, the agent BUILDS it with the
   budget, the concurrency lock, and the kill-switch designed in and **proven-to-catch in CI**
   (DR-0076 §3) — as part of the same work, in the same lane, without pausing to re-ask.
   Citing THREE-BRAKES or Tier C as a reason to defer *building* — or to re-present decided
   work as an open question — is a DR-0111 violation, full stop.

2. **The 6000+ deterministic checks ARE the review; `hold` is the governor's hand.**
   Activation of such a capability rides the normal verified lane: the brake tests are required
   gates like any other, the standing witnesses (site-health, OpsBoard, the budget ledger)
   are the "someone watching," and Darrell's brake is the `hold` label and his word — not a
   scheduled design meeting the agent invents. Tier C's meaning here is CARRY THE PROOF,
   not convene a committee.

3. **What P10/P11/P12 still bind (unchanged):** nothing in this class self-activates
   unattended with the brakes absent or unproven, and first activation still happens with the
   witnesses live — the 2026-06-06 runaway lesson stands. This DR removes the *stall*, not
   the *safety*.

4. **Stop repeating wrong information — status is re-verified at utterance time.** A PR
   state, a subscription, a count, a "next step" is stated only from a fresh check (DR-0076
   provenance), never replayed from an earlier turn's text. Repeating a stale status is
   recorded here as a named defect class for the ways-review (DR-0108 question 2).

5. **Aged items need refresh — obviously.** A recurrence/age finding (the Ari "Lessons
   recurrence" dimension, P30 class) is a WORK ORDER, not a statistic: the agent pulls the
   aged item forward and WORKS it in the standing idle-turn loop (DR-0103 §4) — finished, or
   honestly re-dated with a why (DR-0075) — never merely counted again next pass. A finding
   that appears in two consecutive reviews untouched is itself a ways-review defect.
