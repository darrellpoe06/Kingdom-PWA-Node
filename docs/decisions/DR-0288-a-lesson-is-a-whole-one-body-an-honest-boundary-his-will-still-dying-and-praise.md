## 1. Every Healthy Living lesson now closes as a whole

**What was wrong:** each lesson was a FRAGMENT — one cited work, standing
alone, ending in information. A reader could finish it fully informed and never
once be brought to praise, which is the opposite of why the room exists.

Every lesson now carries four closing movements, at every level, derived and
verse-gated:

1. **THE SAME BODY** — the other witnesses in the room standing on the *same
   Scripture*, named and linked (derived from shared references, so the web
   grows as the room grows; a witness with no kin says so honestly rather than
   claiming company). The frame is explicit: one body by one Maker — *"by him
   all things consist"* — then integrated systems → an integrated **Body** →
   the Kingdom (1 Corinthians 12:12, 26).
2. **WHERE THE WITNESS ENDS AND YAHWEH BEGINS** — the honest boundary. A study
   can time a fast and watch a brain; it cannot see the knitting-together
   (Psalm 139:13), weigh the inward parts (Job 38:36), reach the work from
   beginning to end (Ecclesiastes 3:11), or hold the life that holds you (Acts
   17:28). *"The secret things belong unto the LORD our God"* — the rest is not
   a gap awaiting better instruments; it is His, and it is reached by faith.
3. **HIS WILL IS BEING DONE — AND HE GIVES THE WAY THROUGH** — no protocol is a
   promise; suffering is passed *through* (John 16:33), the way through is
   given every time (1 Corinthians 10:13), and **a body that does everything
   right and still suffers is not a body that failed to believe correctly.**
   The adversary reviews for an opening and is answered with a limit — Job 1:12,
   2:6; Luke 22:31-32 — a reviewer, never a rival authority.
4. **WHY THIS ROOM EXISTS, AND WHAT IT CANNOT DO** — *"My people are destroyed
   for lack of knowledge"* is why the knowledge is here, free and cited; and it
   cannot stop the dying, because He said so in the garden (Genesis 2:17; 3:19;
   Hebrews 9:27). Care of a tent, not a cure for mortality (2 Corinthians 5:1) —
   the hope is the promised body (Romans 8:23; 1 Corinthians 15:53;
   Revelation 21:4), while *"the inward man is renewed day by day."*
5. Closing in **PRAISE** — *"I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and
   wonderfully made"* — naming the actual Yahweh: *"which is, and which was, and
   which is to come, the Almighty"* (Revelation 1:8).

## 2. The Law of Assumption, weighed Word-first

The handed-in transcript became World Issues issue #9. It is the hardest KIND
in the track: not a claim about the world but a claim about **God and the
self**, wrapped around two pieces of real science and one real verse. The three
tiers (DR-0100) do the work:

- **True, said plainly:** the reticular activating system really does gate
  attention, and identity-based motivation is a real published theory tested in
  real school interventions. Kept, cited, and usable.
- **Named, not hedged:** *"collapsing the wave / your belief selects the
  timeline"* is not what physics says — observation is a physical interaction,
  decoherence explains the outcome, and consciousness-causes-collapse has been
  argued inconsistent in the literature.
- **Corrected by the Word:** the teaching's own advocates state its foundation —
  human imagination **is** God. That is the oldest inversion (*"ye shall be as
  gods"*; the creature served *"more than the Creator"*). And its proof text is
  quoted accurately and applied backwards: Isaiah 46:10 finishes *"My counsel
  shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure"* — the finished end is **His**,
  in a passage whose point is that He alone is God. Its central instruction —
  stop praying for it — is the one thing Scripture forbids: *"in every thing by
  prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto
  God."* Even Mark 11:24 opens two verses earlier with *"Have faith in God."*

Four steelmanned perspectives (the teacher's, the careful-science, the
believer's, the pastoral-concern), a grace note condemning no one — not Neville
Goddard, not the teacher, not the believer who has practiced it — and the
skill named: *a true thing used as the doorway for an untrue one.*
