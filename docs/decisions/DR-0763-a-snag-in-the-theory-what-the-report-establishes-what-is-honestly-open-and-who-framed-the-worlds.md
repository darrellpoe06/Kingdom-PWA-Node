## Context

A believer reads a science headline several times a year, and this one is the hardest kind: the leading scientific account of how life began has just taken a documented hit. Living Lessons carried nothing on origins. It carried L198 on curiosity and what Yahweh hides, and L199 on answering a neighbour in good faith, but no lesson that took a reported difficulty in an origins theory and showed a reader what to do with it. Measured before building: no lesson in any catalog anchors on Hebrews 11:3, Romans 1:20 or Genesis 1:1, so this is new ground and not a second telling of an existing lesson (DR-0610).

**The lesson can fail in two opposite directions, and both are real.** The first is the gloat — treating one reported problem in somebody else's framework as a proof of ours. That is the over-claiming DR-0076 forbids, and it has a specific cost: the first time a young believer meets a qualified person who can answer the objection, a faith taught to stand on the gap wobbles. The second is the hedge — going quiet about the Word, filing Yahweh's own account of the beginning as one position among several on a panel. That is the both-sides-for-ratings posture DR-0098 exists to remove. This lesson refuses both, in its own text, and the test gates both refusals rather than trusting them.

**The method is DR-0100's three tiers, taught as the reader's own skill.** That is the durable part of the lesson, worth more than the headline that occasioned it: state plainly what is established and name the basis; name narrowly and honestly what is genuinely open; and where a claim reaches past its evidence, let the Word correct that claim while the real observation underneath it still stands.

## The three tiers, as this lesson sorts this report

**Tier one — established, so stated plainly with the basis named.** Four items. The paper by Benjamin Tutolo of the University of Calgary is real and published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, and its two objections are chemically specific rather than rhetorical: the vent environment becomes alkaline only after it has cooled, and reduced sulfur would likely have been very sparse in ancient ocean vents, which together produce a timing problem. The account that led the field for about a decade now carries a documented challenge from inside the field. The article's own summary of the discipline is repeated without softening — astrobiology is limited by the lack of understanding of how our own world crossed from non-living to living, and no one knows what metabolism the first cells had or where they first appeared. And the authors' three-part requirement is kept as good work: a membrane, an energy-producing biomolecule, and a replicating nucleic acid, arriving together, in a short time, in a confined space, or the system falls apart. Under-reporting a true thing is its own failure of truth (Zechariah 8:16; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22; Exodus 20:16).

**Tier two — genuinely open, so named narrowly.** Three questions and no more: where the first chemistry happened, which chemistry it was, and in what order the three requirements arrived. The paper does not completely rule out the hydrothermal vent theory, and the lesson says so in the authors' own terms. The revived alternatives — surface hot springs, tidal flats, water between ice crystals, supercritical-carbon-dioxide fault zones, panspermia — are proposals under reconsideration, not findings, and panspermia relocates the question without answering it, which the article itself concedes. The lesson states out loud what is **not** in the open set: whether Yahweh made the heaven and the earth. Transferring that question into the uncertain column under cover of a real unknown is exactly how a narrow gap becomes a general agnosticism (Ecclesiastes 8:17; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Job 11:7; Deuteronomy 29:29; Proverbs 25:2).

**Tier three — the claim that reaches past its evidence, corrected, with the observation under it kept.** The article closes: it seems extremely improbable that all three milestones would come together, yet we are here, so there must be a way. The first half is a measurement. The second half is not. The conclusion that there must therefore be a path with no Maker in it is carried by no datum in the paper; it is carried by a commitment already in place before the paper was opened. That claim, and only that claim, is what the Word corrects (Romans 1:19-22; 1 Corinthians 1:19-20; Colossians 2:8; Psalms 100:3). And the half that normally gets dropped is kept explicitly: the three-part requirement under the over-reach survives the correction and is reasoning of the same shape as Hebrews 3:4. The lesson's own sentence is the gate — *We correct a claim. We do not discard a measurement.* One further verse is handled exactly rather than loosely: 1 Timothy 6:20 warns against what is **falsely** so called, and the lesson says in its own words that it will not press that verse into service against a chemist doing honest work.

## What this lesson deliberately refuses to claim

- **That the paper proves Genesis.** It does not, it was never built to, and the lesson says so: a snag in a theory is not a proof. If a researcher answers the objection next year — which the article leaves open — nothing we believe moves, because it was never resting there. It rests on Hebrews 11:3.
- **Any victory over the scientists.** No proof, disproof, debunking or collapse is claimed anywhere in the module, and the test screens the whole module for that vocabulary.
- **Any mechanism the Word does not give.** The Word gives Who, that He spoke, the order, and the boundary after his kind (Genesis 1:3, 1:11, 1:21, 1:24; 2:1; 2:7). It does not give the temperature, the acidity, the reaction sequence, the location on the early earth, or the mechanism by which dust became a living soul. The lesson names that silence and stops in it (Deuteronomy 29:29; Isaiah 55:8-9; Job 38:2, 38:4).
- **Nothing from the report is dressed as Scripture.** In this corpus a double-quoted span is the Word, so the article's own wording is carried in our words, outside quotation marks, and the test fails on report wording found inside a quotation.

## Impact

- **The reader gains a transferable skill, not a headline.** The three tiers of DR-0100 are taught as the sorting the reader runs on the next report he meets: what is established, what is honestly open, and what is ideological over-reach the Word corrects. The science story is the occasion; the sorting is the lesson that outlives it.
- **Living Lessons grows by one module**, L213, with all four age bands at full coverage. Nothing already published changes: no other lesson, course file, surface or workflow is edited by this change, and the catalog-wide lesson gates run over the new module unchanged.
- **The numbering gap becomes visible rather than silent.** L213 lands while 210, 211 and 212 are in flight elsewhere, so the Learn header now reports the unused numbers and the collision gate holds them on its record with the reason. Each held number is deleted by the merge that brings its lesson in; a gap that is not on that list still fails the build.
- **Two refusals become machinery.** The gloat screen and the report-wording-inside-a-quotation screen are gates on the build from this merge forward, so a later edit cannot quietly turn this lesson into a victory lap over the researchers or dress the article in the clothes of the Word.
- **No claim is bought on credit.** Because the lesson rests on Hebrews 11:3 and never on the paper, a future answer to the paper's objection costs this module nothing. That is the durable part of the impact: the lesson cannot be falsified by the next journal issue.

## Decision

- Ship **L213** into **Living Lessons** (the devotional course) as a new module: ten numbered movements, Word first, with macro and micro held together; all four age bands authored at full coverage with nothing reduced; the talk-together close on the lesson and on every band in the lesson's own words, all three directions, each with the skill and the rhythm; and `anchor.ref` naming every verse the lesson stands on.
- **Both refusals ship as gates, not as intentions.** `living-lessons-l213-verses.test.js` fails the build on proof/debunk/collapse vocabulary anywhere in the module, on a both-sides framing around Romans 1:20, on report wording inside quotation marks, on an invented mechanism, and on the removal of the lesson's own refusal sentences, which are pinned in the lesson and in each of the four bands.
- Teach the three tiers as the reader's transferable skill; the headline is the occasion, the sorting is the lesson.
- Where the Word is reticent, stay with what it says and stop. No new DR is claimed beyond DR-0763; no other lesson, course file or workflow is touched.
- **The header names the unused numbers rather than counting them.** L213's arrival made a fourth gap, and the count line's gap clause named at most three before falling back to "4 numbers unused" — a number that hints at the truth instead of telling it. `lesson-order.js` now names up to six (`NAMED_GAPS`), so the real header reads "no L79, L210, L211, L212" and keeps reading the truth as 210 and 211 land. The bound is pinned in the test, and a separate assertion fails if the real catalog ever carries more gaps than the header can name, so the clause cannot quietly go vague again.
- **The numbering gap this lane creates is recorded, not hidden.** L213 lands while lessons on 210, 211 and 212 are in flight on other branches, so the collision gate's `KNOWN_MISSING` holds those three numbers with a comment naming why — the same convention the gate file already used for 193 (held while L194 landed first) and 203 (held while L204 landed first). The gate's second assertion fails if a held number is quietly filled and left on the list, so each entry is deleted by the merge that brings its lesson in. Nothing about the gate is weakened: a gap that is NOT on that list still fails the build, which is the property the 2026-09-02 collision incident proved must hold.
