## DR-0100 tiers applied (verified 2026-09-22)

**Tier 1 — established fact, stated plainly, with source, authors and as-of date.**

- **The Human Genome Project succeeded.** 1990–2003; it determined and published the sequence of nearly all of the roughly three billion base pairs of human DNA (Britannica; Wellcome; as of 2026-09-22). Stated as a success, not a failure.
- **The personalized-prediction promise was made specifically.** Francis Collins told Congress in 2003 that predictive genetic tests would follow within about ten years and that by 2020 gene-based designer drugs would likely be available for diabetes, Alzheimer's disease and hypertension (STAT News; JCI/PMC; genome.gov; as of 2026-09-22). Said without a sneer: parts of the vision *did* arrive — pharmacogenomics, targeted cancer therapy, rare-disease diagnosis.
- **Polygenic risk scores predict individuals poorly — the actual 2023 UCL work, named.** Hingorani AD, Gratton J, Finan C, et al., *"Performance of polygenic risk scores in screening, prediction, and risk stratification: secondary analysis of data in the Polygenic Score Catalog,"* **BMJ Medicine** 2023;2(1) (PMID 37859783; UCL Discovery record 10179716; as of 2026-09-22): 926 polygenic risk scores across 310 diseases; a typical score for a common disease detects about **11%** of those who develop it at a **5%** false-positive rate. This is mainstream medical research published in a BMJ journal by UCL researchers, not a skeptic's talking point. The summary's own "95% of humanity" and "60% of human fatalities" figures are **not** in that paper and are carried as the summary's numbers only.
- **Brownian motion since 1827.** Robert Brown observed the rapid oscillatory motion of particles in pollen grains suspended in water in 1827 and published in 1828, then showed it was general to matter rather than peculiar to living material (Britannica; APS News; as of 2026-09-22). The date checks out. The lesson keeps the detail the summary omits: Brown disproved his own first reading — the same self-correcting move the lesson later admires.
- **Molecule-by-molecule computation of a living cell is intractable**, which is why cell-scale modelling is statistical and coarse-grained (arXiv cond-mat/0504610; cond-mat/0701242; as of 2026-09-22). Said carefully: intractable-*for-us* is a statement about our computers and methods, never a proof about what is or is not determined in the world.
- **Current AI systems have no sensory experience.** Trained on recorded data, run as numerical computation; there is no seeing, hearing, smelling, touching or feeling in the loop. Stated on the architecture and deliberately no further.
- **Denis Noble is real and eminent.** Burdon Sanderson Chair of Cardiovascular Physiology, Oxford, 1984–2004; first computational model of the cardiac action potential in the 1960s; a founding figure of systems biology; his disagreement with Dawkins is genuine, public and long-running (Wikipedia; Voices from Oxford; De Mul 2016 on denisnoble.com; as of 2026-09-22). Carried as a *reported* position rather than a quotation, since the wording came through search summaries rather than a primary document this session fetched.
- **Dawkins's phrase is real.** *"They are in you and in me; they created us, body and mind; and their preservation is the ultimate rationale for our existence."* (Wikipedia, *The Selfish Gene*; Goodreads quotations; as of 2026-09-22). Verified or dropped — it was verified, so it stands, with the whole sentence.

**Tier 2 — genuinely open, named NARROWLY, one clause each.**

- **"Neo-Darwinism is dead" is CONTESTED.** It is **Noble's own verdict inside a live dispute**, not a finding: many, probably most, working evolutionary biologists hold that the Modern Synthesis remains the foundational framework and already accommodates these results; the Extended Evolutionary Synthesis argument is ongoing (PMC12882973; Shan 2024, *Philosophy Compass*; PMC8979413; as of 2026-09-22). The lesson carries the claim with `label: 'opinion'` and the fact row with `status: 'disputed'` whose statement says in terms that **mainstream biology has not conceded**. A test pins all of it. A reader must not leave this lesson believing the field surrendered — it did not, and the Word never needed it to.
- **Transgenerational epigenetic inheritance in humans** is the narrow unsettled part; epigenetic regulation itself is documented and central (`status: 'partly-documented'`).
- **Whether the randomness is inherent in the world** or a limit in our instruments — named as the open part of the stochasticity claim rather than asserted either way.
- **Whether consciousness is explicable at all** — named as open; "consciousness is sensitivity" is carried as Noble's own definition.
- **The video's title is the publisher's claim, not Noble's finding.** *"We Have Been Misled About Biology for 80 Years"* sells; the thumbnail on the same video asks the gentler and better question, *"Has Science Forgotten Its Limits?"* The lesson names that gap as the transferable discernment skill of the whole track — including for headlines we agree with.
- **THE FINITE-MONKEYS CLAIM, handled carefully.** It is carried as **Noble's illustration** (`label: 'opinion'`) with the standard objection **in the same field, in the same breath**: selection is not a pure random search, so a calculation that assumes pure randomness does not describe what the theory claims — cumulative selection with heritable variation is a different mathematical object than drawing letters from a hat. The note says in capitals that it is **NEVER AS A PROOF**, and a separate `disputed` fact row states the objection at length. A test pins both. A lesson leaning on a contested probability argument hands its critic the easiest possible win, and this house does not need it: `Psalms 94:9` does the work without a calculator.

**Tier 3 — over-reach corrected in BOTH directions.**

1. **Genetic determinism.** A man is not a readout of a sequence: *"The soul that sinneth, it shall die."* (Ezekiel 18:20) — the charge is personal, never a thing simply inherited from a father — and *"I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life"* (Deuteronomy 30:19) is spoken to a chooser. The true data underneath stands untouched: genes are real, heritable and consequential.
2. **The opposite over-reach, and it is ours.** **The lesson does not say biology has proved Yahweh, because it has not**, and the claim would be the *weaker* faith — it would rest His Honor on a dispute that could turn next year. *"Through faith we understand"* (Hebrews 11:3) names the instrument, and it is not a journal. Romans 1's logic is a **verdict** — *"so that they are without excuse:"* — not a proof offered for anyone's approval. The deep lens carries this as a named movement, `THE OVER-REACH, CORRECTED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS`, and a test pins the caution's presence.

**Grace note (required; real living people are named).** No condemnation of Denis Noble, Richard Dawkins, Eric Metaxas, Francis Collins, or any scientist who has held a gene-centred account. **"Anti-Yahweh" names the CLAIM, never the person**, and the grace note says so in those words, pinned by a test. Dawkins gets a full, unmocked steelman: the gene's-eye view solved problems that had defeated earlier accounts (sterile workers, unequal parental investment, maternal-fetal conflict), gave the field a unit that actually replicates, generated decades of predictions that came out right, and never required the caricature — the "selfishness" was a bookkeeping metaphor about replicators. `perspectives[]` carries four real steelmen: **gene-centric**, **systems-biology**, the **mainstream-synthesis view that thinks Noble overstates what it overturns** (the view a reader is least likely to be handed, and the third the brief required), and the **Word-first reader**.

## The Word settles what the debate cannot

The deep lens opens `WORD FIRST —` and runs as named movements, the Word leading and the men second.

**The worlds as He framed them.** *"Through faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God, so that things which are seen were not made of things which do appear."* (Hebrews 11:3). *"And he is before all things, and by him all things consist."* (Colossians 1:17). *"Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? declare, if thou hast understanding."* (Job 38:4). And the making of a man is the most precise refutation of a sequence-only account that exists, because it is **two acts and only one of them is material**: *"And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul."* (Genesis 2:7). Dust, then His breath. The breath is not an item in the sequence, and no reading of the dust will find it. With Psalms 139:13-16 — *"curiously wrought"*.

**Consciousness — the centre of the lesson.** The reported point is that silicon can simulate language and cannot see, feel, hear or touch. Read the inventory Yahweh made three thousand years earlier, in the same order: *"They have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not:"* (Psalms 115:5) — *"They have ears, but they hear not: noses have they, but they smell not:"* (115:6) — *"They have hands, but they handle not: feet have they, but they walk not: neither speak they through their throat."* (115:7). **And verse 8 turns it on US, and the lesson does not dodge it:** *"They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them."* (Psalms 115:8). The danger was never that the machine becomes a soul; it is that **the maker shrinks to the size of what he made** — which is precisely what gene-determinism did to man from the other direction. Both errors reduce a living soul to a mechanism, and one verse catches both. With Job 32:8, 1 Corinthians 2:11, Job 12:10, Zechariah 12:1 — and `Habakkuk 2:19`'s *"it shall teach"*, named as **our own exposure**: this platform builds teaching tools, and a tool may carry His words and has *"no breath at all in the midst of it"*.

**Water — the strongest fit.** *"He telleth the number of the stars; he calleth them all by their names."* (Psalms 147:4) — *"Great is our Lord, and of great power: his understanding is infinite."* (147:5); *"Who can number the clouds in wisdom?"* (Job 38:37); *"He bindeth up the waters in his thick clouds; and the cloud is not rent under them."* (Job 26:8). **What is uncomputable to us is not unknown to Him** — a sentence no faster computer can overturn. And the waters were there at the making: Genesis 1:2 and *"For this they willingly are ignorant of, that by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water:"* (2 Peter 3:5). *Willingly are ignorant* lands on the thesis without help from us.

**"Find a way through" is His own signature.** *"Thus saith the LORD, which maketh a way in the sea, and a path in the mighty waters"* (Isaiah 43:16); *"I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert"* (43:19); the same verb in 1 Corinthians 10:13 and John 7:38. And water finishes the idol movement — the idol is stone, gold and silver, crystal and rigid and made; the chip is crystal too; life is water, flux and breath. **Named as a resonance and stopped there**, never as an argument that silicon can never think.

**Chance is a word for what we cannot see, never for what is unruled — this is the release the lesson gives.** *"The lot is cast into the lap; but the whole disposing thereof is of the LORD."* (Proverbs 16:33); *"time and chance happeneth to them all"* (Ecclesiastes 9:11). And the clearest case is an arrow nobody aimed: *"And a certain man drew a bow at a venture, and smote the king of Israel between the joints of the harness"* (1 Kings 22:34) — random by every measure a bowman could give, and exactly as the prophet had spoken (22:17; 22:23). **A believer who flinches at the word "random" has accepted the atheist's premise that chance and providence compete. They do not.**

**Fail-safe design, which Paul already reasoned FROM.** *"For our comely parts have no need: but God hath tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to that part which lacked:"* (1 Corinthians 12:24; 12:25), and distributed causation in one clause: *"the effectual working in the measure of every part"* (Ephesians 4:16).

**The Genome Project, and the honour of searching.** *"It is the glory of God to conceal a thing: but the honour of kings is to search out a matter."* (Proverbs 25:2) — concealing is His glory, searching is our honour; the only thing deflated is the confidence that the search is finished (1 Corinthians 8:2; Psalms 139:6).

**A man who testified against his own work — and the blade turned on us.** *"He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him."* (Proverbs 18:17); *"Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."* (1 Thessalonians 5:21). Then home: a model held eighty years past its evidence is a failure the church commits too — *"Making the word of God of none effect through your tradition"* (Mark 7:13). **If the lesson can praise a biologist for self-correction and cannot ask the reader to do the same, it has failed** — and that is what keeps the thesis from curdling into gloating.

**His knowing precedes the search.** *"He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? he that formed the eye, shall he not see?"* (Psalms 94:9); Isaiah 46:10; Jeremiah 1:5; Proverbs 8:29.

**The anchor** is `Hebrews 11:3; Genesis 2:7` — *He said it first, and the science is arriving late; and the making of a man was always two acts, only one of them material.* **The close is the WHOLE verse:** *"The secret things belong unto the LORD our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law."* (Deuteronomy 29:29). Both halves. Scientific humility is where the conversation ends; the Word goes one step further and hands us an assignment. Ending on mystery alone would be half a verse and half a faith.

**The two courts** are stated: man's court here is the peer-reviewed literature and it **has not ruled**; the eternal court holds every claim ever taught as knowledge, from a journal or a pulpit or an app (Ecclesiastes 12:14; Revelation 20:12; Galatians 6:7) — and the same standard binds a preacher who over-claims a verse as binds a scientist who over-claims a finding.
