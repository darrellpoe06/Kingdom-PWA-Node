   teaches unwatched, early work (Proverbs 6:6-8; 30:25).
2. **No gladness at a fall** (Proverbs 24:17-18; 17:5; Romans 12:15; Psalms 37:1).
3. **Prove your own work; summer before harvest** (Galatians 6:4-5; 1 Thessalonians
   4:11; Colossians 3:23; Luke 16:10; Proverbs 10:5; 22:29).
4. **Not every headline; know your flocks; not in princes** (Proverbs 14:15; 18:17;
   21:5; 27:23-24; 24:30-32; 13:11; 28:20; Jeremiah 17:5, 7; Psalms 146:3); the Lamb
