## Why this exists

Darrell, 2026-09-17, spoken: *"I would like to have a whole business suite of courses. So I used to run rent a center. So use rent a center, rent to own business models as a foundation for inventory control, sales, collections, customer service, marketing. So go research rent to own stores and how they operate."* Then, unprompted, the correction that decided the course's name and its spine:

> "use the term RENT TO OWN. So renting something to own it because I've worked for Rentway first and Rentway taught me way more than Rent-A-Center **because of the way that business operating systems worked**... it taught me how to understand business flow."

And the credential:

> "I started out as an account manager, went from an account manager to an assistant manager, from an assistant manager to the general manager. **I worked in every single position.** There's always something that Yahweh taught me through every single season of my life."

## The decision

**a) The subject is the OPERATING SYSTEM, never a company.** The course is named for the model because that is what Darrell corrected it to. A company can be bought, renamed or closed — Rent-Way was acquired in 2006, 784 stores in 34 states — and a system a man can see, he keeps. The course teaches the flow: where a unit enters, what happens to it, who touches it, what it costs while it waits, and how it leaves.

**b) The five domains Darrell named each get their own lesson**, plus three that his own teaching required: the seats and the ladder (lesson 1), the model and the just weight (2), covenant over contract (7). A gate asserts all five named domains have a lesson AND that each carries a real operating mechanism rather than verses attached to a generic operations manual.

**c) The operating facts are sourced, labelled, and checked SENTENCE BY SENTENCE.** The on-rent / held-for-rent split and the income-forecasting method come from the company's own SEC filings; the return-and-owe-nothing feature from the FTC; the roughly three-in-four voluntary-return rate and the 40–60% early-purchase saving from the industry association; the account manager's own priority from the role as the industry posts it. Every one of these must appear in a sentence that also carries its source.

**d) BOTH TIERS OF HONESTY ON THE PRICE, and they are held APART.** The FTC's finding that rent-to-own total cost can run two to three times retail, and sometimes more, is stated plainly (DR-0100 — under-claiming a verified truth is as much a failure of truth as over-claiming) and weighed against the just weight: Proverbs 11:1, 20:10, 16:11, Deuteronomy 25:13-15, Leviticus 19:35-36, where the weights are called *His own work*. AND the operating discipline is taught as genuinely excellent, because it is. Those are two questions, and the course says so in as many words: collapsing them is how this conversation goes wrong in **both** directions — one side dismissing the operational skill because it dislikes the pricing, the other defending the pricing because it is proud of the skill. A gate requires both wrong directions to be named, not only the convenient one. The remedy the course points to is the FTC's own recommendation — total cost visible to the shopper while shopping — which is a just-weight fix in commercial language. And Proverbs 20:14 disciplines the buyer's tongue too, so it is not one-sided.

**e) The spine is Darrell's, and it is gated.** Honor as the structural mechanism (not a temperament: no balance is owed on ending, so the store has no lever and the only available outcome is a free choice repeated — *"the only time you have any sort of friction is when honor's left the room"*); covenant over contract, with the contract defined by its failure case and **still written down**; Uncle Russell's three takings kept genuinely distinct (information is what happened, understanding is why, skill is being able to do it again tomorrow without being told); feed my sheep as the reason it is poured out now; and *be the best in this too* as the closing standard. The honor runs BOTH directions — Leviticus 19:13 and Deuteronomy 24:15 on paying your own people the same day — because a business gracious to customers and slow to pay its workers has learned a customer-facing imitation of this lesson.

**f) The theological limit is stated, not blurred (DR-0098).** A commercial agreement is not a marriage and is not the everlasting covenant. The POSTURE transfers to commerce — faithfulness that does not depend on the other party's usefulness. The weight of the word stays where Malachi 2:14 and Hebrews 13:20 put it.

## What this cost to get right

**Two defects of the same classes this pass keeps finding, authored fresh in this course by me, and caught by the gate rather than by reading it:**

1. **A fabricated terminal period on Matthew 25:21.** Written as `...ruler over many things."` The verse has a colon there and continues: `: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.`
2. **A compression of Philippians 2:4 presented in quotation marks** — `"look on the things of others"`, where the verse reads `Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.` Un-quoted; the course still emphasises it in capitals, which is our voice and not His.

**And a twelfth false attribution, which is the one worth recording.** The FTC's pricing finding was first written INSIDE quotation marks. `ftc.gov` is egress-blocked from this session, so the Commission's own document was never held — the claim comes from reports of its testimony and staff work. That is exactly the class found eleven times in the living-lessons pass (a misquoted executive, a research paper), pointed this time at a regulator, and authored by us rather than inherited. The claim is KEPT, paraphrased and attributed, with the reason stated in the prose itself: the substance is vouched for and the exact wording is not.

**A gate weakness found by breaking it, then found again.** The attribution checks first asked whether a claim and its source each appeared *somewhere* in the course. Two breaks stayed GREEN. Field-scoping was the first fix and was STILL too coarse — a lesson body is four thousand characters carrying several claims and several attributions, so stripping one claim's source left a different sentence's source holding the check up. The unit a reader actually reads an attribution in is the SENTENCE, so that is the unit checked. This is the same leak found on L90 the same day and recorded in DR-0418; **it is now clear enough to generalise: a corpus-wide `toContain` proves a phrase exists, never that it is doing its job where it stands.**

**One break that was broken, not a gate miss.** The `BE THE BEST IN THIS TOO` break replaced the first occurrence in the file — which was the header COMMENT, not the lesson — so it never touched the content. Retargeted at the lesson's own sentence. Worth recording because a no-op break reads exactly like a passing gate.

**And a content finding from the gate.** The DR-0210 check measured only TWO instances of His covenant name across eight lessons of our own prose. Generic `God` was already at zero, but a Word-first business course that barely names Him is its own failure. Named Him in nine more places where it was natural rather than mechanical.
