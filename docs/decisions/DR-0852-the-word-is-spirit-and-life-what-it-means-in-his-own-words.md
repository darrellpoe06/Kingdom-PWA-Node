## Context

Darrell spoke it into the app on 2026-10-09 as a question with one word under it: *"What does it mean that the Word Is Spirit And Life!!!?!!!! Lesson."*

The sentence is the Lord's own, said at the end of the hardest chapter of His teaching, and the lesson answers it the way this house answers (DR-0098): the Word explains the Word. No school of men is staged beside the text; each term is taught from Scripture's own usage, and the lesson stops where the text stops.

- **Where He said it, and why it cost something.** John 6:63 in its own chapter: the bread of life, the many who went back, the twelve who stayed because His words are the words of eternal life (John 6:35, 6:51, 6:66-69).
- **The Word is a Person before it is a page.** In the beginning was the Word; in Him was life; the Word was made flesh; His name is called The Word of God (John 1:1, 1:4, 1:14; Revelation 19:13; 1 John 1:1; Hebrews 1:3).
- **The Word made everything, and the Spirit was there.** By the Word of the LORD were the heavens made; He spake, and it was done; the breath of life (Psalms 33:6, 33:9; Genesis 1:2-3; Genesis 2:7; Hebrews 11:3; Job 33:4).
- **Why spirit and not letter.** Holy men spake as moved by the Holy Ghost; the Comforter teaches; the letter killeth, the spirit giveth life; worship in spirit and in truth (2 Peter 1:21; John 14:26, 16:13; 2 Corinthians 3:6; John 4:24; 1 Corinthians 2:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:6).
- **Why life: the Word raises the dead.** The dry bones, the breath, the exceeding great army; Lazarus, come forth; the dead shall hear His voice; quicken thou me according to thy word (Ezekiel 37:4-10, 37:14; John 11:43-44; John 5:24-25; Psalms 119:25, 119:50, 119:93; Psalms 107:20).
- **What a living Word does, in its own pictures.** Seed, incorruptible seed, water that cleans, fire and hammer, the sword of the Spirit, bread (Luke 8:11, 8:15; 1 Peter 1:23; James 1:18, 1:21; John 15:3; Ephesians 5:26; John 17:17; Jeremiah 23:29; Ephesians 6:17; Hebrews 4:12; Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4).
- **It is your life, not a vain thing; it does not pass away** (Deuteronomy 32:46-47; Romans 10:17; Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 24:35; Psalms 119:89; 1 Peter 1:25).
- **So read it for the Spirit, eat it, let it dwell, and do it** (Jeremiah 15:16; 1 Peter 2:2; Psalms 119:11; Colossians 3:16; James 1:22; Romans 8:11; Luke 24:32).

Every term is taught in our voice as the Word uses it: *quicken* is makes alive; *spirit* is breath and the breath is His; *life* is not information about life but the thing that raises what was dead.
