## Impact

- **His three words kept.** *Or may not* is a heading of its own in the lesson and every band, and the hold-fast half is pinned in every reader text: see *if* there be, *or no*, prove all things and hold fast that which is good, tried me and shalt find nothing. A proof shows a band that drops the hold-fast half is caught. The lesson states the rule plainly: a way the Word confirms is held fast with thanks, and that is as real a result of soul searching as a correction.
- **His distinction kept.** The gap is to what He *means* by His Word, found by searching the Scriptures, not assumed; pinned in every band with Acts 17:11 and Mark 7:8's tradition of men.
- **Both counterfeits refused.** Self-condemnation (1 Corinthians 4:3-4) and self-flattery (Proverbs 3:7) are named and refused in every band, so the search can be neither grim nor flattering.
- **The foundation surfaces in the lesson.** The six names of the discipline from MIND-OF-CHRIST.md are carried in the lesson and every band and pinned, so the reader meets the one truth under all six labels; the eight questions of Philippians 4:8 are quoted whole in every band.
- **Plain meaning first (DR-0521).** No hard term from the plain-before-the-term list is used; *default* is explained in its own sentence before it is leaned on, in every band.
- **The follow-up shape.** The lesson opens by naming Look at the Record in the lesson and every band; the gate asserts L277 is its catalog neighbour. Neither gate pins a maximum (DR-1316).
- **One drive-by.** ll277's gate carried a stale describe label, "L276 is in the catalog", left by the renumbering in DR-1318; it now reads L277. A label only; no assertion changed.
- **Stories** are parables with invented characters, African American main characters given naturally by name and place (a kitchen in Raleigh, a deacon in Shreveport).
