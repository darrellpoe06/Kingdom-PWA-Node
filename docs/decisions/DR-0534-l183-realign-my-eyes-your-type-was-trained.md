## The trap, named before a word was written

A lesson on this subject does real harm when it over-corrects. The failure mode is preaching it into "noticing beauty is carnal, a spiritual person feels nothing" — which produces a husband ashamed of a good desire and a young person convinced Yahweh is offended by their own eyes. That is not in the Word, and **DR-0100 tier 3 is exactly this case: correct the over-reach while keeping the true data under it.**

So the lesson is built on a distinction it states out loud in every register:

- **Noticing is not the sin.** Scripture says flatly, with no rebuke attached, "Leah was tender eyed; but Rachel was beautiful and well favoured" (Genesis 29:17). A whole book of the canon carries "Thou art all fair, my love; there is no spot in thee" (Song of Solomon 4:7).
- **RANKING is the sin** — seating what the eye can see above what it cannot, and calling the verdict discernment.
- And 1 Samuel 16:7 is read for what it says: Yahweh is not telling Samuel appearance is imaginary, He is telling Samuel to weigh the invisible thing first, the way He weighs it.

**The second guard: Leah is never the punchline.** The Jacob story is usually told so the plainer sister carries the joke. The Word does not do that — Yahweh honoured her, and the line that carries Judah, and through Judah the Lamb of Yahweh, runs through the sister the eye passed over. Every band says so, and the test file asserts it.

## The timeline, and why it is carried WITH its limits

Darrell asked for timelines. A bare number would have been the easy thing and the dishonest one.

- **The "21 days" rule is false and traceable.** It comes from the preface of *Psycho-Cybernetics* (Maxwell Maltz, 1960) — a plastic surgeon's clinical impression that patients took about three weeks to get used to a changed face and that phantom limb sensations persisted about that long. It was never a study of habit formation. Stating that plainly is DR-0100 tier 1.
- **The measured study is Lally et al., 2010** (*European Journal of Social Psychology*): 96 volunteers each performed one daily eating, drinking or activity behaviour in the same context for 12 weeks, rating automaticity daily. Median time to 95% of their own automaticity asymptote: **66 days, range 18–254**.
- **And the limits are carried with the number, every time it appears.** The curve fitted only a subset of participants; the study ran 84 days, so the far end of that range is a model extrapolation rather than an observation; and the behaviours measured were things like drinking water after breakfast — **not visual appetite**. The lesson tells the reader to take the SHAPE (weeks to months, hugely variable, far longer than three weeks) and explicitly not to carry 66 days out as a promise about their eyes.

A test enforces this: wherever the median appears in any register, the range and the myth it replaces must appear with it.

## Extinction is not erasure — the pastoral core

The piece that matters most, and the reason people quit: when an old pathway stops being fed it is **not deleted**. A new competing pattern is built alongside it while the old one stays on the shelf, and under stress, exhaustion, or in the original context it can win again — at which point the person concludes they never really changed.

This is exactly what the panel described when they said the images come back and have to be put down repeatedly, and it is exactly why Paul's verb is continuous rather than finished: "Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ" (2 Corinthians 10:5). **Promising erasure would be a lie that makes a reader quit when the images return.** A test asserts that no band promises it.

## The Word made the training claim first

The science here is a **witness**, never the warrant. Scripture already said perception is trained by repetition: "But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil" (Hebrews 5:14). By reason of USE. Exercised. The harvest law runs the same principle (Galatians 6:7-8), and the accumulated result settles where this house has always located it — the heart as the deep mind, not the feelings: "For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he" (Proverbs 23:7), which is why the command is to guard the intake at the source (Proverbs 4:23).

And He does not leave the channel empty. Philippians 4:8 names what to run through it, because starving an old track is only half the work.

## The subconscious situational analysis Darrell asked about

The fast appraisal of a person runs before it can be narrated — a verdict returned in less time than it takes to build a sentence explaining it. People experience that as intuition and trust it accordingly. **But the fast read is a retrieval, not a revelation.** A trained eye produces a trained gut, and the gut then delivers its verdict in the voice of instinct about a conclusion the feed helped write. That is why "I just wasn't feeling it" is such an unreliable report: something was certainly felt; the question almost nobody asks is who taught it.
