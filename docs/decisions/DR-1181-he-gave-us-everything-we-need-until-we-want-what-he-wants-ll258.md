## Context

Darrell's teaching, rendered for meaning:
- Yahweh expects us to crown ourselves.
- He gave us the instructions. He gave us the systems. He gave us the blueprint. He gave us the Holy Spirit. He gave us His Son. He gave us Himself. He gave us love.
- So He gave us everything we need to fulfill what He wants, not necessarily what we want.
- Until we want what He wants, our desires do not change; when we get it, that becomes the highest priority. His priorities are the highest priority.
- Till we get Him right, we do not get right. After that, we can get right. Real right. In Jesus' name.

The same message carried a second, separate request (the Learn navigation: one scroll, course-only tabs). That is product work, not teaching, and it is recorded on its own; nothing of it is in the lesson.

The Word carries every line without needing a debate:
- **Crown ourselves:** 2 Timothy 4:7-8 (the crown is laid up by Him; the fighting, finishing and keeping are ours); 1 Corinthians 9:24-25; Revelation 3:11; James 1:12; Philippians 2:12-13; Revelation 4:10-11.
- **The instructions:** Deuteronomy 30:11-14; Psalms 119:105; 2 Timothy 3:16-17; Joshua 1:8.
- **The systems and the blueprint:** Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5; 1 Chronicles 28:19; Exodus 31:3; Proverbs 24:3-4; Psalms 127:1; Luke 14:28.
- **The Holy Spirit, His Son, Himself:** John 14:16-17; John 14:26; John 3:16; John 1:29; Hebrews 1:8; Colossians 1:16-17; John 14:23; Romans 8:32.
- **Love:** 1 John 4:19; 1 John 4:10; Romans 5:5; Romans 5:8; 1 Corinthians 13:2; John 13:34.
- **Everything we need, for what He wants:** 2 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 9:8; Ephesians 2:10; Hebrews 13:21; John 6:38; Luke 22:42; Isaiah 55:9; James 4:3.
- **Until we want what He wants:** Psalms 37:4; Psalms 40:8; Ezekiel 36:26-27; Romans 12:2; John 15:7; 1 John 5:14; Jeremiah 29:13.
- **His priorities highest:** Matthew 6:33; Matthew 6:32; Matthew 22:37-38; Haggai 1:4, 1:7-9; Matthew 6:10.
- **Till we get Him right:** 1 Corinthians 3:11; Matthew 7:24-25; Proverbs 9:10; Matthew 7:21; Hebrews 11:6; Proverbs 3:6; Philippians 1:6; Proverbs 16:31 (senior band).
- **The name he closed in:** John 14:13; Colossians 3:17.

One line needed care. **"Yahweh expects us to crown ourselves"** could be heard as pride. The Word settles it in two adjacent verses: the crown is His to lay up (2 Timothy 4:8) and the fight, the course and the faith are ours to finish (2 Timothy 4:7); He works in and we work out (Philippians 2:12-13); and a received crown is cast before the throne (Revelation 4:10). So the lesson teaches "crown ourselves" as *finish what He equipped, so there is a crown to lay at His feet*, and the gate pins the two halves and the laying-down in every band.

## Decision

The gate pins the spine in every place a reader reads:
- 32 strands quoted in every band, among them 2 Timothy 4:7 and 4:8 together, Revelation 3:11, Philippians 2:13, Revelation 4:10, Deuteronomy 30:14, Hebrews 8:5, Exodus 31:3, Psalms 127:1, John 14:26, John 3:16, John 14:23, 1 John 4:19, 2 Peter 1:3, Luke 22:42, Psalms 37:4, Haggai 1:4 and 1:9, 1 Corinthians 3:11 and John 14:13;
- 47 further strands verbatim somewhere in the lesson;
- his lines in every band (Darrell by name, crown ourselves, the instructions, the blueprint, the Holy Spirit, His Son, Himself, love, everything we need, until we want what He wants, till we get Him right, real right, his closing in Jesus' name);
- the ten sections in his order;
- every band closing on Matthew 6:33, His priority first;
- Jesus confessed as the Lamb of Yahweh in our voice, with John 1:29 beside it.

The two stories teach the lesson rather than decorate it:
- **The Birdhouse Kit** (light; Hebrews 8:5): a father's complete kit, a daughter who builds it her own way first, and a wren that moves in once she wants what the drawing wants.
- **The Plans in the Drawer** (solemn; Psalms 127:1): a son who uses the bridge lumber for his own gazebo, a flood, eleven days of families walking around, and a bridge finally built according to the pattern.

## Impact

A reader sees, in their own band, that nothing they need for His work is missing, and that the one thing left to do is theirs: run the race He equipped. A child learns that the kit has every piece and the crown is for giving back. A youth learns that a system is a plan that works on Monday when they do not feel like it. A teen learns the difference between asking for their own plan with His supply and asking according to His will. A senior learns that a long obedience is itself a crown, and that "getting Him right" is where everything else gets right. Every band sends the reader to their parents, their children and a friend with the same question: what has He already given you for the thing in front of you?
