# DR-0357 — One forms engine for every product: the household record, its shelf, and the ladder a workflow climbs

**Date:** 2026-09-11 · **Status:** accepted · **Tier:** B · **Area:** platform · **Principles:** VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE, APP-IS-PRIMARY, DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT, DETERMINISTIC-FIRST, TLC-FIREWALL, DECISION-RECORDS

## Directive, in Darrell's words (2026-09-11)

- *"Create the same type of intake forms for PoeTech and Poe Properties Apps... however make sure they fit the requirements of the product it claims to be."*
- *"We also need a PoeTech family documents upload and a process for analytics and services to be created based on the information; workflows are added to become MVPs until we are systematizing our lives and training our LLMs based on the business needs of our specific family needs... the process needs to be fine-tuned until it's a hardened system that produces the best way to build processes that enables families to gain insights and understanding of their own interests and needs."*
- *"each has their own databases so each can work independently and together as our Ways and documentation state... comprehensive review of the history of the work in our DRs to create these systems for our business systems and services."*

## What is true (SHOULD / ARE / GAP)

- **SHOULD:** each product asks the questions its own work needs, its office edits those questions without a deploy, the answers land in that product's own database, the household can keep its papers, and what all of it says comes back as findings the household can check.
- **ARE, before this change:**
  - The forms engine existed but was TLC's: `lib/tlc-office-forms.js` imported TLC's sections, agreements and handbook, and `0196` resolved the office by `instance_type = 'therapy-practice'` inlined in the read.
  - **PoeTech had no household intake at all.** The Family Roster (DR-0093) names the people; nothing named the household — so every surface guessed at what it was serving.
  - **Poe Properties already had its application** (`modules/properties/intake.js`, 13 sections transcribed from the paper form, SSN and licence deliberately out-of-band, the fair-housing guard as a machine check), live on the door — but **code-only**: not a word could change without a deploy, and the criteria DR-0101 requires in writing did not exist as a document an applicant could read.
  - **There was no family documents vault and no family bucket.** The five buckets in the system are TLC's, the church's, the showcase's and the legal shelves'.
  - Insights were doctrine: `BEHAVIORAL-MIRROR.md` states DATA → TRUTH → IDENTITY → INVITATION and **no code implemented it**; `lib/derived-insights.js` (DR-0249) is a pure library with tests and no mounted surface.
- **GAP:** one product had the way, two did not; the household had no record, no shelf, and nothing that turned its own answers into anything.

## Decisions

1. **The engine is product-free.** `lib/forms-engine.js` is the way TLC proved (DR-0352 → DR-0356), with the product lifted out: every function takes the ORIGINAL it normalizes onto. `lib/tlc-office-forms.js` keeps its whole public API and delegates — TLC's behaviour is unchanged, proven by its existing suites passing untouched. Two corrections the lift forced, both improvements: the validator now distinguishes base from added questions for every product (it was TLC's already, and is the better rule), and **an acknowledgment or a floor question is required by definition**, so a product's default form can never fail its own validation.
2. **A registry, not a fork.** `lib/product-forms.js` names each product's forms and documents, its ORIGINALS and its floor. PoeTech: the household intake and the Household Covenant. Poe Properties: the rental application and two documents that did not exist before — *What we look at in an application* and *How we choose, and how we do not*.
3. **"Its own database" is the instance, and RLS on `instance_id` is the wall** (DR-0060). `product_forms` (0200) carries `(instance_id, product, key)` and **names no `instance_type` anywhere**. A product's forms live in whichever instance runs that product. That is what lets the products work independently and together, and it means the day Poe Properties gets its own instance the table needs no change. TLC's own tables are NOT re-homed: 0196 is live with real versions in it and there is nothing to gain by moving them.
4. **The household record.** `lib/household-intake.js` — 42 questions across nine sections, and **every question names the surface it feeds**, asserted by test: a question no surface reads does not survive review. It holds no account, routing or card number, no password, no Social Security number and no diagnosis (refused by key in the patch guard AND by a table constraint). It asks an **adult about the household** and counts children in ranges — a child's own row stays on the Family Roster under a guardian, because the consent/assent flow DR-0093 owes is still undesigned and nothing here may open a child-facing data stream. Health is logistics, never a clinical record: the TLC firewall is senior to everything.
5. **The covenant.** `lib/household-covenant.js` is signed in place with the same control, sentence, version-pin and server stamp DR-0356 built for TLC — the component now takes another product's writer. It states what the app will never do (sell, send to an insurer or employer, train anything outside the household without the household's word) **and what is not built**: the shelf is not encrypted with a key only the family holds. A private shelf, not a safe deposit box (DR-0329's honesty rule).
6. **The shelf.** `family_documents` + the private `family-documents` bucket (0201): a row is EITHER a file OR a pointer to where the paper lives, both first class, neither-is-refused. **Private to whoever filed it until they share it with the household**, and sharing does not hand over the pen — update and delete stay with the person who filed it.
7. **The findings, and the ladder.** `lib/household-insights.js` is the first code behind the Behavioral Mirror: every finding carries DATA, TRUTH, INVITATION and a `basis` naming the exact rows it read, so any claim can be opened. **No rows produces no finding** — an untouched household gets an honest sentence, not an empty dashboard. The sharpest finding compares what the household asked the app to carry against the answers that make carrying it possible. `WORKFLOW_LADDER` makes the PROCESS visible as data — Noticed → Described → MVP → Systematized → Hardened — and a candidate starts at the bottom, named by the household, rising only when the household says it has.
8. **Deterministic first, and no model anywhere in this chain yet.** Every finding here is arithmetic. That is the rule the chain is built on, not a stage to grow out of: what a calculation can say is never handed to a model. Training anything on the family's own record is a **separate decision that does not exist yet** — see below.

## What this deliberately did NOT do

- **No landlord instance was created and no rentals were re-homed.** The live database holds no `landlord` instance; Poe Properties' 12 doors sit in `poe-family`, because DR-0313 decided one module behind two doors and set its own dated re-review. Moving them is a tenancy decision, and it collides with DR-0313's rule that rent still posts into the PoeTech books. **The Governor's call.**
- **No editor surface for the new products' forms yet.** The engine, the registry, the table and the save function are all in; what is missing is the screen (TLC's `TlcFormEditor` generalized). Until it exists, the new products render their ORIGINALS and the office cannot yet change a word. *re-review: 2026-09-18.*
- **Nothing about training an LLM.** No corpus, no box, no consent mechanism, no gate — and the DR history contains no decision authorizing any of it. Naming it here is the start of that decision, not the making of it.
- **The consumer-report authorization stays blocked.** `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` in `modules/properties/intake.js` names On-Site.com and California law while Poe Properties operates in Illinois; its own re-review date (2026-09-16) is days away. The engine conversion drops that section outright and the test asserts it.
