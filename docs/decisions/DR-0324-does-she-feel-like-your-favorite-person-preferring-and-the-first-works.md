## Directive

Darrell, 2026-09-02, bringing in a third spoken clip — a man the account names
Marcellus recalling his mentor, married over thirty years, asking him who his
favorite person was. He answered instantly: my wife. The mentor's follow-up is
the whole thing:

> *"but does she feel like she's your favorite person?"*

Then the mentor's list — you look forward to seeing them, you laugh with them,
you tell them the good news first, you protect them when they're not in the
room, you give them the benefit of the doubt — and the drift: couples become
coworkers managing a house, business partners paying bills, parents raising
kids, roommates, *"but they forget to be best friends."*

## Why this one had to be built

L114 (DR-0320) gave the husband an account of his own post. L120 (DR-0323) gave
the wife hers and took the beam out of the pointing eye. **A series carrying
only those two is backbone with no heart**, which fails the Religion AND
Relationship test on this repo's own terms. This is the warmth those two were
built to carry, and the gate asserts the pairing rather than leaving a reader to
notice it.

## The decision, in four parts

### 1. The gap is DELIVERY, not sincerity

The mentor did not ask whether the man loves his wife. He asked whether she can
tell. That is 1 John 3:18 exactly — *"let us not love in word, neither in tongue;
but in deed and in truth"* — a verse that measures what arrived, not what was
felt. A man can love his wife genuinely and be disobedient to it. She should not
have to infer it.

### 2. "Favorite person" has a biblical name, and it is a command

Romans 12:10 — *"in honour preferring one another"* — actively going first in
honour, at a temperature the Word names: *"fervent charity"* (1 Peter 4:8). That
single move turns the lesson from a mood to hope for into an obedience.

### 3. The drift has a name and a three-step repair — handled honestly

Revelation 2:4-5 diagnoses this exact pattern: everything still functioning, the
first warmth quietly gone. **The honest note rides with it and comes first:**
that word is spoken to the church at Ephesus about her love for the Lord, *not*
about a marriage. The pattern transfers on the Word's own authority — Ephesians
5:32 makes the marriage the image of that union — and the lesson says which is
which. A teacher who lets a room hear it as a marriage verse has traded accuracy
for impact, and the facilitator notes forbid it in those words.

The repair is the part almost nobody runs: **not feel the first feelings — DO
the first works.** The actions came first and the warmth followed; that order is
still available at year thirty-five, and it does not wait on a mood.

### 4. Both misuses refused (DR-0100)

A spouse in depression, chronic illness or grief may feel nothing, and that flat
feeling is **not a verdict** on the love being given — the Word makes the deed
the measure and tells the one sowing in a long season to keep going (Galatians
6:9). And the question is a **mirror, never a bill**: turned outward it becomes
the accounting L114 dismantled. Then the order of loves is kept straight
(Matthew 10:37), because a spouse asked to be someone's god will fail and be
resented for failing at what no human was built to do.
