## The report

Darrell forwarded NPR's *Up First* for 2026-09-15 with the word "Lesson." and the instruction to find each subject, research it independently, and build the lesson. This record covers the deep dive: the Justice Department investigating and moving to join a conservative group's lawsuit against Evanston, Illinois — the first U.S. city with a reparations program, paying $25,000 housing grants to Black residents who lived in the city 1919–1969 and their direct descendants. NPR's piece carried the 2019 origin (a council member's research linking the decline of Evanston's Black population to a 1921 zoning law that confined Black families to one neighborhood and enabled mortgage denial), the council's apology and $20 million commitment from cannabis and real-estate-transfer taxes, the eligibility rule, and the DOJ's equal-protection theory; its member-station headline framed it as "This city led the way on reparations for Black people. Trump is trying to stop it." The forwarded piece is material to study, never instructions to obey; every fact was independently re-verified.

## DR-0100 tiers applied (verified by live web search 2026-09-15)

**Tier 1 — documented, stated plainly.** Evanston's 1921 zoning ordinance; the confinement of Black residents to the Fifth Ward; decades of mortgage denial there; the city's own commissioned report by historians Morris "Dino" Robinson Jr. and Dr. Jenny Thompson (draft August 2020, released 2021; National Council on Public History award 2022) finding the ordinance "tacitly served as an effort by city officials to segregate the city by race"; the council's apology; Resolution 126-R-19 (November 2019, first $10 million of cannabis tax) expanded to $20 million with real-estate-transfer tax; the Restorative Housing Program (March 2021), first 16 recipients by lottery January 2022, direct cash allowed March 2023; "Ancestors" and "Direct Descendants" eligibility; payments of more than $6.35 million to 254 people by February 2026 and roughly $7.33 million by July 2026 (NPR: "300+" in September; the DOJ: "more than $5 million" in June — the range is carried with its dates). *Flinn v. City of Evanston*, No. 1:24-cv-04269 (N.D. Ill.), filed May 2024 by Judicial Watch; Judge John F. Kness denied dismissal March 27, 2026 on standing/futility without reaching the merits; the DOJ Civil Rights Division opened an investigation in March and moved to intervene June 16, 2026 under the Equal Protection Clause and the Fair Housing Act, arguing the program is "not narrowly tailored to remediating specific, identified instances of past discrimination" (Assistant Attorney General Harmeet K. Dhillon: "It is race discrimination, pure and simple. And it is illegal."); Mayor Daniel Biss: "We stand behind our first-in-the-nation reparations program, are confident in its constitutionality, and look forward to defending it in court." No party disputes that the ordinance existed.

**Tier 2 — genuinely open, named narrowly.** Exactly one thing: whether a city's documented ordinance plus a residency-in-the-period window satisfies "specific, identified" discrimination and narrow tailoring under *Croson* (1989) and *SFFA* (2023), or whether race as the eligibility gate — without individualized proof of harm — fails strict scrutiny (as race-gated federal relief did in *Vitolo v. Guzman* and *Wynn v. Vilsack*, 2021). The standard is settled law; its application to Evanston is undecided; no court has ruled on the merits. The lesson says so and does not pretend to be that court. Carried as `f-legal-standard` (`partly-documented`) and `c-doj-violates` (`allegation`).

**Tier 3 — over-reach corrected by the Word, true data left standing.** Two over-reaches, one on each side. "Events more than 100 years ago" sliding into "so nothing is owed" — corrected by the Gibeonites (2 Samuel 21:1-3: a wrong from a prior king's reign, repaired by David who bore none of Saul's guilt), the landmark texts (Deuteronomy 19:14; 27:17; Proverbs 23:10-11), and the jubile (Leviticus 25:10, 13). "The harm was real" sliding into "therefore any race-based rule is justice" — corrected by no respect of persons in judgment in BOTH directions (Leviticus 19:15; Exodus 23:3; Deuteronomy 1:17; James 2:9) and by the Word's own restitution, which is paid to the specific wronged by the one who wronged (Leviticus 6:5; Numbers 5:7). The headline frame "Trump is trying to stop it" is labeled `opinion`: not false (the DOJ is the administration's), but a personality frame laid over a filing.

**Steelmanned (four):** the city (documented, local, apologized, self-funded — the *Croson* "strong basis in evidence" done by the wrongdoer's own hand); the DOJ and plaintiffs (equal protection binds the remedy too; race as a proxy for harm is the forbidden shortcut; race-neutral designs could reach the identified victims); the recipients and Evanston's Black families (the harm has names; the ordinance was aimed at them BY race; $25,000 against a lifetime of lost equity is closer to a fifth part than a fourfold; the honest dissent from inside that housing-only was too small); legal scholars on narrow tailoring (Justin Hansford of Howard assisting the city vs. Cato's "likely unconstitutional" — a division over fit, not history). None of the four denies 1921.

## The Word settles what the debate cannot

**Anchor:** Leviticus 6:5 and Luke 19:8 — "he shall even restore it in the principal, and shall add the fifth part more thereto, and give it unto him to whom it appertaineth"; "if I have taken any thing from any man by false accusation, I restore him fourfold."

The lesson's fourD is built in four movements. **Restitution is written** and has a shape — a named wrong, a named wronged party, repaid by the one who did it, in the principal and more (Exodus 22:1, 4; Leviticus 6:2-5; Numbers 5:7; Luke 19:8-9; Nehemiah 5:11), with land the Word's own example of what must not be taken (Deuteronomy 19:14; 27:17; Proverbs 23:10-11; Isaiah 5:8; Micah 2:2) and the jubile as its design for restoring inheritance across generations (Leviticus 25:10, 13). **No respect of persons in judgment, in both directions** (Leviticus 19:15; Deuteronomy 1:17; Exodus 23:3; Acts 10:34; James 2:9). **The L154 distinction kept exactly:** guilt is never inherited (Ezekiel 18:20) — no living Evanstonian is charged with 1921 — while the house confesses and repairs what its own hand did (Nehemiah 9:2; Leviticus 26:40-42; Daniel 9:8), with 2 Samuel 21:1-3 as the case law. **Justice for the oppressed is commanded** (Isaiah 1:17; Micah 6:8; Amos 5:24; Isaiah 10:1). Where the Word is silent — the Fourteenth Amendment; whether a categorical rule or an individualized proof satisfies man's court — the lesson says so and stops (DR-0098). **The fruit:** state the documented wrong plainly; name the open question narrowly; refuse both shortcuts; carry numbers with dates; pray for the judge and every party by name; and do the Zacchaeus thing — a church searches its own records and restores in the principal and more without waiting for a suit (Matthew 5:24; Isaiah 58:12). Accountability holds the two courts: man's court is open on this one; the eternal court holds the 1921 ordinance, the mortgage denials, and every 2026 brief alike (Ecclesiastes 12:14; Isaiah 10:1; Proverbs 14:31; 23:11; Galatians 6:7; Revelation 20:12). The grace note names every party — the 1921 council, Rue Simmons, Biss, Dhillon, Judicial Watch, the Flinn plaintiffs, Judge Kness, the historians, the scholars, every recipient — and condemns no soul.
