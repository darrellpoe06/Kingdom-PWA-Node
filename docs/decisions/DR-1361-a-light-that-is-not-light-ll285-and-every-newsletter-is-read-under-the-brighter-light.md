## Context

**The first message, rendered for meaning (DR-0331).** Yahweh is very clear about the enemy: an enemy of good, His enemy, ours, but never at a level where we think he is equal with Yahweh. He has a light that is not light like Yahweh’s light. Yahweh’s light is knowledge and literal life, so you could live forever because you have His light and His life; the enemy’s light is knowledge that corrupts life. His kingdom is eternal; the other is a dying kingdom. That is why we study His way and will and not the enemy’s: he is a liar from the beginning, the father of what lying is, and lies cannot sustain life, while truth does not need sustaining because truth is life. He is too integrated to separate from His Word, His Spirit, His way, His light, His life. The gap in you is where you lie to yourself; the only way not to is to hear what He says about you, so you become the being He designed from the beginning, the way He stepped down and showed us.

**The second message.** The newsletters that come in as lessons deserve more rigor: several lessons in one email, each item explaining something that could be explained in detail; whole human populations discussing things at a high level; even the advertising, which shows what is being offered to the psychological profile of human beings. The highest level is the Word of Yahweh, the best light, and it exposes all the other lights because it is brighter. And we must show that we are doing this inside our processes, and evaluate that too.

## Decision

1. **Living Lesson 285** (`ll285-a-light-that-is-not-light`), four bands, two parables, eight quiz questions, a facilitator guide, dated 2026-11-06; gated by `living-lessons-ll285-a-light-that-is-not-light.test.js`. His second message is its own section, THE BRIGHTER LIGHT EXPOSES THE OTHERS, in the lesson and every band.
2. **Every newsletter is read under the brighter light (the Way).** `lesson_writer.py`: the standard prompt gains A NEWSLETTER OR FORWARDED EMAIL; `row_rules` tells the writer when a row arrived by email (`mail:` tag) or is a split item (`newsletter-item`); `reading_problems` refuses an emailed lesson whose `lesson_intro` lacks THE READING UNDER THE LIGHT or the verbatim Isaiah 8:20 and 1 Thessalonians 5:21, and never fires on a row that did not arrive by email.
3. **One email, several lessons.** The writer may return `further_lessons` (≤8 `{title, item, why}`); after the lesson ships, `file_further` files each as an `agent_inbox` row (`lesson`, `newsletter-item`, `split-from:<parent>`, the `mail:` tag; source `email-split`) and marks the parent `newsletter-split`. Brakes: emailed parents only, never an item, once per parent, ceiling 8, a filing fault reported and never raised.
