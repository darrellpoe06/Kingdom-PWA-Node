A man who said he was 67 answered what combat taught him with *"Life is very short."*, counted *"at my age, I probably only had 10 Christmases left"*, said *"I grew up with nothing."* and *"I look at my life now and say, oh Lord, I don't deserve this."*, answered 100 out of 100 that God is real, and closed by paraphrasing that every knee shall bow and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord.

## Context

**SHOULD.** A spoken teaching captured into the app becomes a lesson the same session, Word-first, in four age bands, with every verse fetched verbatim and every claim it does not own attributed rather than asserted (`CLAUDE.md` "Spoken Teachings Are Build Input"; DR-0076; DR-0100; P60).

**ARE, before this record.** The row was claimed by the NAS lesson builder (DR-0669), which failed it twice and spent its retries. Its own reasons, from the row's tags: attempt 1 `cli-local verse failed (not-the-verse Psalms 39:4); repo gates failed (verse,talkTogether)`; attempt 2 `cli-local repo gates failed (fullLevels,talkTogether)`. So it reached for a verse about the brevity of days and quoted it wrong twice, and never carried TALK ABOUT IT TOGETHER into every band.

**GAPS.** Three, and all three are gated here. (1) Psalms 39:4 was misquoted twice; the corpus writes a **colon** — *"what it is: that I may know how frail I am"* — where a writer working from memory reaches for a semicolon. (2) The three directions were missing from bands, which DR-0795 says is exactly the failure a pooled check cannot see. (3) Nothing in the builder's pipeline distinguished **a third party's words from Darrell's**, and this source is a third party's: DR-0331 licenses rendering Darrell's own words for meaning and explicitly does not reach a stranger's.

**What this recording is, and is not.** It is a recorded street interview Darrell captured **inside the PoeTech app**; the words arrived as a Whisper transcript. The speakers are **not identified**: the transcript marks the interviewer `S1` and the man answering `S2`/`S3` — two labels, one man, split by the transcriber — names the interviewer "James" exactly once, and never names the man answering. There are no `voice:BG` / `voice:DP` tags (DR-0712), so this is **not Darrell teaching and not Bishop Gwin teaching** (DR-0719). The lesson says all of that in its first paragraph and never guesses a voice.

**Proven to catch, run live against the catalog file itself (DR-0076 §3).** One word was altered in one quoted verse in `living-lessons-class.js` — the ll257 copy of Psalms 39:4, *frail* to *fragile* — the gate was re-run, and it failed **by field and reference**: `lesson :: Psalms 39:4`. **167 passed / 0 failed before; 165 passed / 2 failed after; restored and 167 passed / 0 failed again**, with zero occurrences of the altered word left in the file. Two further live mutations are pinned in the gate: the advertising leaking into one band is named by that band, and a dropped movement is named by its band.

**Honest uncertainty, stated rather than papered over.** (1) **Not one figure in this clip was checked by anyone here** — thirty-five software companies, a $670 million exit, a channel of 26 million followers, the age of 67, ten Christmases, combat in the Gulf War in 1991. They are the speakers' own claims — the companies, the exit, the age, the Christmases and the Gulf War the man answering's; the 26 million followers the man FILMING's — attributed in the lesson's own words and asserted nowhere; the money, company and follower figures are pinned **absent from every age band** by test, and the lesson says "Nobody here checked one of them." (2) **The transcriber mis-heard at least three spans** — *"I've been eating 60 billionaires in the last five years."* (almost certainly *interviewing*), *"So they help people start LLCs and escorts."* (garbled; what that company does is **not recoverable** from this recording, so the lesson says so rather than guessing), and *"Which one do you think we made?"* (garbled past use). (4) `talk-together-baseline.json` is left at 609 / 14 / 14 although the live numbers are 673 / 78 / 78 — see Decision 6.

## Impact

A recorded conversation with a stranger, carrying unverified boasts and an advertisement, is now a Word-first lesson in four age bands that asserts none of the boasts, uses none of the advertising, guesses no voice, and teaches the three sentences in it that nobody could fake. Unresolved before: the row had burned both of the NAS builder's attempts and would have stayed uncaptured, and the two faults that burned it — a misquoted verse about the brevity of days, and TALK ABOUT IT TOGETHER missing from bands — had no lesson-level gate naming them. Resolved: both are pinned by name, the misquoted colon of Psalms 39:4 explicitly, and the DR-0331 bright line between Darrell's words and a stranger's is carried in code for the first time on a third-party source.

## Decision

1. **ll257 `ll257-ten-christmases-left-counting-our-days-the-way-yahweh-counts-them`**, added 2026-10-21, the last module in the series, with all four bands and eight movements in order in all five places a reader reads: the LORD of the count (Psalms 90:12; Psalms 39:4-6; Job 14:5; Matthew 24:36), the brevity he learned in a war and the Word said first (James 4:13-15; Psalms 103:15-16; Isaiah 40:6-8; 1 Peter 1:24-25), the man who counted years he did not have (Luke 12:15-21; Ecclesiastes 7:2), "I don't deserve this" grounded in grace and in Who gives the power to get wealth (Ephesians 2:8-9; Titus 3:5; Deuteronomy 8:17-18; 1 Corinthians 4:7; James 1:17; Psalms 103:2; Proverbs 10:22), the charge laid on a man with much (1 Timothy 6:6-19; Matthew 6:19-21; Proverbs 11:28; Proverbs 23:4-5; Ecclesiastes 5:10; Mark 8:36), a way to get rich set beside The Way (John 14:6; Proverbs 14:12; Jeremiah 6:16; Proverbs 19:21; Proverbs 3:5-6; Proverbs 14:15; Acts 17:11), the knee and the tongue with certainty distinguished from confession (Philippians 2:10-11; Isaiah 45:23; Romans 14:11; James 2:19; Romans 10:9-10; John 1:29; John 3:16; Hebrews 1:8; Colossians 1:16-17; 2 Corinthians 6:2), and what a man actually does with ten Christmases (Psalms 90:9-12; Ephesians 5:16; Hebrews 3:13; 1 John 5:11-13; Psalms 139:16).
2. **A generator filled the quotations and derived `anchor.ref` from the same tokens.** Every quotation was written as a `[[Book c:v|span]]` token and expanded from the corpus on disk; the generator refuses a span the corpus does not carry, refuses a declared spoken span that IS in the corpus, and computes `anchor.ref` from the identical token set. So a quotation and its anchor cannot disagree in either direction (DR-0734), and the Psalms 39:4 class of fault is structurally unavailable rather than merely watched for. The generator is a one-off authoring tool and was not committed; the durable verification is the pinned gate, which re-derives both directions from the committed module.
3. **A stranger's words are not cleaned.** DR-0331's licence covers Darrell's own words and is explicitly narrower than it looks; it does not reach a third party. So the 17 spoken spans are quoted exactly as transcribed, **straight apostrophes and all**, while our prose and every Scripture quotation carry the corpus U+2019. Both are asserted by test, because tidying either direction would be a lie about a source. The mis-heard spans are marked as uncertain transcription rather than repaired, and where a garble hides something load-bearing the lesson says the thing is not recoverable instead of inventing it.
4. **The advertising is named as advertising and used for nothing**, and is pinned absent from all four age bands — the call to action and the plugged company name both. A test mutation proves it: planting the call to action in the child band names `child`.
5. **The claims are attributed, never asserted, and the Word is taught rather than debated.** The lesson takes no position on his market advice and says so in those words; it neither endorses nor attacks it, because the Word is not in competition with a business opinion (DR-0098). It pronounces no verdict on the man's own standing, which the facilitator notes say is not ours to give; the distinction it does teach is the Word's own, James 2:19 set beside Romans 10:9-10.
6. **`talk-together-baseline.json` is deliberately not raised in this PR.** It is a shared ratchet three concurrent lanes could touch tonight, and a stale floor is safe while a conflicted ratchet is not — seven number collisions happened in the eight hours before this build. The live numbers are recorded above so the next lane raising it has the measurement. `re-review: 2026-10-28`.
