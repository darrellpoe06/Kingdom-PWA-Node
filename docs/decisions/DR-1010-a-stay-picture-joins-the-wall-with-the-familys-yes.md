## Context

The family's pictures of a door are records or advertising (DR-0906), filed by the family and its workers; a stranger sees only the listing kind. A tenant can file a picture to a work order and a paper to their tenancy; a guest can report a problem from inside the door with no account. Nothing let a tenant or a guest hand the family a picture of life at 805, and nothing let the family show one to the world with the right to do so on the record. The task asks for exactly that, with the rights assignment signed the way the house already signs (DR-0936: a typed name, an attestation, the exact words by version and fingerprint, e-sign consent, the device clock and the server's instant), the family's yes before anything is shown, the poster's ask honored, and every move on the clock.

## What was measured

**Reality trace (DR-0061), stated before building:**
- **Real data.** None existed. `property_photos` (0153) has no poster, no assignment and no moderation; its kinds are the family's nine. `request_documentation` holds work-order pictures. `property_document_signatures` (0263) signs a document the family filed, by the SHA-256 of its bytes, and a tenant may sign only as the tenant of a tenancy — a guest with no account cannot sign anything today. `door_guest_links` (0261) is a key per door, read by anon through `guest_report_door`, written through `guest_report_problem` with a 5-an-hour / 20-a-day rate limit. `door_role_of_user` (0266) answers family / tenant / household / manager / worker / NULL; `door_stays.requested_by` names a guest with an account. `door_events` (0262, 0279) is the clock, its subject an allow-list that is added to, never rewritten.
- **The live system.** The guest card page is `/properties/app/?report=<key>`; the tenant's face is `TENANT_TABS` and its door id is `door_of_my_tenancy`; the family's door panels switch on one `activeTab` for every face; both shelves (the door's and the in-app Properties tab) map `public_vacancies()` rows to `VacancyCard`; `public_vacancy_photos` (0282) returns listing pictures only and the share page reads it.
- **The surface the user uses.** The shelf (Darrell's screenshot of 2026-10-13: Apt 2 and the bed, each a card). A wall "per property" means per building, not per card: the family's doors share a street address and the rentals row carries it.
- **Assumptions written down.** (1) The wording is data, versioned and fingerprinted in the database, so the signer's screen signs the same bytes the database holds (0263's shape). (2) The counsel rule gates ADVERTISING use, not the family's own community wall: the assignment is relied on where a picture is sold with. (3) The poster's ask is honored at once, on the wall and in the listing, and the family keeps the file; §4 of the wording says exactly that. (4) A picked picture becomes a real listing picture (a `property_photos` row, `kind='listing'`, `source='wall:<id>'`) rather than a second public road, so it orders, archives and travels (DR-1006) like every other listing picture; `public_vacancy_photos` keeps its shape. (5) A guest with no account signs through the card's key; the key + the id their own submission returned is their proof for a take-down.

## Impact

- Unresolved: the family has no community wall, no way to use a resident's or a guest's picture in a listing with the rights on the record, and the warmest pictures of 805 — the ones the people who live and stay there take — never reach the shelf.
- The call obligates: nothing is shown without the family's yes; a signature is refused for words other than the ones on file; nothing on the client writes a row; the poster's ask is honored at once; the counsel rule is kept and made the family's own switch; every move is on the clock.

## Decision

1. **The wording is a row** (`stay_wall_wordings`): one per version, never rewritten, fingerprinted by the database (a trigger sets SHA-256 of the exact text). v1 is seeded; `stay-wall.js` carries the same bytes and a test proves it. `stay_wall_wording(p_rental)` hands anyone the current version, its words, its fingerprint, and whether THIS door's family has counsel's attestation on it.
2. **Counsel attests per family** (`stay_wall_wording_attestations`, `stay_wall_wording_attest`: owner or admin). Until then the wording is a draft for that family: the community wall runs on it; `stay_wall_use_in_listing` refuses, naming the version. The form says the draft plainly to the signer.
3. **A submission is signed over at the moment it is made** (`stay_pictures`): the three attestations (CHECKed true), the typed name, `show_name`, the caption, the image and its thumbnail (data URLs, shrunk on the phone), the wording version + fingerprint, the attestation and consent sentences, `signed_on_device_at` and the server's `signed_at`. `stay_wall_submit` (signed in: family, tenant, household, or a guest with a confirmed stay on that door) and `stay_wall_submit_as_guest` (the card's key; 5 an hour, 20 a day per door) share one insert, `stay_wall_file`, which refuses in words: the three things, a two-letter name, a non-image, an oversized picture, a changed or incomplete wording. The family is told through `push_outbox`.
4. **The family decides** (`stay_wall_decide` approved / declined with a reason, `stay_wall_remove` at any time); the family reads every picture of its instance, a poster reads their own, nobody else reads any; the tables carry SELECT only.
5. **The poster's ask is honored at once** (`stay_wall_ask_removal`; `stay_wall_ask_removal_as_guest` with the key and the id): off the public wall and out of the listing (the copy archived) the moment they ask; the row stays, the family removes for good.
6. **Picked for the listing** (`stay_wall_use_in_listing`): approved, no ask pending, the wording attested for this family → a `property_photos` row of `kind='listing'`, the poster's name or "a guest / a resident / the family" as the author, `source='wall:<id>'` (0285's shape widened); off archives the copy; on again brings the same copy back (`property_photos_source_once`).
7. **The public wall is per building** (`public_stay_walls`): approved pictures with no ask and no removal, grouped by family + street address, at most 24 newest a wall, thumbnails only, named **"Life at 805"** when the family shows the street on any door of the building (DR-0977) and **"Life at this home"** when they hold it; returned only for a building with a listed door, with those doors' ids so a shelf hangs it under the right cards. `public_stay_wall_image` hands one picture's bytes while it is on the wall.
8. **The clock:** `door_events` subject `stay-picture` — submitted, status (approved / declined / removed), listing on / off, removal-asked — readable where the picture's row is.
9. **The surfaces.** The tenant's **Life here** tab and the guest card carry `StayWallMine` (the form, then what was shared with "Ask to take it down"); the family's **Life wall** tab carries the desk (pending first, approve / decline / remove, use in the listing, the counsel record, open larger, share one yourself); `LifeWall` hangs under both shelves for the buildings with a card there and opens a picture branded with the Poe Properties band and a QR to the listing (DR-0941).

Not built, and said: a signed-in guest with a confirmed stay may submit (the database allows it) but has no face in the app to do it from — the guest card is the road at 805; `re-review: 2026-10-27` with task #52's lock window, where a confirmed stay earns a screen. The share page (DR-1006) does not yet carry the wall; `re-review: 2026-10-27`.
