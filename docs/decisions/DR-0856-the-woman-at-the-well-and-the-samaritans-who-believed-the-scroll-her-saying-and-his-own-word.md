## What the research found (DR-0100, three tiers)

- **Established, and it answers his question plainly:** the Samaritan finds are not the earliest writing archaeology has found; the oldest writing anywhere is Mesopotamian and Egyptian, more than two thousand years older. The finds tied to Samaria are real: the Samaria ostraca (102 Hebrew pottery receipts for oil and wine, found 1910 on the royal hill of Samaria, first half of the eighth century BC; a 2020 Tel Aviv University study found two scribes wrote 31 of them); the Wadi Daliyeh papyri (Aramaic legal deeds from Samaria, about 375 to 335 BC, hidden by families fleeing Alexander); two Greek inscriptions on Delos by Israelites who offered to holy Argarizein, Mount Gerizim; and the Samaritan community today, about 840 people in the 2021 counts, in Holon and Kiryat Luza.
- **Dated with some debate, said so:** the Mount Gerizim sanctuary (Magen's excavations 1984 to 2006; first phase dated by him to the mid fifth century BC, some scholars later; destroyed about 110 BC by the coin evidence, where Josephus implies an earlier date); the Ketef Hinnom silver scrolls carrying the priestly blessing (Numbers 6:24-26), the oldest Scripture text yet found, about 600 BC by most scholars; the Samaritan Pentateuch, whose oldest copies are medieval though the text reaches back to the last centuries BC.
- **Not established, named narrowly:** the Mount Ebal lead object announced in 2023 as a curse tablet naming YHW from the 14th or 13th century BC; critics answer that it has no datable layer and no letters others can see.

Sources read: the Heritage Science paper and its published refutation; Tel Aviv University's 2020 Samaria ostraca study; the New Encyclopedia of Archaeological Excavations entry on Mount Gerizim; Reinhard Pummer on the Delos inscriptions; the Manchester and Cambridge Samaritan manuscript catalogues; the Israelite Samaritan population survey.

## The Word, first

Her saying and His own Word are both in every band, exactly as written: *"many of the Samaritans of that city believed on him for the saying of the woman"* (John 4:39) and *"many more believed because of his own word"* (John 4:41-42). Her testimony opened the door; His own Word is the Highest Authority. The scroll she held, the five books of Moses, gave her the hope (Deuteronomy 18:15-18; John 4:25), and Jesus said Moses *"wrote of me"* (John 5:46; 5:39; Luke 24:27, 24:44): the scroll is the measure, all of His scrolls. The Samaritans' record both ways (2 Kings 17; Ezra 4:3; John 4:22; Luke 10:33-37; Luke 17:16; Luke 9:55; Acts 1:8; Acts 8). He fulfilled the Word; the sacrifices could not take away sin and pointed to the Lamb (Matthew 5:17; Hebrews 10:1-14; Hosea 6:6; 1 Samuel 15:22; Galatians 3:24; John 1:29); His words that the sacrifices "did not matter" are rendered as what the Word says they could and could not do (DR-0331). The King (John 4:42; Revelation 19:16; Philippians 2:10-11). The spiritual law at the highest level (Romans 7:14, 8:2; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 31:33; Isaiah 2:3-4; Habakkuk 2:14; John 16:33; Matthew 6:10; Luke 22:42).
