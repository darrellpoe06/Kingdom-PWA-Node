## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-21, fed more spoken material across several turns (DR-0089 —
always captured): (a) a Robert Greene segment on **human stupidity** — his
definition: the inability to foresee the consequences of one's actions, an
over-certain "adolescent mentality" that grabs short-term satisfaction and
ignores horrific long-term cost, and the danger of demagogues' one-sentence
"simple solutions"; (b) **"all Knowledge Is Yahweh's first, and others are
getting a glimpse of the 4th-dimensional King and Creator when they have skills
and knowledge and understanding"**; (c) **"study to bring the knowledge into
submission to His Word... His children know His Voice and another they will not
follow"**; (d) **"still small voice, but actions speak louder than words
anyway... like our Father Yahweh and Jesus... Actions."** Directed to L49 (the
Greene source) and L50 (the Mind of Christ), "whenever it makes sense."

## The decision

**L49 (Greene, Word-first):**
- **Foresight vs. "stupidity"** — Greene's definition (attributed, DR-0190) is
  kept and answered by the Word, which drew the wise/foolish line at exactly
  foresight: "A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself" (Proverbs
  22:3); the fool "rageth, and is confident" (Proverbs 14:16); the adolescent
  short-term grab is Esau's sin (Hebrews 12:16); the mature mind has "put away
  childish things" (1 Corinthians 13:11), refuses to be "children, tossed to and
  fro" (Ephesians 4:14), and reads the END first — "the end thereof are the ways
  of death" (Proverbs 14:12); "whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap"
  (Galatians 6:7); "consider their latter end" (Deuteronomy 32:29); count the
  cost (Luke 14:28).
- **All knowledge is Yahweh's first** — a worldly master's real skill is a
  GLIMPSE of the King: "the LORD giveth wisdom" (Proverbs 2:6); in Christ "are
  hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge" (Colossians 2:3); "Every good
  gift... cometh down from the Father of lights" (James 1:17); "what hast thou
  that thou didst not receive?" (1 Corinthians 4:7). We honor the grain BECAUSE
  its Source is our Father, and send the glory home (grounds DR-0127).

**L50 (the Mind of Christ):**
- **Foresight** as a mark of the renewed mind (Proverbs 22:3; Luke 14:28;
  Deuteronomy 32:29; Galatians 6:7; the childish-things pair).
- **All knowledge is Yahweh's / knowledge into submission** — the renewed mind
  studies hard but makes every idea bow: "every high thing that exalteth itself
  against the knowledge of God" is cast down (2 Corinthians 10:5) — knowledge
  submits to the Word, never the reverse.
- **Knowing His voice** — "My sheep hear my voice... a stranger will they not
  follow" (John 10:27,5); "a still small voice" (1 Kings 19:12).
- **Proven in ACTIONS, like the Father and Jesus** — "Be ye therefore followers
  of God" (Ephesians 5:1); "all that Jesus began both to do and teach" (Acts
  1:1 — DO first); "My Father worketh hitherto, and I work" (John 5:17); actions
  speak louder because the God we imitate acts.
