## Context

Spoken after L295 and named a lesson. He asked how Yahweh makes a person willing and obedient, and what the process is. He asked what makes someone follow a leader with humility, trusting that the leader is closer to right than they are, even though the leader cannot see everything. He asked why a follower with no competence and no critical path has so much critique of a leader with real experience. He asked about the mouth a person cannot see, which keeps sliding what the leader said. And he asked what to do when someone calls evil good, keeps arguing, and never hears what was said: manage, stay, or separate.

## Decision

L296, `ll296-willing-and-obedient`, after L295.

- **He works the willing.** Philippians 2:13; John 6:44; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:26-27; Psalms 110:3; Isaiah 1:19. Willingness is Yahweh's work before it is ours, so a leader prays first.
- **The pattern is the Son.** Philippians 2:5-8; Hebrews 5:8.
- **Clothed with humility.** 1 Peter 5:5-6; Philippians 2:3; Romans 12:3; 2 Corinthians 10:12; Proverbs 3:34.
- **Faith under authority.** The centurion in Matthew 8:8-10; Luke 6:40; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:12-13. Exodus 18:24 shows how good counsel reaches a leader who cannot see everything.
- **Speaking against the leader.** Numbers 12:1-2 and 8; 1 Samuel 24:6; Matthew 7:3-5; Jude 1:8; Titus 3:1-2.
- **The mouth we cannot see.** James 3:2 and 5; Proverbs 10:19 and 17:28; Ecclesiastes 5:2; Matthew 12:36-37; Psalms 141:3. Adding a thought is not the same as sliding the leader's plain meaning.
- **When evil is called good.** Isaiah 5:20; Malachi 2:17; Proverbs 1:7 and 15:31-32.
- **Manage, stay, or step back.** Romans 12:10, with marriage and the care of one's own kept under the Word.

The practice is to say the instruction back before adding to it.

His words are rendered for meaning and never put in double quotes. There are two parables with invented people and eight exam questions.
