# DR-0343 — Notifications everywhere: the sender's credentials ride the record, a phone is found by its person, the icon carries the count, and a text says Seen

- **date:** 2026-09-09
- **status:** accepted
- **tier:** B (no schema; one dispatch-only workflow that installs two existing NAS-held keys onto the Pages project the same way the deploy already reads one; a sender audience rule; a service-worker badge; a pure receipt helper. No money, no public surface widened, no timer-driven class.)
- **decides:** why a phone that said "Notifications on" heard nothing, and the four things a notification must do from now on: reach the shade with the app closed, count on the launcher icon, carry the church's own glyph, and let a sender see when the reader saw it
- **pairs-with:** DR-0334 (Web Push + the honest live signal), DR-0336 (the VAPID pair has one source), DR-0310 (REPOINT-ARMED — the app reads the sovereign stack), DR-0107 / DR-0125 (prove the site, not the pipeline), DR-0108 (a "must-be-by-hand" is a premise to challenge), DR-0181 (1:1 messages), DR-0076, DR-0100, DR-0219, DR-0239
- **source:** Darrell, 2026-09-09, three phone screenshots and four lines: *"we need the notifications to show up in all the usual notification ways... like on the app and in the notification list"* — *"Also want to be able to see when and if someone is or saw your text like other messaging apps"* — *"remember users can also like sermons etc... comprehensive review of the process for the users to firm up all workflows"* — *"all processes where the user will have access to... testing for making sure each link and button works and does what we intend"* — *"did we go through to the end of each step of each process?"*

## What the screenshots said (the reality trace, DR-0219)

Three pictures. The app, signed in, on Church → Engagement → Message a member: **"NOTIFICATIONS ON — TURN OFF"**, and a thread from mrspoe06 reading "Hello", Sep 9, 8:57 PM, unread 1. The Android shade at 8:57: a Samsung Link notice, YouTube, Google weather, Gmail, a price drop, a screenshot, an Ari lane-steward line — **nothing from the church app**. The home screen: ConnectBot wearing a **1**; the church icon wearing nothing.

**SHOULD** (DR-0334 §the pipeline): a DM insert → `notifyNewMessage()` → `POST /api/push-send` → ledger row (`push_sends`) → audience read from `push_subscriptions` → RFC 8291 push → the service worker shows it in the shade.

**ARE**, traced end to end with the real database and the real Actions history, not the code's own comments:

1. **The sender had no database.** `/api/push-send` requires `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY` in the Pages project environment and answers `503 not-configured: supabase` without them. `push-vapid-keys.yml` (run 1 on 2026-09-08) installed exactly three names — `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_SUBJECT` — and nothing in the repository has ever installed the other two. The app is fire-and-forget by design, so the 503 was swallowed and every layer reported success. The hosted `push_sends` ledger is empty, which is the same fact seen from the other side.
2. **And the database it would have read is the wrong one.** `infra/nas-supabase/REPOINT-ARMED` (2026-08-19, DR-0310) means the app has read the sovereign NAS stack for three weeks. The hosted project's `push_subscriptions` is empty and its newest `direct_messages` row is 2026-07-27; tonight's "Hello" lives on the NAS. A sender pointed at the hosted project would have found no phones even with credentials.
3. **The audience was filed by the wrong key.** The sender read `push_subscriptions` filtered by the message's `instance_id`. A phone is filed under whichever instance was active when its owner tapped ON (the family space, the church door, a ministry); a message rides whichever space the two people share. Filtered by instance, a person's phone need not be found. The person is the authorization (the row's `user_id` is theirs; `users_can_dm` already gated the message).
4. **Nothing ever set the icon badge.** No `setAppBadge` anywhere — in the worker or the page.
5. **The status-bar glyph pointed at an SVG.** Android masks the `badge` to a monochrome raster; given the seal SVG it drew a generic bell.
6. **`read_at` was written and never shown.** The reader's device stamps it the moment a thread is on screen, and the sender can read it under the participant policy; no surface rendered it.

## The decision

1. **The sender's credentials ride the record, installed the Way the deploy already uses.** `.github/workflows/push-sender-credentials.yml` (dispatch-only) joins the tailnet, reads `ANON_KEY` and `SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` out of the NAS's own `/volume1/docker/supabase/.env` exactly as `deploy-cloudflare-pages.yml` reads the anon key and `nas-clock.yml` reads the service key, and installs `SUPABASE_URL` (the Funnel's `/sb` mount — the same upstream `functions/_lib/funnel-proxy.js` fronts, reached server-to-server), `SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY` and `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` onto the Pages project with wrangler. It refuses to run without `REPOINT-ARMED` (it installs sovereign values only; it never guesses), and it proves rather than reports: (1) the URL + key pair answers `200` on `push_subscriptions` from the runner **before** anything is installed; (2) Cloudflare lists all six sender names back; (3) it dispatches the deploy that binds the values, waits for it, and the **live** `/api/push-send` answers `400` to an empty body instead of `503` (DR-0125: the site, not the pipeline). No human paste, no key displayed (DR-0108, DR-0249).
2. **A message's audience is the person.** `audienceQuery()` in `push-send-policy.js` reads a `message` with a named recipient by `user_id` alone (still opted in, still not disabled); a `live` announcement stays the whole instance that opted in. The sender builds no instance filter of its own (source-pinned).
3. **The icon carries the count, from both sides.** The service worker recounts its own notifications after every show and every tap and sets or clears the app badge (the Badging API is available in the worker scope); the page's `dm-notify` sets the badge to the unread count on every change and clears it at zero or sign-out. A launcher without badges is a no-op, never a fault.
4. **The status-bar glyph is the church's own.** `app/public/badge-96.png` — a white cross on transparency, generated in-repo by a pure-Node PNG writer, 96×96 — replaces the SVG as the worker's `badge`.
5. **A text says Seen.** `receiptLabels()` (pure, `direct-messages.js`) renders one receipt under the **last** of my messages, the way the messengers people know do it: `✓✓ Seen <time>` once the reader opened it (a real `read_at`), `✓ Delivered` while it waits on the server; never a receipt under theirs. It rides the thread's three existing refresh triggers (stream, 15-second heartbeat, visibility), so Seen appears without a tap.
6. **Every control on a member surface acts — a standing gate.** `member-surfaces-every-control-acts.test.js` scans every `Church*.jsx` plus Pulpit, Engagement, DirectMessages, ReactionBar, MyProfile, ProfileCard, PushNotifications, LiveWorshipBar, ServiceProgram, Choir and BusMinistry: no `<button>` without a handler, no `href="#"`. Proven-to-catch on a synthetic dead button and dead link. The behavioural half — a real Chromium tapping every button on every church tab — is the journey walk in the session note, re-run per review, not per push.

## The honest limits (DR-0100 §2 — narrow, named)

- **The last step is a phone.** Every layer up to the push service is proven; the push service delivering to a real Android shade is proven only when Christina or Darrell sends a message after this merges and the workflow has run. DR-0334's `re-review: 2026-09-13` stays open until that happens; this DR is what makes it *possible*.
- **The sandbox cannot sign in**, so the journey walk taps every control as a visitor: it proves nothing throws, every control acts, and every link goes somewhere. Signed-in behaviour (a like registering, a DM sending, a profile saving) is proven by the unit and render suites, not by the walk.
- **Presence ("is typing", "online now") is not built.** The sovereign realtime leg is the stack's one sick container (measured 2026-08-22); a typing indicator that rides it would lie by silence. Receipts ride the row instead. `re-review: 2026-09-30` — build presence when the realtime leg is proven healthy for a week.
