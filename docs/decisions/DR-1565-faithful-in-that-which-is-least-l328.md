## Decision

1. **Placement: Living Lessons.** A devotional teaching on faithfulness and
   trust for every house, not a project-method course (INTAKE-AND-FIT).
2. **Trust is a record, not a receipt** (Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21;
   Proverbs 20:6; 1 Timothy 3:10). **Found faithful in the open**
   (1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 8:20-21; 1 Samuel 12:3-4; Daniel 6:4).

   do the list and the weightier matters; Matthew 7:20; James 1:22), and the
   children furthest behind are not a statistic (Matthew 25:40; 18:14).
