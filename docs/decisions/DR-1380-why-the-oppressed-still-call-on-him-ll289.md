## Context

**The send.** A video by Garrison Hayes asking why Black Americans still identify as Christians at a high rate when the faith was a tool of slavery, oppression and colonial rule. Dr. Anthony B. Pinn asks how a loving God can sit beside the disproportionate suffering of one people and says many assume they are the favored community. Claudia Allen sees herself in Jesus, a brown, poor refugee killed by the state, and says African peoples knew the God of the Bible before any colonizer. The Black church is credited as the first free institution and faulted for stopping at race. Hayes keeps the faith for three reasons: context, liberation, human agency.
