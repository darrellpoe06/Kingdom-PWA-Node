## Why this exists

Darrell, 2026-09-17, spoken into this channel and marked lesson twice (rendered for meaning per DR-0331): see the Word through the eyes of the people nearest Jesus, so His will and His way and who He was are **accented** and we see Him clearer. **Peter in vivid detail** — the man who can say taste and see because he actually walked it, and the same man who said he did not know Him while the bird did what it did. **His brothers**, who did not believe until after He was gone. **Who is my mother, my sister, my brother.** The **local community** not showing Him love. The **people present at the death.** The **burial details.** The **resurrection witnesses including the five hundred.** The **history and archaeology** — when it was pulled out of the ground, what did the people digging see — because he wants His existence inside time to be visible. And **a claim of his to check carefully:** that there is more data about Jesus than about Julius Caesar, while people accept Caesar.

## The decision

**a. Peter is given in both halves, because either half alone is a man who never existed.** The one who says taste it for yourself (1 Peter 2:3, quoting Psalms 34:8) is the one who answered rightly when the room went quiet (Matthew 16:16), was told in advance to the hour and the sound how he would fail (Matthew 26:34), cursed and swore that he did not know Him (Matthew 26:74) — and was **being looked at while he said it** (Luke 22:61), wept bitterly (Luke 22:62; Mark 14:72), and was restored by name regardless (John 21:16). The accent Darrell asked for lands here: what you learn about Jesus, you learn from what He did with Peter afterward.

**b. The brothers' unbelief is stated the way the Word states it, in six words with no excuse** (John 7:5) — and what changed it is named: He came to James himself (1 Corinthians 15:7), who is in the prayer room afterward (Acts 1:14) and whom Paul, no flatterer, calls the Lord's brother (Galatians 1:19).

**c. "WHO IS MY MOTHER" KEEPS ITS SETTING.** The saying is quoted constantly without the detail that His own family stood outside asking for Him, which is what makes the redefinition generous rather than cold: the qualification is doing the will of Yahweh (Mark 3:33-35; Matthew 12:50), extended to people in that room with no blood relation to Him at all. And He was not disowning His mother — the same Man, dying, arranged her care from the cross (John 19:26-27). Both halves are checked per band.

**d. The town is given in His own three circles, widest to closest** — country, kin, house (Mark 6:3-4; Luke 4:24; John 4:44). Familiarity is not knowledge, and being unrecognised at home happened to Him first.

**e. The people at the death are named, which is not how legends are written** — a Roman officer whose profession was execution (Matthew 27:54), a crowd that went home changed (Luke 23:48), the women from Galilee (Luke 23:49), and His mother by name (John 19:25). And then what He did with His last minutes (John 19:26-27): dying, He was arranging housing for His mother.

**f. The burial is taught as the most checkable material in the account** — a named councillor going to the governor (Mark 15:43), Pilate verifying the death with the executing officer rather than taking anyone's word (Mark 15:44-45), fine linen and a rock tomb (Mark 15:46) never used before (Luke 23:53), about a hundred pound weight of spices (John 19:39), a garden (John 19:41), and the authorities themselves making the site tamper-evident with their reason on the record (Matthew 27:64-66).

**g. AND THE RIVAL EXPLANATION THE WORD ITSELF RECORDS IS THE HINGE OF THE WHOLE LESSON.** The money paid to the soldiers (Matthew 28:12), the line they were told to say (Matthew 28:13), and the concession that it worked — *"this saying is commonly reported among the Jews until this day."* (Matthew 28:15) A document inventing a resurrection does not write down the competing account, price it, and admit it spread in the very region where the document was published. Every band draws that conclusion in our own words, and the gate holds it.

**h. The witnesses are counted with the clause nobody quotes.** *"of whom the greater part remain unto this present"* (1 Corinthians 15:6) — go and ask them. And the man who refused it on report was **given** his evidence rather than scolded for asking (John 20:25,27-29), with the physical insistence kept (Luke 24:39,42-43), over forty days of many infallible proofs (Acts 1:3), from men who could not be silenced (Acts 4:20; 2 Peter 1:16; 1 John 1:1).

**i. THE WORD PULLS THE HISTORY OUT FIRST, centuries before any archaeologist.** Luke states his method and his purpose — certainty, not sentiment (Luke 1:2-4) — dates the events by five named office-holders at once (Luke 3:1-2), anchors the birth to an imperial census under a named governor of Syria (Luke 2:1-2), and the execution carried a public notice in the three working languages of the province which the governor refused to amend (John 19:19-22).

**j. THE MATERIAL THAT IS NOT SCRIPTURE IS SEPARATED AND SAID AT ITS OWN STRENGTH, disputes named.** Every band states that the section is not Scripture. Solid: Tacitus records the execution under Pilate in Tiberius' reign (Annals 15.44), generally accepted as genuine across the spectrum; Josephus calls James the brother of Jesus who was called Christ (Antiquities 20.200), which the large majority of scholars accept; the limestone block from Caesarea Maritima (June 1961) naming PONTIUS PILATUS as prefect of Judea — the only inscription naming him yet found, and the man Luke dates the ministry by; and the Yehohanan heel bone from Giv'at ha-Mivtar (1968) with an iron nail and olive wood still in it, the only unambiguous skeletal evidence of a Roman crucifixion ever recovered. **Said out loud rather than glossed:** Josephus' longer passage (Antiquities 18.3.3) is accepted as having an authentic core **with later Christian editing in it**, and the first published reconstruction of the Yehohanan body position was **corrected in 1985** by a re-examination — which is what working scholarship looks like, and is taught as such.

**k. AND THE DISPUTED ITEM IS NAMED, LABELLED AND REFUSED AS PROOF — the check Darrell asked for by name.** The ossuary inscribed James son of Joseph brother of Jesus: an Israel Antiquities Authority committee judged the inscription a forgery in 2003; a trial of more than five years ended in 2012 with the owner acquitted of forgery, and the judge said explicitly that the acquittal does **not** establish that the inscription is ancient. Every band says the forgery finding, says that the acquittal is not an authentication, and refuses the item — because a disputed artefact pressed into service as proof does more damage to the case it was meant to help than silence ever would. Four checks hold that per band.

**l. THE CAESAR CLAIM IS ANSWERED IN THE FORM THAT IS TRUE, WITH BOTH HALVES SAID.** The true half is stronger than most people realise: on the textual record there is no comparison — more than 5,800 catalogued Greek manuscripts of the New Testament, with tens of thousands more in other languages and fragments within a couple of centuries of the writing, against a few hundred manuscripts of Caesar's own Gallic War whose earliest are from about the ninth century, roughly nine hundred years after he lived. The half that has to be said with it: **Caesar has categories of evidence Jesus does not** — coins struck with his portrait and titles while he was alive (he was the first living Roman granted that), his own surviving writings, contemporary inscriptions, portrait sculpture and letters from men who knew him. A carpenter's son executed in a minor province leaves no coinage. So the sentence that survives a hostile question is not "more data of every kind"; it is that the **written** testimony to Jesus is earlier, wider and more abundant than for anyone else in the ancient world, while a Roman dictator naturally leaves state evidence a provincial never could. Under-claiming a verified truth and over-claiming an unverified one are both failures of truth (DR-0076 with DR-0100), so both halves are in every band and both are gated.

**m. All four bands carry the whole message.** Measured (adult prose 2,020 words, quotations stripped): child 1,255 (ratio 0.621, floor 0.50), youth 1,680 (0.832), teen 2,116 (1.048), senior 2,683 (1.328). Reading ladder 1.30 / 5.97 / 7.90 / 9.93 — monotone, child far under the 5.0 ceiling a new lesson is held to. Yahweh named 4-6 times per text.

## Verification (DR-0076)

- **133 verses fetched VERBATIM from this repo's own KJV corpus before a word was authored**, and the finished module's **335 quoted spans are 335 verbatim under the STRICT comparison, 335 referenced, 0 unreferenced, 0 ellipses.**
- **THE NON-SCRIPTURE CLAIMS WERE VERIFIED AGAINST LIVE SOURCES, not asserted from training data**, which is the first time this series has needed to: the Pilate stone's 1961 discovery at Caesarea and its wording; the Yehohanan heel bone, its 11.5 cm nail, the olive wood, its status as the only such find, and the 1985 Zias/Sekeles correction of Tzaferis' first reading; the James ossuary's 2003 IAA forgery finding, the 2012 acquittal, and the explicit judicial statement that the acquittal does not establish authenticity; Tacitus 15.44's general acceptance; Josephus 20.200's majority acceptance and 18.3.3's partial-interpolation consensus; the 5,800+ Greek manuscript count against the Gallic War's few hundred with a ninth-century earliest; and Caesar's lifetime portrait denarii of January and February–March 44 BC. One intended source (a Gospel Coalition comparison article) was blocked by the network egress proxy and simply was not used.
- **A DEFECT IN A CHECK, caught by the break harness rather than by a reviewer.** The check that each band says what He did for His mother matched `home` anywhere in the band — and every band contains "home town" three sections away, so a band that had stopped saying He housed her stayed green. It is now windowed at John 19:27. The same shape appeared a second time in the Caesar section: `both` was satisfied by "both halves of him" in the Peter section, so the concession check survived a band that had dropped the concession outright; it is now windowed on the Caesar mention, which occurs once per band. **Sixth and seventh faces of this pass's recurring finding: check the property, in the place it is supposed to be doing its job.**
- **AND ONE OF THE 95 WAS A BAD BREAK RATHER THAN A MISS, which is worth recording because the distinction matters.** Stripping the child band's capitalised title line left the very next sentence still naming the lesson in its own words — which IS the property `title-in-narrative` measures, so the gate was right to stay green. The break was rewritten to remove the naming itself, and then it was caught. A harness that cannot tell a bad break from a missed break would quietly weaken every gate it touches.
- **A COLON BEFORE A QUOTATION CORRUPTED ONE VERSE, and the strict audit caught it in the same minute.** Applying the DR-0462 colon-to-full-stop fix here, a blanket replacement crossed a string-literal join that fell **inside** a quotation of Mark 15:44, turning *"already dead: and calling..."* into *"already dead. and calling..."*. The pre-insertion audit reported exactly one non-verbatim span, the colon was restored, and the audit came back clean. That is the audit earning its place: a fix for one instrument defect produced a different one, and the gate did not care whose fault it was.
- **32 checks; 95 breaks applied for real, 95 caught, 0 missed, 0 no-ops** (93 on the first pass, plus the two replacements after the fixes above). Every break global inside the L170 block only, each asserting the edit landed before the gate ran.
- The three shrink-only baselines each changed by exactly one line (the count); no debt entry added. Painted META count 168 → 169; crosslist pin 27 / 508 → 27 / 509 with the reason written in beside it.
