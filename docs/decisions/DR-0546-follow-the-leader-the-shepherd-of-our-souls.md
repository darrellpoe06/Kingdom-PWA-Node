## The audit he asked for

He asked for it explicitly — *"make sure that what I'm saying is sound based on only the word"* — so every claim was checked before a word was written.

**The strongest finding: the money claim is not an inference, it is a recorded sequence.** Mark 11:15 the tables overturned → 11:17 *"a den of thieves"* → **11:18 *"And the scribes and chief priests heard it, and sought how they might destroy him."*** Consecutive verses. Their own council supplies the motive in their own mouths, *"take away both our place and nation"* (John 11:48), and the price is itemised at thirty pieces (Matthew 26:15). That sequence is asserted in the test file so no later edit can soften it.

**Three places where his phrasing and the Word's differ, taught as BOTH rather than flattened:**

1. **"Love is the principal thing."** Proverbs 4:7 gives that exact title to **Wisdom**; 1 Corinthians 13:13 gives **Love** the rank, after 13:1-3 sets the stakes. His substance is right and Love is senior (DR-0530), so the lesson carries both sentences rather than putting Proverbs' words on the wrong noun.
2. **The footstool.** Psalm 110:1 is spoken **to the Son**. What is handed to the believer is the treading and the bruising — Luke 10:19, Romans 16:20. Flattening it builds a triumphalism the Word does not support, so the lesson says: the footstool is His throne room, the treading is your Tuesday.
3. **"Lead us not into temptation but He's testing us."** The Word holds those apart: *"neither tempteth he any man"* (James 1:13), and yet *"to humble thee, and to prove thee"* (Deuteronomy 8:2). **His instinct is confirmed by the chapter itself** — the stated purpose of the proving is *"to do thee good at thy latter end"* (Deuteronomy 8:16), **two verses before** *"power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant"* (Deuteronomy 8:18). His two claims sit in one chapter, in that order.

One small correction inside the temple scene: Luke 2:46 reads *"after three days they found him"* — that is the search duration — and it was the **temple**, not a synagogue. Everything else in the scene is as he told it.
