## Context

The forwarded piece was a vendor note inside a retail-industry newsletter, and it advanced three claims worth naming exactly: that innovation is usually defined by whichever technology is newest; that meaningful change does not begin with the technology but with understanding how customers actually shop; and — the sharpest line in it — that the best way to evaluate a technology may be to ask what would happen without it.

Darrell did not ask for a retail summary. He asked what **His Ways** say about the thing that note is circling, and how that governs what we build inside our own apps. So the material is studied and the Word rules it, which is the ordering DR-0098 requires: the Word is taught, man-agreement is not staged as a co-equal authority, and the reader is never handed a some-say / others-say fork to settle for himself.

**The handling that made this a lesson rather than a critique.** DR-0100's three tiers were applied claim by claim rather than as a posture. The sound observations are stated plainly as true, because under-claiming a verified truth is as much a failure of truth as inventing one. The bent principle is corrected by the Word while the true observation underneath it stays untouched. And the boundary is held: the note is named for what it is — an advertisement, with a good observation placed in front of a download — because that is printed on its own page, while no company, product, publication or person is named anywhere in the lesson and no verdict is passed on anybody's heart (1 Samuel 16:7, quoted in the lesson itself). That boundary is a gate in the test, not an intention.

**Placement.** The teaching is organizational — it is about how a build is ordered, what research is for, and what a measure owes the person reading it — and it sits squarely beside the Project Management course's own frame of counting the cost and knowing the state of the flock. It is nevertheless shipped in **Living Lessons**, as `ll210`, for one reason: the `ll` namespace, the four-band shape and the dates file it joins are the Living Lessons artifacts, and the Project Management course is a twelve-module `pm*` sequence this lesson does not belong inside. Its Project Management affinity is real and is named here so the cross-link is not lost.

## Impact

Living Lessons gains **L210**, `ll210-what-really-drives-innovation-know-the-flock-and-build-what-edifies`, added 2026-10-06, the 209th module in the course and its newest.

Ten movements, Word first and in order. The subtraction test the note proposes, re-grounded in the standard Paul actually keeps — expedient and edifying, not lawful (1 Corinthians 10:23; 1 Corinthians 14:26). Knowing the people as a command older than commerce, with the Word's own method for it and the order of the work (Proverbs 27:23-24; Proverbs 20:5; Proverbs 18:13; Proverbs 24:27; Luke 14:28-30). The correction: Yahweh is first and the Resources work in sequence, so behaviour data is **Knowledge**, which fills chambers and frames nothing, and a standard taken from appetite ends in Judges (Psalms 127:1; Proverbs 16:3; Proverbs 24:3-4; Judges 17:6; 2 Timothy 4:3). The hinge — the same knowledge will feed a flock or shear it (John 21:16; Ezekiel 34:2-3; 1 Peter 5:2-3). Friction under its older names, a burden and a stumblingblock, against a yoke that is fitted and light (Matthew 23:4; Matthew 11:28-30; Leviticus 19:14; Romans 14:13). Novelty as no measure of worth, and the newness that is genuinely His (Ecclesiastes 1:9-10; Acts 17:21; Isaiah 43:19; Lamentations 3:22-23; 2 Corinthians 5:17). Every price, fee and total read as a weight on a scale He regulated first (Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 20:10; Deuteronomy 25:13-15; Leviticus 19:36). The note named for what it is, with the boundary held (2 Peter 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; 1 Samuel 16:7). The ranking that settles it, Knowledge puffing up while Love edifies, with greatness redefined as service and the least of these at the centre (1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Corinthians 13:1-3; Philippians 2:3-4; Mark 10:43-45; Matthew 25:40). And what all of it binds inside our own apps (Colossians 3:23-24; 1 Corinthians 4:2; Luke 16:10; Proverbs 22:29; Proverbs 21:5; Galatians 6:9; Matthew 7:24-25; James 1:5; Proverbs 3:5-6). The lesson and every band close with the Lamb (John 1:29), the three talk-together directions, and the confession.

**What the lesson affirmed and what the Word corrected**, said plainly because that distinction is the lesson:

- **Affirmed.** The subtraction question is sound and Scripture asks a stricter version of it. Behaviour must genuinely be known before anything is built for people, and that is a command with a method attached, not a marketing discovery. Friction is real and worth hunting, and the Word is harsher about it than the trade is. The note's own admission that innovation usually just means newest is honest.
- **Corrected.** *Start with the shopper* as a first principle: the Word puts Yahweh first and ranks Knowledge below Wisdom and Understanding, so research is a second step; when measured preference becomes the standard, the numbers point at the itch and the builder follows. *Innovation as a goal*: newness never measured worth, and the real article belongs to Him. *Insight-led as the top of the ladder*: insight is Knowledge, and Knowledge puffs up while Love edifies, so the ladder is one rung short. And the unstated assumption that behaviour knowledge is a crop: it is a trust, and Ezekiel 34 shows the identical knowledge used to shear a flock that was very well understood.

The lesson also states in its own text what the Word has **not** said: it names no feature, no vendor and no roadmap, those being wisdom decisions, and it refuses the opposite error of reading itself as permission to stop learning about the people we serve. Both refusals are pinned in the test.

## Decision

- Ship L210 into Living Lessons as the newest module, with all four age bands authored at full coverage, the talk-together close in the lesson and in each band in the lesson's own words, and `anchor.ref` naming every verse the lesson stands on so Search it out derives its links rather than being told them.
- **Place it in Living Lessons rather than the Project Management course**, because the `ll` id, the four-band shape and the dates file are Living Lessons artifacts and the `pm*` course is a closed twelve-module sequence; the Project Management affinity is recorded above rather than lost.
- **The no-company, no-heart boundary ships as a gate, not a note.** `living-lessons-l210-verses.test.js` fails the build on a link, a company suffix, a call to the vendor asset, or lifted newsletter furniture anywhere in the module, and the lesson's own boundary sentences are pinned so removing them fails too.
- **Both halves of DR-0100 are pinned.** The affirmation sentences and the correction sentence are each asserted, and a some-say / others-argue / both-sides / you-decide construction anywhere in the module fails the build. A lesson that only corrected would be as dishonest as one that only agreed.
- Teach the claims, never the company. Where the Word is reticent, stay with what is written and stop.
