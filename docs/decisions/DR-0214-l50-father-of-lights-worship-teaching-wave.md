## The decision

Wove the whole stream into **L50 (the Mind of Christ)** as new benefits and a
long culminating lesson passage in the "sent into the world / proven in works"
movement, all Word-first:

- **The Father of Lights** shines through the Son AND His children (James 1:17;
  1 John 1:5; John 8:12; 1:4,12; Matthew 5:14,16; Ephesians 5:8; Philippians
  2:15) — light shines through GOOD WORKS.
- **Light in darkness / the unseen realm** — established science stated honestly
  (DR-0100/DR-0190: the eye sees only a narrow visible band, "a tiny fraction of
  one percent" of the EM spectrum — attributed to scientists, no bare
  percentage) as a WITNESS to the unseen (Hebrews 11:3; Colossians 1:16; 2
  Corinthians 4:18); His light reaches the dark (John 1:5; Psalms 139:12; 104:2;
  1 Timothy 6:16; 2 Kings 6:17; Psalms 36:9). **Light is DATA** — science reads a
  star's composition from its light; Scripture joins light to knowledge (Psalms
  119:130; 119:105).
- **He gives His all — and so does the Son** (John 3:16; Romans 8:32; 1 John
  4:8; John 9:25; 2 Corinthians 4:6; 1 John 3:1; John 10:11; 15:13; Galatians
  2:20; Revelation 1:5).
- **He cannot die or fail, and sustains all things seen and unseen** (1 Timothy
  6:16; 1:17; Psalms 90:2; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6; Lamentations 3:22; Isaiah
  40:28; Hebrews 13:5; 1:3; Colossians 1:17; Acts 17:28) — "Amazing Yahweh."
- **The enemy is on Yahweh's leash and turned to growth** (Job 1:10,12; 2:6;
  Luke 22:31; Romans 8:28; James 1:3; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Genesis 50:20;
  cross-refs L43/L47; adversary lowercase per Typographic Theology, KJV "Satan"
  left only inside verbatim quotes).
- **Spirit / Light / 4th-dimension / Godhead — same Yahweh, four true angles**
  (the differences named: nature=Spirit John 4:24; glory=Light 1 John 1:5;
  transcendence=the 4D FRAME, named as our frame not a Bible word, Romans 1:20 /
  1 Timothy 6:16; tri-unity=Godhead Colossians 2:9).
- **He gives sight and knowledge and sends His children to do His works** — the
  blind receive sight, the lame walk, in His name (Psalms 146:8; Ephesians 1:18;
  Daniel 2:21; John 14:12; Matthew 11:5; Acts 3:6; Matthew 10:8) — because
  "Yahweh is GOOD" (Psalms 34:8; 145:9); and **eagles' wings over weary striving**
  (Isaiah 40:31).
