> "Game is read by some cultures as a person going through the game of life taking all the abuse and still having Joy... all a part of the game of Life... still Love Yahweh... so game can mean a man who tells you all the truth from the beginning and it sounds like a lie because you meant it from the first time you saw her... because she loves Him more than she love you... now you tell her the truth and it sounds like game to her so you start to prove your love... like never before!!!!!"

Then, in pieces: *"It was all a game until Him and the her!!!"* · *"Then her!!!"* · *"Without Him I would have never seen her!!!! Thanks!!!"* · *"Grateful and thankful eternally!!!"* · *"Got my family and kids from His Ways!!!"* · *"Money can't buy this... ever."* · *"He gave me the game in Proverbs and psalms!!!"* · *"Be true!!!"* · *"Prudence etc...."* · *"When you hear information like it's game you take the good with the bad information in stride... then pray to Yahweh to help."* · *"HE told me about me and her!"* · *"It's game until you believe Him then He's Him!!!"* · and the summary: *"know how to play the game ask Yahweh and read His Word!!!!"*

## Why this is a correction, and not only an addition

**L146 flattened the word.** It treated *game* as purely pejorative — talk a man cannot back — and said so: *"Game is the word for the other thing."* That reading is honest, but it is one of four, and building a lesson on a single reading of a word that carries several is the exact failure this new lesson is about. L149 does not retract L146's use; it supplies the other three and names the general discipline.

## The decision: four readings, a method, and an order

1. **READING ONE — talk a man cannot back.** The gap between a sentence and a receipt. Honest, and why the word is usually an insult.
2. **READING TWO — the game of LIFE.** Having game means you took the whole beating and came through still joyful, still generous, still loving Yahweh. A report on endurance, not on a man's mouth. *"count it all joy"*; *"the joy of the LORD is your strength"*; and Habakkuk with nothing left saying *"Yet I will rejoice in the LORD."*
3. **READING THREE — truth told too early and too whole**, so its own completeness is what makes it sound manufactured.
4. **READING FOUR — WISDOM, and Yahweh wrote the book of it.** Proverbs states its own purpose on page one: *"To give subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion."* A Father sitting a son down and giving him the game, in writing, so he does not have to learn every single thing by being hurt by it. And it names **prudence** — *"I wisdom dwell with prudence"*; *"the prudent man looketh well to his going"* — which is street sense, in the Word, put there by Yahweh. **This reading redeems the word**, and it is Darrell's own testimony: He gave me the game in Proverbs and Psalms.

**THE DISCIPLINE.** Misreading is named by the Word, not by us: *"He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him"* — folly and shame, a **competence** failure before it is a love failure, which is why it can be learned. And *"Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment."* Paul made the study a stated **policy** — *"I am made all things to all men"* — and at Athens read the city, opened from their own altar and quoted their own poets. It bought him a hearing, not a softer gospel; he preached the resurrection and was mocked.

**THE SCENE.** Saul tells the truth in Jerusalem and *"they were all afraid of him, and believed not that he was a disciple."* Taught with **both sides reasonable**: the suspicion was earned by his own past, not by their small hearts. Barnabas supplies not sincerity but **evidence**, and stakes his name. Then time: *"he which persecuted us in times past now preacheth the faith which once he destroyed."* They kept the memory and added the change.

**THE HEART — TOLD IN ADVANCE.** He knew from the first time he saw her because **he was told**: *"The secret of the LORD is with them that fear him"*; *"I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go."* A man told something by Yahweh speaks with a certainty that has **no visible basis** — and certainty with no visible basis *is* the definition of a line. **The very thing that made him sure is the thing that made him sound false.** That is not a wording problem; it is the ordinary cost of being told in advance, and it is why proving is the only road out — as the Son Himself offered: *"or else believe me for the very works' sake."*

**THE ORDER — HIM, THEN HER.** *"all these things shall be added unto you."* She is **added**, never the foundation; reversed, she becomes an idol carrying a weight no person was built to carry. And she was worth wanting for the stated reason: *"a woman that feareth the LORD, she shall be praised"* — her loving Him more is the guarantee, not the threat. The family and children came from His Ways: *"children are an heritage of the LORD."* And money reaches none of it: *"if a man would give all the substance of his house for love, it would utterly be contemned."*

**THE METHOD for information that arrives like game** — mixed, some true, some not, all confident: *"Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."* Not swallow all; not reject all. And when the sorting is beyond you, *"let him ask of God... and upbraideth not"* — He does not make you feel stupid for asking.

**THE LAST WORD.** It is game until you believe Him, and then He is simply Him. *Game* is a category assigned from the **outside**, to a man whose claim outruns your evidence; when the evidence arrives the category does not get revised, it evaporates, because it never described him — it described the distance between you. Samaria proves it: *"Now we believe, not because of thy saying: for we have heard him ourselves."* They did not believe the messenger better. They stopped needing her.

**AND THE WHOLE THING REDUCES TO ONE INSTRUCTION:** if you want to know how to play the game, **ask Yahweh and read His Word.** Both moves are free.
