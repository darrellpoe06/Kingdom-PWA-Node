## SHOULD / ARE (DR-0219)

**SHOULD.** A church owner or admin should be able to see, from the church door, how a person gets onto it, who is on it and at what standing, what each person may and may not do, and change any of that in the same place. DR-0221 already said "Inside the Love Corner App also"; DR-0242 already built the per-person checklist; DR-0065 says the app is where the flow runs.

**ARE, traced.** Governing the church instance lived in ONE place: the family **Admin** tab's "Role & stewards" panel (`AdminConsole.jsx`), reached through a space picker fed by `list_my_admin_instances`. That panel is gated `family/governor` — the family allowlist. A church owner or admin who is not family (a pastor added by invite, an admin on the 0014 allowlist who is not on the family list) had **no surface at all** on which to govern the door they administer. The join process itself was legible only by reading migration 0014: a leader on the allowlist is joined on sign-in; anyone else is joined only when an owner/admin has invited their email and the invite is still open when they sign in. Nothing on any screen said so, and no number said how many invites were open, how many people were on the door, or how many were waiting on a confirmation.

**GAPS.** (1) No governance surface on the door itself. (2) The gate was the family list, not the church standing. (3) The join process was undocumented on-screen. (4) A person's rights were shown as a role label and a checklist, never as the plain may / may-not a steward asks for. (5) No faces on the roster.

## The decision

1. **A Members tab on the church door**, `components/ChurchMembers.jsx`, mounted by the registry (`surfaces.js`, id `church-members`, sub `members`) and one render line in the shell. The nav entry shows for any **signed-in** person; the tab's content is decided by the database.
2. **The gate is the real standing.** `loadChurchGovernance()` (`lib/church-members.js`) asks `list_my_admin_instances` for a church space where I am owner or admin. With one: the roster (STRICT read, so an error says it is one — DR-0100), the grants, the open invites (read directly under `instance_invites_admin_read`), and the pending claims. Without one: a signed-in member sees **the way in** (the process, no numbers) and a plain line saying governing opens for the door's owners and admins. Signed out: a sign-in note. The client mirrors the server's guards exactly as Admin does (`grantableRoles`, `canEditCapabilities`); the RPCs remain the wall.
3. **The way in, as the server runs it, with live counts.** Five steps, each stated from migration 0014's `join_church_instance` and 0081's claim flow, each with the number it is measured by: owners + admins (leaders joined on sign-in), open invites (unaccepted, unexpired), people on the door, claims waiting on confirmation, members + viewers whose standing and rights can be adjusted. A count that cannot be read is `null` and not rendered — never a painted zero (DR-0076). This is the answer to *"how can we see and edit the onboarding process"*: the steps are seen here; the controls on the same tab are how it is edited.
4. **People by standing, top-down, with faces.** Owner → admin → member → assistant → successor → child → viewer (`ROLE_ORDER`), each person with their picture and chosen name (`useProfiles`, DR-0342), their standing as a control (`grantableRoles` — never an owner, never yourself, an admin never grants admin), and a **Rights** fold.
5. **Opportunities and constraints as MAY / MAY NOT.** `rightsFor(role, grants, userId)` states each standing's rights in the words `ROLE_LABELS` already agreed to (surface-says-truth, DR-0241), then lists every extra right from the catalog (`CAPABILITIES`) as *may (extra right)* when granted and *may not* when not — for members and viewers, the two standings the checklist applies to. The same extra rights are checkboxes in the fold for an owner/admin, calling `set_member_capability`; the boxes re-read the real grants after every change.
6. **Remove from the door, in the same fold.** Two taps, never an owner, only an owner removes an admin, never yourself (`canRemove`, the mirror of 0130).
7. **Invite from the door.** Email + standing → `invite_to_church` (member / viewer / admin; never owner); the open invites are listed with their expiry; a family-style claim waiting on confirmation has its Confirm button here too (`confirm_invite`).
