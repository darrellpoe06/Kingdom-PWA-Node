## Context

Darrell fed a **Robert Greene** conversation (mastery, the "life's task", focus,
apprenticeship/learn-by-doing, human nature, envy + "Mitfreude", loneliness vs embodied
connection, discernment against demagogues/groupthink, studying history, boldness), 2026-07-21.
Greene is a strong voice but **not a Christian teacher** — his best-known book ("The 48 Laws
of Power") is explicitly Machiavellian, and he counsels "channeling the dark side." Per
DR-0089/DR-0168 it is captured into Living Lessons, but this is a **mixed source**, so it is
handled by the established Word-first correction method (as L34 Kokotajlo, L37 crime/race, L40
lost-tribes, L41 apologetics): honor the true grain, ground it in Scripture, and firmly
correct the amoral core — never platform an ethic contrary to Christ.

## The decision

Added **L49 — "Mastery Without Manipulation — Your Life's Task, Envy, and the Sober Mind"**
(weeks 48 → 49; the `weeks === MODULES.length` gate). Six movements:

1. **Your life's task, learned by DOING** — gift received to minister (1 Peter 4:10; 2 Timothy
   1:6); doers not hearers (James 1:22).
2. **Mastery through focus** — "this one thing I do... press toward the mark" (Philippians
   3:13-14); hand to the plough, no looking back (Luke 9:62). Capstone (Darrell's directive,
   "seek ye first the Kingdom"): the FIRST focus that orders all others is "seek ye first the
   kingdom of God" (Matthew 6:33), and mentorship saves years — "in the multitude of
   counsellors there is safety" (Proverbs 11:14); "Faithful are the wounds of a friend"
   (Proverbs 27:6); pass it on (2 Timothy 2:2).
3. **THE HINGE — mastery WITHOUT manipulation** (the central correction): the Kingdom inverts
   the 48-Laws instinct — greatness is service (Matthew 20:26); love works no ill (Romans
   13:10); plain, un-gamed speech (Matthew 5:37); the deceitful heart is PUT OFF, not
   channeled (Jeremiah 17:9; Ephesians 4:22); boldness is righteous, not Machiavellian
   (Proverbs 28:1; 2 Timothy 1:7).
4. **Master envy** — Greene's "Mitfreude" IS "Rejoice with them that do rejoice" (Romans
   12:15); charity envieth not (1 Corinthians 13:4); envy the rottenness of the bones
   (Proverbs 14:30); admit it (Proverbs 28:13); emulate (Hebrews 6:12).
5. **Real connection over the lonely screen** — not good to be alone (Genesis 2:18); two
   better than one (Ecclesiastes 4:9-10); flee fornication/porn (1 Corinthians 6:18); don't
   forsake assembling (Hebrews 10:25).
6. **The sober mind in dangerous times** — follow not a multitude to do evil (Exodus 23:2);
   prove all things (1 Thessalonians 5:21); the prudent looketh (Proverbs 14:15); try the
   spirits / false prophets (1 John 4:1; Matthew 7:15); history = no new thing (Ecclesiastes
   1:9). Closes by redeeming Greene's coming "sublime": awe points to the Author (Psalms 19:1;
   Romans 1:20).
