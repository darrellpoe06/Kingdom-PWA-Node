- **Principles:** THE-APP-IS-THE-PRIMARY-ARTIFACT (DR-0065), REALITY-TRACE (DR-0061: observed on his phone), VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE (DR-0076: the voice road measured before a word was written; what is not measured is said), DR-0716 (nothing floats over the Word; the bottom bar is the one row), DR-0738 (the reader keeps its own trip), DR-0100 (established fact stated plainly: Android stops the phone's own voice off screen), DR-0075 (feel, flow, smoothness), HOLD-THE-HAND (DR-0621).
- **Grounds:** Darrell, 2026-10-01, two screenshots of L206 in the installed app on his phone, Midnight theme, the mini-bar on one line and Controls / A− / A+ / Feedback / Give / More on a second, a white strip above the page and a white strip below it: *"Why are we not able to continue to listen when inside the downloaded app... and the controls can't be further condensed to fit one level together instead of two at the bottom... also why have the white bar at the top and another one at the bottom so 3?!"*

## Context

**SHOULD.** DR-0716: the bottom bar is ONE row, and nothing floats over the Word. The reader's own chrome stays out of the way of the words. In the installed app the page owns the screen: the phone's status bar and navigation bar wear the page's color, not the browser's. And a reading that a person starts keeps going when they leave the app (DR-0633, "like a radio"), or the app says plainly which voice does and which does not (DR-0718).

## What was measured (SHOULD → ARE → GAPS, DR-0219)

**ARE, the bar (his screenshots).** Inside a lesson on a phone, `index.css` gave the reader's slot its own line (`order: -1; flex-basis: 100%`) the moment the mini-bar or the minimized pill was in the bar, and the slim row (Controls, A−, A+, Feedback, Give, More) kept the line below. Twelve 44 px buttons in two rows, 88 px of the reading screen. They do not fit one line: the Fold's cover screen is about 432 CSS px wide and twelve buttons need about 580.

**ARE, the bars (his screenshots).** The page was black (Midnight) and the strip above it and the strip below it were cream. An installed app's status bar and, on Android, its navigation bar take their color from `<meta name="theme-color">`. `index.html` ships it `#FAF8F4` for the first paint, the manifest says the same, and the only code that ever wrote that meta was the TLC public door. The theme store (`theme-css.js`) changed `data-theme` on the app's wrapper and nothing else, so the browser's own bars never followed.

**ARE, the listening (measured, not his screenshots).** The reader's background path is unchanged by this record and is stated here because he asked. `background-audio.js` holds a silent audio session so the page is not frozen off screen; what then keeps SPEAKING depends on the voice. A real audio clip (the System voice from the NAS, `/voice-lite`, or a saved lesson) keeps playing when the app leaves the screen. The phone's own Web Speech voice is stopped by Android when the app leaves the screen, in the installed app and in Chrome alike; `tts.js` re-speaks the held sentence when the app is seen again, and the panel says which voice will do which before Play (DR-0718). The NAS voice road was up when he wrote: `voice-lite-probe` run 36926132891 (21:04 UTC), outside-in through `https://poetech.us/voice-lite`, HTTP 200, 866 characters to 21.2 seconds of audio. Which voice was speaking on his phone at 3:56 PM is NOT measured: the reader's trip (DR-0738) is on his phone and nothing carried it off the phone. That is the gap this record closes for the next report.

**GAPS.** (1) Two rows where one is the rule. (2) The browser's bars never wore the theme. (3) A note about the reader arrived without the reader's own account of what happened.

## Impact

While the Word plays on a phone the bottom is one row of 44 px, the same as when it does not play; the comfort buttons are one tap away under More, and nothing is removed. On Midnight the status bar and the navigation bar go black with the page; on a light theme they match it; the app owns the whole screen. A feedback note sent within three hours of a reading closes with the reading's own line, so the next "it stopped" names the voice, the sentence and the dark screen, and the fix is aimed at a measurement.

## Decision

1. **One row while the reader is live** (`ChromeDock.jsx`). The bar reads from its own slot whether the mini-bar or the pill is in it (`useReaderLive`, a MutationObserver on the slot) and whether the screen is a phone (`usePhoneWidth`, below 640 px). When all three hold (in a lesson, on a phone, reader live), the slim row is not drawn inline: Controls, A−, A+, Feedback and Give ride inside More, as a wrapped row of the same square buttons, and the row is More then the reader. The bar carries `data-one-row` so the CSS follows it. Wider than a phone, everything stays inline as before; when the reading stops, the slim row returns.
2. **The theme paints the browser's bars** (`theme-css.js`). `syncThemeChrome(theme)` writes the theme's page color to `<meta name="theme-color">` (creating it on a door that has none) and to the document element's background, so the edge under the navigation bar and any overscroll are the page's color. It runs on every `setThemePref` and once when the theme hook mounts, so the first frame the app owns wears the chosen theme. The first-paint meta and the manifest stay cream, the first-run theme, so the splash and the first frame agree.
3. **The reader's trip rides with a feedback note** (`reader-trip.js`, `feedback-sync.js`, `FeedbackCenter.jsx`). `recentTripLine()` is the last trip's one-line summary when it started or ended within three hours, else nothing; the form hands it in as `readerTrip`, and `composeFeedbackBody` closes the body with it, marked `[reader]`. An old trip is not handed in, so a note about something else never carries a stale reading.
4. **The listening itself is unchanged and said plainly** (DR-0100): the phone's own voice stops off screen, the System voice and a saved lesson keep playing, and the NAS voice road measured up at the time he wrote. The next report carries the trip, and the fix after this one is aimed at it.

## Verification

- `floaters-live-in-the-bars.test.jsx` (26 tests): the slim row is inline before the Word plays; on a phone, once the mini-bar is in the bar, `data-one-row` is `true`, the slim row is not drawn, each of the five comfort buttons is inside More exactly once, More's name says what it holds, and the mini-bar is in the bar; inside More, A+ steps the text and Feedback calls the same action and closes the menu; when the reading stops the slim row returns inline; **proven to catch:** wider than a phone the row stays inline while the Word plays; the CSS that gave the mini-bar a second line is gone and More is never hidden while the Word plays.
- `the-bars-wear-the-theme.test.js` (8 tests): `themeColorFor` is each theme's page color, cream for an unknown key; `syncThemeChrome` writes an existing meta and the document element, creates the meta on a page without one, and writes nothing with no document; **proven to catch:** picking Midnight turns the meta black and picking Cream turns it cream again, which fails against the old store; an unknown key changes nothing; `index.html` and the manifest keep the first-paint cream.
- `the-readers-trip-rides-with-feedback.test.js` (5 tests): a trip that ended within the window is handed in as its summary, naming the phone's voice, the sentence and the dark screen; **proven to catch:** a trip older than the window is not handed in, and no trip is an empty string; an open trip counts from its start; `composeFeedbackBody` composes as before with no trip and closes every shape of note with `[reader]` and the line.
- `reader-radio-mini-bar.test.jsx`, `reader-floats-and-docks.test.jsx`, `tts-control-chrome-cap.test.jsx`, `reader-trip.test.js`: unchanged and green.
- Lint clean.
- **Live proof after deploy:** on his phone, Midnight, L206 playing: one row at the bottom, a black status bar and a black navigation bar; a note sent from the lesson reads `[reader] Last reading at …` on Projects → Feedback. `re-review: 2026-10-03` against those screenshots.

## Limits, stated

1. Whether Android colors the navigation bar from `theme-color` depends on the Chrome version on the phone; the status bar always follows it. The screenshot after deploy is the measurement; if the navigation bar stays white, the next step is the manifest's `display_override` and is a new record.
2. A−, A+, Controls, Feedback and Give are one tap further away while the Word plays on a phone (inside More). That is the condensation he asked for; the reader panel still carries text size and colors of its own (DR-0524).
3. The background listening is not changed here. The phone's own voice cannot be kept speaking off screen by the page; the System voice can, and the saved lesson can. If his phone was on the System voice and still stopped, the trip in his next note is the measurement that aims the fix.
