## Context

On 2026-09-30, after L201 (How Long Before It Came?, DR-0689) shipped, Darrell spoke back into the app, as a teaching. His words, verbatim, in the order he gave them:

1. *"How Long Before It Came? — David, the Prophets, and the Years from Each Promise to Jesus  Lesson.  How is David first and not Moses or even Noah?"*
2. *"Isaiah? Job? Etc?"*
3. *"You can create an new lesson with all of them from the beginning...."*

**His point.** The promise did not start with David. L201 opened with David because its question named David; the Word's line of promise runs from Eden. The first plan was to extend L201 with movements before David; his third message changed it: leave L201 as it is, apart from one line pointing onward, and build a NEW lesson with every promise from the beginning. This record is that lesson.

**Placement.** Living Lessons **L203**. L202 (Bishop Gwin, DR-0690, #1873) merged to `main` while this was built, so the next free number, derived from the data, was 203. DR-0691 was taken by the Capacity tab; this record is DR-0692, the `Next ID` on `origin/main` when written.

**Neighbours linked, not repeated.** L201 (the years from David and the prophets; Daniel's weeks), L200 (what the first hearers had in hand), L196 (the promises gathered), and the Who He Is course timeline (the whole Word by era).

## What was measured

- **The rule for "all of them", derived from the KJV New Testament, not hand-picked.** Every verse that joins *fulfilled* with *spoken / written / scripture / prophet / prophecy* (L200's rule, applied to the whole New Testament), or says *this is he / that which was spoken / of whom it is written*, or *as / thus it is written*: **69 verses.** Each is classified in the test: **34** name an Old Testament passage kept in Jesus or set on Him; **11** say the Scriptures were fulfilled in Him without naming one passage (Matthew 2:23, 26:24, 26:54, 26:56; Mark 14:21, 14:49; Luke 24:44, 24:46; Acts 3:18, 13:27, 13:29); **24** use the same words of another matter and are named in the lesson (Mark 7:6; Luke 2:23, 21:22; John 6:31, 17:12; Acts 1:16, 7:42; Romans 1:17, 2:24, 3:4, 3:10, 4:17, 8:36, 9:13, 10:15, 11:8, 15:9; 1 Corinthians 1:31, 2:9, 10:7; 2 Corinthians 4:13, 8:15, 9:9; James 2:23). ## Impact

Without this lesson, Darrell's question stands unanswered where he asked it, and a reader of L201 could take David for the start of the promise. With it, the reader sees the answer first, in plain words, then the whole line from Eden to Malachi: every promise the New Testament itself sets on Jesus, chosen by a rule anyone can re-run, grouped by era so it reads on a phone, with the years each promise waited counted from the Word's own numbers, and the undated left undated. The dated, era-grouped timeline renders under the lesson through the existing timeline block.

## Decision

L203, **The Whole Line of Promise — Every Promise from Eden to Malachi, and the Years to Jesus**, joins Living Lessons with eleven Word-first movements, four authored bands, a nine-question quiz (the first: why did L201 begin with David?), eleven facilitator talking points and a 64-entry timeline (7 era headings, 8 anchors, 49 promise rows):

1. **Why David came first, and why the line is older.** Luke 1:70; Acts 3:21; Luke 24:27; Hebrews 1:1-2.
2. **How we know which promises are His.** The derived rule and its counts; Haggai 2:7 and Daniel named.
3. **The years before the kings, from the Word's own numbers.** 1 Kings 6:1; Exodus 12:41; Galatians 3:17; Exodus 19:1; Genesis 12:4; 21:5; 25:26; 47:9; 47:28; 11:10, 26, 32; Acts 7:4; Genesis 5:3; 7:6.
4. **Before the flood.** Genesis 3:15; Galatians 4:4; Hebrews 2:14; Genesis 4:1; 5:3, 23-24; Jude 1:14.
5. **The patriarchs.** Genesis 9:26, 28; Luke 3:36; Genesis 12:3; 22:18; Galatians 3:8, 16; John 8:56; Genesis 49:10; Hebrews 7:14; Revelation 5:5.
6. **Moses and the law.** Exodus 12:46; John 19:36; Psalms 34:20; Numbers 24:17; Matthew 2:2; Revelation 22:16; Deuteronomy 18:15; Acts 3:22; John 5:46.
7. **Judges and kings.** 1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 1:69; 1 Samuel 7:2; Acts 13:21; 2 Samuel 23:2; 2 Samuel 7:12, 14; Hebrews 1:5, 13; Psalms 2:7; 16:10; 22:18; 41:9; 69:4, 9, 21; 110:1 with their keepings.
8. **The prophets before the exile.** 2 Kings 14:25; Jonah 1:17; Matthew 12:40; Amos 1:1; 9:11; Acts 15:16; Hosea 11:1; Micah 5:2; fourteen passages of Isaiah; Jeremiah 31:15.
9. **After the exile.** Zechariah 9:9; 11:13; 12:10; Matthew 27:9 (Matthew names Jeremy; the words stand in Zechariah 11:12-13; both given as the Word gives them); Malachi 3:1; 4:5; Matthew 11:14.
10. **The promises the Word does not date.** Job 19:25-26 with Ezekiel 14:14, James 5:11 and Galatians 3:13; Joel 2:28 with Acts 2:16; Psalms 45:6-7, 78:2, 102:25-27, 118:22.
11. **The whole line on one page.** The derived table, by era; Luke 24:44.

And the close: Galatians 4:4; Joshua 21:45; John 20:31.
