## Context

Week 42 of Sovereign A.I., built from a forwarded trade digest about who gets
educated, for how long, and at whose cost. Its subject story: as A.I. reshapes
the job market, three-year bachelor's degrees are gaining traction to reduce
student debt, and Christina Clark, president of La Roche University, calls the
approach misguided for truncating an education during what she calls a critical
period of brain development. Nine other items ran beside it — a Kentucky work
college, a closure, a surplus, a tiered permission, a disputed federal report,
four states and a fifth that changed what a sojourner pays to learn, a
retention argument, two access items, and the issue's closing quotation.

**ONE THING ABOUT THIS BUILD IS UNUSUAL AND IS SAID AT THE TOP OF THE PAGE
ITSELF, NOT ONLY HERE: we read the DIGEST and not one full article.** Not one.
Every figure and every position on the page is the digest's own summary of
somebody else's reporting, which is one remove further from the source than this
house normally stands. Nobody was telephoned, the California statute was not
read, the disputed federal report was not read, and no figure was reproduced.
A tidy page would have implied otherwise, so the page says it instead — in the
lesson, in the youth band, in the teen band and in the facilitator's first
talking point.

## Impact

**What the reader gets that they did not have.** A way to read a sector's
cost-cutting argument through the Word rather than through a camp. The spine:

- **Forming is bought with use over time, and that is a mechanism, not a
  sentiment.** *"But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even
  those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good
  and evil."* (Hebrews 5:14) Beside Luke 2:52's increase in four directions,
  Isaiah 28:10's line upon line, and Luke 6:40 — the disciple becomes like his
  master, which scheduling cannot compress.
- **The three-year degree is OBEYING Luke 14:28-30 and counting the wrong
  number.** A degree carries two costs, the money and the forming, and only one
  of them is printed. Cut the year and the money line improves where everyone
  can see it; the forming line moves too and no invoice records it.
- **THE COUNTER-WITNESS THE WORD ITSELF GIVES, WHICH THIS PAGE MAY NOT HIDE.**
  Scripture records a three-year curriculum by name — *"so nourishing them
  three years, that at the end thereof they might stand before the king."*
  (Daniel 1:5) — with a selected cohort (1:4) and an outcome ten times better
  than the realm's whole professional guild (1:20). So nobody leaves this page
  claiming Scripture forbids a short degree. And the text names WHY it worked,
  and the length is not among the reasons: a purposed heart before the first
  lecture (1:8) and knowledge GIVEN (1:17). Shortness is not sin; length is not
  virtue.
- **Work beside learning is apostolic, not experimental.** Berea's ten hours a
  week against Acts 18:3, 1 Thessalonians 2:9 and 2 Thessalonians 3:8-10 — and
  older still, Bezaleel's craft as a Spirit-given filling in **Wisdom,
  Understanding and Knowledge** (Exodus 31:3-5; 35:30-35) that arrived WITH a
  commission to teach it onward, so the skill would outlive the craftsman.
- **The thing everybody is actually fleeing is debt, and Yahweh reaches for a
  different lever.** Proverbs 22:7 names it as a change of standing;
  Deuteronomy 15:1-2 is a scheduled release with His own name on it. Not an
  amputated education.
- **Retention is feeding, not keeping** (Ezekiel 34:2-4; John 21:15-17;
  1 Peter 5:2) — a person can be fully retained and completely unfed.
- **The close is the issue's own closing quotation turned into the Word's
  test.** Robert E. Hall's three questions — was it actually new, was it right,
  did it matter — answered by Ecclesiastes 1:9, Proverbs 25:2 with
  1 Thessalonians 5:21, and 1 Corinthians 13:1, which holds Love senior to
  every capability a person can acquire.

**TWO PLACES WHERE GETTING IT WRONG WOULD DO REAL DAMAGE, BOTH HANDLED OUT
LOUD.**

1. **The surplus.** A 100.4 million dollar surplus reported in a year employee
   pay fell by 104 million dollars is the easiest wage-theft sermon available,
   and the page refuses to preach it. Yahweh's standard is stated without
   softening — *"At his day thou shalt give him his hire, neither shall the sun
   go down upon it"* (Deuteronomy 24:15), with Jeremiah 22:13 and James 5:4 —
   and then the limit is named: a surplus after reductions is a **datum, not a
   verdict**; a reduction in force is not a withheld wage; the verses address
   wages kept back. Proverbs 18:13, Proverbs 18:17 and Deuteronomy 19:15 are
   why one digest convicts nobody. Under-claiming the standard would have been
   as much a failure of truth as over-claiming the verdict (DR-0100).
2. **The stranger.** Four states ended in-state tuition for undocumented
   students and a fifth banned enrollment. The measured facts are stated
   plainly and the Word's standard is **taught, not staged** (DR-0098):
   *"But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born
   among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the
   land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God."* (Leviticus 19:34) with Exodus 22:21
   and Deuteronomy 10:18-19 — His character before our command. And the page
   refuses the party frame in either direction and names the Word's own
   reticence: Scripture is not a statute book for a modern immigration system,
   names no visa class, residency rule or tuition schedule, and we will not
   invent one and attach Yahweh's name to it. What it settles is the treatment
   of the sojourner already dwelling among His people, and any policy argument
   a believer makes has to survive that sentence. A test asserts no party name
   appears anywhere on the page.

## Decision

**1. Ship it as `sov42`, four bands, Word first.** Twelve numbered movements in
the adult lesson; eight benefits; eight quiz items; eleven facilitator talking
points, a ten-segment run sheet and eight discussion prompts.

**2. Every verse is filled from the corpus by a generator, never typed**
(`scripts/sov42-fill.mjs`). The generator is kept in the repository rather than
thrown away, because it is the evidence for the claim that nothing was typed
from memory, and it is the thing to re-run if the module ever needs rebuilding.

**3. The anchor is DERIVED from the quotations**, not written beside them, which
is how DR-0734 holds in both directions without depending on anybody's
attention.

**4. The splice refuses to guess.** `SOVEREIGN_AI_MODULES` is not closed by the
first `];` in its own file — measured, the first is at byte ~5,507 and the
array's close at ~2,341,412 — so the generator finds the array by its opening
line, takes the first `\n];` after it, and **throws** if the first `];` in the
file ever becomes the array close, if the array does not end in a
comma-terminated module literal, if the id is already present, if the week
count does not rise by exactly one, if the weeks on disk are not contiguous, or
if the new id appears more than once. Week numbers are counted by **ONE**
numeric regex, numerically sorted: two different regexes once reported a wrong
maximum and nearly placed a lesson on top of an existing one (DR-1114).

**5. The four-band ratchet moves by hand, 246 → 247,** with its dated note
naming sov42, because that number is meant to be seen moving.

**6. What was not checked is printed in the lesson itself**, not only here: no
full article read, nobody telephoned, no statute or report read, no figure
reproduced, the sponsor copy named as advertising and not taught, and an A.I.
agent named as the drafter with the gates — not its confidence — deciding
whether it ships.

### What I got wrong, caught by the gate rather than by my confidence

Two of my own assertions failed on their first run, and both are recorded
because a test that fails for the wrong reason is the easiest thing in the world
to "fix" by weakening it:

1. The counter-witness check required `three years` or `Daniel 1:5` in every
   band that weighs a claim, and the **senior band said "three-year
   curriculum"** and never the number. The gate was right and the band was thin:
   the senior band now reads Daniel 1:5 aloud and lets the room hear the number,
   which is better teaching than the assertion I nearly relaxed.
2. The two-sided correction was asserted against the teen band's words
   (`shortness is not sin and length is not virtue`) on the **adult** lesson,
   which says it in its own words (`a shorter programme is not a sin and a
   longer programme is not a virtue`). Fixed as two assertions, each against the
   place that actually says it — the shape of the earlier mistake in DR-1114,
   where a per-band check was asserted as names rather than records.

## What this does not do

It does not verify a single figure in the digest, and does not pretend to: the
numbers are attributed, the outlets are named, and the distance is printed on
the page. It does not tell anybody whether the California statute is good,
whether a particular closure was avoidable, whether any Harvard worker was
wronged, or whether the Office of Federal Student Aid will become an agency —
each of those is named in the OPEN tier instead. It does not resolve immigration
law, and says the Word does not either. And it does not label the Gmail thread
captured: that write has been behind a harness permission gate in this lane
since DR-1024, so the catalog-and-ledger duplicate check remains what actually
stands between a resurfacing thread and a duplicate lesson. `re-review:
2026-11-16` — by then the course should have a measured answer to the one
question this lesson could not ask of itself: whether readers who met the
counter-witness (Daniel's three years) report the lesson as teaching rather than
as a position.
