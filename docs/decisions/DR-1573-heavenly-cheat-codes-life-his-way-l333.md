## Decision

`ll333-heavenly-cheat-codes-life-his-way`, ORDER 333, ADDED 2026-11-19:

1. **The code is not hidden** (Deuteronomy 30:11, 14; Proverbs 2:6). Codes seeded in consciousness are the speaker's and not taught.
2. **The seed** is the Word (Luke 8:11; Mark 4:26-28; Isaiah 55:11; 1 Peter 1:23).
3. **Where you keep it**: mouth, mind, deed, then success (Psalms 119:11; Joshua 1:8; James 1:22; Matthew 7:24-25).
4. **Why it looks like cheating** (Genesis 39:2-3; Daniel 1:17, 20; Psalms 119:98-99; Deuteronomy 4:6), and where possibility sits (Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37; Ephesians 3:20).
5. **David's code** (1 Samuel 17:4, 26, 37, 45, 47): the chapter calls Goliath a Philistine champion of Gath and no more, so the lesson says no more.
6. **How Jesus played**: it is written, three times (Matthew 4:4, 7, 10; Ephesians 6:17).
7. **How it spreads**: it grows and cannot be bound (Acts 6:7; 12:24; 19:20; 2 Timothy 2:9), but it is received, not slipped in (James 1:21; Luke 8:15).
8. **The one who actually cheats, but Yahweh** (Revelation 12:9; John 8:44; Ephesians 2:4-5; Colossians 2:15; 1 John 4:4). Our voice says but Yahweh, as he said it; Ephesians 2:4 is quoted as written.
9. **What winning means His way** (Mark 8:36; John 16:33; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37; Revelation 12:11).

Two parables use invented people (Kenya, Wales). The quiz has ten questions.
