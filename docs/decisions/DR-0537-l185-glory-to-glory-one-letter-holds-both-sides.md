# DR-0537 — L185 Glory to Glory, and letting one letter settle a fight

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
- **Type:** content
- **Relates to:** DR-0098 (teach the Word, do not platform man's disagreement), DR-0076 (measure, do not claim; proven-to-catch), DR-0100 (state established truth plainly), DR-0210 (Yahweh in our own voice), DR-0459 (no ellipsis inside a quotation), DR-0534 (L182 — the same posture, applied to the Father and the Son)

## What Darrell sent, and the question

Eleven messages carrying a Trackstarz podcast on the viral confrontation where a man challenged a preacher mid-service — the challenger holding that those in Christ are perfect in the spirit, the preacher pressing repentance. Then the question:

> *"Christians still fussing about this and need clarification... can the Word clear this up?"*

Two sharpenings mid-build shaped every band: **"Holy Spirit's telling us when we are wrong..."** and **"Results show up... in you..."** — the second is the lesson's whole answer to the evidence question.

And then he answered the draft with three words: **"Glory to glory..."** That is 2 Corinthians 3:18, it is the exact frame both men were missing, and it became the title and the spine.

## The move the lesson turns on

Both parties held a **real verse**, so this is not truth against error. The settling move is not a clever synthesis — it is one short letter. John wrote:

- *"If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us"* (1 John 1:8)
- *"Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin"* (1 John 3:9)

One author, one document, a few paragraphs apart. So **any reading that makes either cancel the other is wrong before the argument opens** — which disqualifies both stage positions as they were stated. That is DR-0098's own standard: the Word explains the Word.

From there: the seed and the man (1 John 2:1), the clause the fight is built on (Romans 8:1 does not end where it is quoted), glory to glory as a **tense** with a passive verb and a named agent, the three terms taken from Scripture rather than an outline, the unfinished fear-and-trembling quotation, fruit against works, where fruit is actually tested, the results showing up **in you**, the list as a mirror and never a ruler, the Spirit's reproof as evidence of belonging, the unreliable heart, *I never knew you*, whose hand does the keeping — and the warnings that are His words too.

## The five things this lesson could most easily have got wrong

1. **Scoring the fight.** Naming the men turns a passage into a team sport. Every band grants that both held a true verse before it resolves anything.
2. **Quoting only the comfortable column.** Hebrews 6:4-6 and 2 Peter 2:20 are in the Word and addressed to churches. Leaving them out would do to Hebrews exactly what the challenger did to Romans 8, so they are carried in our own registers and a test asserts it.
3. **Sending the reader inward.** Jeremiah 17:9 says the instrument is unreliable, so the lesson's move is a **request to be searched**, never a verdict.
4. **Softening *I never knew you*.** It is not softened, and the lesson names who it lands on — the entirely confident, never the one lying awake afraid.
5. **Flattening the prodigal.** The father's own words are *"was dead, and is alive again"* — sonship and the language of death in one sentence. A teacher who smooths that over is editing.

## What the reference gate caught during the build

**Twenty spans quoted across a verse boundary while citing only the first verse:** Galatians 5:22 running into 5:23, Psalm 139:23 into :24, Ephesians 2:8 into :9, Ephesians 1:13 into :14, Hebrews 6:4 into :6.

The **quotations were verbatim**; the **references were wrong**. That is precisely the defect `scripts/quoted-verse-is-the-verse.mjs` was built to see and which the older blob-search gate could never have found — it would have confirmed the words exist somewhere in the KJV and passed. All twenty now cite their true range, and L185's own test pins each case in both directions: the range contains the later clause, and the opening verse alone does **not**.
