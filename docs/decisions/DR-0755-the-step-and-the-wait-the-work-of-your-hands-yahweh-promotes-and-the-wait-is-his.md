## Context

A believer does the diligent work — assembles the record of what his hands can do, writes the letter that explains him, puts the proof of his study beside it, sends the whole of it in — and receives back the one line every applicant receives: it has been received. Nothing more. From that moment the matter is outside his reach: he cannot compel a reading, cannot sit in the room where it is weighed, cannot move the date, and cannot know.

That interval is a universal household experience and the Word is not thin on it, but Living Lessons had no lesson that took it directly. The catalog carried L208 on where skill comes from and what a certificate witnesses to; it did not carry the next question, which is what a believer does in the gap between submitted and answered.

**The privacy line, which is binding and is the reason this record says so little about the occasion.** A pending application belongs to the person standing in it. The lesson teaches the KIND of moment and never his particulars: no employer, no institution, no department, no title, no identifier, no address appears anywhere in the module, in this record, or in the test, and nothing in the lesson states or implies anything about where he currently works. The provenance is still honest and complete — his own word, by email, on 2026-10-06, marked Lesson, about an application he had just submitted — because that is true and it is enough. This is not an intention; it is checked (see Verification).

## Impact

Living Lessons — the devotional course, a department of its own in `learn-catalog.js` (DR-0598) — gains **L209**, `ll209-the-step-and-the-wait-the-work-of-your-hands-yahweh-promotes-and-the-wait-is-his`, added 2026-10-06, the 208th module in the course and its newest. It is a lesson about what Yahweh says concerning the work of our hands, Who actually promotes, and how to stand in a wait — not a lesson about job-hunting technique, and it carries no advice of that kind.

Nine movements, Word first and in order: the work already in the hand and the command to do it well (Exodus 4:2; Proverbs 24:27; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Proverbs 22:29; Colossians 3:23-24); the committing that settles the thinking before any answer arrives (Proverbs 16:3; Psalms 90:17); the order the Word keeps for asking, from Nehemiah praying inside a pause and then asking plainly, and Esther fasting and then going in (Nehemiah 2:4-5, 2:8; Esther 4:16); the line where a man's part ends (Proverbs 16:9; Proverbs 19:21; James 4:13-15; Psalms 31:15; Isaiah 55:8-9); Who promotes, with three directions ruled out by name before the Judge is named (Psalms 75:6-7; 1 Samuel 2:7-8; Daniel 2:21; Proverbs 21:1; Psalms 127:1-2); the commands that fill the wait, which make it a place where obedience is possible (Psalms 27:14; Psalms 37:7; Psalms 62:5; Psalms 40:1; Isaiah 40:31; Lamentations 3:25-26; Micah 7:7; Habakkuk 2:3; Isaiah 30:18); what to do with the weight, with the promised result read exactly — a guarded heart and mind, not a guaranteed yes (Philippians 4:6-7; 1 Peter 5:6-7; Matthew 6:33-34; Romans 12:12); what the years did to Joseph, including the follow-up that failed and the two further years (Genesis 39:2-3; 40:14; 40:23; 41:14; Psalms 105:19; Galatians 6:9; Hebrews 10:36); and what we do, ending where the Word ends (Proverbs 3:5-6; James 1:5; Matthew 6:10; Luke 22:42; Romans 8:28; Psalms 37:23-24; Philippians 4:11-13; Psalms 1:3). The lesson and every band close with the Lamb (John 1:29), the three talk-together directions, and the confession.

The lesson states plainly, in its own text, what the Word has **not** said: it gives no verdict on any particular application, names no date, and guarantees no specific door — and says that inventing one for the comfort of the household would be a cruelty dressed as encouragement. That is DR-0076 and DR-0100 held together in teaching prose: state what is established, flag honestly where the Word is silent, and never manufacture a promise to close a feeling.

## Decision

- Ship L209 into Living Lessons (devotional) as the newest module, with all four age bands authored at full coverage, the talk-together close in the lesson and in each band in the lesson's own words, and `anchor.ref` naming every verse the lesson stands on so Search it out derives its links rather than being told them.
- **The privacy constraint ships as a gate, not as a note.** `living-lessons-l209-verses.test.js` fails the build on an email address, an employer or institution suffix, a posting identifier or a link anywhere in the module, and on any wording that would say he is leaving or looking while employed. The lesson's own privacy sentences are pinned, so removing them fails too.
- Teach the kind of moment; never the particulars. Where the Word is reticent about an outcome, stay with what is written and stop.
- The one assertion this change necessarily moves is the newest-lesson day in `living-lessons-order.test.jsx` (2026-10-02 to 2026-10-06). Nothing else in the catalog, no other lesson and no other course file is touched.
