# DR-0127 — Every knowledge space opens with Yahweh's knowledge and perspectives when we have them — Word-first, derived, never invented

- **Status:** accepted
- **Tier:** B (a rendering law + derived lead on the Learn engine; extends to knowledge surfaces as they are touched)
- **Scope:** every knowledge space in the app (Learn courses, the Scripture study, the Eternal Algorithms rooms, the witness rooms, future teaching surfaces)
- **Date:** 2026-07-08
- **Principles:** WORD-FIRST, VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE, APP-IS-PRIMARY, DECISION-RECORDS

## Directive

Darrell, 2026-07-08, holding the Study seeds (outcome + 4D source leading each card) and the 3rd-Dimension Witness banner ("Yahweh's Perspectives are 4th-dimensional... the science describes the frame Yahweh made; His Word governs"): *"All knowledge spaces start with Yahweh's knowledge and or perspectives if we have them involved — make sure the other spaces do too like this first image."*

## Decision

1. **The law:** a knowledge space opens with Yahweh's knowledge/perspective — the Word, His conditional, His frame — BEFORE the material it carries, wherever we have it. The witness rooms and Study seeds already carry this posture; it is now binding for the rest.
2. **The lead is DERIVED, never invented:** `wordFirstLead(course)` (lib/eternal-algorithms-course.js) resolves a course's own declared lead, else its first session's Scripture anchor. The Learn header renders it above every course. A space with no derivable lead renders nothing and the census test reports it as a finding — a missing lead is a work item, not an occasion to fabricate one (DR-0076).
3. **By construction where we build:** the derived processing courses (DR-0126) open every session's big idea with the Scripture references and Yahweh's own conditional before any practice — pinned by test.
4. **The ways-review (DR-0108) gains the question:** "did this stretch add or touch a knowledge space whose opening is not the Word?" A yes is a finding: derive its lead, or record the why.

## Constraint

The lead selects from what is already authored and verified (anchors, declared frames, the KJV corpus by reference). Where a surface genuinely has no Word content yet, the honest state is a reported gap — never a machine-authored perspective of Yahweh.
