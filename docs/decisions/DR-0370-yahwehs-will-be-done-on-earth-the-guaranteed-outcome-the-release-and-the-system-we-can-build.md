## Decisions

1. **Teach it as an economy with mechanisms, before it is ethics or grievance.** The release on a seventh-year clock, the prohibition on usury from a brother with its stated motive, restitution at principal plus a fifth, the gleaning edges, the open hand, the fiftieth-year return. Mechanisms first is what keeps a mixed room whole.

2. **Lead with the guarantee, because that is Darrell's actual claim.** `Save when there shall be no poor among you` is an outcome attached to a command. No programme, degree or certification attaches a guarantee of that kind to itself, which is precisely why His documentation outranks ours and why treating a human framework as its peer is a category error rather than humility. `it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth` keeps the capacity a gift.

3. **Name the rival by the text's own name.** Not a caution about budgeting: `Ye cannot serve God and mammon` says one of the two is already master.

4. **Say DOCUMENTED, and say that nobody present is accused — in the lesson, not just in the room.** Darrell was careful about this and the care is load-bearing. The record is ordinary state paperwork: HOLC neighbourhood grading 1935-40, the agricultural and domestic exclusions written into the 1935 Social Security Act, union exclusions permitted under the Wagner Act, and separate-but-equal written into Hill-Burton as the price of Southern votes. Forms, statutes and recorded votes are not weather. A test pins that both the word and the disclaimer survive.

5. **The unpaid-labour fact is named by the verse that names it exactly.** `useth his neighbour's service without wages` and `the hire of the labourers... kept back by fraud, crieth`. No modern vocabulary is needed, and historians' 1860 valuation of enslaved people against the nation's railroads, factories and banks is cited rather than asserted.

6. **The turn is the whole reason this is a lesson and not an indictment, and it is his.** The wealth was demonstrably produced by people who owned nothing, were kept from education on purpose, held no capital and kept none of the return — so capacity was never the missing variable. What was taken was the proceeds. Therefore it is producible, and the present position is better in every input. `The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former.`

7. **Correct our own side's numbers out loud.** Darrell said there was statistically no unemployment under segregation. Census data shows Black unemployment nationally *slightly below* the white rate in 1930, the gap opening in the 1940s, and federal data by 1954 at roughly five percent white against ten percent Black. The lesson states the accurate version and says plainly that the overstatement is not correct — because the true figure, a people locked out of housing finance, unions, hospitals and wage protections working at those rates, is stronger and cannot be waved away. **This is DR-0100 turned on ourselves, and it is pinned by test.**

8. **Be precise where the modern evidence is contested.** Federal Reserve research documents a large mortgage denial gap; a Minneapolis Fed analysis finds lender-stated reasons do not explain it; other Fed work finds observable risk factors explain much of the denial gap and that the unexplained interest-rate gap itself is small. The lesson says the documented part is documented and the argued part is argued. Precision is what makes an argument undismissable on a detail.

9. **End on what a congregation can do without permission:** restitution in the Word's arithmetic, and the mutual economy that is documented history rather than sentiment — burial societies from 1787 and 1843 growing into funeral homes, insurance companies and banks, NC Mutual founded in 1898 by a man born enslaved. `Neither was there any among them that lacked` reports a community that was neither wealthy nor in power.

10. **The release was never about money.** Its stated purpose is that there be no poor and `that thy brother may live with thee`. Quality of life was always the objective; money was the instrument. Close on `They shall not build, and another inhabit`.
