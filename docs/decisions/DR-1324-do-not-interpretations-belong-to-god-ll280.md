## Decision

**The lesson is built on who owns an answer that comes in the night, and it concedes the channel before it refuses the technique.** `ll280-whose-answer-comes-in-the-night`, titled *Whose Answer Comes in the Night — the Channel Yahweh Keeps, the Technique That Cannot Open It, and Chaff or Wheat Before You Act*. Fifteen numbered movements in the adult lesson, four age bands, and the one Answer nobody had to dream for at the close.

**The three claims inside the advertisement's one sentence are separated before any is answered**, because collapsing them is how a reader is talked into all three at once: **ownership** (the wisdom is yours behind a lock you hold), **control** (a dream can be steered, scheduled, aimed), **usefulness** (it will solve your challenges — which quietly makes the dream equipment and the reader its operator).

**The concession comes first and is a third of the lesson.** Yahweh genuinely speaks in the night and states it as standing policy — *"I the LORD will make myself known unto him in a vision, and will speak unto him in a dream"* (Numbers 12:6) — at the hour a person can argue least, *"For God speaketh once, yea twice, yet man perceiveth it not"* (Job 33:14), *"In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men"* (Job 33:15), with the purpose named, *"Then he openeth the ears of men, and sealeth their instruction"* (Job 33:16), the mercy in it, *"That he may withdraw man from his purpose, and hide pride from man"* (Job 33:17), and a rescue, *"He keepeth back his soul from the pit"* (Job 33:18). It met a king who had prepared nothing (1 Kings 3:5), guarded the childhood of Jesus twice (Matthew 1:20; Matthew 2:12-13), and is promised forward rather than treated as finished (Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17). **That concession is the argument, not a weakness in it: a counterfeit is only ever struck from something real.**

**Then the three claims fail, one at a time, on the Word's own account.** CONTROL fails because in every recorded case the thing was **given and never operated** — He opens a *sleeper's* ears, and sleep itself is His gift (Job 33:15-16; Psalms 127:2) — while the night hours a believer may genuinely use are all a person going **to Yahweh and to what He already wrote** (Psalms 63:6; Psalms 119:148; Psalms 16:7), never to his own sleep to make it produce. OWNERSHIP fails because the interpretation never belonged to the dreamer: *"Do not interpretations belong to God?"* (Genesis 40:8), *"It is not in me: God shall give Pharaoh an answer of peace"* (Genesis 41:16), and a **salaried guild of specialists** could not supply it (Daniel 2:27) for a reason that was never a weak method — *"But there is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets"* (Daniel 2:28), *"this secret is not revealed to me for any wisdom that I have more than any living"* (Daniel 2:30) — with the boundary laid down as statute, *"The secret things belong unto the LORD our God"* (Deuteronomy 29:29). **So unlock is the wrong verb: the lock was never fitted on our side of that door.** USEFULNESS fails on the least romantic passages in the Bible — *"For a dream cometh through the multitude of business"* (Ecclesiastes 5:3), *"but fear thou God"* (Ecclesiastes 5:7), and *"as when an hungry man dreameth, and, behold, he eateth; but he awaketh, and his soul is empty"* (Isaiah 29:8). **A guided dream was to solve your challenges; the Word says a dream will feed a starving man all night and hand him an empty soul at dawn.**

**The practice is named in Scripture in almost the advertisement's own vocabulary, and its real aim is stated.** *"neither hearken to your dreams which ye cause to be dreamed"* (Jeremiah 29:8) — **caused to be dreamed**, identified and refused centuries before anybody packaged it. The material came from the dreamers themselves, *"they are prophets of the deceit of their own heart"* (Jeremiah 23:26), *"they speak a vision of their own heart, and not out of the mouth of the LORD"* (Jeremiah 23:16). **And then the objective, which is the gravest line in the lesson because it is not about the dreamers at all:** *"Which think to cause my people to forget my name by their dreams which they tell every man to his neighbour"* (Jeremiah 23:27). Informing anybody was never the aim; moving His name out of the middle of a life and seating something else there was — and a dependable supply of answers that begin inside you, concern you and serve you accomplishes that patiently, over years, **without ever once sounding hostile.**

**THE TEST IS THE SHARPEST THING IN THE BUILD, AND IT IS A CONTROL CASE SCRIPTURE SUPPLIES ITSELF.** One night vision is introduced with *"In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men"* (Job 33:15) and its word was true. **Another man in the same book described his own in all but identical language** — *"In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men"* (Job 4:13) — with the atmosphere (Job 4:14), a presence (Job 4:15), a voice (Job 4:16), and even the modesty of a man who knows he received a fragment (Job 4:12). At the end of that book Yahweh said to him: *"ye have not spoken of me the thing that is right"* (Job 42:7). **The experiences cannot be told apart. Same hour, same deep sleep, same awe, same voice out of the silence. Vividness did not separate them, technique did not separate them, and whether it felt real did not separate them. What separated them was what was said about Yahweh, and in the record nothing else did.** Which is the Word's own question: *"The prophet that hath a dream, let him tell a dream; and he that hath my word, let him speak my word faithfully. What is the chaff to the wheat?"* (Jeremiah 23:28).

**And the test survives a direct hit, which is the hardest demand in the lesson and is pinned in every band.** *"If there arise among you a prophet, or a dreamer of dreams, and giveth thee a sign or a wonder"* (Deuteronomy 13:1), *"And the sign or the wonder come to pass, whereof he spake unto thee"* (Deuteronomy 13:2) — it worked, it landed — *"Thou shalt not hearken unto the words of that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams"* (Deuteronomy 13:3). **Accuracy is not a credential.** The measuring line is the written Word (Isaiah 8:20) and the posture is examination without exemption (1 John 4:1).

**The destination is awake.** *"If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him"* (James 1:5), with Him as the supply rather than a vault (Proverbs 2:6) and the method refusing the very thing the advertisement offered — *"lean not unto thine own understanding"* (Proverbs 3:5), *"In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths"* (Proverbs 3:6) — carrying a light that works at night with no procedure attached (Psalms 119:105), audited the way the noble hearers audited an apostle (Acts 17:11). **And the close is the reason none of it ends in cynicism: the greatest secret there has ever been refused to stay in the dark where only a method could reach it.** It was pointed at in daylight, by a man standing in a river, with his finger — *"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world"* (John 1:29) — **Jesus, the Lamb of Yahweh and the Eternal Son of Yahweh** (DR-0210), of whom the Father says *"Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever"* (Hebrews 1:8); and the mind wanted for whatever is in front of the reader needs no incubating, because *"But we have the mind of Christ"* (1 Corinthians 2:16).

**The three actions the week ends on are checkable:** **take it awake** — name the challenge you were tempted to hand to your sleep, write it down, ask Him for Wisdom in one sentence and read His Word on it until you hold one verse you can say back; **thresh it before you act** — if a dream comes, write it out before acting on any fragment and run the one question across it, what does this say about Yahweh and does it agree with what He has already written, keeping the wheat and letting the chaff go; and **refuse the technique outright** — do not try to steer your sleep and do not buy a method for it.
