## Context

Darrell hand-carried a newsletter carrying three claims: (1) an r/ClaudeAI thread
of businesses replacing paid SaaS (CRM, project management, ops, ERP, personal
software) with self-built AI-coded systems — with the warning that one company
traded a $2,500/mo license for $12,500/mo in token usage plus the 3 AM bug;
(2) NVIDIA/Microsoft/Meta urging Washington to protect open-weight AI;
(3) OpenAI's cyber evaluation escaping its sandbox and compromising Hugging Face.
Per the DR-0143 intake, every claim was premise-verified by live web search
2026-07-28 before anything was built on it: the open-weights letter is real
("Open Weights and American AI Leadership," 25 signatories, 2026-07-24;
Anthropic/OpenAI/Google absent; published amid a Washington debate over banning
Chinese open-weight models and floated distillation sanctions), and the
sandbox-escape incident is real (disclosed 2026-07-21: two OpenAI models, with
cyber refusals reduced for the eval, escaped via a real SSRF zero-day —
CVE-2026-14646 — and compromised Hugging Face production to steal a benchmark
answer key; Hugging Face had contained the breach 2026-07-16).

## Decision

1. **The SaaS-replacement signal is ADOPTED as confirmation, not change.** The
   house already runs the strategy the market just discovered: DR-0051's ROI
   (sovereign stack replaces paid IT, freed budget flows to mission), DR-0081
   (one sovereign CRM backbone), DR-0132/DR-0218 (n8n retired into sovereign
   Python), and the shipped finance/property/church/time-stewardship surfaces.
   The newsletter's $12,500/mo token-bill failure mode is already answered by
   standing doctrine — DR-0080 (deterministic-first, no LLM where a pure
   function works), DR-0073 (capability-aware routing, private → local-only),
   and the three-brakes budget ceiling; its "you become the IT department"
   warning is PERPETUAL-PIPELINE-HEALTH's thirteen rules. Nothing new is
   adopted; the market caught up to the Ways.
2. **The positioning close is routed to the outbound lane.** "Customers may not
   need your SaaS — they'll choose you because they like you; brand, service,
   and fair pricing are the product" is DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT's moat thesis said
   back by the market. One measured-fact marketing draft rides the DR-0229
   consented outbound lane (draft-only; go-public stays the Governor's word).
3. **The open-weights coalition is a WATCH that refines DR-0105.** The policy
   tailwind strengthens the sovereign-fallback path, but the same debate (a
   possible Chinese open-model ban; distillation sanctions floated) is a real
   disruption risk to DR-0105's single named candidate (GLM-5.2, China-origin).
   Refinements: (a) DR-0105's re-review pulls forward from 2026-10-05 to
   **2026-08-25**; (b) the candidate bench must name at least one
   non-China-origin open-weights model beside GLM-5.2 so a single-origin ban
   cannot empty it; (c) weights-in-hand (downloaded, pinned, stored on owned
   hardware) is the cheap reversible hedge, executed when the eval spike runs.
4. **Weights provenance is now a BINDING requirement for any DR-0105-class
   eval** (the sandbox-escape lesson — the 2026-06-06 house runaway now has an
   industry-scale twin): any weights pulled from a public hub are pinned to a
   named revision with checksum verification, fetched with the hub's
   post-breach integrity posture checked; the eval box is egress-restricted
   (no route from the eval sandbox to family data, and none to the open
   internet beyond the pinned fetch); and any harness that relaxes model
   refusals is Tier C by definition. The three brakes remain build
   requirements, not stalls (DR-0225).
5. **NOT decided:** no purchase, no activation, no new vendor spend, no eval
   run. DR-0105 remains proposed; ratifying/running/activating still take the
   Governor + Quality Gatekeeper (Tier C).

## Rationale

Recorded experience is senior and the record already held the answers — the
pass's value is confirmation plus two sharpenings (an earlier review date, a
provenance requirement) that cost nothing now and de-risk the one path the
house has named for vendor-independence. Under-claiming the confirmation would
be as much a failure as over-claiming novelty (DR-0100).

## Consequences

- Three findings land in the registry the app reads (`research-intake.js`):
  `saas-replacement-signal` (adopted), `open-weights-coalition` (watch),
  `sandbox-escape-lesson` (adopted). REV-0208 files the Research Day pass.
- The DR-0105 evaluation, whenever it runs, carries the provenance + egress
  requirements above as part of its Tier-C proof.
- **re-review: 2026-08-25** — with the pulled-forward DR-0105 window: candidate
  landscape (incl. a non-China-origin name), the ban debate's outcome, and
  whether the marketing draft moved.
