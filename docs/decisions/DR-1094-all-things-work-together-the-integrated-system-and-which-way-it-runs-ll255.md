## Context

The whole thesis is his one sentence, and the lesson keeps his three moves distinct and in his order rather than flattening them into a general comfort:

1. **ALL THINGS WORK TOGETHER** — Romans 8:28, read whole. Not "some things", and not "each thing alone": *together*. The verse is taught inside its own paragraph, because it has a verse on either side and neither is decoration: the Spirit's intercession where we do not know what to ask (Romans 8:26-27) and the purpose the working-together produces (Romans 8:29, conformity to the image of His Son).
2. **LIKE INTEGRATED SYSTEMS** — the working-together is *systemic*. Parts coupled into one running whole, not a pile of unrelated events. Colossians 1:16-17 ("by him all things consist") is the Word's own statement of the integration — CLAUDE.md already names Him the Outside Agent by whom all things consist; Hebrews 11:3 frames the worlds; Ephesians 1:11 makes the running purposive; and 1 Corinthians 12:12, 21, 25, 26 teaches the integration on a body, where "whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it."
3. **FOR OR AGAINST, DEPENDING ON THE WILL AND WAY** — the *same* system runs either direction, and the sign is set by alignment. Romans 8:28's own condition says so; Genesis 50:20 is the proof that identical events carry opposite signs by whose purpose they serve; Galatians 6:7-8 is the mechanism; Deuteronomy 30:19 is the fork, with His counsel attached; Isaiah 1:19-20 writes both branches in one breath; and Jeremiah 6:16 carries BOTH signs in one verse — the good way offered, rest promised, and "But they said, We will not walk therein."

Then the sharpening, which is the lesson's sharpest distinction and the thing it must never blur — **The Way is KNOWN; our own path is NOT:**

4. **THE WAY IS KNOWN** — revealed, and a Person before it is a method (John 14:6); the old paths one may stand in and ask after (Jeremiah 6:16); the name the first believers were known by (Acts 9:2; `THE-WAY.md` is the foundation doc). It can be learned, taught, walked, and held to.
5. **OUR OWN PATH IS NOT** — the route of one life is not given to the one walking it (Proverbs 20:24; Jeremiah 10:23). Not a ban on planning: we devise and He directs (Proverbs 16:9; 19:21), and His own word for the route is *paths* (Proverbs 3:5-6; Psalms 37:23).
6. **HE DIRECTS US DAILY** — a word at the turn, not a map of the county (Isaiah 30:21). The lamp point is made explicitly, because it is the heart of why daily is enough: a lamp carried at the feet is built to show the next step, and the one carrying it is not in the dark but in exactly as much light as walking requires (Psalms 119:105). The supply reloads on the same schedule (Lamentations 3:22-23), discipleship is set to that clock (Luke 9:23), and the asking is by the day (Matthew 6:11; 6:34).
7. **HE IS INTO THE DETAILS OF ALL THINGS** — his exclamation, and the Word is specific: Matthew 10:29-30, Luke 12:7, Psalms 139:16. This is what makes "integrated systems" more than a metaphor: the integration runs down to the detail, and so does His attention.
8. **HIS WAYS ARE HIGHER, AND HIS WORD STILL LANDS** — Isaiah 55:8-9 held together with 55:11, so "higher" reads as beyond our audit rather than hidden from use.
9. **WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES WITH WHAT HURTS** — Hebrews 12:11, 2 Corinthians 4:17, James 1:2-4.
10. **HE FINISHES WHAT HE BEGAN** — Philippians 1:6, and the close that sends the reader back into the text.

**Two places needed care, and the gate pins both.** First, Romans 8:28 is **never printed without who it is for**: the promise and the condition were written in one sentence, and the half usually dropped is the one that makes it bearable. So the lesson never turns the verse into a promise that everything feels good, and the gate fails any quoted span carrying "all things work together for good" without "to them that love God". Second, the "against" direction is taught as the Word teaches it — the honest second half of a real choice, with the offer as the louder half — and **never as a club to frighten anyone with**; the facilitator notes say so in those words and the gate asserts them. Religion AND Relationship both: backbone in the two branches, warmth in the lamp and the sparrow.

## Impact

The congregation gets a lesson that answers the question his sentence raises: *how do I live inside a system whose sign depends on alignment, when I cannot see the whole system?* You keep the Will and the Way, which you CAN know, and He directs the path, which you cannot. That removes both errors at once — the presumption of mapping your own road, and the despair of thinking that because the road is invisible you have nothing to hold. Four bands, each carrying the whole of it; two parables (the chore wheel that ran both ways; the years the same harvest took back) that teach the two signs rather than decorate them; seven quiz questions; facilitator notes with the two guard rails named.

Ship `ll255-all-things-work-together-the-integrated-system-and-which-way-it-runs` as a Living Lesson dated 2026-10-17, the day it was spoken, with its own pinned gate. The ten movements run in the same order in all five places a reader reads, and the two halves of his distinction travel together in every one of them.
