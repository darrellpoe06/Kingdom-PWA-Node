# DR-0112 — Fathers provoke to good works, not to wrath: the pastoral spine of every father↔child surface

- **Status:** accepted
- **Tier:** B (a governing foundation doc + design posture; it constrains but does not itself ship a surface)
- **Scope:** every father↔child / guardian↔child surface — above all the child money-visibility view (DR-0094), the Successor seat + promotion (DR-0111 successor), and the "Handed Forward" succession curriculum
- **Date:** 2026-07-06
- **Principles:** THE-SOURCE-OF-ANSWERS, QUALITY-OF-LIFE, DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT, GOVERN-EXECUTE-ADVISE, VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE, DECISION-RECORDS

## Directive

Darrell, 2026-07-06: "A father provokes his children to good works. He doesn't just watch — he also prays, and provokes to good works, not to wrath. Very clear instructions about this."

## Decision

Adopt `docs/00-foundations/_root/FATHERS-PROVOKE-TO-GOOD-WORKS.md` as a **Layer 3 foundation** and the binding **pastoral posture** for every surface where a parent can see or shape a child. The industry's answer to "what is a monitoring feature for?" is surveillance (watch → catch → correct); the Word's answer is the opposite and is binding here:

- **Two provocations, one commanded.** A father must NOT provoke a child to wrath/anger (Ephesians 6:4; Colossians 3:21 — the named failure is *discouragement*), and MUST provoke them to good works (Hebrews 10:24 — same verb, opposite direction).
- **He doesn't just watch — he is active, prayer first.** Pray for/with the child (Job 1:5; David for Solomon, 1 Chronicles 29:19), teach diligently through ordinary life (Deuteronomy 6:6-7), train toward a direction that holds (Proverbs 22:6). The goal is a child who walks in truth (3 John 1:4), mirroring the Father who gives good gifts (Luke 11:13).

**What it binds (Strategies / Opportunities / Constraints are in the foundation doc):**
- The child money view (DR-0094, in build) is framed **provoke-to-good-works** — the giving/tithe and savings/buffer foregrounded as good works to imitate, not the family's shortfalls to police; it opens in a praying-father posture, never an audit.
- **Discouragement is the defined failure (Colossians 3:21):** any father↔child surface whose primary effect is watch-and-catch, or that routinely leaves a child shamed/exposed/defeated, is out of bounds — that is provoking to wrath by another name.
- The guardian provokes; the platform is his instrument, never an autonomous judge of the child (no automated shaming/scoring/"gotcha" — GOVERN-EXECUTE-ADVISE).
- Child-safety floors and the verified RLS gate are untouched (DR-0111 successor / DR-0094 / DR-0076); warmth never overrides the safety floor, and truth is still told in an age-right way (DR-0100).

Every KJV verse in the foundation doc was fetched verbatim from the repo Scriptures (DR-0076); none from memory.

## Consequences

- The "both, per child" child money-view build (Darrell's 2026-07-06 choice on DR-0094) is written against this posture: whichever view (raw books or teaching view) a guardian picks, it provokes to good works and never to wrath.
- The succession curriculum ("Handed Forward") gains its explicit pastoral spine — the father provokes the heir to the good work he could not finish (David→Solomon), grounded in prayer.
