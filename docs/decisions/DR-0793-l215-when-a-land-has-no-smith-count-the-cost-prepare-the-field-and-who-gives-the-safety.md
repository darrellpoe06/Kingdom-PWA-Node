## Context

The lead item was a defence story. Carried exactly as reported and no further: a government that buys warships has been receiving them below the rate it set for itself; its own audit office had already published the finding that the private yards were not positioned to meet those goals; a company out of the technology trade, new to shipbuilding, has been awarded a substantial contract for components of a submarine programme that is behind schedule and has committed billions of its own money to a yard that will not open until the end of the decade; and in the interval it intends to make smaller components at a smaller facility, opening years earlier and deliberately so, to develop and validate its manufacturing methods before the larger yard exists. Elsewhere on the same page: a record half-year of profit in the money trade, rising pay and bonus pools, and a small number of the largest listed companies carrying close to two fifths of a broad index.

**Why this material needed a boundary before it needed a lesson.** It touches weapons, public money and named living people, which is the exact combination that turns teaching into verdict-passing. So the boundary was set before a word of teaching was written and is kept to the last line: no company, product, publication or person is named anywhere in the module, and no judgement is offered about anybody's motives or character, because the one thing Scripture plainly withholds from us here is the very thing a trade press exists to supply — *"for man looketh on the outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart."* (1 Samuel 16:7), quoted in the lesson itself. That boundary is a gate in the test, not an intention.

**The second boundary is DR-0098.** The lesson says in its own text that it will not stage rival human positions and invite the reader to pick the one that suits his temperament. It teaches what the Word shows and works it the way the Word explains itself — most visibly in movement five, where Isaiah's plowshares and Joel's swords are both quoted and the error named is building a doctrine out of a preferred half in either direction.

**DR-0100's three tiers were applied claim by claim rather than as a posture.** Tier one, stated plainly because under-claiming a verified truth is as much a failure of truth as inventing one: a manufacturing capacity that has been lost is a genuine wound to a people, the audit finding belongs to that class of fact, and repairing such a capacity is honourable work. Tier two, named narrowly and about the future rather than about anybody's honesty: it is not established that the announced yard will deliver what was announced, because it is not built; nor that methods validated on small components transfer intact to very large structures; nor what the eventual delivered cost will be. Tier three corrects two opposite over-reaches while leaving the true observation inside each untouched — that making the means of defence is in itself sin (Romans 13:4; Nehemiah 4:17-18; Ecclesiastes 3:8), and that sufficient capacity is the same thing as safety (Proverbs 21:31, which leaves the horse prepared and moves the trust).

**Placement, and the premise that was checked rather than assumed.** The builder's brief reserved `ll215` in Living Lessons on the premise that the subject reads as business, defence contracting and stewardship rather than as A.I. engineering, and said to say so if the teaching turned out to be organizational. Read against the material, the premise holds and Living Lessons is where it belongs: the teaching's centre is a heart lifted up at the summit of a success (2 Chronicles 26:15-16), faithfulness in another man's money (Luke 16:12), and where safety actually comes from — stewardship and trust, not a method a project is run by. Its **Project Management affinity is real and is named here rather than lost**: movements one, three and eight are counting the cost, preparing the field before the house, and keeping a promise at the size it was made, which sit beside that course's own frame. It is nevertheless shipped in Living Lessons for the DR-0759 reason, re-verified this session: the `ll` namespace, the four-band shape and `living-lessons-dates.js` are the Living Lessons artifacts, and `project-management-course.js` is a closed twelve-module `pm*` sequence a new lesson does not go inside.

And where the Word stops, with what is revealed binding our own hands instead of a distant industry (Deuteronomy 29:29; Proverbs 27:23-24; Colossians 3:23-24; Luke 16:10; Proverbs 22:29; Proverbs 16:3; Matthew 7:24-25; Galatians 6:9; James 1:5; Proverbs 3:5-6; Micah 6:8). The lesson and every band close with the Lamb (John 3:16; John 1:29), the three talk-together directions, and the confession.

**What the lesson affirmed and what the Word corrected**, said plainly because that distinction is the lesson:

- **Affirmed.** A lost manufacturing capacity is a real national wound, and Scripture recorded one before auditors existed. Repairing it is honourable work; the Word is not fastidious about smiths or yards. Proving a method in a small shop before building the great one is not a modern insight but the Word's own order of work, and praising it is part of telling the truth. The ruler's sword is lawful. Earnings are not sin, labour is commanded, and the labourer is worthy of his reward.
- **Corrected.** That making the means of defence is inherently sin — Romans 13:4 and Nehemiah 4:18 correct that claim while the real danger of delighting in war still stands (Psalms 68:30). That sufficient capacity is safety — Proverbs 21:31 corrects that claim precisely while leaving the horse prepared, so the shortfall is still worth repairing. That a season may be made into a doctrine — Isaiah and Joel are both kept, and deleting either half is named as the error. And the quiet assumption that strength is the safe condition — Uzziah's two verses put the crisis at the summit, not in the shortfall.

The lesson also states in its own text what the Word has **not** said: not how many submarines a nation ought to hold, not which builder should receive which award, not what a just unit price is this year, not whether a programme should be accelerated. Those are wisdom judgements for the people who carry the office, and a verse manufactured to settle them would be the fabrication this platform exists to remove. That refusal is pinned in the test, as is the refusal of the opposite error — treating every profitable year as a moral failure.

## Decision

- Ship L215 into Living Lessons as the newest module, with all four age bands authored at full coverage, the talk-together close in the lesson and in each band in the lesson's own words, and `anchor.ref` naming every verse the lesson stands on so Search it out derives its links rather than being told them.
- **Place it in Living Lessons rather than the Project Management course.** The brief's premise was checked against the material and holds: the teaching is stewardship- and trust-shaped, not a project method. The `ll` id, the four-band shape and the dates file are the Living Lessons artifacts, and the `pm*` course is a closed twelve-module sequence (DR-0759). The Project Management affinity of movements one, three and eight is recorded above rather than lost.
- **The no-name, no-verdict boundary ships as a gate, not a note.** `living-lessons-l215-verses.test.js` fails the build on a link, a company suffix, lifted newsletter furniture, a currency figure, a percentage or a character verdict anywhere in the module, and the lesson's own boundary sentences are pinned so removing them fails too.
- **Both halves of DR-0100 are pinned.** The affirmation sentences and the correction sentences are each asserted, the narrowly-named open questions are asserted, and a some-say / others-argue / both-sides / you-decide construction anywhere in the module fails the build. A lesson that only corrected would be as dishonest as one that only agreed.
- Teach the Word to the kind of situation, never to the particulars of a company or a person. Where the Word is reticent, stay with what is written and stop.
