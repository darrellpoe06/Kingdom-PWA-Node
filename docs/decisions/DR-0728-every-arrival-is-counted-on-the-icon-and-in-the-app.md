# DR-0728 — Every arrival is counted: one number on the icon and in the app, and a ring for each

- **Status:** accepted
- **Tier:** B (the header gains a control on every door; a migration adds columns, a trigger and a realtime publication; the push sender gains a one-person topic; no money, no schema a tenant writes)
- **Type:** feature
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Scope:** `app/src/lib/arrivals.js` (new, pure), `app/src/lib/arrivals-watch.js` (new), `app/src/components/ArrivalsBell.jsx` (new), `app/src/lib/dm-notify.js` (badge writer removed), `app/src/main.jsx`, `app/src/poe-financial-mvp-v28.jsx` (+2 lines: the import and the mount), `app/src/components/LessonInbox.jsx` / `MemberLessonQueue.jsx` / `FeedbackCenter.jsx` (mark seen), `app/src/components/UiIcon.jsx` (bell), `app/src/lib/push-send-policy.js` (`lesson` topic), `scripts/push-outbox-drain-over-tailnet.sh`, `infra/supabase/migrations-auto/0245-…sql`, `scripts/arrivals-ci-bootstrap.sql`, `scripts/arrivals-ci-smoke.sql`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (the `arrivals-push` leg), `app/src/lib/feature-registry.json`, `scripts/system-flow-registry.mjs`, tests
- **Principles:** APP-IS-PRIMARY (DR-0065), VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE (DR-0076: measure first, proven-to-catch), REALITY-TRACE (DR-0061), THREE-BRAKES (P10/P11/P12; the drain keeps DR-0400's), feature registry (DR-0726), HOLD-THE-HAND-OF-THE-PROCESS (DR-0621)
- **Grounds:** Darrell, 2026-10-01, in the installed PoeTech app on his phone, beside another app's launcher badge reading "19": *"Why don't the PoeTech App have those types of notifications and the number of them if I haven't checked them yet?"*

## Context

He wants a number on the app icon and inside the app for everything that arrived that he has not looked at yet, not only direct messages, and a notification when each thing arrives. The app had the plumbing for one kind: unread messages set the launcher badge and the "(N)" title (DR-0455) and rang the OS while hidden. Everything else that arrives for a person was silent.

## What was measured

Walked on origin/main fe14096a before a line was written:

- `app/src/lib/dm-notify.js:80-87`: the badge and the title were set from `unreadDmCount(rows)` and nothing else. `AppAlerts.jsx` read the same event. There was no other writer and no other count.
- Lesson arrivals exist as rows and tags the app already reads: the NAS builder (DR-0669) moves a row from `lesson-building` to `awaiting-review` (`lesson-pipeline.js:205`), the reader tags `lesson-published` + `lesson-id:` (`lesson-review-messages.js:22-23`), the Governor decides a member's request with `lesson-approved` / `lesson-declined` (`member-lesson-review.js`), and `member_lesson_queue()` (migration 0237) lists what waits for him. "Your lessons" (`LessonInbox.jsx`) and "Lessons to decide" (`MemberLessonQueue.jsx`) show them; nothing counted them.
- A reply on feedback exists: a steward moves `triage_status` off `new` with `triage_notes` (DR-0616, migration 0233), and the sender reads it through `fetchMyFeedback` inside the Feedback sheet (`FeedbackCenter.jsx:331-337`). There is no timestamp for the move, so it can be counted by id but not timed.
- With the app closed: the only road from the NAS to a phone is `push_outbox` + `push-outbox-drain.yml` (DR-0400). The outbox had no way to address a person (`target_role` only), the sender had no topic for it (`SENDABLE_TOPICS = live, message, fault`), and the drain sent its machine token as `authorization: Bearer`, which `functions/api/push-send.js:81-84` reads as a person's JWT; the sender reads a machine caller from `x-push-token`. `agent_inbox` was not in the realtime publication (no migration adds it), so an open app would only have heard the flip on a clock.
- Every device that turned notifications on from the app's offer carries the topic `message` alone (`AppAlerts.jsx` → `PushNotifications topic="message"` → `enablePush topics: [topic]`). A new topic name on the subscription row would reach no phone that has already said yes.
- The monolith stood at 5,308 lines against a budget of 5,312.

## Impact

One number, everywhere a number can be: the launcher icon, the "(N)" title, and a bell in the header beside Help that lists every arrival newest first and opens its screen in the door the person is in. A lesson that finished building rings the phone while the app is in the background, and reaches a closed app through the outbox once the drain is armed. Reading a message, opening Your lessons, opening Lessons to decide or opening the Feedback sheet drops that kind's share of the number. The seen mark is kept per account, per device, in localStorage; said plainly, a second device starts with its own marks (messages are the exception: the database holds their read state).

## Decision

1. **One model, pure, tested** (`arrivals.js`): six kinds from the rows each screen already reads: `message`, `lesson-ready`, `lesson-review` (members only), `lesson-published`, `review-queue` (the Governor only), `feedback`. Combined newest first; a per-account seen set that a screen sets and the bell never does; the count is unread messages plus every other unseen item.
2. **One watcher, one badge writer** (`arrivals-watch.js`, started in `main.jsx` after the DM watcher): folds the DM watcher's event with the person's own `agent_inbox` rows (realtime + a 60 s heartbeat + a refetch on becoming visible), the Governor's queue and the person's own feedback; sets the icon and title to the combined count; publishes `poetech:arrivals`; rings a local notification through the service worker registration for a NEW non-message arrival while hidden and permission already granted (never asked for here), landing on Your lessons by the door's own worker. `dm-notify.js` no longer writes the badge. The lesson modules are imported after sign-in so the boot chunk stays as light as it was.
3. **The bell** (`ArrivalsBell.jsx`): header styling, 36 px target, focus ring, the number in a ring, a list in the shared Modal. Registered in `feature-registry.json` (control and mount); the header walk renders it.
4. **With the app closed** (migration 0245, the drain, the policy): `push_outbox` gains `target_user`, `url`, `dedupe_key` (unique); a trigger on `agent_inbox` `UPDATE OF tags` enqueues one row per TEACHING per event when a lesson row gains `awaiting-review` or `lesson-published` (the transcript row names its parent with `of:`), addressed to `created_by`, best-effort as 0220. The sender gains topic `lesson`: an explicit one-person audience and the outbox key are required, never a broadcast; its audience is the person's own `message` opt-in, the one consent on a device that means "tell me when something is for me". The drain sends kind `lesson` rows to that one person and now carries `x-push-token`, which also makes DR-0400's fault path deliverable. A person may read the outbox rows addressed to them. `agent_inbox` joins the realtime publication.
5. **Delivery to a closed phone still rides DR-0400's arm switch.** `PUSH_OUTBOX_DRAIN_ENABLED` is the brake DR-0400 set on the day a real phone is proven to receive (DR-0334 re-review); this record does not flip it from the sandbox. Until it is armed the outbox fills, nothing is lost, and the open or backgrounded app counts and rings every arrival itself. Arming is one repository variable. **re-review: 2026-10-15**: arm the drain on the phone proof, watch one lesson row flip on the live database produce one `push_outbox` row and one `push_sends` row, and read the person's badge on his Fold.
6. **Not built, and why:** a timestamp for a feedback reply (the database holds none; the arrival is counted by id and ordered by the note's own time, which the row says); a second-device sync of the seen marks (localStorage first; a `seen` table is the next cut if two devices prove to disagree in use). re-review with item 5.
