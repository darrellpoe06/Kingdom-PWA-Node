## The Way, proven this session (L18–L23 + World Issues 5–6, eight lessons)

The repeatable pipeline from a spoken teaching to a live, verse-checked lesson — every step already exercised and gated:

1. **CAPTURE** — the family speaks or pastes a teaching, video summary, or link into the one input surface (One Voice / Yahweh Hears You). A *bare link is a request*; its CONTENT enters as a summary (vendor-AI hand-carry today) or, once built, the sovereign transcription lane.
2. **PREMISE-VERIFY, BELIEVE-FIRST (DR-0166 / DR-0143)** — receive the human voice believing-and-honoring first; verify only its claims about OUR system against the repo; strip foreign project framing (the "Project SHALOM" catch, twice).
3. **VERSE-VERIFY (DR-0076)** — every Scripture fetched verbatim from the repo KJV before it ships; never from memory; the book-name convention held (`Psalms`, not `Psalm`).
4. **TIER (DR-0100)** — established fact stated plainly; genuine uncertainty flagged narrowly; over-reach corrected by the Word while the true data beneath still stands. Under-claiming a real harm is as much a lie as over-claiming a false one.
5. **TEACH, DON'T DEBATE (DR-0098)** — name a debate to educate past it; never stage pick-a-side; the Word is not put to a vote.
6. **AUTHOR into the right surface** — Living Lessons for worldview/teaching; the World Issues discernment engine for claims-with-evidence (labeled claims, dated sources, steelmanned perspectives, the two-courts accountability block); Godhead/Eternal Algorithms for doctrine — following the tested module shape.
7. **GATE + SHIP** — lint (`--max-warnings 0`), full Vitest, real build, the render gate (built ⇒ surfaced, the ≥40-lesson floor, category coverage); merge on green; **deploy-verify at the merge SHA (DR-0107)** — CI-green is never accepted as deployed.
8. **REFLECT, SAME SESSION (DR-0120/DR-0158)** — a REV record, a session note carrying the O&C, and Ari's duties + reports refreshed to match — all inside the app, derived from the record, no static data.
