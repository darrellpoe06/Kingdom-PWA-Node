## DR-0100 tiers applied (verified by live web search 2026-09-15)

**Tier 1 — documented, stated plainly with sources and dates:**
- Public Law 88-260, January 23, 1964: Congress renamed the National Cultural Center for President Kennedy as a living memorial and authorized $23 million (Congress.gov S.J.Res.136; the Kennedy Center's memorial page).
- February 12, 2025: the President dismissed the Biden-era trustees (eighteen, including chairman David Rubenstein); the remaining, mostly new, board elected him chairman; Deborah Rutter out, Richard Grenell in (Kennedy Center press release; The Conversation). $257 million for renovation in the 2025 reconciliation bill (IBTimes UK; BroadwayWorld).
- Departures by name and date: Rhiannon Giddens, Issa Rae, Renée Fleming (resigned as advisor; later withdrew May 2026 NSO concerts), Ben Folds, Shonda Rhimes (Feb 2025); Hamilton's producers (Mar 2025, for the Mar–Apr 2026 run); Béla Fleck, Brentano String Quartet, Vocal Arts DC; Washington National Opera cut ties January 10, 2026 citing the break-even policy (NPR; Forbes; CNN).
- December 18, 2025 renaming vote; the White House called it unanimous; Beatty says she was muted; Beatty v. Trump, 1:25-cv-04480 (D.D.C.), filed December 22, 2025 (CNN; Civil Rights Litigation Clearinghouse).
- May 29, 2026: Judge Cooper's 94-page ruling — *"Congress gave the Kennedy Center its name, and only Congress can change it"* — signage down within fourteen days; two-year closure blocked; scaffolding June 12 (NBC; PBS; Courthouse News; CNBC). August 13, 2026: board voted 20–3 for the "Restored and Renovated by President Donald J. Trump" inscription and "President Donald J. Trump Plaza"; Beatty's emergency motion August 21; DOJ: the inscriptions are "not renaming" (CNN; Time; NPR; The Hill).
- August 25, 2026, The Washington Post's confidential documents: nearly $100 million short of the revenue target; officials projected a 70 percent miss; subscriptions down about $1.6 million (~36%) from 2024; ~43% of tickets unsold in fall 2025 vs ~93% sold/comped in fall 2024 and 80% in fall 2023.
- September 5, 2026: part of the Grand Foyer ceiling fell in heavy rain; no injuries; spokeswoman Roma Daravi blamed decades of deferred maintenance (UPI; The Hill; NBC).
- September 14, 2026: the two draft resolutions, quoted exactly — "unsafe for continued occupancy"; "in such a precarious fiscal position that it will not be able to support its payroll obligations, nor routine maintenance contracts within a matter of weeks"; "The Board understands that without such appropriate recognition it is unlikely that President Trump will provide the fundamental oversight of the renovation of the main building and lead the fiscal rescue of the Center"; ten inscription options (three quoted) (NPR ×2; ABC; AP via WTOP).
- How a large arts nonprofit is funded, measured: 60/30/10 across the sector; performing arts ≈46% admissions, ≈41% contributed (individuals 20.7%, government 4.3%), endowment ≈5% (Americans for the Arts); the Kennedy Center ≈$268M budget under Rutter, tickets ≈half, federal appropriation ≈$45M/yr (FY2023 $45.38M) restricted to upkeep of the memorial, no programming (CRS IF12911; the center's FY25 budget justification).
