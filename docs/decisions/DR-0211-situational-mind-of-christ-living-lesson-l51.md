## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-21: **"Yahweh explains how to address people in various
situations — can we pool each situation and list them for situational analysis
and effectiveness of The Mind Of Christ situationally... from the biblical
Scriptures... to prove His Authority."** The applied, situational companion to
L50 (which renews the mind): L50 forms the mind; L51 shows that renewed mind
reading the situation and giving each person the FITTING word, from Scripture,
under the King's authority.

## The decision

Added **L51 — "The Mind of Christ, Situationally — How the Scriptures Address
Each Person (Proving His Authority)"** (weeks 50 → 51; the `weeks ===
MODULES.length` gate). A pooled, scannable catalog, each case with its verbatim
directive:

- The ANGRY → a soft answer (Proverbs 15:1; James 1:19).
- A BROTHER who wronged you → privately first (Matthew 18:15).
- The FALLEN → restore in meekness (Galatians 6:1).
- The GRIEVING → presence over speech (Romans 12:15; Job 2:13).
- An ENEMY / persecutor → love, bless, feed, pray (Matthew 5:44; Romans 12:14,20).
- The SEEKER → answer with meekness and fear (1 Peter 3:15).
- The CONTENTIOUS → avoid strife, gently instruct where possible (2 Timothy 2:23-25).
- The SCORNER → discern; do not cast pearls (Proverbs 9:8).
- The FOOL → the two-sided word (Proverbs 26:4-5).
- ELDERS / YOUNG, men / women → as father/brother/mother/sister (1 Timothy 5:1-2).
- RULERS → render, no reviling (Matthew 22:21; Acts 23:5).
- ALL, no partiality (James 2:1; Romans 12:16).
- A MIXED GROUP at once → warn/comfort/support/be-patient (1 Thessalonians 5:14).
- PUBLIC unrepented sin → firm rebuke (1 Timothy 5:20; Galatians 2:11; Titus 1:13).
- A time for SILENCE (Ecclesiastes 3:7; Proverbs 17:28).
- Governing rule: grace aimed at the hearer (Colossians 4:6; Ephesians 4:29,15).

**The frame Darrell named — proving His authority:** that Scripture has a
righteous, fitting word for EVERY situation, and Jesus embodied each perfectly
(Matthew 11:28; John 20:27; Isaiah 53:7), is a demonstration of authority — "he
taught them as one having authority" (Matthew 7:28-29); "Never man spake like
this man" (John 7:46); "All power is given unto me" (Matthew 28:18). Word-first
(DR-0098): the Word is the authority, not human communication theory.

levels, quiz, deep lesson, facilitator).
