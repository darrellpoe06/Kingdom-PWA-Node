## Context

**Sent in by:** Christina, 2026-10-28, in four messages. The ask: make Money "as intuitive and visually polished as Monarch Money" while adding budgeting, planning and debt-repayment depth; open Money on one question — *"How much money can I safely spend or put toward debt after accounting for everything I need to pay?"*; replace the crowded twelve-tab strip with nine sections; preserve every existing feature, record, import and synchronization; label every balance by its source; never invent a missing value; and — after the engine audit — "a reliable debt repayment planner that shows exactly how much to pay toward each debt every month and when each debt is projected to be paid off." Explicit holds: no merge, no deploy, no database change, no change to synchronization behavior without separate approval.

## What was measured

- **Money's wiring** (checklist §A): `view === 'books'` + `booksView` in the shell; twelve sub-tabs at `poe-financial-mvp-v28.jsx:4273`; the default `calendar` in `lib/nav-history.js` and the shell's fallback literal; the homepage tile (`lib/life-hub.js:84`) sets only the view, so it landed on whatever tab was open last.
- **Why Upcoming read zero** (`BooksTransactions.jsx:535-559`): only hand-typed obligations whose `nextDue` is still in the future count; the date never rolls forward; detected rhythms are never included; real accounts lose their seed obligations on sign-in (shell `:888-901`).
- **The existing debt engine** (`lib/lifecycle-and-flow.js`), on synthetic debts, recorded executable in `__tests__/debt-engine-audit.test.js`: (A) a partial final payment is double-counted — $50 owed with a $100 minimum pays $50 and rolls the full $100 the same month, $200 from a $150 budget (measured $850 and $790 where the budget allows $900 and $890); (B) the interest method follows the size of the rate (above 10% daily, else monthly), not the loan's terms; (C) exhausting the horizon reads as "cleared" with a dated month and a nine-figure balance open; (D) a debt with no known rate and no minimum is projected at 0% and $0 in the Big Picture headline.
- **Persistence of a new settings key:** `lib/snapshot-sync.js buildSnapshotPayload` copies every non-table key of `data`, and the pull merges `{ ...d, ...snapData }` — so `data.moneyPlan` rides the family snapshot with no sync code change. The push is gated by `remainderIsSeed(data)` (shell `:863-880`), which reads only `recurringObligations, taxCalendar, events, capexItems`: a world whose only real edits are settings never publishes.
- **The merge lane:** `auto-open-pr.yml` opens a PR on a `ccr-**` push and arms auto-merge unless the PR carries `hold`; `auto-merge.yml` disarms every hold-labeled PR; `deploy-cloudflare-pages.yml` runs on `push: main` only. A development branch cannot deploy.

## Impact

Left as it was, the first screen of Money is a calendar of mostly-expired obligations, the headline debt-free date rests on an engine that overpays cleared debts and reads unknown terms as free money, and the question Christina named has no answer anywhere in the app. The change is additive: the twelve components mount with identical props inside sections; every `?sub=` deep link, gate, mutator, import path, sync and report is untouched (checklist §A–B, verified by the pinned suites). Reversible by reverting the branch. What it obligates: the audit's four defects are now a standing comparison the legacy Debts tab and Big Picture still read from — switching those two callers to the corrected engine is the next decision, not this one.

## Decision

1. **Money opens on the Overview.** `overview` is the default `booksView` (`lib/money-nav.js` owns the default and the valid list; `nav-history.js` reads them). The tab, the dock, the homepage tile and a bare `?view=books` all land there; every legacy `?sub=` still resolves; the Imported guard returns to the Overview.
2. **Nine sections, one map.** Overview · Accounts (Accounts, Entities, Legal) · Transactions (Transactions, Imported) · Budget · Bills (Upcoming, Calendar, Subscriptions, Owed) · Goals (Goals, Plan) · Debt Freedom (Planner, Debts) · Reports (Reports, Taxes, 1099s) · Settings. A collapsible rail at laptop width; the section strip below it; inside Money the phone's ONE bar shows Overview · Transactions · Budget · Goals and More leads with the rest of Money (no second bar). Every id reaches in at most three taps (`money-nav.test.js`).
3. **Safe to Spend is chronological** (`lib/safe-to-spend.js`): verified ledger cash on the spendable accounts, every dated item laid day by day through the period (obligations rolled forward, debt minimums on their due day, mortgages, tax items, incidents, future-dated rows, inferred rhythms labeled), undated reserves accrued daily, the LOWEST projected balance minus the cushion, floored at zero; shortfall reported apart. Transfers are never income; business cash is never personal; each dedupe rule is a test. Status is verified / estimate / unavailable with reasons; the card names its source as ledger-calculated, never a bank balance, with the ledger's as-of date.
4. **Nothing is invented.** The guided setup (Settings) captures pay schedules, debt due days, rent and mortgage due days, recurring bills (through the shell's own `addRecurring`), tax amounts, planned savings and the cushion into `data.moneyPlan`; a step left empty stays visibly missing on the Overview. The sync line reads local / pending / synchronized from the shell's own snapshot marker.
5. **A corrected debt engine** (`lib/debt-freedom.js`) powers Debt Freedom: per-debt interest method (daily, monthly, none; promo until a date; rate changes; fees), minimums first and capped at what is owed, the unused part of a clearing minimum rolling once and the whole minimum thereafter, snowball / avalanche / custom order, recurring extra and one-time payments, missed payments, a complete month-by-month schedule, per-debt payoff dates, no debt-free date when the horizon runs out, unknown terms excluded by name, "paid off" as a planner flag that keeps the record. Independently verified against closed-form amortization and the published 30-year figure (`debt-freedom.test.js`, 23 cases written before the engine ran). **Not decided here:** switching the legacy Debts tab and the Big Picture headline to this engine.
7. **Refined after Christina's review of the preview (same day, same branch, before any push):** the phone is the priority — the amount owns the card's first row with its reliability label and source line beneath; a SIMPLE breakdown (available cash, income received by the low point, bills and payments due by then, reserves set aside by then, the lowest balance, the cushion, the result) sits on the card and reconciles with the engine to the cent because its lines are the walk's own terms through the lowest day (`lines.toLowest`, tested) — the calculation was not changed to simplify the display; the twelve detailed lines, the projected cash line and the explanation ride one disclosure; the entity selector is one dropdown on a phone; Bills, Budget and Debt Freedom are one tap under the card. Debt Freedom opens on a summary band (total debt, required monthly, extra, debt-free date, next to clear) and a "Show my monthly payment plan" control that lists, per debt, the exact projected payment (minimum, extra, one-time), the balance after it and the expected payoff, beside the actual pace the ledger has seen; the amortization schedule rides a disclosure. Budget gains a by-paycheck view (`lib/money-paychecks.js`): periods are cut at the engine's income events, bills due before the next paycheck are funded from cash on hand after the cushion, a paycheck counts only from its own day, a leftover carries forward and a shortfall never does, and every short period is named. The guided setup marks each step Required or Optional, shows a progress bar and an "essentials complete" state (paychecks, bills, cushion).
8. **Phone-first at every width below the laptop rail (Christina 2026-10-28, tested on her Galaxy Z Fold 7):** unfolded, the 984px inner screen fell in the 640–1023px band where the sideways section strip showed and the bar carried no tabs — "like a desktop application". Inside Money the ONE bar now carries Overview · Transactions · Budget · Goals · More at every width below `lg` (`MONEY_PHONE_QUERY`, 1023.98px, `lib/chrome-dock.js`); the strip is removed (the header keeps the current-section label); the entity selector is the dropdown below `lg`. Outside Money the bar keeps its 640px rule untouched. Debt Freedom's band leads with the four figures she named — total debt, monthly payment (minimums + extra), extra payment, projected payoff — then next to clear; the strategy comparison moves below each debt's terms. Measured on Playwright's Samsung descriptors (S9+ 320 → Fold 7 inner 984, plus landscape): five-button bar, dropdown, no strip, zero horizontal overflow on all nine sections. Three realistic-household engine cases (balances, rates, minimums, due days; snowball vs avalanche) added to `debt-freedom.test.js`.
6. **Not decided here, proposed for separate approval:** letting a settings-only world publish its snapshot — one line in `remainderIsSeed` (or its caller) treating `data.moneyPlan.updatedAt` as a real-world marker. It changes sync behavior and so waits on Christina.

## Rationale

Because the question a family asks first is not "what is my balance" but "what of it is mine to spend", and a month-end total cannot answer it when Tuesday's mortgage lands before Friday's pay; because a figure that is inferred, stale or unentered must say so or it is a lie dressed as a number (DR-0076 by its grounds); because an engine that overpays a cleared debt and dates a debt that never clears cannot be the planner Christina asked for, and measuring it first is how a repair is proved rather than claimed; and because nothing Money already did deserved to disappear for a cleaner menu.
