## The word, as spoken

Darrell, 2026-09-15: *"Don't take anyone's death so personal that you undermine your way home. Yeah, we got hurt when people die, always. But it's never to the end, meaning it's over. The reason why we have hope is because he existed before time already anyway. So why, why be so angry with him that we don't do what he say? When he is still him. Let him be him. Lesson."*

The hurt, allowed (John 11:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:13; Matthew 5:4; Psalms 34:18; 147:3). Never to the end (John 11:25-26; 1 Thessalonians 4:14-18; Revelation 21:4; 1 Corinthians 15:26; Psalms 116:15; Isaiah 57:1-2). The hope older than the grief (Psalms 90:2; Colossians 1:17; Isaiah 46:9-10; Malachi 3:6; Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17). The anger, named (Ruth 1:20-21; Jonah 4:9; Hebrews 12:15; Ephesians 4:26-27). Job, read whole (Job 1:21-22; 2:10; 13:15; 42:1-6). David's washed face (2 Samuel 12:22-23). He is still Him, so what He said still stands (Hebrews 13:8; Job 23:10; Hebrews 12:5, 11; 2 Corinthians 4:17-18; Genesis 50:20). The way home is open (John 14:1-3; Luke 23:43; 2 Corinthians 5:8; Philippians 1:21-23; 1 Peter 1:3-4; Hebrews 11:13-16; Ruth 4:14-15). Tonight (Psalms 62:8; 30:5; 126:5; 90:12; Ecclesiastes 7:2; Hebrews 10:36-39; Micah 7:8; Psalms 23:4; 73:26; Isaiah 41:10).

## The doctrinal care (DR-0098 / DR-0100)

- "Never to the end" is taught as the Word bounds it: for the one who believes in Him (John 11:25-26), and with the last enemy destroyed (1 Corinthians 15:26); the lesson does not promise beyond the text about any particular person, and says "if they were His" where the Word would.
- Sovereignty and goodness are spoken in one breath, as the Word speaks them (1 Samuel 2:6 beside Lamentations 3:33); the senior band names the two ditches a room falls into when it keeps only one.
- Anger at Him is neither excused nor condemned in the abstract: Naomi and Jonah said theirs to His face and were not struck; the Word's warning is on anger kept (Hebrews 12:15; Ephesians 4:26-27). That is exactly Darrell's sentence about undermining the way home.
