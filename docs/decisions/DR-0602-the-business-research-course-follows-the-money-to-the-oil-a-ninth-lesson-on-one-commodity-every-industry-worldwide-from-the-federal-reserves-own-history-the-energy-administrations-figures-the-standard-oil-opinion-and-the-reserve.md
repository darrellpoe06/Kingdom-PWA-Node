## Context

The eight competencies were worked on rivalries (DR-0594), given witnesses (DR-0600) and worked cases with figures (DR-0601). Every one of those rivalries ran on one commodity: Netflix’s discs moved by truck, Boeing’s aircraft burn fuel, and the first great monopoly case the Supreme Court decided against a single industry was about oil. Darrell asked for the money tied to oil followed through every industry, worldwide, with data. The lesson applies all eight competencies to that one commodity at three scales: the shock (the two oil crises on the Federal Reserve’s own record), the nation (the Energy Information Administration’s figures for use, trade and origin), and the company (the Standard Oil opinion, 221 U.S. 1), with the Strategic Petroleum Reserve read as Joseph’s pattern.

## Impact

Without the lesson, the course teaches the craft on rivalries and never shows the student the one commodity beneath all of them, so the "money tied to oil" stays a phrase; with it, the student reads a barrel price from the central bank that had to answer for it, follows it into the sector shares the statisticians measured, sees the same commodity monopolised on the Court’s record and laid up in a reserve on the Energy Department’s, and hears the Word name the store, the reserve and the heart before any of it. The cost is a longer course (nine weeks), two hosts on the business record list, and one more lesson every catalog gate walks.

## Decision

1. The Business Research course carries a ninth lesson, `br9-follow-the-oil`, that applies the eight competencies to the money tied to oil at three scales (the shock, the nation, the company) and the reserve, on the records the runner fetched and no others.
2. OPEC’s own account is not quoted while its page refuses a runner; the lesson says so under *still open*, and a future re-probe that answers may add it as a voice under the same gates.
3. A voice from an undated page is dated by the day the runner read it, with a timeline entry that names the run; a voice from a dated quotation (Burns, 1974) is dated by the words.
4. The Energy Information Administration and the Energy Department join the business record list; the Federal Reserve’s history and the Court are accepted through the History lists the shared gate already reads, so no host is listed twice.
