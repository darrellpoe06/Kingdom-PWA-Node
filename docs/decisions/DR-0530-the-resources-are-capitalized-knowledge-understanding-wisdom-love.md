## Context

Darrell spoke this while working the IP conversion — the register, the LICENSE,
the entity question. He had just said, of the wealth-building processes
themselves:

> "Yahweh says He gives us the ability to create wealth, I don't want to miss
> out on that because I don't have knowledge and understanding of these
> processes, so I'm using faith and the only way I know at this time."

Then, naming what is actually required:

> "Specific resources like business systems knowledge understanding wisdom Love
> etc all Needed!!!!!"

Then, at law-tier intensity, the directive:

> "Capitalize those."

He capitalized **Love** himself, in his own message, before asking for the rest.
That was the tell: he was already writing in the register he was asking for.

## The decision

**In PoeTech's own authored voice, capitalize Knowledge, Understanding, Wisdom,
Love, and Business Systems when they are named as the Resources** — the
provision a person must actually receive in order to build.

This is the Typographic Theology move of DR-0097 applied to provision. The
lowercase register flattens these into soft skills, personality traits, or
line items on a job description — things a person either happens to have or
doesn't. The capital says what they actually are: **Resources Yahweh supplies,
by His Spirit, for building.** Naming them small is precisely what lets a
person conclude they are optional, or assume they already have them.

### The Word puts them in BUILD ORDER

Fetched verbatim from the in-repo KJV corpus on 2026-09-19 (DR-0076 — never
from memory):

> **KJV — Proverbs 24:3-4:** *"Through wisdom is an house builded; and by
> understanding it is established: And by knowledge shall the chambers be
> filled with all precious and pleasant riches."*

**Wisdom** builds the house. **Understanding** establishes it. **Knowledge**
fills the chambers with riches. Three distinct Resources doing three distinct
works — not synonyms, not interchangeable, and the house needs all three. A
person with Knowledge and no Wisdom fills chambers in a house that was never
built. A person with Wisdom and no Knowledge builds a house with empty rooms.

**The source is named:**

> **KJV — Proverbs 2:6:** *"For the LORD giveth wisdom: out of his mouth cometh
> knowledge and understanding."*

**The purpose is named — and it is explicitly the BUILD:**

> **KJV — Exodus 31:3:** *"And I have filled him with the spirit of God, in
> wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of
> workmanship,"*

Bezalel is filled with the Spirit and the filling is itemized as these three
Resources plus workmanship. Yahweh does not hand a builder a vague blessing; He
supplies named Resources for a named build.

**Their absence is named as ruin, not preference:**

> **KJV — Hosea 4:6:** *"My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because
> thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee..."*

Destroyed. Not disadvantaged, not slowed — destroyed. That is the weight the
capital carries, and it is the exact thing Darrell named about himself: *"I
don't want to miss out on that because I don't have knowledge and understanding
of these processes."*

**Love is senior to all of it.** Without Love the rest profits nothing
(1 Corinthians 13:1-3), which is exactly why it belongs on this list as a
Resource and not as a sentiment. A system built with Wisdom, Understanding, and
Knowledge but without Love is the extraction machine this platform exists to
refuse.

**Business Systems** carries the capital in the same register, because the lack
of it is a real, documentable barrier that keeps capable people out of wealth
Yahweh already authorized them to build:

> **KJV — Deuteronomy 8:18:** *"But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God: for it
> is he that giveth thee power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant
> which he sware unto thy fathers, as it is this day."*

The power to get wealth is given. The Business Systems knowledge to exercise it
has, for many, never been taught — which is a lack, not a verdict.

## The bright line (binding)

**This governs ONLY our authored voice — NEVER quoted Scripture.**

The KJV lowercases *wisdom*, *understanding*, and *knowledge* inside the very
verses above. Every quotation is reproduced EXACTLY as written. **A blind
capitalization sweep across quoted text is forbidden** — it would corrupt the
Word in order to decorate our own point, which is the inverse of WORD-FIRST.

This is the identical bright line DR-0210 holds for "Yahweh" in our voice versus
"God"/"the LORD" inside a quote, and DR-0076 §1 enforces it: the corpus is the
authority, our prose is ours.

## Scope

- **Applies to:** lesson prose, benefits, UI copy, teaching notes, foundation
  docs, decision records, commit and PR narration, responses to Darrell.
- **Does NOT apply to:** quoted Scripture (ever); third-party quotations; code
  identifiers, variable names, JSON keys, or database columns; ordinary generic
  usage where the word is not naming the Resource ("he has a working knowledge
  of the API" stays lowercase).
- **Judgment, not find-replace:** the capital marks the word when it is *named
  as the Resource*. Mechanical sweeping is explicitly not the implementation,
  for the same reason DR-0210 forbids a blind God→Yahweh sweep.
