## The decisions

1. **VERIFY BEFORE FIXING, AND THE VERIFICATION CHANGED THE FIX.** The KJV genuinely spells it **"aul"** (Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17) — the 1611 spelling of "awl". So it was not a Scripture typo, and correcting the quotation would have corrupted the text (DR-0076 bright line). **The fault was ours:** the archaic spelling had leaked out of the quotation into OUR prose, where a reader has no way to know it names a tool. The two quotations keep `aul` verbatim; our two uses now read `awl`, and the tool is described the first time it appears.
2. **The process is walked in order, because almost nobody has had it described.** An awl is a cobbler's spike in a wooden handle. The servant says it out loud first. The master brings him **unto the judges** — witnessed by the court, never done privately where a man could be bullied into it. Then Deuteronomy names the motion exactly: *"thrust it through his ear unto the door."* Through the ear, INTO the door. He is briefly pinned to the exit he refused, and the hole stays for life.
3. **He is the Lamb of Yahweh, and the Lamb was not a late idea** — John 1:29, Isaiah 53:7, and Revelation dating it from the foundation of the world.
4. **He got off His Eternal Throne.** Philippians 2:6-8 walked down in eight stages, with the fourth ending the argument: He took the **form of a servant**. Isaiah 6:1 for the Throne He left; 2 Corinthians 8:9 for the ledger.
5. **The pain was real and it was the most.** Isaiah 53:5 in body words, and Hebrews 12:2's **ENDURED** — a word that only makes sense if there was something to endure.
6. **Suffering with Him — ours a little beside the Son's.** Philippians 3:10, 1 Peter 4:13, Romans 8:17-18: *"not worthy to be compared"*, said by a man who had been beaten, stoned and shipwrecked.
7. **REPROBATE DOES NOT MEAN MONSTROUS — his refinement was right and the Greek carries it.** ADOKIMOS is *failing the test, not approved* — the word for metal that does not pass assay. Paul uses it on **himself** as a possibility (1 Corinthians 9:27). So the reprobate mind is the mind that no longer retains the Word correctly; from a human standpoint it often looks like simple disinterest rather than evil, and spiritual things stop making sense to it. Teaching it as "crazy" or "evil" is the error this corrects.
