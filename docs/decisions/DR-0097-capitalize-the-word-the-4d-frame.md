## Decision

**Whenever we discuss the biblical Scriptures, capitalize "the Word" (and "His Word").** The capital is a *frame*, not decoration.

- **It marks the 4th-dimensional register.** The Word is the higher-priority, pre-temporal reality — Yahweh as the Author who **framed the worlds before time began** (Hebrews 11:3), the Outside Agent **by whom all things consist** (Colossians 1:16-17), the Programmer of the worlds. Writing about Scripture in the lowercase register flattens Him into 3rd-dimensional operational data.
- **He is the "outside agent" the sciences seek and will not honor.** *"For the invisible things of Him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even His eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse: because that, when they knew God, they glorified Him not as God"* (Romans 1:20-21, KJV). The design testifies; the wise see the code and withhold the Honor and Glory. The capital keeps them where they belong.
- **Applies everywhere** the existing typographic-theology rules apply: file content, commit messages, responses, summaries, code comments, and every in-app surface. His-reference pronouns follow the existing He/His/Him rule.

## The one honest edge (unchanged from the naming doc)

Scripture quoted **verbatim** (KJV public-domain, per DR-0076 fetch-don't-paraphrase) keeps its source capitalization *inside* the quotation marks — the Word is not edited. Everywhere **outside** a verbatim quote, this capitalization rule governs.
