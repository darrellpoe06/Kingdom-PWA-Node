## Context — the question

The Project Management course teaches how one project is delivered, counted, staffed, dated and closed. His words name the next lesson: not the project but the organization — how it learns, prioritizes and decides — and the correction that keeps it from being a seminar: the same capability is prudence, and love shown by letting Yahweh inspect our ways in both registers. It lands as lesson 11 of that course, in the course's own order (the Word's case, then the industry's name).

## What his words became

**pm11 — How the organization learns, prioritizes and decides — from information to decision-ready intelligence, with our ways inspected.** The Word's cases first: Jethro naming the governance system that lives in one head as the thing that wears the leader and the people away, and giving Moses a taught standard, a tiered structure of qualified men, and an escalation rule (Exodus 18:13-23); Joseph reading the pattern across fourteen years and converting it at once into officers and a stored fifth (Genesis 41:33-40); Issachar's two-part definition of the intelligence function (1 Chronicles 12:32); the apostles prioritizing by calling under load and delegating to the qualified (Acts 6:1-4); Nehemiah's night survey before building; Habakkuk's vision written plain enough to run; Solomon's safety in many counsellors. Then his addition: prudence (Proverbs 14:15), love shown by keeping (John 14:15), inspection from Yahweh's side in two registers — quantitative ("Consider your ways" with its ledger, Haggai 1:5-7; the lord who "reckoneth with them", Matthew 25:19) and qualitative ("of what sort", 1 Corinthians 3:13; "by their fruits", Matthew 7:16-20) — and the purpose of all of it, "Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven" (Matthew 6:10). Then the industry's names: decision intelligence, decision support, governance. The hands-on step ends on the OpsBoard, at the purpose line DR-0607 put there.

## Decision

1. pm11 ships as the course's eleventh lesson, in the course's own order.
2. His two framings — the purpose sentence and the drowning-in-information sentence — are carried as his framings, rendered, never as statistics.
3. The inspection is taught in two registers, quantity and quality, both from Yahweh's side, pinned in the senior band.
