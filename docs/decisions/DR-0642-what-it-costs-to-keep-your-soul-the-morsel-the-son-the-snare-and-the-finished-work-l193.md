## What it became

**L193 — What It Costs to Keep Your Soul — the Morsel, the Son, the Snare, and the Finished Work.** Placed in **Living Lessons from the Word** as lesson 193, the next number after L192 on `origin/main`. Its first anchor is Mark 8:36-37, so it shelves under **The Gospels**.

Ten movements, macro and micro, each answered from the Word:

1. **The question Jesus asked first.** Mark 8:36-37; Matthew 16:26. The soul is lost by *exchange*, which is how Henrie describes it. The first offer of the world for worship was made to Jesus, and He answered from the Word (Luke 4:5-8).
2. **One morsel.** Esau, faint, reasoned "what profit shall this birthright do to me?" (Genesis 25:29-34); Hebrews 12:16-17. Desperation makes a small price look like life; the refusal is settled before the hungry hour.
3. **The son before the role.** Romans 8:14-16; Galatians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 6:18; 1 John 3:1; Matthew 3:17 (the Father's approval spoken before the public work); Ephesians 1:6; Isaiah 43:1.
4. **The snare.** Proverbs 29:25; John 12:43; John 5:44; Galatians 1:10; Proverbs 27:21.
5. **Train up a child.** Proverbs 22:6; Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Luke 2:51-52.
6. **When success feels empty.** Ecclesiastes 2:11; 1 Kings 19:4-5 (bread and sleep before instruction); Psalm 42:5; 1 Peter 5:7; Philippians 4:7. Every band names the practical step: tell a parent, a pastor, a believing friend, a doctor.
7. **The way back.** Luke 15:17-20; Barnabas (Acts 4:36; 9:27) for the encouraging friend; Jesus withdrawing to pray (Mark 1:35; Luke 5:16; Matthew 6:6) for the monastery; confession to Yahweh (1 John 1:9, 1:7) and to one another (James 5:16; Proverbs 28:13); brothers for accountability (Proverbs 27:17; Ecclesiastes 4:10, 4:12; Hebrews 10:24; Proverbs 13:20).
8. **Saying no at a price.** Hebrews 11:24-26; Daniel 1:8; Matthew 6:24; Luke 12:15; Proverbs 23:23.
9. **Suffering and the finished work.** Romans 5:3-5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Romans 8:18, 8:28; Philippians 3:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 1:4; then John 1:29; John 19:30; Hebrews 10:10, 10:12, 10:14, 10:18; Hebrews 9:12.
10. **Ten losses, walked together.** John 11:35; Matthew 5:4; Psalm 34:18; Psalm 147:3; Psalm 56:8; Psalm 139:13, 139:16; 2 Samuel 12:22-23; 1 Thessalonians 4:13; Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Revelation 21:4.

Close: Matthew 16:25, and the house's confession, "Jesus is the Lamb of Yahweh and the Eternal Son of Yahweh."

## Impact

A reader who meets 'selling your soul' as a Hollywood phrase leaves with the Word's own account of it: the exchange Jesus priced (Mark 8:36-37) and the morsel Esau took (Hebrews 12:16). Left unbuilt, a summary Darrell sent as a lesson would be dropped, which Layer 0 forbids; built carelessly, it would put words in a living man's mouth, stage a denominational argument against him, or blur the finished work of Jesus under the language of meritorious suffering. The call obligates three things the tests now hold: provenance stated on every surface, only the summary's own quoted phrases, and "It is finished" (John 19:30) stated in every band.

## Decision

1. L193 ships as one lesson in ten movements, answering the whole summary from the Word, placed as the next Living Lesson after L192.
2. Henrie's account is reported as his; his Catholic practices are neither argued with nor adopted; the Word's teaching on confession, withdrawing to pray and suffering is taught in their place, and the finished redemption in Christ is stated plainly.
3. What is commendable in his testimony is honoured by name.
4. The lesson ships on summary provenance and says so; the transcript upgrade is dated below.
5. Decided by Darrell's standing rule (a "Lesson" he sends is build input, DR-0312); built by the lane.

## How the tradition-specific parts were handled (DR-0098)

- **His account is reported as his.** The monastery, confession and the 'meritorious redemptive' language are given as the summary gives them, with "from his Catholic faith" where the summary grounds it. No camp is named against him; he is not argued with.
- **Then the Word is taught.** Confession: to Yahweh, who forgives through the blood of Jesus (1 John 1:9, 1:7), and to one another for prayer and healing (James 5:16). Withdrawing to pray: the Lord's own practice (Mark 1:35; Luke 5:16). Suffering: fruitful, never wasted, and serving the Body (Romans 5:3-5; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 1:4). And stated plainly where it bears on "meritorious" suffering: the payment for sin is finished in the one offering of Jesus, the Lamb of Yahweh (John 19:30; Hebrews 10:10-18). The lesson's sentence is *"We suffer as the redeemed, not in order to be redeemed."* Every band states "It is finished" (John 19:30); a test pins it.
- **What is commendable is honoured by name:** refusing roles for conviction ("That is commendable"), putting himself under accountability, and a husband and wife keeping their faith through ten losses.

## Limits, stated

1. **Summary provenance.** The video was not watched and no transcript was read; the lesson says so in the adult text, every band and `inApp`, and a test pins it. `re-review: 2026-10-24`: when a transcript is fetched through the NAS lane (`.github/workflows/source-transcript-nas.yml`, as DR-0555/DR-0556 did), re-read every reported position against his own words, upgrade the provenance note, and correct anything the summary thinned. **No video id was sent with the summary.** A web search on 2026-09-24 found a YouTube upload titled "David Henrie: What Selling Your Soul Actually Means in Hollywood" (`HbfsMH3RSow`); it was not confirmed to be the full conversation the summary's 1:10:15 marks describe, so the lane was **not** dispatched on a guess. The one value needed is the confirmed video id.
2. **No living man is quoted beyond the summary's own quoted phrases.** Press coverage of the same interview carries longer quotations; none was used, because none was read from the primary source.
3. **The Word's reticence on the unborn is kept.** The lesson gives David's words at his infant son's death (2 Samuel 12:23) and Yahweh's knowledge of a child in the womb (Psalm 139:13, 16), and says in words that it adds no map beyond what is written.
4. **Depression and anxiety** are treated as real and the step to a pastor, friend or doctor is named; the lesson does not give medical advice beyond that.
