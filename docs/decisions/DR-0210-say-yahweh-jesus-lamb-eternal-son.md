## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-21: **"say Yahweh not God because other people call other gods
and I want to be clear and also that we worship Jesus as The Lamb Of God And
Eternal Son Of Yahweh etc."**

The generic word "god" is claimed by many faiths for many different beings. To
be unambiguous about WHOM we worship — the one true God who named Himself to
Moses (Exodus 3:14-15) — we prefer His covenant name **Yahweh** in our own
authored voice, and we confess **Jesus** plainly as **the Lamb of Yahweh, the
Eternal Son of Yahweh.**

## The decision

**In PoeTech's own authored voice** (lesson prose, benefits, UI copy, teaching
notes, responses, commit/PR narration):

1. **Prefer "Yahweh"** over the generic "God" when naming the Father / the one
   true God, wherever it reads naturally — so the reader is never left guessing
   which "god" is meant. "The Father," "the LORD" (as a title), "the Godhead,"
   and "the King" remain available; the point is clarity of identity, not a
   mechanical find-replace.
2. **Confess Jesus explicitly** as the **Lamb of Yahweh** (John 1:29) and the
   **Eternal Son of Yahweh** (John 3:16; Hebrews 1:8; Revelation 13:8) — the Son
   we worship, co-eternal, "by whom all things consist" (Colossians 1:16-17;
   DR-0097).
3. Capitalization unchanged: Yahweh / Jesus / the Father / the Son / the Holy
   Spirit / the Word, and His-reference pronouns, all capitalized (the existing
   Typographic Theology); the adversary's names stay lowercase.

## The bright line — never alter verbatim Scripture (DR-0076)

**This applies ONLY to our own authored voice, NEVER to quoted Scripture.** The
KJV renders the covenant name as "God" and "the LORD"; those quotations are
fetched verbatim and are left EXACTLY as written. We do not substitute "Yahweh"
into a Bible quote — that would corrupt the text and violate the
verify-verbatim doctrine (DR-0076) and the source-text-conflict rule
(CLAUDE.md, "When Source Text Conflicts With These Rules"). So: **"Yahweh" in
our prose; "God"/"the LORD" untouched inside every quotation.** A blind
God→Yahweh sweep of existing content is expressly forbidden for this reason;
Yahweh-in-prose edits are made carefully, leaving all quotes intact.
