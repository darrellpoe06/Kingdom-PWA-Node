# DR-0345 — Every lesson in two renderings (the Word on click), Illinois on every training, one training a week, and a therapist schedules a lesson for a client

**Date:** 2026-09-10 · **Status:** accepted · **Tier:** B · **Area:** tlc · **Principles:** VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE, WORD-FIRST, APP-IS-PRIMARY, REALITY-TRACE, DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT, TLC-FIREWALL, DECISION-RECORDS

## Finding (DR-0219)

The client track carried lessons with the Word woven into the prose (Finding Peace), lessons with no Word at all (the psychoeducation and witness modules), and no way for a reader to choose. The library's 24 courses carried a Yahweh strand as a course-level braid but no Word on the lesson itself, and no Illinois rule anywhere but the CE tracker. The training map was 24 hours a month over 36 months, not a week's training. Nothing let a therapist put a lesson in front of a client.

## Decisions

1. **Every lesson has two renderings: plain by default, the Word on click.** A module's `levels` are the plain rendering and carry no Scripture reference (a gate proves it); `module.word = { principle, verses, reflection }` is the Word rendering, shown by a "Show the Word" button on the lesson (`aria-pressed`), or from the start when the page-wide Show-the-Word switch is on. Verse text is never typed into a lesson: the reader loads each reference verbatim from the corpus (`VerseBlock` over `lib/bible-kjv.js`, DR-0340), so nothing can be misquoted. `lib/lesson-word.js` resolves the rendering: the module's own `word`, or, for a library lesson, the course's Yahweh strand and anchors (`lib/tlc-course-strands.js`). The Illinois lesson has none by design.
2. **Finding Peace is restructured, not rewritten.** The eleven chapters' plain levels are Christina's practical tips with the verses removed and the faith framing neutralised so any client can use them; the Word rendering is her chapter as she wrote it, every verse verbatim. The plain distillation is `validated:false` like everything she has not yet ratified in-app.
3. **The author-inline set is named, not silently skipped.** Christina's six session scripts and the covenant-intimacy course are therapist material whose teaching quotes the Word in its own sentences; a plain rendering would rewrite her text. They keep the Word inline and are listed by name in the gate.
4. **Illinois on every training.** `lib/tlc-illinois-policy.js` is one registry of eleven rules (continuing education for renewal; LCSW supervised experience; mandated reporting; confidentiality; disclosure to warn; minors and outpatient counseling; telehealth; 988 and CESSA; records and PIPA; cultural competence and implicit bias; ethics and sexual harassment prevention), each with its citation, what the state requires, the procedure TLC follows because of it, its sources, a quiz question, and a dated `asOf`. Every course in the library closes with an "Illinois: policy, program and procedure" lesson composed from the renewal rule plus its field's rules (`illinoisModuleFor`), so the state's rule is taught inside the training, cited, never linked out.
5. **The facts were checked, and how is stated.** The figures were verified 2026-09-10 through web search against the sources named on each rule (68 Ill. Adm. Code 1470.95 and 1470.20, 225 ILCS 20, 325 ILCS 5, 740 ILCS 110, 405 ILCS 5/3-501, 225 ILCS 150, IDHS 988, 815 ILCS 530). The primary IDFPR, ILGA, Cornell LII and Justia pages are not reachable from the build sandbox, so every rule is marked `verbatim:false`, worded as a faithful paraphrase, and the lesson says so on screen. Established fact is stated plainly (DR-0100): thirty hours a cycle; three ethics, three cultural competence, one each sexual harassment, implicit bias, dementia; the one-time six hours of clinical supervision training first due for the cycle ending November 30, 2027; no CE at the first renewal; 3,000 post-MSW hours over at least two years, an LCSW supervisor averaging four hours a month, groups of five at most; a minor twelve or older may consent, eight ninety-minute sessions under seventeen until parental consent or two documented attempts plus the minor's written consent; an Illinois license for a client located in Illinois; ANCRA retraining every three years; 988 by call or text. Genuinely open points are flagged narrowly in `smeConfirm` (whether the six supervision hours sit inside the thirty; the dementia trigger; the Social Work Licensure Compact's status in Illinois; the retention period, which the Confidentiality Act does not set; the DCFS hotline number, not carried from memory).
6. **One training a week.** `buildWeeklyPlan` schedules every course once, one per week, rotating the ten fields, each carrying its Illinois lesson; open weeks are honest gaps. The Training map leads with it; the 24-hours-a-month runway stays beneath as the multi-year record.
7. **A therapist schedules a lesson for a client.** Migration 0189 `tlc_lesson_assignments`: instance-scoped, RLS on; the therapist (an office member) reads, writes, updates and deletes rows they created; the client reads rows addressed to the email on their verified session and may mark one reviewed, a trigger holding every other column fixed; NULL-safe guards; both overlays re-run. No client health information: the lesson id and title, a date, a short note. `lib/tlc-assignments.js` is the seam (validated on the device, bounded, fail-soft); the pure half is `lib/tlc-assignments-core.js`. On Training: "Assign to a client" on every lesson for staff; a "For you" area for a signed-in client (open the lesson onto Lessons with it expanded; mark reviewed); an "Assigned" area for the therapist with the client's review state.
8. **The hand-offs are proven end to end** in the real door: Team → Open Onboarding lands on Onboarding's Invite area; approve a packet → the Roster area lists the card → Find your therapist shows it; Team → Open Training lands on Training with Lessons selected and the Assigned area on the strip.

Rendered under the course's lessons for staff only ("For Christina to evaluate · from MVP to the hard-coded workflow").
