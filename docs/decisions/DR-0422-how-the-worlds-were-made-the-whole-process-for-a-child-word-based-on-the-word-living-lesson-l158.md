## What the Word supplies

The Person before the beginning (Genesis 1:1; Psalms 90:2; John 1:1-3; Colossians 1:16-17; Proverbs 8:22-30). The method, speaking (Psalms 33:6, 9; Hebrews 11:3; 2 Peter 3:5; Psalms 148:5; Jeremiah 10:12). The seven days in the text's own order, each with its pattern of word, fulfilment, verdict, naming, evening and morning (Genesis 1:2-31; 2:1-3), with the Maker's own account of day three (Job 38:8-11), the lights as clock and calendar (Genesis 1:14; Psalms 147:4; Isaiah 40:26; Psalms 19:1-3), people as the point of day six (Genesis 1:26-28; 2:7; 2:18-23; Psalms 8:4-6; 139:14), and the week traced to the making (Exodus 20:11). Why it matters (Psalms 24:1; Nehemiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:8; 45:18; Acts 17:24-25; Romans 1:20; Revelation 4:11). The holding and the song in the bands (Hebrews 1:3; Job 38:4, 7; Psalms 104:24, 30; 121:4).

## Reticence kept (DR-0098 / DR-0100)

Two places the text does not explain itself are taught as reticence and pinned by the gate: the source of day-one light, and the missing verdict on day two. The lesson says what the Word says and stops where it stops; it does not stage the origins contest, and it does not supply an explanation for the days beyond the text's own six days, evening and morning, restated at Sinai.
