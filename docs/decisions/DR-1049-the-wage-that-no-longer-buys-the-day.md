## The Word settles what the debate cannot — the measure, and the hire

**Word first.** The wage that will not buy the day is named exactly, and in context from Yahweh's own command two verses earlier: "Consider your ways" (Haggai 1:5), then "he that earneth wages earneth wages to put it into a bag with holes" (Haggai 1:6). It is reasoned from, not merely cited: the bag is not stolen and the wage is not withheld — the earning is real and the holding fails, which is precisely what a sixth straight month of prices outrunning pay does to a household. **The measured wage against the measured bread.** Revelation 6:5-6 was verified before anything was built on it: the rider carries "a pair of balances in his hand" (Revelation 6:5), and the voice prices the day — "A measure of wheat for a penny, and three measures of barley for a penny" (Revelation 6:6). The Word explains the Word: a penny is a day's wage, because the householder "had agreed with the labourers for a penny a day" (Matthew 20:2). A day's work for a day's grain, and the oil and the wine untouched. **A just measure is Yahweh's own requirement, with His reason stated:** Leviticus 19:35-36 (and He signs it, "I am the LORD your God"), Proverbs 11:1, Proverbs 16:11 ("all the weights of the bag are his work"), Proverbs 20:10, Micah 6:10-11, Deuteronomy 25:13-15, Amos 8:5 — the Word's standing judgement on a debased measure, **however** it is debased, which is why it reaches a currency and not only a merchant's scale. **The hire is owed, in full and on time:** Leviticus 19:13, Deuteronomy 24:14-15, Luke 10:7, 1 Timothy 5:18, Matthew 20:8, Jeremiah 22:13 — and withheld wages "crieth" into "the ears of the Lord of sabaoth" (James 5:4). That is the Word over the 62% datum and over the Netflix cut alike. **The barns and the buildout:** Luke 12:15-21 and 1 Timothy 6:17 speak to a buildout whose own champion approved nine to twelve figures "before seeing any results" — not against building, against being rich toward self and not toward Yahweh. **The believer's posture, collapsed into neither fear nor indifference:** Matthew 6:31-33 with Matthew 6:11, 1 Timothy 6:6-10, Philippians 4:11-12, Hebrews 13:5, Proverbs 30:8-9, Proverbs 22:7 on the borrower, Psalms 37:25 and Philippians 4:19. **And the Resources (DR-0530), capitalized in OUR voice only:** Wisdom builds the house, Understanding establishes it, Knowledge fills the chambers (Proverbs 24:3-4) — inside the quotation the KJV's own lowercase "wisdom", "understanding" and "knowledge" are left exactly as written, which the lesson points out on purpose; and Love is senior, for without charity "it profiteth me nothing" (1 Corinthians 13:3). Anchor: Haggai 1:5-6; Proverbs 11:1 — "the measure can be debased; the Measurer cannot."

**Stewardship:** a household that knows its own numbers (Proverbs 27:23) before it argues about anyone else's; a church that pays its own people first and on the day (Leviticus 19:13); an employer who does not quietly add the work of a new tool to an old wage; a family that stores in the good months (Genesis 41:35-36) and borrows with its eyes open (Proverbs 22:7).
