- **Date:** 2026-09-02
- **Principles:** SPOKEN-TEACHINGS-ARE-BUILD-INPUT, WORD-FIRST (DR-0098), THREE-TIER-HONESTY (DR-0100), VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE (DR-0076), TRUST-BUT-VERIFY (DR-0190), RELIGION-AND-RELATIONSHIP

## Directive

Darrell, 2026-09-02, bringing in a fourth clip — a panel debate between an
unmarried man opposing the **legal** half of modern marriage and a married man
defending it. The unmarried speaker's question, demanded as a yes or no:

> *"Would you sign a contract that the other party is incentivized to break?"*

The married speaker answers around it, then attacks the asker: *"you're not
qualified to open your mouth right now. You're not married."* Both men interrupt
throughout. One repeatedly calls the other feminine, tells him to *"control the
panty,"* and calls him *"a boy trapped in a grown man's body."*

This is the **capstone of four** (L114, L119, L120, L121): what a man brings,
what her post actually is, the warmth that keeps a house, and finally how to
argue about all of it without forfeiting.

## The decision, in six parts

### 1. Answer the question — do not dodge it as the married speaker does

It is a serious question and it has an answer, in two halves. **As a contract,
no** — nobody rational signs terms that reward the other side for leaving, and
the man raising it is not a coward. **Which is exactly why Yahweh never made
marriage one.** He entered covenant with a party He had *already announced would
break it* (Deuteronomy 31:16), described the moment as a wedding (Ezekiel 16:8),
named the breach in this debate's own language (Jeremiah 3:20), commanded a
prophet to live the picture (Hosea 1:2), and settled it at the cross for people
with no incentive to keep anything (Romans 5:8).

The question is answerable; the **frame** is what is wrong. That sequence does
more for a doubting young man than any defence of the institution, because it
stops arguing that the risk is small and shows Whose pattern taking it is.

### 2. Matthew 22:21 ends the spiritual-or-legal fight

The married speaker reaches for this verse and garbles it. Intact: *"Render
therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things
that are God's."* Caesar keeps a register — licence, filing, property law.
Yahweh holds the covenant. **Both exist, neither replaces the other**, so
refusing the paper does not make the covenant holier and signing it does not
make the covenant a transaction.

### 3. The load-bearing premise is falsified from the Word, not from statistics

The whole structure rests on "before modernity, women weren't really working."
Proverbs 31:14,16,18 has her importing, buying a field, planting a vineyard and
evaluating her own margin; Luke 8:3 has women funding the Lord's ministry from
their own substance. This is L119's ground, and it is where a Word-first house
settles a factual claim.

### 4. Three honest tiers, middle one included (DR-0100)

**Tier 1, plainly:** incentives shape behaviour and the civil apparatus really is
a separate instrument — a man noticing that is not a coward, and the room should
stop calling him one. **Tier 2, named and deliberately NOT adjudicated:** the
historical account of why those statutes were written, and the
benefits-the-woman-only claim, were measured by *neither* speaker; naming them
unmeasured is the honest move, and smearing the whole subject as uncertain would
be the dishonest one. **Tier 3, corrected:** the conclusion does not follow —
unfavourable civil terms and withholding a covenant are two different decisions.

Psalm 15:4 is the hinge: *"He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not."*
The Word takes the young man's caution **more** seriously than he does — do not
vow lightly, better not to vow at all than to vow and not pay (Ecclesiastes 5:5;
Numbers 30:2) — while closing the escape: a promise conditional on the other
party's incentives is not a covenant on bad terms, it is not a covenant.

### 5. The qualification fight, settled better than either man

The status attack is forbidden by name (1 Timothy 4:12); the test is fruit
(Matthew 7:16); lived experience genuinely does qualify for teaching office, so
the married speaker was reaching for something real (1 Timothy 3:4-5). And it
ends on the fact neither man reached: **Paul wrote most of the New Testament's
marriage instruction while unmarried** (1 Corinthians 7:8). Applied
consistently, the rule would delete the very chapters the married speaker was
standing on.

### 6. The manner forfeited it — for BOTH men

A yes-or-no demanded and talked over (Proverbs 18:13); a man who cannot receive
the answer was not asking (Proverbs 18:2); heat that cannot produce
righteousness (James 1:19-20); contempt-names under judgement (Matthew 5:22);
"feminine" as a slur when the meekest man on earth was Moses (Numbers 12:3) and
Yahweh made both in His image (Genesis 1:27); and the verdict — *"I am become as
sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal"* (1 Corinthians 13:1). **You can hold the
correct position and still be noise.**

Naming only the cruder man would have made the lesson the thing it corrects, so
both are corrected and the gate asserts it.

**The close:** two men argued at length about what marriage **costs** a man and
neither once asked what a man is **for** in it. That is L114's question and
L120's. A conversation about marriage conducted entirely as risk analysis has
already lost the thing it is arguing about.

## Names are deliberately absent — and that is a gate

The transcript reaches us through automatic captioning that garbles both
speakers' names into several different spellings across the same clip. Attaching
real, identifiable men to an unflattering account **on a guess** is the
fabrication DR-0076 forbids. The lesson says UNMARRIED SPEAKER and MARRIED
SPEAKER, states why in its own text, and carries `DO NOT NAME THE SPEAKERS` into
the facilitator notes so a future teacher inherits the constraint. All three are
asserted by the gate.

## The REASONED pass found three of my own over-claims (DR-0281 / DR-0239 dim. 8)

Darrell asked, mid-delivery, whether the Ways and documentation had actually been
reviewed. They had not been — not to the standard. Reading
`COMPREHENSIVE-REVIEW-STANDARD.md` and running its **dimension 8** by hand (the
Word's accuracy **REASONED**, not only quoted) caught three errors in this lesson
that **every gate had passed**, because each one lived *between* quotations that
were already letter-perfect:

1. **Matthew 22:21 stretched past its occasion.** The draft said Jesus "ALREADY
   SETTLED" the spiritual-or-legal fight. That verse answers a question about
   **tribute money**. The principle transfers cleanly; the ruling was not about a
   marriage licence. Borrowing the Lord's authority for a claim He did not make
   there is the exact failure DR-0281 names. The movement now discloses the
   occasion, says what transfers is the PRINCIPLE, and states that He did not
   rule on marriage law in that moment.
2. **The transfer was unlicensed.** L120 carefully licensed its Revelation 2 →
   marriage move with Ephesians 5:32. This lesson made the same kind of move —
   Yahweh's covenant with Israel → a human marriage — and assumed it. Without the
   licence the whole answer is an analogy we assembled. Ephesians 5:32 now carries
   it in text.
3. **The Paul claim was one letter too wide.** The draft said he was unmarried
   "when he wrote" the New Testament's marriage instruction and named Ephesians 5.
   No text states his situation at that writing; 1 Corinthians 7 states it inside
   the chapter itself. The claim is scoped to what the verse supports, and says so
   out loud.

All three are now **pinned in the gate**, so they cannot silently regress —
`living-lessons-l121-verses.test.js` asserts the disclosed occasion, the licence,
and the scoped claim. That is DR-0239 dimension 7: every miss leaves a machine
check behind.

**The honest generalisation:** a verbatim gate is necessary and not sufficient. It
proved 201 spans letter-perfect across these three lessons and could not see any
of these three errors, because none of them is a misquotation. Dimension 8 is the
only thing that catches this class, and it is run by hand.
