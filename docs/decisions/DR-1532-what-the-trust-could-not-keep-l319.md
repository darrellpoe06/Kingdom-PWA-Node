## Context

The summary relays Barron's reading of SEC Form 4 filings: two ten-year GRATs
funded by Jensen Huang and his wife in March 2016 ended on 2026-03-18 and
delivered 58,962,602 Nvidia shares into a family remainder trust, reported above
$10.5 billion, none sold. On 2026-11-12 he resigned as chief executive after
reports of affairs outside his marriage. The money plan held to the day; the
heart plan was never written. A money-and-heart teaching: a Living Lesson.

## Decision

1. **Honour the plan, then teach what it could not keep.** Planning for heirs is
   wisdom (Proverbs 13:22; Luke 14:28); a trust holds shares, not a heart
   (Proverbs 22:1; 4:23; Matthew 16:26; Psalms 127:1).
2. **Tiered.** Figures stated as reported; GRAT mechanics stated as law; his
   admission rendered, never quoted.
3. **Left out, and gated:** the unproven espionage allegation (Exodus 23:1) and
   the children's names (the filings name no beneficiary).
4. **No gloating, no advice.** Every band carries Proverbs 24:17 and Galatians
   6:1; the lesson and card say not financial, tax or legal advice.
