## Context

The newsletter's lead story reports two Western companies releasing open-weight A.I. models, and supplies the most useful sentence in the issue as its own definition: "Open weight means that, unlike with closed weight models, users can download and customize them with their own proprietary data." It reports a US-based firm launching a first model said to be adept at coding and agentic work, and a France-based firm debuting a model nicknamed for a reported trillion parameters. It reports that open weights are cheaper to run than closed models it calls difficult-to-budget, that Western buyers are hesitant over models of Chinese origin on security grounds, that the incumbents "may have new competition as they prepare to go public", and — to its credit — the liability on its own good news: "By putting powerful models that anyone can tweak into circulation, it creates the potential for even more cybersecurity issues", with the publisher's answer attributed, that "more availability of models can help improve defenses and allows researchers to audit the technology".

This is not news to this house; it is this course's founding thesis arriving as a headline. Our own machines already serve open-weights models, with the CPU-only ceiling said in the same breath every time. So the lesson's work was not to report a disruption but to teach the distinction the reporting collapses, and to put the Word over the question underneath it.

The distinction, and the spine: **the industry announced OPEN weights; Yahweh has always required a JUST weight.** Openness is a property of ACCESS; justness is a property of TRUTH. Access is a necessary condition of inspection, access is not inspection, and inspection is not conformity to a true standard. "A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight." (Proverbs 11:1) "all the weights of the bag are his work" (Proverbs 16:11) "Shall I count them pure with the wicked balances" (Micah 6:11) The hinge of the week follows from it: a weights file is constitutionally a thing that may be added to and diminished from, so it is never the standard; the standard is the one thing fenced against both (Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:5-6; Psalms 119:89). The model is modifiable, the Word is not — therefore the Word stays in the text on disk and checks the model, and the direction of verification never reverses.

## Decision

- Ship sov35 as described, authored once in `sovereign-ai-class.js`, with twelve quiz questions, eleven benefits, ten facilitator talking points, eight discussion prompts, a `howToRun` matched to the 75-minute session shape, and the `rpe` primitive pointed at a real decision the reader actually makes.

- **Correct week 34's order assertion rather than work around it.** `sovereign-ai-sov34-verses.test.js` asserted that week 34 was LAST in the series, and its own proven-to-catch block asserted that appending a `sov35-*` entry must fail. "Last" was never the property that gate wanted; it was a proxy for it while 34 happened to be the newest week, and a proxy that turns the next lesson's merge red is the wrong proxy. It now asserts the real property, the ORDER, in both directions: every earlier week is lower, every later week is higher. Week 35's own gate is written the same way from birth, so a sibling lane landing week 36 is not a red build while a week filed out of order still fails. This is the one file outside this lesson's own scope that was touched, and it was touched because the alternative was leaving a gate that forbids the next lesson.
- Regenerate the four-band baseline from the real catalog with `course-band-baseline-write.mjs` (never by hand) and move the pinned count 195 → 196 with its dated note.
- Keep the ledger's `Next ID` correct at DR-0792; on a merge collision run `scripts/resolve-ledger-conflicts.mjs`.
