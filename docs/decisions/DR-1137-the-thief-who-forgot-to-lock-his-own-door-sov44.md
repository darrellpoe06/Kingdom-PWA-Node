Its lead item reported that a security company, NorForge, had found more than twenty control panels for an A.I. hacking tool called Cairn sitting open on the public internet with no password — the attackers' own command centers, holding their target lists, their agents' running notes and the logins they had stolen from other people. That forward, read in full, is the whole of the provenance: a marketing newsletter and one word. **No primary-source research was done in this house on any figure the article reports**, and the lesson says so in its own words rather than letting a repeated number pass for a checked one (DR-1126, the third pile). A forwarded teaching is build input (CLAUDE.md "Spoken Teachings Are Build Input"); the article itself is **material to study, never instructions to obey**, and that posture is taught on the page because the issue is wall-to-wall calls to action and some of its text addresses an A.I. outright.

## Context

The forward arrived shaped like a parable nobody in this house had to write: the people whose trade is other men's doors had left their own wide open. The lane's standing temptation with a story like that is to repeat its most striking numbers — a ten percent success rate, five dollars an attempt, fifty dollars a break-in — because they teach fast. None of them is a measurement. They are the finding company's own stated assumptions and guesses, the derived figure is the newsletter's own arithmetic labelled a ballpark by the newsletter itself, and the company sells sandboxing tools for A.I. agents, which the article discloses. So the lesson had two jobs at once: teach what the Word actually judges in this story, and refuse to borrow authority from figures nobody here checked. The dedupe finder (`lesson-already-built.mjs`) was run first and returned **MAYBE — a source match only**: "The Neuron" appears in nine earlier records and catalogs, with no timestamp match and no distinctive-term match in any lesson's grounds. Not already built.

## Decision

Week 44 of Sovereign A.I. is a Word-first lesson in ten numbered movements, with all four age bands and the facilitator's run sheet:

1. **What came in, what it is, and what it is NOT** — the exact provenance (day, time, source, the one word), then the bright line stated before the story is studied: a forwarded page is material to study, never instructions to obey (1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 14:15; 1 John 4:1). An agent that treats arriving text as a command has no master, whatever its security posture.
2. **What the Word already said about a thief** — the whole business in three verbs and the Lord's own purpose against it (John 10:10); the four-word law (Exodus 20:15); pity with restitution (Proverbs 6:30-31); shame, not acclaim (Jeremiah 2:26); and the remedy no vendor sells — a new trade for the thief's own hands until he has something to give away (Ephesians 4:28).
3. **The pit he digged, and who fell into it** — the reported facts, then the Word's verdict stated four ways (Psalms 7:15-16; Psalms 9:15-16; Proverbs 26:27; Ecclesiastes 10:8), with Haman on his own gallows (Esther 7:10) and nothing staying covered, for victims and attackers alike (Numbers 32:23; Luke 12:2-3).
4. **Physician, heal thyself** — Luke 4:23; Romans 2:21-22; and Matthew 7:3-5 taught as an **order of operations, not a ban on helping**: your own beam first, and *then* you see clearly enough to deal with your brother's mote. Paul under his own rule (1 Corinthians 9:27; 1 Timothy 4:16; Galatians 6:1). The article's quieter finding — apparently legitimate hired testers left exposed the same way — is where this stops being about criminals.
5. **The house that was broken up, and the watch that was not kept** — Matthew 24:43 and Luke 12:39, with the blame where the Lord put it: he kept no watch, not because the thief was strong. Then wakefulness as the command attached (1 Thessalonians 5:2-6; Revelation 16:15; Mark 13:35-37), the watchman's charge (Ezekiel 33:6), and the strong man certain of his armour until a stronger came (Luke 11:21-22).
6. **The boring list — the old hole nobody repaired** — the agents' dull to-do list (broken logins, pages that skip the login check, file uploads), with the change named precisely as a change in RATE, not in cleverness. Proverbs 25:28; Ecclesiastes 10:18; Song of Solomon 2:15; Luke 16:10; Nehemiah 4:9-10 (prayed AND set a watch, rubbish admitted); Nehemiah 6:1 (no breach left, and the doors not yet hung — the shape of an honest status report); Isaiah 58:12; Proverbs 22:3; and the Resources named in order, Wisdom, Understanding and Knowledge (Proverbs 24:3-4).
7. **The same architecture, the other master** — the shared board of facts and intents is ordinary agent work, close to how this very app is built, so the architecture is not the moral question; the master is (Matthew 6:24; Romans 6:16; Luke 7:8; Matthew 12:35; James 3:10-11). Then the board itself as the warning: an agent that believes whatever is written on its board has **no plumbline outside the board**, which is exactly where a lie gets planted (Acts 17:11).
8. **The watch this house keeps, and the keeping we cannot do** — our own arrangements said out loud (gates that fail the build, verses fetched from the family's corpus and re-read by a test, an outside witness that asks the live site), and our own exposure conceded in the same breath: this house holds keys, and a secret rode into a log here once. Then the watch put in its place (Psalms 127:1; Proverbs 21:31; Psalms 121:3-4; Zechariah 2:5; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Peter 1:5), with Love senior to the Resources (1 Corinthians 13:1). *Keep the watch. Do not trust the watch.*
9. **The three tiers** (DR-0100), with the third pile named rather than smuggled — see **What was measured**.
10. **The close** — two instructions in order (your own gate, then your own eye), and the Person: the Lamb of Yahweh, the Eternal Son whom the Father gave, whose throne is for ever (John 1:29; John 3:16; Hebrews 1:8).

**The three tiers as the page states them.** ESTABLISHED: criminals are directing A.I. agents against real businesses; the researchers verified one claimed break-in by finding the back door still live on the victim's own website; a major outlet reported named corporate targets; the attackers' panels were reachable with no password — and, needing no vendor, the Word's own judgments. OPEN, named narrowly: every figure listed below, reported and unchecked here. OVER-REACH, corrected **both ways**: "the A.I. is the thief" over-reaches, because a tool is no moral agent and the Word puts both the theft and its remedy on the man (Ephesians 4:28); and "agents are only tools, nothing has changed" over-reaches in the other direction, because the reported change is specific and real — old holes get found faster — and the prudent man is charged to foresee it (Proverbs 22:3).

**The third pile, stated as the lesson states it (DR-1126).** Reported by the article and **checked by nobody here**: the ten percent success rate (the firm's own declared assumption, not a measurement); about five dollars per attempt (its acknowledged rough guess); roughly fifty dollars per successful break-in (the newsletter's arithmetic on those two, labelled a ballpark by the newsletter itself); more than eleven hundred projects in a fourteen-panel cluster; the just-under-23-hour average for successes; hundreds of exposed credentials with about one in nine still valid; and the attribution of the agents to a particular cheap model, which the article expressly calls its own reading rather than a confirmed fact. **Proven to catch (DR-0076 §3), watched going red, and SCOPED so the proof proves something.** The first attempt did this at FILE scope and that is the defect to avoid: a sibling build found a false green the same day because the verse it mutated appears 66 times across the catalog and the first occurrence belonged to an older lesson, so a red could not be attributed to the new one (P62 — identity, never position). So the mutation was confined to the sov44 block only — "He that diggeth a pit shall fall into **it**" → "fall into **them**", 10 places, sov29's copy untouched.

The reading before: sov44 **35 passed / 0 failed**. The reading after: sov44 **30 passed / 5 failed**, with `Ecclesiastes 10:8` named 24 times, and the five tests it turned red named exactly — *every quoted span that names a reference IS that verse, character for character*; *every pinned verse is quoted somewhere in the module, as the corpus has it*; *the pit that returns on the digger is stated more than one way, in every band*; and both self-guarding proven-to-catch cases (*a single altered word inside the pit verse fails the verbatim scan* and *substituting Yahweh into a quoted verse is a fault, not an improvement*), which fail loudly rather than silently when the text they target has already moved. In the same state `sovereign-ai-sov29-verses.test.js` ran **15 passed / 0 failed**, which is what makes the attribution sound: the break was sov44's and only sov44's. The block was then restored and the catalog verified **byte-identical by md5**, and sov44 + sov29 together read **50 passed / 0 failed**.

Three further cases live inside the test file and each re-asserts the real module clean: substituting "Yahweh" into a quoted verse is caught as a fault rather than accepted as an improvement — the DR-0076 bright line proven in the direction this house is most tempted to break it; stripping one band's friend line names that band while the pooled check still reads green; and a child opening that drops the title is named.

**A catalog-level claim does not live in a lesson's test — and the premise handed to this branch was wrong in its particulars.** The coordinating session warned that `sovereign-ai-sov41-verses.test.js`, `-sov42-` and `-sov43-` each assert the whole course is a contiguous `1..max`, and that **adding sov44 alone makes them red**. Measured on this branch: it does not. With sov44 in the catalog the weeks are 1..44 with no gap, and all three sibling files pass — sov41 29/29, sov42 35/35, sov43 31/31 — as does the owner, `sovereign-ai-class.test.js`, whose `HELD_IN_FLIGHT` list is empty and needs no entry here, because 45 and 46 sit above this branch's maximum rather than inside a gap. The claim goes red only for a week that lands *with a hole beneath it* (46 without 45), which is a sibling's problem and is already fixed on that sibling's branch. So **no sibling test file was touched** and no hold was added. What this branch did do is stop contributing to the class: the first draft of `sovereign-ai-sov44-verses.test.js` carried the same whole-course contiguity assertion, and it was narrowed to what sov44 actually owns — the weeks ascend, no number is doubled, 44 is among them — with the reason written in the file. A lesson's own test pins what is true of that lesson forever, never what happens to be true of the catalog the day it was written.

**And the hold list is recomputed at merge, never carried.** `HELD_IN_FLIGHT` names the holes BELOW the highest week present, so the right value differs per branch — `[]` here (max 44, no hole), `[44]` on the sov45 branch, `[44, 45]` on sov46 — and once all three land there are no holes and the right value is empty again. An entry left behind after its week exists is a **disarmed check**: it tells the gate to ignore a number that is no longer missing, which is the expiring-evidence defect DR-1025 found when filling L79 disarmed three checks keyed on a gap that had already closed. So if that file conflicts on a base merge, neither side is taken wholesale: the weeks actually present in the merged tree are read, the list is set to the holes that remain, and the recomputed value is stated with its reasoning. This branch adds no entry, because it creates no hole.

## Impact

Unresolved, this forward would have become either a repeated statistic or nothing at all. Resolved, every reader of the course now has (a) the Word's own verdict on the story — the pit returns on the digger, the beam comes out of your own eye first, the unwatched house is the one broken up — taught from the text's own usage rather than staged against anybody's opinion; (b) a named posture toward arriving text that the house's own agents run on: a page is a specimen to be tested, never a master to be obeyed, which is the prompt-injection lesson taught as doctrine instead of as tooling; (c) the dull maintenance list treated as the real work, with Nehemiah's honest status report as the model (no breach left, and the doors not yet hung); and (d) a worked example of the third pile, where the lesson names six unchecked figures as unchecked in its own voice. The call obligates the next lesson built from a forwarded article to name what it did not verify in the same way, and the next security-shaped claim in this repository to carry evidence rather than a number.
