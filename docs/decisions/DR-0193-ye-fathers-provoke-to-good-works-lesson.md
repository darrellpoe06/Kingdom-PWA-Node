## The lesson (L46)

**The two provocations — one active stirring, opposite aim.** FORBIDDEN: provoke to
wrath/anger (Ephesians 6:4; Colossians 3:21). COMMANDED: "provoke unto love and to
good works" (Hebrews 10:24). A father is never neutral; he stirs — the only question
is toward what.

**Why "ye fathers" explicitly (the answer to Darrell's question):**
1. He is the named HEAD and the child's **first image of the Father** — "of whom the
   whole family in heaven and earth is named" (Ephesians 3:14-15).
2. His strength can **build or crush** — the danger named to him is precise, "lest
   they be discouraged" (Colossians 3:21).
3. He is the **covenant hinge** — the testimony passes through the fathers
   (Deuteronomy 6:6-7; Psalm 78:5-6), and the OT closes on his heart (Malachi 4:6).
4. His discipline is the **earthly template of God's** (Hebrews 12:9; Proverbs 3:12).

**The active father:** PRAYS (Job 1:5), TEACHES (Deuteronomy 6:7), TRAINS (Proverbs
22:6), PROVOKES toward good — joy is a child who "walk[s] in truth" (3 John 1:4). And
comfort for the fatherless: "A father of the fatherless... is God" (Psalm 68:5; Luke
11:13).

## Neuroscience as WITNESS — verified BEFORE ship (DR-0190/DR-0192), tiered (DR-0100)

Independently traced to primary sources, NOT priors:

- **TIER A** — (a) involved fatherhood **lowers a man's testosterone**; his biology
  re-tunes toward care (Gettler, McDade, Feranil, Kuzawa, *PNAS* 2011, Cebu
  longitudinal, N=624). (b) Father involvement is tied to better child outcomes
  (Sarkadi et al., *Acta Paediatrica* 2008), real but **more modest** than slogans
  (McLanahan, Tach, Schneider, *Annual Review of Sociology* 2013). (c) **Paternal
  hostility/harshness** is the parenting most tied to child depression/anxiety/low
  self-worth (Kane & Garber meta-analysis, *Clinical Psychology Review* 2004) — the
  lab's own witness to "lest they be discouraged."
- **TIER B (attributed)** — the paternal caregiving brain (Abraham/Feldman, *PNAS*
  2014); Paquette's "activation relationship"; rough-and-tumble play → self-regulation,
  quality-dependent (StGeorge & Freeman, *Infant Mental Health J* 2017).
- **TIER C — REFUSED:** the viral "fatherless-home" stat sheet (63%/85%/90%/4.6x)
  traces to no primary dataset (the compiler conceded it faulty); kept OUT
  deliberately. And "a child's God-image is shaped by his father" is held as
  **theology** (Ephesians 3:15), never asserted as a study.

Audience-facing text attributes generally and asserts NO bare statistic (the
research-integrity gate passes); citations live in the lesson comment + here.
