## Context

Each lesson's gate asserts `termFaults(M.lesson)` — the adult field. A reader does not read the adult field: a reader reads **one band**, and for whoever opens it that band is the whole lesson.

## What was measured

**It leaked, on a live lesson.** sov71's gate checked the adult field only and read green. The catalog-wide `the-plain-meaning-comes-first` gate caught **`assay`** in its senior band — the first entry in `HARD_TERMS`, and the word on Darrell's phone that founded DR-0521 — and the miss surfaced as that gate's shrink-only debt rising 65 → 66 rather than as sov71's own failure.

**Exactly two other gates carry the narrow form.** Measured across the 16 gates that call `termFaults`: `sov69` and `sov70`. No others.

**Both are clean today.** Probed before changing anything: 6 reader places each (`lesson`, `bigIdea`, child, youth, teen, senior), **0 faults**. So this closes a latent hole rather than fixing a live defect — the hole was closed by luck, and is now closed by machinery.

**The narrow form provably cannot see it.** With `assay` planted in sov69's senior band, the narrow assertion reports **36 passed**; the widened one fails naming the place (`senior: ["assay"]`). The planted module file and test were both restored byte-identical with `cmp`.

## Impact

A per-lesson gate that checks one field pushes band-only defects into slower, catalog-wide gates, where they read as a global debt figure moving instead of as this lesson's failure. The author who could fix it cheapest never sees it.

## Decision

**1. Both gates check every reader place** — `lesson`, `bigIdea` and all four bands — and the failure message names which place, so it is actionable rather than merely red.

**2. The original case is kept, not replaced.** Each file still asserts separately that the terms *that lesson introduces* are glossed at first use, which is a different claim from the registry's and worth keeping.

**3. Nothing was added to `HARD_TERMS` and no baseline was touched.** Weakening a cue or recording a new fault so a lesson passes is the edit `plain-before-the-term.js` exists to prevent, and its own header says so. The sov71 builder fixed its prose rather than the registry; this record follows that.
