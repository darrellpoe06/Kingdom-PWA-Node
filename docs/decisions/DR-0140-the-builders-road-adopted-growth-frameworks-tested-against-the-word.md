## The research, processed per DR-0100's three tiers

The interview's six teachings were each tested against the Word before adoption:

1. **Demand testing before building** (waiting lists, discussion groups, customer-needs analyses) — **ADOPTED WHOLE.** It IS Scripture's build order: *"Prepare thy work without... and afterwards build thine house"* (Proverbs 24:27); count the cost before the tower (Luke 14:28-30). It is also our own Verification Doctrine applied to commerce: "people want this" is a claim, and claims need evidence (real names). Bend: the test itself must be honest — a waiting list that exists, scarcity that is true; the existing `no-deceptive-funnel` guardrail governs.
2. **The 7-11-4 rule** (≈7 hours of content, ≈11 touchpoints, ≈4 platforms before a stranger trusts you) — **ADOPTED AS A NAMED HEURISTIC** (tier 2). A practitioner rule of thumb, not research we verified; every surface that carries it says so. The biblical constant beneath it: trust is earned in accumulated genuine value — faithful sowing over time (Galatians 6:9; Ecclesiastes 11:6).
3. **"Key Person of Influence" / personal branding** — **ADOPTED WITH THE WORD'S CORRECTION** (tier 3 on the over-reach). The practice (be visibly, genuinely good at your calling in public — Proverbs 22:29; Matthew 5:15-16) is kept. The over-reach (identity AS the brand, self-exaltation) is corrected: identity is in Christ; what is stewarded in public is a good NAME (Proverbs 22:1), and another's mouth does the praising (Proverbs 27:2).
4. **Name-Same-Fame-Aim-Game** (the five-part intro frame) — **ADOPTED WHOLE.** Clarity is kindness; *"if the trumpet give an uncertain sound, who shall prepare himself to the battle?"* (1 Corinthians 14:8). The claim linter still screens every distinctness claim.
5. **The leverage ladder** (skill → IP → media → data/software) — **ADOPTED WHOLE, WITH THE HOUSE AS PROOF.** PoeTech is the family's own worked example (skills → teaching → media → the app); the book flywheel (2026-06-25) already runs the IP→media rungs. Bend: the ladder serves people at scale — serve-not-extract.
6. **Environment over willpower** (change the room) — **ADOPTED WHOLE.** *"He that walketh with wise men shall be wise"* (Proverbs 13:20); module 4's group economics already named the first room — the Body pooling its strength.

Over all six stands the governing guard the interview does not carry: James 4:13-15 — the buy-and-sell-and-get-gain plan submitted to *"If the Lord will"* — and the soul-first order (3 John 1:2) that module 1 set.
