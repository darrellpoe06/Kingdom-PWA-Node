## Where the Word is reticent, and how the lesson stays with it

- **Hiram the bronze-worker's faith.** 1 Kings 7:14 gives his mother (of Naphtali) and his father (of Tyre) and his skill; it does not state his faith. The lesson says so and adds nothing. So 1 Kings 7:13-14 fits as skilled hands fetched from Tyre for the work, not as a proven unbeliever. The clear outside hands are Hiram king of Tyre's servants and the strangers of 2 Chronicles 2:17-18.
- **A sound board by name.** The Word gives the pattern, not the equipment; the lesson says "The Word names no sound board" and leaves application to the Spirit's governing of each house.
- **Why Jesus chose Judas.** The Word gives "that the scripture may be fulfilled" and no more; the lesson stops there.
- **2 Corinthians 2.** The lesson says "a man the church had punished" and does not claim he is the man of 1 Corinthians 5; the text does not say so.
