## Context

A title and keyword search filled the Learn Relationships shelf with a few dozen lessons. Darrell, rendered for meaning: almost every lesson about life is about relating; relationships are the whole range of emotion and the fruit of the Spirit, how humans relate in general, to Yahweh, to ourselves inside, and then outward, so a quality relationship with one person later has something to stand on. Then a second teaching widened it: our relationship with learning, study, mood, the hard necessary thing, change, another person's situation, the Word as mirror, the servant king, spirit, soul and body.

## Decision

1. **The definition is written down** as a Layer 3 foundation: the directions (Matthew 22:37-39: up, out, and in as thyself), the range (emotion governed, not absent: John 11:35, Ephesians 4:26, James 1:19-20), the measure (Galatians 5:22-23), Word first; then seven verified research citations with what was not read back named. It states the shelf's inclusion criteria in six groups.
2. **L344, How Relating Works** teaches the first teaching: four age versions, two parables, ten questions, facilitator. 106 verses filled from the corpus.
3. **L345, The Mirror** teaches the second (James 1:23-25 the mirror; Hebrews 4:12 soul and spirit; Mark 10:44 the servant king; Psalms 1:3 the tree; John 14:6). Where the Word is reticent (whether the human spirit pre-existed), the lesson teaches only what is written and says so.
