## The word, as spoken

Darrell, 2026-09-15: *"We are all children of Yahweh!!!!!!!!"* — *"Lesson!!!!!!!"* — *"No matter how old we will for ever be children to Him!!!!"*

## The two floors, kept as the Word keeps them

The lesson's one doctrinal decision: Darrell's sentence has two floors in the Word, and the lesson builds both without collapsing either. **By making, all** — one Father made every person (Malachi 2:10; Acts 17:26-29, said to an unconverted crowd; Luke 3:38; Genesis 1:27; Psalms 100:3). **By adoption, offered to all and received** — power to become the sons of Yahweh is given to those who receive Him (John 1:12-13; Galatians 4:4-7; 3:26; Romans 8:14-17; Ephesians 1:5; 1 John 3:1-2; 2 Corinthians 6:18; Revelation 21:7). The gate pins both sentences. Neither floor is softened into the other: the first grounds the dignity of every person; the second is the open door, and the lesson says plainly that it is received, not assumed (DR-0098: teach what is written; DR-0100: state it plainly).

## What the Word supplies for "no matter how old"

The family word (Matthew 6:9; Luke 11:2; Matthew 7:9-11; Romans 8:15; Galatians 4:6). A child as the door and the posture, not a stage (Matthew 18:2-4; Mark 10:14-15; Matthew 11:25; Psalms 131:1-2). Carried to hoar hairs (Isaiah 46:3-4; Psalms 71:9, 18; 92:13-14; Deuteronomy 1:31; Hosea 11:1-4). Pity and rod (Psalms 103:13-14; Hebrews 12:5-11; Proverbs 3:11-12; Revelation 3:19). Little children, to the mature (John 13:33; 21:5; 1 John 2:1, 12-14; Galatians 4:19). The father who ran, and went out to the elder brother (Luke 15:20-24, 31-32; Jeremiah 3:19, 22). Never an orphan (Psalms 27:10; 68:5-6; John 14:18; Isaiah 66:13; 49:15-16; Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 91:4). The resemblance (1 Peter 1:14-17; Ephesians 5:1-2; Matthew 5:9, 44-45; Luke 6:35; Philippians 2:15; 1 John 5:1-2; Romans 8:19). In the bands: the shoulders (Luke 15:5) and the eagle (Deuteronomy 32:11).

## Boundaries

- The Genesis 6 / Job "sons of God" usage is not touched; the lesson stays with the human sonship the Word teaches directly.
- The prodigal is taught as the Lord told it, both sons; no verdict is added on the elder brother beyond the father's own words to him.
- Nothing in the lesson makes adoption automatic; nothing in it makes creation-fatherhood less than the Word says.
