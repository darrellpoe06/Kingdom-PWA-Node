## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-19, spoke a lesson into the platform (captured in
`docs/00-foundations/_root/KINGDOM-SYSTEMS-AND-THE-WAR-OF-THE-MIND.md`) and closed
it with a design law:

> "The PoeTech App and systems should reflect the importance of the Godhead and
> data-driven information flowing throughout the process for His Will... period.
> However we don't want to run away users who don't know they need Him and will
> love His Ways... opportunities and constraints."

Two poles that a lesser build treats as opposed — be explicitly God-centered, OR be
accessible to the unchurched — and that this platform must hold together.

## Decision

**The Godhead is explicit and non-negotiable at the core; the door is winsome.**

- **Explicit (period):** the Godhead is evident throughout, and data flows through
  every process *toward His Will* — never hidden, never diluted to a vague
  "spirituality," never both-sidesed for comfort. Grounded in `THE-WAY.md`, the
  Source-of-Answers rule, DR-0098 (teach the Word, do not debate it), and DR-0100
  (speak established fact).
- **Winsome (never off-putting):** the person who does not yet know they need Him is
  **met, not bludgeoned** — served genuinely first, so the Ways become lovable
  through lived excellence. Grounded in the Excellence Standard (religion AND
  relationship — "representatives of the King"), the Behavioral Mirror (DATA → TRUTH
  → IDENTITY → **INVITATION**, never condemnation), the mirror-that-never-judges
  (`QUALITY-OF-LIFE-AS-NORTH-STAR.md`), and the Council Chamber posture
  (Hear → Mirror → Anchor → **Invite**).

**The reconciliation:** the two poles are not in tension when excellence and
invitation are *how* the Godhead is made lovable rather than off-putting. God-explicit
core, winsome door.

## Bright lines (the constraints)

1. **Explicit ≠ preachy.** The Godhead is reflected through excellence, truth, and
   invitation — never forced as a toll-gate before help. Preachiness that walls out
   the unchurched violates this law as surely as hiding Him would.
2. **Winsome ≠ watered.** "Don't run them off" never means removing the Godhead,
   both-sidesing the Word, or trading truth for reach (DR-0098 / DR-0100). The line
   is **truth in love**, not truth withheld.
3. **Never auto-switch the posture.** A practical/dev-ops request is met practically
   (`MODE-ROUTING.md` — never preach at a wrench question); a seeking moment is met
   pastorally. The system reads the person; it does not force-convert the mode.
4. **Invitation, never coercion.** The mirror shows; it does not judge. The step
   toward Him is always the user's — the system equips the mind with the Word and
   opens the door; it does not shove (1 Peter 5:8's *may*, not *must*).

## Opportunities

- Excellence is the witness — a genuinely helpful, sovereign, beautiful system earns
  the trust that lets the Godhead be received rather than resented.
- Invitation scales where condemnation repels — the not-yet-believer uses the whole
  system, is served in truth, and meets Him at the *invite*, on their own step.
- Data-for-His-Will (not extraction — `DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT-NOT-EXTRACTION.md`) is the
  moat mainstream tech cannot copy; the God-explicit telos and the winsome door are
  the same differentiator.

## Consequences

- Every faith-, Body-, mind-, or His-Will-touching surface reads
