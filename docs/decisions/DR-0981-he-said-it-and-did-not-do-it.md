## Context

Darrell sent a photograph of his open Bible — **Numbers 14:8-12 in the English
Standard Version, every verse in that span marked in his own hand** — and wrote
one line over it:

> *"Lesson... He said! 'I will make of you a nation greater and mightier than
> they.'"*

Then, while the lesson was being built, he asked the question that decided its
whole shape:

> *"Did that happen or is it happening according to the Word?"*

A spoken or sent teaching is build input, not commentary (CLAUDE.md, 2026-07-03;
DR-0610 makes this lane a lane of record). The sentence he isolated is the last
thing inside his own highlight, and it is **Yahweh's** sentence, not Moses'.

## What was measured

**His question has a two-part answer, and the Word is precise about which part
is which.** Every verse below was fetched verbatim from
`app/public/bible/kjv` before a word of the lesson was written.

1. **It did not happen to Moses — he refused it twice.** The same offer was made
   at the golden calf first (**Exodus 32:10**) and again here
   (**Numbers 14:12**). It is the Abrahamic promise aimed at one man
   (**Genesis 12:2**). Moses never argued the people's merit: he argued
   Yahweh's name among the nations (**Numbers 14:13**, **14:16**) and prayed
   Yahweh's own self-proclamation back to Him (**Numbers 14:18**, from
   **Exodus 34:6**), and at the calf he held up the sworn oath
   (**Exodus 32:13**). The answer: *"I have pardoned according to thy word"*
   (**Numbers 14:20**); *"And the LORD repented of the evil which he thought to
   do unto his people"* (**Exodus 32:14**).
2. **The Word names the category itself.** *"had not Moses his chosen stood
   before him in the breach"* (**Psalm 106:23**) — so the theme is not imposed
   on the passage; Scripture supplies it.
3. **It did happen, slowly.** Pardon is not the absence of consequence:
   **Numbers 14:22-23**, **14:29**, forty years.
4. **A greater nation WAS made — out of the excuse.** Their stated reason for
   turning back was their own children (**Numbers 14:3**); Yahweh's answer:
   *"But your little ones, which ye said should be a prey, them will I bring
   in"* (**Numbers 14:31**), with Caleb and Joshua (**14:30**) and Caleb's
   *"another spirit"* (**14:24**).
5. **It is happening — the Word puts it in the present tense itself.**
   *"they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are
   come"* (**1 Corinthians 10:11**), *"let him that thinketh he standeth take
   heed lest he fall"* (**10:12**), and the diagnosis that is never the giants:
   *"they could not enter in because of unbelief"* (**Hebrews 3:19**), with the
   warning written to believers (**Hebrews 3:12**) and a daily remedy
   (**Hebrews 3:13**).
6. **The Intercession never steps out of the gap.** Moses stood in the breach
   and died outside the land (**Deuteronomy 34:4-5**); there is one counted
   worthier (**Hebrews 3:3**) who *"ever liveth to make intercession for them"*
   (**Hebrews 7:25**) and is doing it now (**Romans 8:34**) — so the nation is
   still being gathered (**1 Peter 2:9**).

**Measured on the build itself:** 33 distinct verses, every one compared
against the corpus before generation; adult lesson 1,372 words of authored
prose; bands at 0.56 / 0.66 / 0.63 / 0.63 of it against floors of 0.50 / 0.60 /
0.60 / 0.60.

## Impact

Unresolved, his word goes uncaptured, which breaks the covenant the
spoken-teachings rule records: he sends these here **because** they get built.
And the specific loss would be the answer to his question — a lesson that gave
only one half ("no, Moses refused") would be false to **14:29**, and one that
gave only the other half ("yes, they died in the wilderness") would be false to
**14:20**. The chapter requires both, and the reversal in **14:31** is the part
most readers walk past.

Reversible: one catalog entry, one dates line, one pinned test, two regenerated
files. No schema, no money, no product surface outside the Learn catalog.
Tier B — it is teaching the Word to his family and church, so it carries
verse-by-verse proof rather than a reading.

## Decision

1. **Living Lesson 240** — *He Said It And Did Not Do It: The Offer Moses
   Refused, The Breach He Stood In, And The Nation Made Of The Children They
   Gave Up On* — joins **Living Lessons** (devotional), self-paced, with all
   four age bands.
2. **His question is the lesson's structure, answered in BOTH halves**, and the
   pinned test asserts both in every band: the refusal and the breach, and the
   forty years. A band that dropped either would fail.
3. **The reversal is taught explicitly**: 14:3 and 14:31 are quoted in every
   band and the line between them is drawn out loud. The excuse became the
   inheritance.
4. **Scripture supplies the theme, not us** (DR-0098). Psalm 106:23 names "the
   breach" and 1 Corinthians 10:11 supplies the present tense, so neither the
   motif nor the application is asserted on our own authority.
5. **The hard clause is named, not avoided** (DR-0076 rule 8). The KJV ends
   Numbers 14:34 *"ye shall know my breach of promise"*; the lesson says so
   plainly, uses the verse for the forty years only, and builds no argument on
   that phrase. The test requires the flag to be present wherever that phrase
   appears.
6. **Our voice says Yahweh; every quotation is left exactly as written**
   (DR-0210 / DR-0076 bright line). The prose confesses Jesus as the Lamb of
   Yahweh and the Eternal Son; the KJV's "the LORD" inside every quoted span is
   untouched.
7. **The screenshot is the ESV; the lesson quotes the KJV on this device**, and
   says so, because that is the corpus that can actually be verified here. No
   verse is carried from memory.
8. **All three directions in the lesson and in every band** (DR-0733 /
   DR-0795), each with the skill and the rhythm — and the rhythm **names
   Deuteronomy 6:7's four places** rather than citing the reference, so a
   reader who does not know the verse can still act on it today.

## Verification

- `living-lessons-l240-verses.test.js` — **12 tests**, including a strict
  verbatim comparison of **every** `**KJV — Ref:** *"span"*` in the lesson and
  all four bands against `app/public/bible/kjv`, whitespace normalized and
  nothing else.
- **The check caught a real error before the lesson existed.** 1 Peter 2:9 was
  typed into the verse table as *"an holy priesthood"*; the corpus says
  *"an holy nation"* — **the one word this lesson turns on**. It was caught by
  comparing the whole table against the corpus before generating, and the
  mistake is now the file's proven-to-catch case.
- **Proven-to-catch:** restoring that single altered word makes the span check
  name `1 Peter 2:9`.
- **A green on a missing module is worth nothing, and that happened here.** The
  first insert landed inside `LIVING_LESSONS_SESSION_FLOW` instead of
  `LIVING_LESSONS_MODULES` — the file parsed, and the talk-direction check
  reported *every band whole* while the lesson was not in the catalog at all.
  The builder now anchors to the modules array, and the test asserts the module
  exists before it asserts anything about it.
- **The band floors were measured against the right yardstick on the second
  try.** The raw word counts cleared the floors; the gate counts **authored
  prose with quoted spans removed** (`scripts/full-levels.mjs`), by which the
  youth band was short by 43 words. It was fixed by authoring a movement the
  band genuinely lacked — Caleb's *"another spirit"* and Hebrews 3:13's daily
  remedy — not by padding. Final shares 0.56 / 0.66 / 0.63 / 0.63.
- **A regeneration was reverted rather than shipped.** Rewriting
  `full-levels-baseline.json` would have **deleted its historical note** (the
  2026-09-20 and 2026-10-10 annotations recording why two lessons left the debt
  list) for no gain: the gate compares `short` and `lessonsShort`, and this
  lesson adds no short band. The file is untouched and the gate passes.
- **11 lesson gates green, 177 tests**: full-levels, band-differentiation,
  age-appropriateness, id-collision, lesson-dates, course-band-coverage,
  talk-together, talk-about, course-bands-reach-the-reader,
  learn-catalog-render, and this lesson's own.
- `lesson-dates.json` and the course-band baseline regenerated with their own
  scripts, never hand-edited.
- **Not verified:** that the lesson reads well aloud to a child in his house.
  That is a reading, not a test, and it belongs to him. `re-review: 2026-10-19`.
