# DR-0350 — Hire through the TLC app: jobs on the door, the atomic hire, and the hand-off recorded

**Date:** 2026-09-10 · **Status:** accepted · **Tier:** B · **Area:** tlc · **Principles:** VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE, APP-IS-PRIMARY, REALITY-TRACE, TLC-FIREWALL, DATA-AS-EMPOWERMENT, DECISION-RECORDS

## Opportunities and constraints (the review Darrell asked for)

**Opportunities.** The second half of hiring already existed (DR-0344: the invite, the packet bound to the colleague's own login, approval writing the roster and the public card), so the first half is one migration and two surfaces, not a new system. The website already links the door, so a careers link on the site is one list served from one source (`?tlc=1&jobs=1`), never a second copy. Every application is text, so nothing in it is client health information and the TLC firewall holds. The hire is one atomic step, so the office sees one pipeline from applied to active on the platform.

**Constraints, stated plainly.** (1) The telehealth / scheduling platform the handbook names (SimplePractice, "Use of Approved Scheduling Platform") has no public API for adding a team member; the app records the hand-off and carries the message, it does not create the account. Christina confirms the platform name and the steps (`TLC_TELEHEALTH.confirm`). (2) The TLC office and the Poe family share one instance today, so the Members list on Governance shows family seats (a child) beside office seats; a separate office instance is a Tier B decision of its own, `re-review: 2026-09-24`. (3) The database knows five roles; the three member seats (supervisor, therapist, therapist-in-training) are one role with three titles, so the app cannot tell a supervisor from a trainee; that is a future column, not a today's one, `re-review: 2026-09-24`. (4) An anonymous apply is a public write, bounded: one open application per email per posting, five a day per email per office, every field capped, an audit row per application.

## Decisions

1. **A job is an office row; an open job is public.** Migration 0191 `tlc_jobs`: instance-scoped, RLS on; office members read, the owner/admin post, edit, open, close and remove (NULL-safe guards). `tlc_public_jobs()` (anon, SECURITY DEFINER, the `tlc_public_roster()` shape) returns only open postings.
2. **An application is written only through `tlc_apply()`.** `tlc_job_applications` has RLS on and no insert policy. The function refuses a closed job, a second open application from the same email to the same job, and a sixth in a day; it bounds every field in the same words the device uses (`lib/tlc-hiring.js` mirrors it); it writes an audit row. Applications are read and updated by the office owner/admin only; a member, the assistant and a viewer never see them. An update trigger holds who applied and to what fixed and stamps the telehealth hand-off.
3. **Hire is one transaction.** `tlc_application_hire()` (owner/admin) marks the application hired and mints the DR-0344 invite for that email in the same transaction, linking the invite id; hiring twice returns the same invite. The packet, approval, roster and card stay DR-0344's.
4. **The hand-off to the telehealth platform is recorded, not pretended.** `TLC_TELEHEALTH` names the platform from the handbook with `confirm: true`, the steps the office does there, and the message to the hire; the application row carries `telehealth_status` (not-started → invited → active), stamped with time and by whom on every change. The pipeline the office reads (applied · reviewed · hired/invite sent · packet · platform) is computed from the row and the packet it produced, never painted.
5. **The surfaces.** On the door, Join the team under Insurance (leading when `?jobs=1`, the website's careers link; `&job=<id>` opens one posting's form): the open postings, Share, Apply. On Onboarding for the owner/admin: Jobs (post, open, close, remove, share the jobs page) and Applicants (by station, the pipeline, Hire with the invite link ready to send, the telehealth hand-off with its steps, message and marks).
6. **The user types the workflows need (Darrell's question, answered from the code).** The office has eight seats over five database roles (DR-0346). The hiring workflow needs two standings BEFORE membership, and they are now in the governance data and the matrix: the **applicant** (no login; reaches the door and Join the team only, through `tlc_public_jobs` and `tlc_apply`) and the **new colleague** (hired, with a login and a packet, not a member until approved; reaches what any signed-in person reaches). Ten seats.
7. **How a signature is acknowledged (Darrell's question, answered from the code, and one gap closed).** The packet's three acknowledgments are: the document read in the app, the checkbox "I have read this and I agree", the full legal name typed as the signature, a date; the server refuses a submit without all three (`tlc_onboarding_save`); the readout shows "Signed <name> on <date>"; the contractor agreement may also carry an attached signed copy. The gap: nothing recorded WHICH text was signed, and no consent to sign electronically was shown. Closed: `lib/tlc-signing.js` gives every document a content-hash version (`v` + FNV-1a of the text as the app renders it), the form stamps `signedAt` and `docVersion` at the first keystroke of the name and shows the ESIGN consent line, the readout shows the version and the device time, and the server's own `submitted_at` bounds the time. A later edit to the handbook changes the version; the old signature still says what it signed.

## Amendment (2026-09-10, later) — the visitor's slider, the tabs each group sees, and Learn open at any hour

Darrell, on the shared lesson stacked above the booking page: *"The lessons should be on another tab for those who are not signed in... we like the sliders.... remember? also what tabs do each group need to see and or not?"* Then: *"We want clients to have information about subjects available 24/7..."* and *"understanding is the principle thing."*

8. **Signed out, the door is the same one-row slider as signed in:** Find your therapist · Mental skills (Darrell: *"Maybe a mental skill building place tab"*) · Join the team. A shared lesson lands on Learn (the taste note, that one lesson open); `?jobs=1` lands on Join the team. Nothing stacks above the booking page.
9. **Mental skills, signed out (headed "A mental skill building place"), is the client lessons free to read at any hour** — the lead-magnet tracks a client sees (Understanding & Coping; What to expect & support), with or without the Word, no areas, ledger, assignments or SME gate — and a **subject finder** over every client lesson (title, big idea, text), so a client at three in the morning can find "anxiety" or "a loved one" without signing in. Its headline is Darrell's word rendered for meaning (DR-0331), "Understanding is the principal thing," with Proverbs 4:7 read from the corpus on tap.
10. **The tabs each group sees are data** (`DOOR_TABS`, `doorTabsFor`), derived from the same resources as the governance matrix so the chart and the door cannot drift: signed out, Find · Learn · Join the team; a signed-in client, reviewer or assistant, Training · Team · Assistant (plus Find); a member (therapist, supervisor, trainee), Inquiries · Client Growth · Revenue · Training · Team · Assistant; the owner and admin, all of those and Onboarding (with Jobs and Applicants). Pinned in `tlc-governance.test.js`; the door render pins the visitor's three tabs, the Learn tab's lessons and finder, and that Join the team is its own tab.

11. **The person's photo, in this app too** (Darrell: *"User photo for all apps especially this one..."*). Signed in, the TLC door's bar wears the person's picture from their profile (DR-0342), initials and "+ photo" until they add one, and opens My profile (picture, name) — the same `HeaderAuthButton` the PoeTech shell uses, so one picture follows the person across every app; Governance shows every member's picture beside their name. The onboarding headshot stays the roster card's; the profile picture is the person's own. Pinned in the onboarding render suite.

12. **The therapist first** (Darrell, on the live build: *"the training or lessons etc shouldn't over shadow the therapist... we want good flow"*). A lesson that arrives by link gets no Learn banner above it; the Mental skills tab gets a compact header, never the long blurb; and under every lesson a visitor reads sits the next step — "Meet the therapists" (one tap to Find your therapist) and "Book an appointment". The lesson walks a visitor to the door, never past it. Pinned in the door render.

13. **An opening for a specific seat, when needed** (Darrell: *"apply here has no where to apply to"* / *"How do we create the openings for the specific roles when needed?"*). The door's Join the team lists only OPEN postings, so until the office posts one it says so and names where: Onboarding · Jobs, signed in as the owner or admin. There, "New posting" starts from a seat on the governance chart — Clinical Supervisor, Therapist, Therapist-in-training, Office Assistant — and fills the title, the summary (what the seat governs) and the requirements (what it may do) from the same data the matrix reads; edit anything, "Post it (open)", and it is on the door and the website's careers link at once; Close it when filled. Pinned in the hiring render.
