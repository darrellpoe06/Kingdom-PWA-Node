Just tweaks for better function and flow!!!!!!!"* — so the course header stays and the title keeps its two lines and its fold.

## Context

**SHOULD.** Once a person has chosen a lesson and started it, the screen is theirs for reading. What describes the course belongs to the course page (DR-0688). The pinned bar is there to say where you are and give the way out (DR-0652), not to hold a quarter of a phone for the whole read (DR-0438). A tap on Next should put the next words where the eye already is. And the way through the lesson, by taps or by scrolling, is the reader's to choose, not the app's.

## What was measured (SHOULD → ARE → GAPS, DR-0219)

**ARE, on main `6e329a99`, with L105 open and its guide open (his screenshots).**

1. Above the lesson: the "Church · Learn" eyebrow, the course title, the Word-first lead block, "The 31 weeks", the cohort pill, "Starts Saturday, August 1, 2026…", and the Governor's facilitator box. The title and the lead name the course and are wanted ("I want the course header!!!"); the rest is the course's schedule furniture and says nothing about the lesson. (`ChurchLearn.jsx` rendered all of it whether or not `focusModule` was set.)
2. The card: the action row (Share · Copy lesson · Copy link · Download · Close the guide · Play · Present · Mark done) rendered TWICE, back to back, with only the timeline line between them, both ABOVE the guide. The second row was placed before the guide in the tree, so "a finished reader never scrolls back up" was not true: the row sat at the top either way.
3. The sticky block: bar (44 px) + two-line title + the step row and progress bar held the top for the whole read and never moved; the body scrolled under it.
4. A tap on Next or Back in the paced core (`AgePacedLesson.moveTo`) and on Next part (`LessonFlowAudience.goTo`) changed the words and left the scroll where it was; the reader scrolled up to find the new step's first line each time.
5. The Teach core had one way through: the pager. The whole-lesson reveal (`showAll`) existed only for the read-along's benefit.
6. The guide opened on the time chips (5M … 90M), the teacher card and the stage rail every time, including on a lesson picked up at step 8 of 16.

**GAPS.** (1) The course's schedule furniture over the lesson. (2) Two identical rows over the guide. (3) A pinned block that never yields. (4) No landing on a step change. (5) One way through. (6) The same opening furniture on every return.

## Impact

With the guide open, the course header (its name and Word-first lead) stands above the lesson space, which holds the bar and the two-line title with its fold, the head row with Close the guide, ▶ Play and More, and the lesson. The bar slides up as the reader scrolls down and returns on the first flick up or at the top. A tap on Next, Back, a part or the rail lands the new words with their first line just under the bar. Beside Ari's name a two-way switch, Step by step or Scroll it all, is remembered on the device; scrolling it all shows every part and every step in order, and the progress bar and the saved place follow the step under the eye. A lesson picked up past its start opens with the pace chips folded under one line that says the pace it has. Nothing is removed: the course's schedule lines are on the course page, the full row is under More and after the guide, the time chips are one tap away.

## Decision

1. **The course's schedule furniture lives with the course; the header stays** (`ChurchLearn.jsx`): while a lesson is open, "The N weeks", the cohort pill, the start date and the Governor's box wait in the course view; the course title and its Word-first lead stay above the lesson, as Darrell asked — and had asked before this record ("Said that last time!!!"), so it is pinned in the gate below and is not to be touched again by any clutter pass; the Governor's facilitator switch rides the lesson's More menu (only on a lesson that carries a facilitator guide).
2. **One head row, folded under More while the guide is open**; the second row follows the guide (`renderActions({ head })`): the head keeps Close the guide and ▶ Play; More opens Share, Copy lesson, Copy link, Download, Present and Mark done in place; the foot row renders whole after the guide so the finished reader meets it there. The date and the timeline line wait for the guide to close. With the guide closed the row is exactly as before.
3. **The chrome steps aside** (`useChromeAutoHide`, `nextChromeState`, `.lesson-space-sticky[data-chrome='hidden']`): while the guide is open, a scroll down of more than `CHROME_DELTA` (24 px) past `CHROME_HIDE_AFTER` (120 px) slides the block up out of view; a scroll up of the same brings it back; within 120 px of the top it is always shown. It stays `position: sticky`; only its transform moves. The title keeps its two-line ceiling and its fold control exactly as DR-0605 settled them — the height comes back by the block stepping aside, never by shrinking what Darrell firmed up.
4. **Every step lands under the chrome** (`announceStep`, `landStep`, `stepScrollTop`): a tap on Next or Back in the paced core, or on a part or the rail in the flow, announces the new element on `poetech:lesson-step`; the lesson space shows its chrome, waits two frames for it to paint, and scrolls so the element's first line sits `STEP_LANDING_GAP` (8 px) under it. Proportional re-steps on a level change (DR-0418) and the initial place do not scroll; only the reader's own move does.
5. **Step by step, or scroll it all** (`useFlowMode`, `FLOW_KEY = poetech:lesson-flow`): the reader's switch beside Ari's name, default Step by step; Scroll it all renders every part and every step (the `showAll` reveal the read-along already proved) and, as the page scrolls, the step whose top is last above the reading line is reported through the same `onStepChange` a tap reports, so the progress bar and the saved place follow the eye and a switch back to Step by step opens the pager on that step.
6. **The pace controls fold on a return** (`lesson-pace-fold`): a lesson picked up past its first step or part opens with the time chips and the teacher folded under "Pace · N min ▾"; a fresh open shows them as before.

## Limits, stated

1. The chrome hides on the reader's scroll only; the read-aloud's follow-scroll also moves the page, and while the Word plays the bar may hide and show with it. If that reads as flicker on his phone, the follow-scroll will be excluded the way the place-saver already excludes it (DR-0631).
2. Scroll it all reports the step under a reading line fixed just under the chrome; a very short step may be passed in one flick and reported for a moment. The saved place is still the right one when the reader stops.
3. The pace fold decides once, when the guide opens; a reader who moves past step one in the same sitting keeps the chips open until the next open.
