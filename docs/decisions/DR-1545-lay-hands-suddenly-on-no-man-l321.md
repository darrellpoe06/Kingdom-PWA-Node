## Decision

1. **Two restorations.** The person quickly and gently (Galatians 6:1; Proverbs
   28:13); the office slowly and on proof (1 Timothy 3:2, 3:10). Moses (Numbers
   20:12; Deuteronomy 34:10) and Peter (John 21:17) carry it.
2. **Who decides.** Not the crowd (1 Samuel 15:24; Mark 15:15), but those
   charged, bound by witnesses, both sides and no favourites (1 Timothy 5:19,
   21; Proverbs 18:17); usefulness is not fitness (Proverbs 28:21).
3. **The anchor cuts both ways** (1 Timothy 5:22): no rush out, no rush back.
4. **Tiered and narrow.** Every claim about people is reported; the espionage
   allegation and the other people named are left out (Exodus 23:1); no
   verdict on any heart; no gloating (Proverbs 24:17). Placed in Living
   Lessons beside L317 and L319, where this story's teachings already live.
