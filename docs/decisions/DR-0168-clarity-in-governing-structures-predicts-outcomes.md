## Declared by Darrell, 2026-07-11

> "Always look for the governing structures to better predict outcomes. Humans are corruptible creatures and we see this — however systems with clarity make bad people respond like good ones, and good people don't mind clarity because they want to create more good."

## The principle

**Predict outcomes from the STRUCTURE, not from the professed goodness of the people at the top.** People are corruptible (Jeremiah 17:9); a structure that concentrates power without clarity will produce corruption *regardless of who sits in the chair* — and a structure with clarity will pull even a bad actor toward good behavior, while a good actor welcomes the clarity because they want to create more good. Swap a "moral" CEO into an unaccountable empire and the outcomes are the same; the architecture, not the individual, is the determinant.

## The Word says this exactly

The principle is not a management theory borrowed into faith — it is stated in Scripture with startling precision:

- **John 3:19-21** — *"every one that doeth evil hateth the light, neither cometh to the light, lest his deeds should be reproved. But he that doeth truth cometh to the light, that his deeds may be made manifest, that they are wrought in God."* This is Darrell's principle verbatim: the corrupt *mind* clarity (they hate the light); the upright *welcome* it (they come to the light) — because their deeds are wrought in God and they want them seen.
- **Luke 12:2-3 / Luke 8:17** — *"there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed."* Ultimate clarity is a fixed feature of the universe; a wise structure aligns with it early rather than being exposed by it late.
- **Ephesians 5:11-13** — *"all things that are reproved are made manifest by the light: for whatsoever doth make manifest is light."* Transparency is not merely a safeguard; it is the mechanism by which good is separated from evil.
- **1 Corinthians 4:5** — the Lord *"will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts."* The final audit is certain; clarity now is agreement with it.
- **Proverbs 29:2** — *"When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn."* Authority-structure determines the people's condition.
- **The just weight** — *"a false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight"* (Proverbs 11:1); *"a perfect and just weight, a perfect and just measure shalt thou have"* (Deuteronomy 25:15). A transparent, standard measure is the oldest governing-clarity structure in Scripture, and it is commanded.
- **The woes on opaque power** — *"Woe unto them that decree unrighteous decrees"* (Isaiah 10:1); *"Woe to him that buildeth a town with blood, and stablisheth a city by iniquity"* (Habakkuk 2:12). Structure built in the dark is judged as structure.

## The two applications

**1. Reading power (the discernment application).** When evaluating an institution, a leader, or an empire, ask FIRST what the governing architecture incentivizes — not whether the person is "visionary" or "villainous." This is exactly Karen Hao's central thesis in World Issues Issue 6 ("governance is reality"; swap a moral CEO into the imperial structure and the extraction continues), and it is why this house judges the FRAME and the FRUIT, never the heart (which belongs to God, Romans 14:4). The "is he a good person?" debate is often a distraction from the accountable-structure question that actually predicts the outcome.

**2. Building and adopting (the operating application).** This is the standing evaluation lens for Ari's research (DR-0143) and the house's own build: **prefer tools, paths, and Ways with clarity and auditable governing structure over those that ask for trust in an operator.** A transparent, open-source, self-hosted, gated tool (its behavior inspectable, its data in the house, its failures caught by a check) is structurally safer than an opaque vendor black box, *even if the vendor is currently well-behaved* — because clarity, not the vendor's goodness, is what holds when incentives shift. This is already the platform's own doctrine seen from a new angle: the deterministic gates are the brake, not the agent's goodness (DR-0076/DR-0103); the two-courts accountability states the wrong on the record (DR-0130); merge = deploy runs on a lane whose clarity does not depend on any one session's virtue. **We build for ourselves the very clarity we say power owes the public** — good people don't mind clarity; we want more good.
