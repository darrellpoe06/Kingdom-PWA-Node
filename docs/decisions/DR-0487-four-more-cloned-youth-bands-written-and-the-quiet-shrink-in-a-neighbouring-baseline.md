- **Status:** accepted
- **Tier:** B (lesson content on the corpus reader path)
- **Date:** 2026-09-18
- **Type:** content
- **Scope:** `app/src/lib/living-lessons-class.js` (ll82 youth, ll91 youth + teen, ll81 youth, ll100 youth), `app/src/lib/band-differentiation-baseline.json` (18 -> 14), `app/src/lib/title-in-narrative-baseline.json` (440 -> 438 unnamed bands)
- **Principles:** EVERY-BAND-IS-THE-WHOLE-MESSAGE (DR-0418), VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE (DR-0076 §4), PERPETUAL-IMPROVEMENT (DR-0075)
- **Grounds:** DR-0484 (the measure), DR-0485, DR-0486

## What was measured, and what it showed

DR-0484 predicted that most of this debt would be a **youth-band job**, because the youth band was added later (DR-0418) and in these lessons it was cloned from teen rather than written. Four lessons in, that prediction holds, and the clone is visible with the naked eye once you look: **the youth band is the teen band with its sentences chopped shorter.** Same wording, same examples, same order, a full stop swapped for a comma. That is a formatting change wearing the name of a reading level.

| lesson | worst before | worst after | bands written |
|---|---|---|---|
| ll82 love-fulfils-the-law | 0.86 (youth~teen) | **0.11** | youth |
| ll91 the-authors-own-code | 0.86 (youth~teen), 0.70 (teen~senior) | **0.22** | youth, teen |
| ll81 tongues-weighed-word-first | 0.83 (youth~teen) | **0.29** | youth |
| ll100 guard-the-little-ones | 0.76 (youth~teen) | **0.48** | youth |

Recorded debt **18 -> 14 lessons**. Each lesson's own gate passed (ll82 35 checks, ll91 37, ll81 38, ll100 37), every quoted span kept verbatim with its reference, no band short of its floor, and every FK ladder still rising.

**ll91 needed a second pass on a measurement, not on the prose.** Its first re-authored teen band read **FK 8.30 against a senior band at 8.20** — an inverted ladder, caught by measuring rather than by reading it back and feeling satisfied. Fourteen of its densest sentences were split; teen came to 6.34 and the ladder rose again. Writing a band at the right register is not the same as writing it well, and only the measurement distinguishes them.

## The finding: a repair shrank a baseline nobody was looking at

Rebasing the differentiation baseline was expected. What was not expected: the **title-in-narrative** gate failed, reporting that ll91's youth and teen bands **now name their own lesson** and must therefore leave its baseline, with the corpus total falling **440 -> 438** unnamed bands.

Nothing in this work set out to fix that. Both new bands simply opened by naming the lesson, because that is how a band should open — and a *different* shrink-only baseline noticed and refused to stay padded.

That is the discipline behaving exactly as designed, and it is worth recording as a pattern rather than a footnote: **a repair in one dimension can silently improve another, and a shrink-only baseline that does not fail when its debt falls would quietly keep the credit.** The five baselines are not independent ledgers of separate concerns; they are five views of the same prose. Touching the prose means re-running all five, not the one the work was aimed at.
