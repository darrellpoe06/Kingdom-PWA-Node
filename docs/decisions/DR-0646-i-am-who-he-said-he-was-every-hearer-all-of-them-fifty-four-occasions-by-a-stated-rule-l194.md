## Decision

1. **L191 is kept exactly as it is.** It gains one added sentence, right after its opening: *All of them: L194, I AM: Who He Said He Was — Every Hearer, All of Them, walks every occasion the four Gospels record, fifty-four by a stated rule.* A test pins that the sentence appears exactly once and that L191's own sixteen and its title stand.
2. **L194 is titled "I AM: Who He Said He Was — Every Hearer, All of Them."** "I AM" is in capitals because it is His self-name (John 8:58 beside Exodus 3:14).
3. **The counting rule is stated before the count:**
   - An occasion counts when, in one recorded scene, Jesus does one of four things:
     - (A) names who He is to those present, with an I am, a title (Son of man, the Son), or by calling Yahweh His Father;
     - (B) receives or corrects a confession or address spoken to Him;
     - (C) answers a direct question about who He is;
     - (D) meets unclean spirits crying out who He is, and silences or expels them.
   - One scene means one time, one place and one set of hearers.
   - Parallels across the Gospels are joined into one occasion, with every reference cited.
   - A scene with several kinds is counted once.
   - The order is the order of events.
4. **Six kinds are excluded, each named with its reason:**
   - testimony about Him that was not spoken to Him (John 1:29; Matthew 27:54);
   - challenges He did not answer about Himself (Matthew 4:3, 4:7; Matthew 27:40; Luke 23:9);
   - claims made by an act, with no word about Himself (Mark 4:41; Luke 7:49; Luke 5:8; Matthew 8:8; Luke 17:15-18);
   - the passion predictions (Mark 8:31; 9:31; 10:33-34);
   - claims woven through His teaching to the crowds (Matthew 5:17; 7:21-23; 10:32-33; Luke 12:8; 17:22-30);
   - everything outside the Gospels (Acts 9:5; Revelation 1, which is L191's sixteenth hearer).

   The lesson says in words that counting these in gives a larger number, never a different Christ.
5. **The count is fifty-four.**
   - By kind:
     - (A) in 34;
     - (B) in 18;
     - (C) in 8 (15, 23, 30, 37, 38, 44, 45, 46);
     - (D) in 4 (8, 9, 14, 18).
   - These overlap. Each occasion is counted once, and the union of the four is all 54.
   - Reconciled with L191:
     - 18 occasions are the scenes L191 walks. Its fifth hearer covers two (John 8 and John 10) and its fifteenth covers three (Emmaus, Thomas, the mountain in Galilee).
     - L191's sixteenth hearer (Patmos) is outside the Gospels.
     - One occasion (Bethesda) is quoted in L191 but not walked.
     - 36 occasions are new.
   - The confessions and questions the brief named are all walked:
     - Nathanael 3; Peter 21 and 23; the Samaritan woman 6; the man born blind 29; Martha 31; Thomas 52;
     - John's disciples 15; the high priest 44 and 45; Pilate 46.
6. **Not of this world, and the worlds He framed.**
   - The Word first: John 18:36; John 8:23; Hebrews 11:3; Colossians 1:16-17.
   - Then the science, labeled as an illustration and never a proof. Hebrews 11:3 itself says "Through faith we understand."
7. **The eyes of the body and the eyes of the soul**, from Darrell's second word, built from the Word: 2 Kings 6:17; John 9:39-41; Matthew 13:13-16; Ephesians 1:18; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Hebrews 11:27.
8. **I AM: Who Jesus Said He Was, Is, and Is to Come** is Darrell's title, and the movement follows the not-of-this-world movement.
   - WAS: John 8:58 pinned beside Exodus 3:14; John 17:5; John 1:1, 3.
   - IS: the seven I am sayings (John 6:35; 8:12; 10:9; 10:11; 11:25; 14:6; 15:1); John 10:30; Matthew 28:18.
   - IS TO COME: John 14:3; Matthew 24:30; Revelation 22:12-13, signed "I Jesus" in 22:16.
   - Joined by Revelation 1:8 and Hebrews 13:8.
9. **Worthy.**
   - Scripture: Revelation 5:9-12; 1 Peter 1:18-19; Hebrews 9:22; Leviticus 17:11.
   - "His blood has the Code" is attributed to Darrell as his own framing: worth beyond any thing, and redemption written in the blood. The lesson says in words that no doctrine is built from the phrase.

## Limits, stated

1. **Fifty-four is the count under this rule, not a claim that the Word holds exactly that many.** Where an occasion begins and ends is sometimes a judgment call. John 9:1–10:21 is counted as one conversation, and so is John 6:22-71. Luke 22:66-71 is counted apart from the night trial because Luke marks it "as soon as it was day." The lesson states the rule so anyone can check it. `re-review: 2026-10-08`: whether the Son of man sayings in the discourses belong in a companion list.
2. **The 0.0035% figure is a public approximation.** It is labeled as one, and the lesson rests on the established fact (a tiny fraction) rather than on the digits. `re-review: 2026-10-08`: whether to cite a named reference range on the surface.
3. **ESV is not in the repo**, so every quotation is KJV. This follows the in-repo corpus, as L191 does.
