## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-21, spoke a new program into the channel: **"Starting program for The Mind
Of Christ... Yahweh's Perspectives, His Way and Knowledge and Understanding"**, with the
pillars: *metacognitive processes developed FROM Word directives*; *"actions speak louder
than words" / "faith without works is dead"*; *daily reading the Word for cognitive
development, brain function, therapeutic regulation and relationships*; *"Father Psychological
Safety From Yahweh Who IS the Highest 4th-dimensional King"*; and *"Identity From Him."* Per

foundation (NOTICE → TEST → CAPTURE → REDIRECT; the vocabulary register).

## The decision

Added **L50 — "The Mind of Christ — Thinking Yahweh's Thoughts, Proven in Works"** to
`LIVING_LESSONS_MODULES` (weeks 49 → 50; the `weeks === MODULES.length` gate). It integrates
the five pillars Darrell spoke:

1. **THE MIND — given and renewed** (metacognition): "we have the mind of Christ" (1 Corinthians
   2:16); "Let this mind be in you" (Philippians 2:5); "renewing of your mind... prove... will
   of God" (Romans 12:2); "bringing into captivity every thought" (2 Corinthians 10:5); humbled
   under His higher thoughts (Isaiah 55:8-9; Proverbs 3:5-6).
2. **DAILY WORD as the input/formation**: meditate day and night (Psalms 1:2; Joshua 1:8); "Thy
   word have I hid in mine heart" (Psalms 119:11); the test-standard for thoughts (Philippians
   4:8; Colossians 3:2).
3. **PSYCHOLOGICAL SAFETY from the Father** — Yahweh the 4th-dimensional King (DR-0097): "perfect
   peace, whose mind is stayed on thee" (Isaiah 26:3); "perfect love casteth out fear" (1 John
   4:18); "a sound mind" (2 Timothy 1:7); "refuge... in trouble" (Psalms 46:1); therapeutic
   regulation that overflows into relationships (2 Corinthians 1:4).
4. **IDENTITY from Him**: "God created man in his own image" (Genesis 1:27) — think from a
   settled self (carries L47).
5. **PROVEN in works** (the hinge Darrell named): "doers of the word, and not hearers only"
   (James 1:22); "faith without works is dead" (James 2:17, 26); hears AND does = the rock
   (Matthew 7:24); "why call ye me, Lord... and do not" (Luke 6:46). Obedience is the PURPOSE
   of the renewed mind — Romans 12:2 renews it "that ye may prove... the will of God".

Full module at the L42–L49 bar (bigIdea, inApp, 7 benefits, child/teen/senior levels, quiz,
deep lesson, facilitator). Form: a Living Lesson (the proven harvest pattern); an in-app
Mind-of-Christ metacognition surface may follow (APP-IS-PRIMARY) as the program grows.
