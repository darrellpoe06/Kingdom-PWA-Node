## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-20, after L46 shipped:

> "How should other who are not fathers understand the Will of Yahweh for them on
> this — what is their take away — and also what should the father take away from
> the watchers?"

## The decision

Extend L46 with the two reciprocal halves that complete the lesson (built into the
module: benefits, lesson body, quiz, facilitator talking points + discussion prompts).

### 1. For those who are NOT fathers — the same verb, wider hands

The command "provoke... unto love and to good works" is, in its home verse, spoken
to the WHOLE Body, not to fathers (Hebrews 10:24). So the will of Yahweh for the
non-father is the same charge:
- **Everyone stirs someone** — aim it at good, not wrath (Hebrews 10:24).
- **Spiritual fatherhood/motherhood is real without a child** — Paul begot churches
  (1 Corinthians 4:15); Elisha's "my father" over Elijah (2 Kings 2:12); Lois and
  Eunice (2 Timothy 1:5); the aged women teach the young (Titus 2:3-4).
- **All are commanded to father the fatherless** — "A father of the fatherless... is
  God" (Psalm 68:5), done through His people, "God setteth the solitary in families"
  (68:6); "Pure religion... is this, To visit the fatherless" (James 1:27).
- **The childless bear eternal fruit** — "a name better than of sons and of
  daughters... an everlasting name" (Isaiah 56:4-5).

### 2. What the father takes FROM the watchers — humility

L46 told the father not to reduce his child to someone he merely watches; the flip
side is that HE is watched, and it is grace:
- **No one fathers alone** — "a threefold cord is not quickly broken" (Ecclesiastes
  4:9-12); the Body is the cord, not an audience.
- **He is a SON still being fathered** — by "the Father of spirits" (Hebrews 12:9);
  even the Son "learned obedience" (Hebrews 5:8) — so stay teachable.
- **The Body's correction is grace, not surveillance** — "faithful are the wounds of
  a friend" (Proverbs 27:6); "iron sharpeneth iron" (Proverbs 27:17). (Ties to L44
  safe-to-be-corrected and L45 honour.)
- **Even his children disciple him** — "Out of the mouth of babes... perfected
  praise" (Matthew 21:16).

**The full circle:** the watcher is called to become an active spiritual parent; the
father is called to become a humble, watched son — and the whole Body fathers the
next generation, no one alone, everyone provoking to good.
