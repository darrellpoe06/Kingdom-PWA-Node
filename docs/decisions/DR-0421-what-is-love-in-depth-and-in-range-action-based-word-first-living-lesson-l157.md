## The word, as spoken

Darrell, 2026-09-15: the English word love is not as detail-oriented as languages that have many words for it; so what is love, in depth and in range, and how does love work. He and a coworker told each other they loved each other after a conversation about still having joy in a day, because every life will have some suffering in it and that is already written. So how do we love each other, really; what exactly did Yahweh ask; what does He mean by loving a person as soon as you meet him. But there are wolves, and you are still expected to love your enemy and your neighbour as yourself while staying diligent, because he roams around seeking whom he may devour. Action-based, and Word first.

## What the Word supplies

Depth: love defined by its verbs (1 Corinthians 13:4-8, 13; Colossians 3:14), charity and love as one Greek word. Source: He went first (1 John 4:7-10, 19; Romans 5:8; Jeremiah 31:3; Romans 8:38-39). Range, in commanded rings: Him (Deuteronomy 6:5; Matthew 22:37-38), the neighbour (Matthew 22:39-40; Leviticus 19:18), the brother (John 13:34-35; Hebrews 13:1), the stranger (Leviticus 19:34; Hebrews 13:2), the enemy (Matthew 5:44-45; Luke 6:27-28), the standard (John 15:12-13). Love on meeting: the Samaritan (Luke 10:33-37; Romans 12:10; Galatians 6:10; Philippians 2:3-4), with the distinction the lesson keeps: love is owed at once, trust is built. The wolves: 1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 7:15-16; 10:16; John 2:24-25; Proverbs 27:6; 1 John 4:1; Proverbs 22:24-25; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Amos 3:3; Psalms 1:1; the garden (Luke 22:48). Enemy-love as actions: Matthew 5:44; Romans 12:17-21; Proverbs 25:21-22; Luke 6:35. The coworker: Job 14:1; John 16:33; James 1:2; Nehemiah 8:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Proverbs 17:17; Galatians 5:22. Tested: Ephesians 4:32; Luke 17:4; Matthew 18:15; 1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 4:15; Psalms 97:10. The test of love itself: 1 John 3:18; 4:20; John 14:15; Matthew 25:40; Micah 6:8. The close: John 21:15-17; Matthew 24:12. The fuel in every band: Romans 5:5.
