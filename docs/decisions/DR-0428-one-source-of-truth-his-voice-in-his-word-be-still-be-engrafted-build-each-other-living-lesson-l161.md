## The word, as spoken

Darrell, 2026-09-15 (verbatim capture in the session's scratch; rendered for meaning per DR-0331): the Word of Yahweh is the only place to hear His voice in the earth, and whatever is said against it is against Him; one source of truth, true as a Person, His Son's voice; be quiet, sit everything down, focus on the words He used, imagine it, and be engrafted into it; the Christ-like mindset that holds you and locks you in; love covers a multitude of sins; we are still in the conversation that began at the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, with an enemy who is a being and not a man, still angry; all of one blood with the Son; He has removed those who do not love Him across every age and says so; we are blind and cannot see; so stop demonizing, undermining, assuming and talking slick about each other; believe we can see, pray for each other, build each other, move where the King says. In Jesus' name.

## Where his words and the Word's differ, the Word's are used (DR-0331 / DR-0076)

- *"Love conquers a multitude of sin"* → the Word's word is **covers** (1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 10:12), and the lesson says so and teaches covering as forgiving (Colossians 3:13).
- *"All men are in the bloodline of Jesus Christ"* → taught in the Word's precise words: **of one blood** (Acts 17:26) and **He took part of the same** flesh and blood (Hebrews 2:14), with Luke 3:38 and Romans 5:8; no formula beyond the text.
- *"He gets rid of those who don't love Him"* → taught plainly (Psalms 5:5; Deuteronomy 7:10; Genesis 6:7) **and** beside it, in the same voice, that He has no pleasure in the death of the wicked and is not willing that any perish (Ezekiel 33:11; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4; Psalms 145:9). Both halves are His; the gate pins the sentence that holds them together.
- *"So we all love Him, we just blind"* → rendered as the Word says it: we are blind (2 Corinthians 4:4; John 9:41; Revelation 3:17; 1 Corinthians 13:12), everyone still breathing is inside His waiting, and therefore no one alive is treated as the removed; the case against demonizing is made from blindness (Matthew 7:1-5), not from a claim the Word does not make.
- *"He's a being outside of a man"* → Ephesians 6:12; Revelation 12:12; 1 Peter 5:8.
