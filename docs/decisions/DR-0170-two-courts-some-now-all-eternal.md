- **Governs:** how the discernment track states accountability — the two-courts frame, made visible in the benefits, not only in the deep lens

## Declared by Darrell, 2026-07-11

> "We have judgement in this life in some ways and others are eternal. I just want that to be known and understood. Some may not go to prison that are guilty, and innocent will be imprisoned even now — about 10% are innocent in prison. So we all suffer and die is the point; we might as well do it His way for the eternal rewards."

## The doctrine (the sharpening)

The two-courts frame (DR-0130) already said man's court is not the court of record and the eternal court holds every dismissed or hidden thing. This adds the second half that makes it whole:

1. **Judgment lands in BOTH courts — some in this life, all in eternity.** Some wrongs are addressed now (a jury finding, a real consequence); all of it is settled in the eternal court after this life. *"It is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment"* (Hebrews 9:27); *"God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing"* (Ecclesiastes 12:14); *"we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ"* (2 Corinthians 5:10).

2. **Earthly justice fails BOTH ways — and Scripture named it first.** The lessons already showed the guilty escaping (the documented visa example). The other half is the innocent suffering: *"there be just men, unto whom it happeneth according to the work of the wicked; again, there be wicked men, to whom it happeneth according to the work of the righteous"* (Ecclesiastes 8:14) — the innocent get what the guilty deserve and the guilty get what the righteous deserve. **Joseph** was jailed innocent (*"put him into the prison,"* Genesis 39:20); **Christ Himself** was condemned sinless — *"this man hath done nothing amiss"* (Luke 23:41); suffering wrongfully while doing well is *"acceptable with God"* (1 Peter 2:19-20). Darrell's real-world anchor: a meaningful fraction of the imprisoned are innocent (wrongful-conviction studies commonly estimate on the order of several percent, cited around 4-10% — the exact rate is estimated and debated, but that real people sit innocent while some guilty walk free is established, DR-0100). The imperfection of the earthly court is *precisely why* the eternal one is the true court of record.

3. **Since all suffer and die regardless, the wise path is His way — for the eternal reward.** The point is not to secure a fair earthly outcome (which may never come); it is that everyone suffers and dies either way, so the rational choice is to live God's way for the reward that outlasts it. Moses *"[chose] rather to suffer affliction with the people of God... esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt: for he had respect unto the recompence of the reward"* (Hebrews 11:24-26); *"our light affliction... worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory... the things which are not seen are eternal"* (2 Corinthians 4:17-18); *"the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed"* (Romans 8:18); *"if in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are of all men most miserable"* (1 Corinthians 15:19); *"lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven"* (Matthew 6:19-20).

## What ships

- **Made visible in the benefits (not only the deep lens).** Every one of the 6 World Issues discernment lessons gains a benefit bullet naming the two courts explicitly: earthly justice is real but imperfect (the guilty escape and the innocent suffer — Ecclesiastes 8:14; Genesis 39:20; Luke 23:41), the eternal court misses nothing and lands after this life (Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 9:27), and the wise path is His way for the reward that outlasts it (2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 11:24-26).
- **A deterministic gate, correctly scoped.** `discernment-track.js` adds `accountability/eternal-court-not-in-benefits`: where a lesson carries documented accountability, at least one benefit must make the two courts visible. **Scoped to `hasDocumented`** — a lesson that does not deal with documented wrongdoing is never forced to carry it (Darrell's caveat: *"unless it's not about that subject or doesn't discuss it"*). Proven-to-catch: tests confirm it fires when the benefit is missing and stays silent when there is no documented harm.
