- **Tier:** A/B (lesson content + a required engine field + a proven-to-catch audit rule; doctrinally grounded in DR-0098/DR-0100/DR-0129)
- **Scope:** `app/src/lib/discernment-track.js` (lens.accountability normalized + required by audit), `components/DiscernmentStages.jsx` (the rendered block), `lib/world-issues-class.js` (all three issues authored), the facilitator guide + lesson prose, `ARI_STANDING_DUTIES` (duty text sharpened)

- **Principles:** SPEAK-ESTABLISHED-FACT (DR-0100), TEACH-DONT-DEBATE (DR-0098), VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE (DR-0076), WORD-FIRST, PERPETUAL-IMPROVEMENT

## Directive

Darrell, 2026-07-08, correcting DR-0129's Musk lesson the same day it shipped: *"Accountability is not being clearly stated."* And the doctrine that governs it: *"Not just what the jury says, evidence we have even the evidence the judge dismisses comes into the eternal 4th dimensional courts and the impact on life during life even though the governments let you cheat steal lie like getting into the USA and staying past visas etc... some people lie kill steal destroy others hide those facts or do less still undermining others to gain is what is still seen and impacts."*

## The gap

DR-0129 taught the lesson to NAME documented deeds plainly and to lead with Jesus' justice pattern — but it never STATED what accountability the Word actually requires: from the wrongdoer, from us, and on which court's record. Naming without a stated requirement is still incomplete justice. And the frame was silently bounded by man's court: a jury finding was the ceiling of what the lesson called proven, when the Word's court is senior to every human docket.

## Decision

1. **The two-courts doctrine is the accountability frame, stated in the lesson's own voice.** Man's court is real but it is not the court of record. The evidence a judge dismisses, the suits that settle, the records sealed, the wrongs a government permits or never prosecutes — all of it enters the eternal court: *"God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing"* (Ecclesiastes 12:14); *"there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed"* (Luke 12:2); *"all things are naked and opened unto the eyes of him with whom we have to do"* (Hebrews 4:13). The impact on lives DURING life is seen and weighed now, not only at the end (James 5:4 — the withheld wages cry into His ears). The same standard binds the billionaire and the migrant at any border (Proverbs 15:3; Isaiah 10:1-2).
2. **What the wrongdoer owes is named:** confession, not spin (Numbers 5:6-7); restitution to the actual person wronged, principal plus a fifth (Leviticus 6:4-5), the pattern Zacchaeus fulfilled fourfold (Luke 19:8-9); and fruits meet for repentance (Matthew 3:8) — changed conduct, not a press release.
3. **What we owe is named:** reprove the works, never help hide them (Ephesians 5:11); a witness who conceals what he knows bears his own iniquity (Leviticus 5:1); minimizing another's wrong to gain is itself in the record (Proverbs 17:15); plead the cause of the wronged (Proverbs 31:8-9). No one gets away (Galatians 6:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Revelation 20:12).
4. **The engine requires it (proven-to-catch).** `lens.accountability` is a first-class normalized field, rendered as its own labeled block ("Accountability — what the Word requires (the two courts)"), included in the facilitator guide and print prose, and REQUIRED by the audit gate for any named-real-person issue with documented harm — `accountability/missing` fails the build. The test proves the gate catches.
5. **The worked example Darrell named is in the record, per the three tiers.** The Musk lesson carries a new documented-facts item (`f-visa`): the Washington Post's October 2024 reporting that he worked in the U.S. in 1995 on a student visa without enrolling at Stanford; his own 2005 email ("no legal right to stay in the country"); the Zip2 board member's and his brother's recorded statements; his denial, labeled as his claim; and the fact that no court ever ruled — which is the point: man's system never ruled, and the eternal court still holds the whole record. Sources cited with as-of dates (CNN 2024-10-28; Snopes).
6. **All three published issues carry the block** — the Musk case (the full doctrine), the beauty-supply case (structural accountability: a rigged channel is a false balance, Proverbs 11:1, and collective punishment of the innocent is equally in the record), and The Game Changers (witness accountability: Matthew 12:36 for the film's overclaims and for what we repeat).
