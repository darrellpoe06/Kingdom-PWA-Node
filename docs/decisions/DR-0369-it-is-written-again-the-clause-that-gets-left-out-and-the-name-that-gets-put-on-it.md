# DR-0369 — It is written again: the clause that gets left out, and the Name that gets put on it

**Date:** 2026-09-12 · **Status:** accepted · **Tier:** B · **Area:** word · **Principles:** VERIFICATION-DOCTRINE, WORD-FIRST, APP-IS-PRIMARY, TEACH-DONT-DEBATE, SPEAK-ESTABLISHED-FACT, DECISION-RECORDS

## Directive — spoken into the app, in about thirty messages

Darrell relayed a friend's position across 2026-09-11 into 2026-09-12 and answered it in real time. The friend, in his own words: he did not want the indoctrination, either by others or self-imposed, that religion demanded, and chose to be a good person as the bible teaches without giving his power to others to dictate at their interpretation; he wholeheartedly holds what he calls the fundamental principle of the bible, to love others as you do yourself; he does not believe Yahweh is good because of hell, his biggest point being that Yahweh is finding reasons to put people there; and from a thread of short videos, that slave owners, the Klan and other white people were all Christians, and that white churches excluded black people.

Darrell's answer became the spine:

> *"like the devil... I said that too"* · *"crafty or incompetent actually both and the Word explains it"*

> *"they can't taste and See that He Is Good"* · *"Nebaconezzer saw the King in the fire with those Hebrew kings... King rocking with His kings"*

## What is true (SHOULD / ARE / GAPS)

**SHOULD:** a member handed a clipped verse about Yahweh should have somewhere in this app that restores the page without staging a debate, and that tells the truth about what professing Christians actually did.

**ARE — traced before authoring:**

| Claim | Reality |
|---|---|
| The series has an apologetics track | **No.** There is no per-lesson tag, track or category at all. Objection-answering is a recurring lesson SHAPE (ll118, ll41, ll28, ll37, ll76), and the nearest track is a separate course, `world-issues`. |
| Something already answers the atrocity objection | **No lesson in the series names slavery, the Klan or segregated churches as the occasion.** |
| Verse text can be trusted to an author's memory | **Never, and the repo already enforces it** — every quoted span is gated against `app/public/bible/kjv`. |

**GAPS:** the four planks most commonly used against Yahweh had no single answer in the app, and the one method underneath all four had never been taught as a method.

## Decisions

1. **The method is taught before any objection is named**, so nobody is ambushed and the tool transfers. The exhibit is provable from the corpus rather than asserted: Matthew 4:6 quotes Psalm 91 accurately and omits `to keep thee in all thy ways`, which is the clause that rules out jumping, and the test asserts that omission against the corpus itself. Jesus answers by restoring the page — `It is written again` — not by denying the verse or attacking the interpreter.

2. **Both of Darrell's causes are kept, because one verse names both.** `they that are unlearned and unstable wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, unto their own destruction` gives incompetence and craft together, and puts the destruction on the wrester rather than on the Word. We never make the reader choose which one a person is.

3. **Every true half is conceded first, fully, without hedging.** This is the pastoral rule the lesson runs on and the facilitator notes enforce it. Most people never trimmed anything — they were handed it short. We are answering a method, not sentencing a man, and explicitly not the friend who prompted it.

4. **The historical record is stated plainly and never softened (DR-0100 tier 1).** Augusta 1845 and the SBC's own 1995 repentance; the 1838 sale of 272 people from four Maryland plantations to fund a college, nearly half of them children; St. George's 1787 and the denominations born out of it; the 1915 Klan under a former preacher with the burning cross as its emblem; and the 1807 Bible printed with the deliverance from Egypt and all the Psalms removed. One correction of fact is carried because it is true, not as a softener: ministers and church councils also opposed the Klan, so *all* was never accurate.

5. **The Word out-charges the objection rather than excusing those men.** Profession was never possession; the test was fruit, never paperwork; man-stealing carried the death penalty in the very book they carried; `menstealers` are listed among the lawless; and Yahweh declares Himself against those who `use their tongues, and say, He saith`. Peter names the exact crime: `through covetousness shall they with feigned words make merchandise of you`. **That is why they had to cut the Book** — the 1807 Bible is the method in printed form.

6. **The oldest instance answers the objection's premise.** Religious leadership killed the Son and used the government to do it, saying `It is not lawful for us to put any man to death` and finally `We have no king but Caesar`. Jesus predicted the pattern by name: `whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service`. Religious atrocity is not a fact the faith failed to anticipate; it is one the faith predicted and was founded in the middle of.

7. **Paper and perception are refused as proof, and government is put in its place.** `man looketh on the outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart`. Yahweh listed what a human king would take before Israel had one, ending `ye shall cry out in that day because of your king which ye shall have chosen you` — so the disappointment is real and the surprise is not required. The only government that ends it is a shoulder.

8. **It ends on an invitation, not a proof.** The friend's actual claim is that Yahweh is not good. Scripture's answer is a table: `O taste and see that the LORD is good`. Sight is what is missing, not intelligence, and no argument produces sight — which is also why the lesson invites the reader to audit the lesson.

- Typography **measured**: Yahweh 58 times in our own voice, zero generic uses, zero capitalized adversary names.

## What this does NOT do

- **It does not answer the video thread from the source.** No link reached the session and a logged-in algorithmic feed is not reproducible here, so the claims were answered from Darrell's summary. If he sends links, each specific claim can be checked. **re-review: 2026-09-19.**
- **It does not close the conversation.** Darrell said plainly that this is an entire conversation to pick up when he is back. Three threads arrived after the lesson closed and are recorded in the session note rather than forced in: who is given sight and when, the friend's next reply, and the video links.
- **It does not speculate past the text.** Where the Word is reticent about the mechanics of judgment, the lesson teaches what is written and stops (DR-0098).
