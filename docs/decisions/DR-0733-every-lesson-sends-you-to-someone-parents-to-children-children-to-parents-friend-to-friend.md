## Context

The Word gives the family its talk in every direction: parents teach children in the four daily places (Deuteronomy 6:7), children are expected to ask (Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6), the last promise of the Old Testament turns hearts both ways (Malachi 4:6), and friends sharpen friends (Proverbs 27:17). Darrell asked whether our lessons do this, and asked for it to be a workflow, not a wish, with a lesson that teaches it.

## What was measured

- Before a line was written, every registered course was measured with `talkTogetherCoverage` (`ownPrompts` finds each direction in the lesson's own words): **593 lessons; 4 prompt parents toward children, 20 prompt children toward parents, 23 send friend to friend, 0 carry all three, 42 carry any.** The honest answer to "are all of them doing this?" was no. The baseline file records `all: 0` on 2026-10-01 and may only rise.
- A first draft of L205 stated 12 / 18 / 36 from an earlier, looser count; the test re-measures the catalog and refused the draft, and the lesson now states the numbers above. The numbers in the lesson are the numbers the catalog gives, by test.
- L205 as shipped: lesson 4,774 words; bands child 2,382 / youth 3,230 / teen 3,517 / senior 3,860 words; 403 quoted spans on five surfaces, 403 verbatim against the King James text, 0 faults; fullness child 0.52 / youth 0.61 / teen 0.61 / senior 0.62 (floors 0.5 / 0.6); reading grades child 0.7, youth 3.4, teen 3.9, senior 6.5 (ascending; child under the 5.0 ceiling); band differentiation worst pair 0.09 (ceiling 0.5). With L205 in, the catalog is 594 lessons and exactly one carries all three in its own words: L205.

## Impact

Every lesson card in the PoeTech app (Church > The Word and every course on the same reader) now ends with **Talk about it together**: a prompt to parents, a prompt to children, a prompt to friends, each carrying the skill (ask, listen to the end, retell, teach one verse) and the rhythm (once today in one of Deuteronomy 6:7's four places; one friend this week), the aim ("so we all get healthy together, until we see that Yahweh has been right") and Deuteronomy 6:7 verbatim. Where a lesson wrote its own prompts, its own words are shown and marked as its own; where it did not, the standing prompts stand and say so. The print path carries the same line. From 2026-10-01 every lesson added must carry its own three, in the app's tests and in the builder on our own machine, so the count can only rise. L205 is the first lesson written under the rule and teaches the rule: eight movements, the Word's two kinds of measure (fruit you describe, Galatians 5:22; days and occasions you count, Deuteronomy 6:7, Hebrews 3:13, Psalms 90:12), the Word's growth chart (Luke 2:52), its skills (James 1:19; Proverbs 20:5; Malachi 3:16) and strategies, and the two lines that hold the rest (Mark 12:30-31).

## Decision

- `talk-together.js`: `ownPrompts(module)` (three regex families over the lesson and its bands), `hasAllThree`, `talkTogetherFor(module)` → `{ aim, prompts[{to, text, own}], method, verse, allOwn }`, `talkTogetherCoverage(modules)`, `mustCarryOwn(added)` (binding from `TALK_TOGETHER_SINCE = '2026-10-01'`); the standing prompts are built from the lesson's title; every verse constant is the KJV text verbatim.
- The card: `data-testid="lesson-talk-together"`, `data-own`, each line `data-talk-to` / `data-talk-own`, the method line `talk-together-method`; registered as `card-talk-together` on the lesson-header surface.
- The gate, in three places that must agree: `talk-together.test.js` (every lesson whose `added` ≥ 2026-10-01 has all three; `coverage.all` ≥ baseline), the builder's `band_gates.mjs` (`talkTogether`, importing the same `ownPrompts`) and `lesson_gates.py` (`talk_together_gate`, in `passed`), and the writer's STANDARD prompt (the rule the writer is given before it writes).
- L205 `ll205-talk-about-it-together-parents-children-friends-until-we-see-yahweh-has-been-right`, added 2026-10-01. Numbers 203 and 204 are claimed by lanes in flight (L203 #1889, L204 #1927); this PR goes green on the numbering ratchet when they land and main is merged in.
