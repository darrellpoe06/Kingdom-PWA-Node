## Context

Spoken after L296 as a thanksgiving, and named a lesson. Darrell said:

- Yahweh is the only counsel that actually hits the spot. It shows where to put energy, actions, will and way, and nothing explains life's nuance better than His Word, precept upon precept.
- The Word brings you back off the emotional side, so you decide sound and steady, not double-minded. You do His will rather than what your feelings want, and you know it because the Word reads you while you read it.
- You fight the fleshly will to align with the eternal will of Yah, because the flesh is weak.
- Following the King is a skill and a discipline. The Word has to be part of every day, so you can evaluate well and the gaps never grow too wide to see.
- The King wants us to win, and it is our daily bread. King, help us.
