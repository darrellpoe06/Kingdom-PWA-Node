## Context

The lesson keeps his order. Doing before understanding (James 1:22-25; John 7:17; 13:17; Psalms 111:10; 119:100; Hebrews 5:14; Romans 5:3-4; Job 42:5; John 9:25). The two responsible spies and the bread (Numbers 13:33; 14:9; 14:24; Joshua 14:12), with the situations authored (Proverbs 16:9; 20:24; Jeremiah 10:23) and the manna as one bread with the Word (Deuteronomy 8:2-3; Exodus 16:4; Matthew 6:11; John 4:34; Hebrews 3:19; 4:2). Success by His interpretation of the living soul's way to eternal life (Joshua 1:8; Matthew 16:26; Genesis 2:7; John 17:3). No glory (Isaiah 42:8; 1 Corinthians 1:29-31; John 5:44; 12:43; Jeremiah 45:5; Matthew 5:16; Psalms 115:1; Luke 17:10; Matthew 25:21). Actions speak louder than words (James 2:17-18; 1 John 3:18; Matthew 21:28-31; 23:3; Titus 1:16; Ezekiel 33:31-32; Matthew 12:50).

## Impact

The reader does one command before feeling ready and writes what they understood after; names the hardest thing in front of them as bread; hands the credit back before it is given. Two stories: the boy who read the whole swimming book, and the man who taught giving.

## Decision

The lesson states success as His to define and the glory as His to keep, from the text, without softening Darrell's question ("why do you want it?") and without turning it into a debate.
