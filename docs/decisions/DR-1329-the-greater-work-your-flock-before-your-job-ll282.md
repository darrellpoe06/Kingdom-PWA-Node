## Context

**The teaching, in two messages.** The first, rendered for meaning (DR-0331): we cannot allow our positions, jobs or titles to interfere with what Yahweh calls the work of the Lord; He tells us to pay attention to our flocks, and if we have children and families we cannot let the stress of work undermine the work at home, which is a greater work; the souls of my children and how they experience Yahweh are more important than the job stresses; do not let anyone at work undermine the peace of the home; you war for peace so you can actually achieve it, and the weapons of our warfare are not carnal but mighty through Him to pull down strongholds; most of the time peace is your perspective and the stronghold you have; humans are in flesh and no good thing comes from flesh, so hope all things but expect nothing, be prepared, be prudent, count the cost; the one who always has your back and knows what is coming is Yahweh, and the truth is a person; the soul that sinneth shall die the second death, and that second death is the key, because we all die the first; do not let busy work or even quality work undermine the highest-priority work Yahweh gives the soul, because He is the Shepherd of the soul; listen to the right leadership and reverence the eternal leadership, an eternal kingdom; we are in a phase collecting children who believe His way and walk out His will, actions speaking louder than words. The second, minutes later, verbatim in its key lines: *"Make sure we're programming the future generation psychological profiles, which happen to be our children, to understand Yahweh's way. That's highest level kingship actions. And it also impacts generationally... They need to see him winning for you. They need to see you failing and then him talking and therapeutically helping you to analyze it, using the word of God... We're in the wilderness season, like understanding what a season is... parents being truthful and clarity driven about how they operate."*
