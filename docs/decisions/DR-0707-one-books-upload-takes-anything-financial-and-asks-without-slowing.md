## Context

Before this there were four separate doors, each on one tab and each for one shape of file: the CSV / Excel / OFX importer on Tx (`BooksTransactions`), the card statement upload on Debts (`DebtStatementUpload`), the tax PDF upload on Taxes (`lib/tax-upload.js`, to the NAS only), and the receipt photo pool on Tx (`lib/receipts.js`). None took a PDF statement or a photo of a bill, and a file the door did not expect was refused with an error. A person holding a paper had to know which tab, which door and which format. Darrell asked for one place that takes anything and makes it work with the books.

## Impact

One control, top right of the Books tab row, identical on all twelve sub-tabs (Entities, Accounts, Debts, Owed, Plan, Tx, Imported, Cart, 1099s, Taxes, Calendar, Legal). At 360 px it is a 44 x 44 icon button; from 380 px it also reads "Upload". The tab it is opened from is only a hint to the classifier, worth 1.5 points against a page's own signals, and never a route: a card statement opened from Taxes is still read as a card statement. Every document takes the same road, and nothing uncertain reaches the books.

## Decision

**One pipeline, identical for every file** (`lib/books-intake-pipeline.js`):

1. **Store first, never reject.** The original, with its received time, is written to the device store (IndexedDB) before any reader touches it. When signed in and not in demo, a copy goes to the household's private document shelf (`family-documents` bucket, `family_documents` "The money", migration 0201's RLS). No new table and no new bucket.
2. **Read.** CSV / TSV / TXT / Excel / OFX through the proven `statementFileToCsv` and `parseStatementText`. For a PDF, the text layer is read with pdf.js, lazy-loaded; a page with no text is rendered and read by OCR. For a photo or scan, OCR, **sovereign-first**: the family's NAS at same-origin `/books/ocr` with the bridge bearer, then the device itself (tesseract.js in the browser, where the picture never leaves the phone). Family documents are never sent to a third-party cloud AI.
3. **One common shape**, whatever the file: `{doc_type, confidence, dates:{document, period_start, period_end, due, paid}, amounts[], parties[], account_hint:{last4, institution}, entity_hint, property_hint, line_items[], tax_year, source_file}`. Each document kind has a plug-in extractor (`registerExtractor`): transactions download, card, bank and loan statements, bill, invoice, receipt, pay stub, and tax form.
4. **Match** to known data. An account is taken from its last-4 when exactly one account shares it, or from the proven filename router (`detectAccount`); otherwise it is a question. Entities come from the matched account or a name on the page, properties from the address, and vendors from subscriptions, contractors and payees. Rows are checked against the ledger through `planAccountImport` / `planBulkImport` (FITID, or content plus running balance). A new account is only ever **proposed** behind a question and never created silently.
5. **Reconcile and build the timeline.** A card or loan statement closes as previous − payments + purchases + fees + interest = new, and its line items must carry the previous balance to the new one. A bank statement closes as previous + deposits − withdrawals = new. On a pay stub, net must not exceed gross. For a download, every row is either ingested or rejected. Each document keeps its period, its due date and whether it is overdue; a month missing between two statements of the same account is named as a gap.
6. **Ask without slowing.** Uncertain items become plain questions with suggested answers, stored inside each document. Any question may be skipped, and every other document keeps moving. Blocking questions hold the write. **A document that does not reconcile is never written as fact**, whatever the answers are; the refusal is enforced twice, in `planWrite` and in `canWrite`.
7. **Confirm, then write through the existing paths only**: `commitImportedRows` (with `addAccount` only for an answered "create", and `updateAccount` for the due day, minimum payment and APR); `uploadTaxDoc` for tax papers, to the NAS only; `addPending` for receipts. Pay stubs, bills and invoices are stored with their dates and amounts and proposed. There is no ledger posting for them yet (see slice 3).
8. **Learn.** Each answer is remembered against the document's layout (its institution, its kind, and the header signature or the words at the top of the page). The next document with the same layout asks nothing, unless the last-4 printed on its page contradicts the remembered account. A document answered as "something else" is counted on a teach-the-system queue.

Each document shows its status: Received → Read → Needs your answer → Ready to confirm → Added, with what it became (rows added, account created, filed on the NAS, receipt pooled). **Demo mode writes nothing.** It uses a throwaway memory store, makes no cloud copy and calls no write sink, and the Add button reads "Demo — nothing is added".
