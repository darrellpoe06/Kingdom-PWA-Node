## Context

The first message carried most of the spine: existing is Yahweh's Way of authoring our lives; this life is the curriculum; made in an image that lets a person be His temple, which the angels were not, though they are curious; humble yourself to His narrative and He directs your paths; the Godhead, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, eternally I AM; a covenant deeper than a contract; Understanding, Knowledge and prudence dwell with Wisdom; knowing Him is the most important leadership metric; the beam in your own eye; the One inside His temple who knocks on the door of the heart.

The next twenty-one followed within the hour: your life is the evidence; seated in heavenly places now; opposition on His path is bread; the Godhead has never not agreed; one, like a healthy family; the curriculum of families, so the soul can decide about the Son; a warning before destruction is honourable; let Yahweh be Himself and every man a liar, because He cannot lie; start with the Word; study to show yourself, with the Word and with creation; the beauty of the One who framed the worlds, whom no one has seen but the Son; same nature, Ways and skills; not robbery; before Abraham was, I AM; the I AM family adopts us; eternal and never less; humble, meek, strength under control, all authority, power, mighty.

Twenty-seven more arrived while it was being written, and they are taught in their own section (SEVENTEEN in the adult lesson; before the practice in every band), in the order he sent them:

- Press toward the mark, for the prize.
- Let no pride undermine your understanding of the Word.

- Yahweh is looking for profit, and so should we, never at the cost of the soul.
- He measures differently than the world.

Three lines needed care, and each was settled by the Word, not by a school:
- **Message 26: "all humans that metanoia themselves the Way The Way Says will die the 2nd death."** Read literally, it says the opposite of the surrounding messages and of Acts 3:19. It is rendered for meaning (DR-0331): every human who will *not* turn, metanoia, the way The Way says, will die the second death. It is taught from Acts 17:30, Acts 3:19, 2 Corinthians 7:10 and Acts 4:12, and Darrell is asked to confirm the reading.
- **Message 29: the ancestors watch in the cloud of witnesses.** The Word says "compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses" (Hebrews 12:1) and names who they are (Hebrews 11:4, 11:39-40). It says no more about how they see, so the lesson says so in plain words and stops there. Message 30 then supplies the boundary from the Word: no worship, consultation or prayer to the dead (Leviticus 19:31; Isaiah 8:19; Deuteronomy 18:12; Matthew 4:10; Revelation 22:9; Acts 10:26). Looking unto Jesus (Hebrews 12:2) is the direction of the gaze.
- **Messages 42-44: Love covers a multitude of sins.** Proverbs 28:13 keeps the Word's own distinction. Love covers another's sin by forgiving it and not repeating it (Proverbs 17:9 in sense; 1 Peter 4:8). It never hides our own.

Works and grace were held in the Word's own order (Ephesians 2:8-10; James 2:17-18; 1 Samuel 2:3; Revelation 20:12), without staging a debate.

## Impact

A reader meets their own life as His curriculum, written in His book before it began (Psalms 139:16), and their body as the house He chose (1 Corinthians 6:19), which the angels look into with curiosity (1 Peter 1:12). They are taught to start with the Word because He cannot lie (Titus 1:2; Romans 3:4), to read it His Way (Luke 24:45), and to take the beam out of their own eye with it (Matthew 7:5; Hebrews 4:12). They learn that obedience is weighed and wins in both lives (1 Samuel 2:3; 1 Timothy 4:8), that He looks for profit by His own measure (Isaiah 48:17; Mark 8:36; Luke 16:15), and that the One with all authority is meek and lowly (Matthew 11:29; Matthew 28:18). And they learn that He just wants community with them (1 John 1:3; Revelation 21:3), which is why every band closes on the One who knocks and asks to sup (Revelation 3:20).
