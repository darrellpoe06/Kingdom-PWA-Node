## Why the capstone next, rather than course two

Darrell named the two ends of this department himself: *from why owned property is a principle through management as stewardship.* And when asked which to take first he answered plainly: **"Real-estate first then keep closing the gap!!!!!!!"**

So the two ends are now built. Course one settled **whose** the ground is. This one answers the question the footing leaves open: how does a person **run** ground that belongs to Another and to the people living on it?

## The arc, one argument in eight moves

1. **Faithful is the measurement** — *"Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful"* (1 Corinthians 4:2), taken at the least (Luke 16:10), and note the symmetry that convicts: the unjust in the least *is* unjust in much. Present character, not future risk. This deliberately names the conflict with the measure the trade actually uses, which is yield.
2. **Entrusted by ability, judged by action** — five, two and one *"to every man according to his several ability"* (Matthew 25:15); the two commendations are **word for word identical**, and even five talents is called "a few things". The third servant is charged with **inaction**, not a bad return: *"Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers"* (Matthew 25:27) — the lowest-effort option in his world, and he did not take it.
3. **Faithful in a very little, authority over ten cities** (Luke 19:17) — scope is granted on evidence rather than appetite, and the reward for good management is **more management**.
4. **The manager his owner stopped worrying about** — *"he knew not ought he had, save the bread which he did eat"* (Genesis 39:6), with the counterfeit named: indispensability through **opacity**, and the test that separates them — could the owner take it back tomorrow and find everything where the records say? Then Genesis 41:48 as a logistics decision: each district's harvest stored in its own city.
5. **The battlement: safety is blood, not a line item** — *"that thou bring not blood upon thine house, if any man fall from thence"* (Deuteronomy 22:8). **The one place in this department where cost-benefit reasoning is ruled out by the text.** It attaches at construction, the hazard is foreseeable, the protected person is anonymous and cannot inspect or negotiate, and the consequence is blood on the house rather than a fine.
6. **Their portion in due season** — the steward set over a **household** *"to give them their portion of meat in due season"* (Luke 12:42), so a right thing done late can equal the thing undone; and the requirement scales with what has been committed (Luke 12:48). Measured on the **outliers**, because an average of two days hides the household that waited eleven.
7. **When management became extraction: restore, this day** — nothing in Nehemiah 5 was illegal, and the remedy still carried a date: *"Restore, I pray you, to them, even this day, their lands, their vineyards, their oliveyards, and their houses"* (Nehemiah 5:11). Nehemiah's own entitlement was real and his restraint voluntary: *"but so did not I, because of the fear of God"* (Nehemiah 5:15).
8. **The account given with joy, and not with grief** — *"that they may do it with joy, and not with grief: for that is unprofitable for you"* (Hebrews 13:17), where the cost of a grieved steward lands on **the people**; then *"enter thou into the joy of thy lord"* (Matthew 25:21), and the frame closes back on Leviticus 25:23 — the title Yahweh kept.

**No passage is shared with course one**, and that separation is checked against course one's own reference list rather than trusted. A capstone that re-preached the footing would feel weighty and teach nothing.

## What the measurement caught before any reader did

**Six elisions inside quotations** in the first draft — Genesis 41:48, Nehemiah 5:15 and Matthew 25:21, each shortened with an ellipsis. DR-0459 forbids exactly that, and its only remedy is a shorter genuinely-verbatim span. Each became two verbatim spans joined by our own prose. The generator refused to emit the module until all six were gone, which is what a gate is for.

**Eleven band failures.** All eight teen bands measured **0.41–0.46** of the adult lesson against a 0.6 fullness floor, and three senior bands measured over the grade-10 ceiling (`mgmt4` 11.14, `mgmt8` 10.79, `mgmt5` 10.21). The teen bands were extended with real application content in their own register — a deposit is not yours while you hold it; grab the railing and see if it moves; look at the worst response time, not the average — and the long senior sentences were split. Never padded, never re-registered from a neighbour.

**Four anchor themes that were almost entirely quotation** — `mgmt5` had **zero** words of our own prose framing the verse, `mgmt6` had five, `mgmt7` nine, `mgmt8` twelve. A theme that is only a quotation states the verse without teaching why it is the anchor. All four were given their framing. Worth recording how this was found: the first three were fixed one at a time across three runs before the whole set was measured at once, which is slower and dumber than measuring the dimension across all eight first.

After the corrections, every bar holds: teen **2.1–4.0**, senior **6.8–9.1**, teen easier than senior in all eight, shares **0.60–0.76**, overlap **0.00** teen-to-senior and at most 0.20 against the adult text.
