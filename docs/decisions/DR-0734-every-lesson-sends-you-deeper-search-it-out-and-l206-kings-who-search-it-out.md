## Context

Darrell asked that lessons be integrated so a reader finishes one wanting more of Yahweh and the mysteries of His Word, and that this produce kings as the Word says. The Word's own frame is Proverbs 25:2 (the honour of kings is to search out a matter) and its own king-making method is Deuteronomy 17:18-20 (the king's own copy of the law, read every day of his life). The catalog already carried the ground for this: every lesson names the verses it stands on in `anchor.ref`.

## What was measured

- Before a line was written, on 2026-10-01: **595 lessons across 43 courses; 595 carry anchor references; 356 have at least one other lesson in their course standing on a shared verse (or, failing a shared verse, a shared chapter); in Living Lessons 200 of 203.** The lessons without a link sit in short courses where no two lessons share a verse yet. The numbers are floors in the tests, because lessons that land later can only raise them.
- L206 as shipped: lesson 6,381 words; bands child 2,567 / youth 4,281 / teen 4,888 / senior 5,176 words; 499 quoted spans on five surfaces, 499 verbatim against the King James text, 0 faults; fullness child 0.51 / youth 0.62 / teen 0.62 / senior 0.61 (floors 0.5 / 0.6); reading grades child 0.9, youth 4.1, teen 4.4, senior 6.5 (ascending; child under the 5.0 ceiling); band differentiation worst pair 0.21 (ceiling 0.5); carries its own three talk-together prompts on every surface (DR-0733's gate); stands on shared ground with L205 (Acts 17:11, Romans 3:4, Joshua 1:8) and L202 (Joshua 1:8).
- The first draft's bands fell short of the fullness floors (child 0.33); two passages were added to each band, "A king's week" and "The three questions", and the floors were re-measured before the DR was written.

## Impact

Every lesson card in the PoeTech app now ends with **Search it out**: three questions that send the reader back into the text and out to a person (what this lesson shows about Yahweh; which quoted verse you will search out elsewhere in the Word; whom you will tell and which lesson you will open next), the lessons in the same course that stand on the same verses, each a button that opens that lesson, with the shared references named, and Proverbs 25:2 verbatim. The links are derived from the anchors, never typed, so the part can never claim a link the Word does not support and never goes stale. The print path carries the same line. L206 teaches the whole of Darrell's word in nine movements, each in the order he gave it, with the Word quoted inside the teaching: kings who search it out; the Word as the book; the Spiritual Mindset for eternity; success His Way, Heaven from inside; the Joy of the Lord as eternal Strength in Nehemiah 8's order (the book, understanding, joy, strength); perpetual growth on promises that do not fail; the Godhead all agree; who else matters; and what we built and measured. Every band carries "A king's week", the Deuteronomy 17 reading plan cut into seven days.

## Decision

- `search-it-out.js`: `refKey`, `anchorRefs(module)`, `chapterKey`, `sharedGround(module, modules, { limit })` (shared verses first, then shared chapters; never itself; sorted most-shared then by number), `searchQuestions(title)`, `searchItOutFor(module, modules)` → `{ verse, revealed, aim, ground, questions, next }`, `searchItOutCoverage(modules)`.
- The card: `data-testid="lesson-search-it-out"`, `data-ground`, `data-next`; questions `data-search-question`; linked lessons `data-testid="search-it-out-next"` with `data-search-next={id}` buttons (36 px, focus ring) calling `openLesson`; registered as `card-search-it-out` on the lesson-header surface.
- No writer rule is needed: the part is derived, not authored, so every lesson, past and future, carries it the moment it has anchors (which the catalog test already requires of every lesson).
- L206 `ll206-kings-who-search-it-out-the-word-sets-the-mind-for-eternal-growth-the-joy-of-the-lord-is-strength-the-godhead-all-agree`, added 2026-10-01. Numbers 203 is claimed by a lane in flight (L203 #1889); this PR goes green on the numbering ratchet when it lands and main is merged in.
- One lesson, not three: L205 stays the talk-together lesson, L206 intertwines all of the perspectives Darrell named in this run, and L202 is linked for Joshua 1:8. A third lesson would repeat what the Search it out part now does by design.
