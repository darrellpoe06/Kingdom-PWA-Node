## Context

Darrell, 2026-10-09: *"Will humans have angel like experiences after death? Lesson."*

The Word answers in three parts that must stay together, and the gate requires all three in every band:

- **Like the angels in the two ways Jesus names:** no marriage and no death (Matthew 22:30; Luke 20:36), and all live unto Him (Luke 20:38).
- **Never angels:** a different order, ministering spirits sent to serve the heirs of salvation (Hebrews 1:14); the Son took on the seed of Abraham, not their nature (Hebrews 2:16); the redeemed will judge angels (1 Corinthians 6:3); angels desire to look into salvation (1 Peter 1:12) and refuse worship as fellowservants (Revelation 22:9). The kindly saying that a loved one became an angel is named as not true, and the truth as better.
- **Like Him:** we shall be like Him, for we shall see Him as He is (1 John 3:2; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:42-49); not a ghost (Luke 24:39, 42-43; Job 19:26-27).

At death, for those who are His: absent from the body, present with the Lord (2 Corinthians 5:8; Philippians 1:23; Luke 23:43), carried by the angels in the Lord's own account (Luke 16:22), Stephen's sight and prayer (Acts 7:56, 59), rest (Revelation 14:13). The experiences the Word describes: His face, full knowing, joy, no more tears, shining, worship with every nation beside the angels, serving and reigning, a place prepared, forever with the Lord (Revelation 22:3-5, 21:4, 7:9-11; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 13:43; John 14:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:17). Where the Word is silent the lesson is silent (2 Corinthians 12:4; 1 John 3:2), and it borrows nothing from films or near-death accounts. Both halves are kept: the promise is for those who are His, judgment follows death (Hebrews 9:27), and the way is a Person (John 11:25).
