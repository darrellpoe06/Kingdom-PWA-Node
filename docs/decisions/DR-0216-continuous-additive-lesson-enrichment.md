## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-21, declared the enrichment standard across several turns:

> "Enrichment of any and all lessons continuously so the curriculum stays
> current... fresh... not changing or undermining just richer because of their
> rigor..." — "Ways and documentation..." — "And new courses and lessons are
> comprehensive and helpful each time..."

And, on the judgment of new-lesson-vs-enrich (giving latitude for a spoken
teaching): *"Or add it to the others if it's not enough for a lesson etc
according to the Ways and documentation."*

## The decision

**The curriculum is expected to get RICHER, perpetually — and richer is ADDITIVE,
never subtractive of what is already taught.** Enrichment deepens rigor,
cross-links, worked examples, verified Scripture, stories, and situational
application; it does NOT rewrite, water down, or undermine the lesson's existing
teaching. This is the curriculum-specific expression of DR-0075 (perpetual
improvement) bound to the DR-0215 content-preservation covenant.

1. **Additive, non-undermining.** A pass over a lesson may ADD — a verified
   verse, a worked example, a story, a cross-link, a tier of honest data, a
   clearer movement — but it must not delete or contradict the standing
   teaching. If a genuine correction is needed (an error, a doctrinal drift),
   that is a distinct, surfaced change with its own record, not silent
   "enrichment." Enrichment ≠ rewrite.

2. **Richer BECAUSE of rigor.** The enrichment earns its place through rigor,
   not volume: every added verse is KJV-verbatim (DR-0076), every claim is
   attributed/tiered (DR-0100/DR-0190), every teaching is Word-first and does
   not stage debate (DR-0098/DR-0127). Rigor is the reason it is richer — not
   more words for their own sake.

3. **New lessons/courses are comprehensive each time.** A newly harvested lesson
   ships COMPLETE on the tested module shape — anchor, big idea, benefits,
   age-tiered levels (child/teen/senior), a real quiz, the full teaching, ≥2
   stories, and a facilitator run-of-show — helpful start to finish, not a stub
   to be filled in later.

4. **New-lesson vs enrich is a judgment, made by the Ways (REV-0086).** When a
   spoken teaching arrives: if it is a distinct subject, it becomes a new
   comprehensive lesson; if it deepens an existing one, it ENRICHES that lesson
   additively. Either way it is captured (DR-0089 — never dropped), verified
   (DR-0076), and shipped through the normal lane.

5. **Everything through the Ways + documentation.** Every enrichment or new
   lesson is recorded (a REV for the work; a DR when a way changes) and rides the
   gates + PR lane. "Ways and documentation" is not optional polish — it is how
   the enrichment is trusted and preserved (DR-0215 covenant: nothing of Yahweh's
   perspective is lost to time).

## Verification (DR-0076)

The standing test for any enrichment pass: (a) did it ADD without deleting or
contradicting the standing teaching (diff is additive)? (b) is every added verse
KJV-verbatim and every claim attributed/tiered? (c) is a new lesson comprehensive
on the full module shape? (d) is it recorded in the ledger and green through the
gates? First application: L54 "The Same Word, Different Soil" (the Parable of the
Sower) — the distinctive teaching from Darrell's doers-not-hearers word, built new
because the sower's four-soils subject was not yet a lesson, while the
doers-not-hearers thread it shares is cross-linked to L38/L50 rather than
duplicated. Recorded as REV for the work; grounds this DR.
