## Context

Darrell, 2026-07-20, forwarding an IT-workplace session:

> "Lesson for Ari and claude to add to other lessons when appropriate... review
> our Ways and documentation and create a new lesson and add this context to
> those that it will help add quality to the lessons and even more context for our

The source is a practitioner session, **"Recognition, Power, and Equity in IT
Work"** (Courtney Fleeger & Mark McCarthy): how credit and recognition function as
POWER on a team; how uneven recognition harms colleagues across different
identities and career stages; and practical ways to share credit more
intentionally and build inclusive team cultures. It is a natural sibling of L44
(Safe to Speak) — another IT-workplace topic the Word founded deeper.

## The decision

**Harvest it into a live Living Lesson (L45), the same way L44 was harvested
(DR-0168), AND bind its core as a standing conduct standard for the platform's AI.**

1. **New lesson L45 — "Give Honour Where It Is Due."** Authored into
   `living-lessons-class.js` on the tested module shape (anchor, bigIdea, inApp,
   benefits, levels child/teen/senior, quiz, lesson, facilitator). `meta.weeks`
   bumped 44 → 45 (the `lesson-flow` count gate holds).

2. **The Word is senior (DR-0098/0100), and it goes past mere fairness.** The spine
   is 1 Corinthians 12:23-26 — God gives *"more abundant honour to that part which
   lacked"* — divine EQUITY that STEERS honour toward the overlooked, not everyone
   grabbing equally. Recognition is a **due** (Romans 13:7; Proverbs 3:27; a wage
   "kept back by fraud" cries out, James 5:4), the unthanked have a Recorder
   (Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 6:4), the credit-grab is guarded on the other side
   (Matthew 6:1; Proverbs 27:2; John 5:44; 1 Corinthians 3:6-8; Galatians 6:4),
   partiality is sin (James 2:1-9), and the model is the servant-king (Mark
   10:43-45; Luke 14:10-11).

3. **Trust-but-verify (DR-0190) — separate research DONE, matching the L44 pass.**
   The session is a talk, not a numbered study, so the lesson **asserts no bare
   statistic**; but the underlying claims it teaches were independently traced to
   PRIMARY, peer-reviewed sources (not the blurb's word):
   - **Heather Sarsons, "Recognition for Group Work: Gender Differences in
     Academia," *American Economic Review* 2017, 107(5):141-45** — women are less
     likely to receive tenure the more they coauthor, while men are not penalized;
     the effect is muted when women coauthor with women. Extended with experiments
     in **Gërxhani, Reuben, Sarsons & Schram, *Journal of Political Economy* 2021,
     129(1):101-147** — credit-attribution bias driven by gender + stereotypes
     under source-ambiguity.
   - **Ross, Glennon, Murciano-Goroff, Berkes, Weinberg & Lane, "Women are credited
     less in science than men," *Nature* 2022** (PMC9352587; UMETRICS administrative
     data) — women ~**13% less likely** to be named as authors on their team's
     papers/patents, the gap "present across most fields and almost all career
     stages" — the empirical spine of the session's "across different identities and
     career stages." Tier a (established fact).

   These figures are recorded HERE and in the lesson's trust-but-verify comment,
   ATTRIBUTED; the audience-facing lesson text attributes generally and drops no
   bare number, so the research-integrity gate passes and the Word stays senior
   (DR-0098/0100). Courtney Fleeger verified as a Champaign, IL designer/researcher
   — COLG's own city (COMMUNITY-FIRST), stated as context, not over-claimed.

4. **Conduct standard for Ari/Claude (the "lesson for Ari and claude" half).** The
   AI is bound to **render honour to whom honour**: name the human contribution,
   give credit back to the family and to the quiet contributor, attribute rather
   than absorb, and never let the platform (or the machine) take the applause for a
   person's labour of love. This composes with GOVERNANCE-EXECUTION-ADVISORY and
   DR-0111 — Claude advises, Darrell governs, the Foundation executes; **the vision
   is the family's, not the machine's.**

5. **Add the context to sibling lessons (the "add to other lessons" half).**
   Cross-refs woven in where they add quality: L12 (If One Member Suffers — same
   chapter is the recognition charter), L42 (the servant-king also governs credit),
   L44 (safety + recognition are the two halves of an honest team).
