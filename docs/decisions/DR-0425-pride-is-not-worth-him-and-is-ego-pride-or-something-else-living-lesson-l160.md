## The ego question, answered from the Word and not the culture

Ego is not a Bible word; the self is. The lesson's one doctrinal decision: (1) a **sober measure of the self is commanded** (Romans 12:3), with both wrong directions named — too high (Galatians 6:3) and too low, which argues with the price paid (1 Corinthians 6:20); (2) **pride is the self lifted above its measure and cut loose from its Maker** (the Deuteronomy 8:17 sentence); (3) the measure is fixed by 1 Corinthians 4:7 and Luke 17:10; (4) the proof is the towel — the Lord, knowing all things were in His hands, washed feet (John 13:3-5): the fullest self-knowledge produced the lowest service; knowing who you are is not pride, pride is that knowledge without the towel; (5) the Word does name a self to deal with (Luke 9:23; Galatians 2:20; Ephesians 4:22-24): a gifted self put to work and an old self crucified, not reformed. The gate pins these sentences. No competing psychology is staged (DR-0098).
