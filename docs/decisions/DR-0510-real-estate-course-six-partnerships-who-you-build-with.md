## Where it sits

Courses three through five covered the transaction, the tenant and the trades — the things a man buys, signs and repairs. This one covers the thing he cannot inspect: **the other person in the deal.** A partnership is the only asset in real estate that can walk out of the building, and it is the one most often entered on a handshake and a good feeling.

## The arc, one argument in eight moves

1. **Can two walk together, except they be agreed?** (Amos 3:3) The prophet asks it as one of a chain of questions whose answers are obvious, which is the point: agreement is not the fruit of walking together, it is the **precondition**. The lesson makes it measurable — write what each partner believes the venture is for, separately, before signing.
2. **Two are better than one** (Ecclesiastes 4:9) — and the reason given is not productivity but **rescue**: *"woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up"* (4:10). The partnership that cannot survive one partner's bad year was never the thing this verse commends.
3. **Unequally yoked** (2 Corinthians 6:14) — and what a yoke actually is. A yoke is a **direction-setting** instrument, not a friendship. The lesson refuses the two common distortions: it is not a ban on doing business with unbelievers, and it is not a formality either. The test is whether the instrument binds your direction to another's.
4. **And the ships were broken** (2 Chronicles 20:35-37; 1 Kings 22:48-49). Jehoshaphat joined himself with Ahaziah *"who did very wickedly"*, and the fleet never sailed. The lesson names the shape: the venture failed **before it produced anything**, and the partner's character was known in advance.
5. **Let there be no strife** (Genesis 13:8-9). Abram separated from Lot **while the relationship was still good**, gave away first choice, and kept the brother. Separation is not failure; it is the tool that preserves what strife would consume. Made measurable: the exit written on day one, before there is anything to fight over.
6. **The contention was so sharp that they departed asunder** (Acts 15:39). Paul and Barnabas split over Mark — and the Word does not tell us who was right. The lesson stops where Scripture stops (DR-0098) and teaches what it does show: both men kept working, and Mark was later called *"profitable to me for the ministry"* (2 Timothy 4:11).
7. **In the multitude of counsellors there is safety** (Proverbs 15:22) — and the counsel that costs you nothing is worth what it cost. The lesson distinguishes the counsellor with no stake from the one who will bear part of the loss.
8. **The people had a mind to work** (Nehemiah 4:6) — the capstone. A wall half its height in a hostile field, built because the will was shared, with every man's sword on his side. Shared purpose plus honest defence, which is what the seven lessons before it were assembling.

## Proven to catch

40 checks. **And one real finding in this course's own text, which none of the five before it had:** the test `says Yahweh in OUR voice` failed — **no adult lesson body named Him.** Eight lessons of partnership doctrine, every quotation verbatim, every band in range, and His covenant name appeared nowhere in our own prose (DR-0210). The remedy was not a find-replace: His name was added where it genuinely belonged in five lessons — the agreement Amos calls for is agreement about **Yahweh's** purpose; Abram could afford to give away first choice because Yahweh had already promised him the land; the wall was built because the people had a mind to work for **Yahweh's** city. The other three lessons carry Him in their quoted anchors and did not need our prose to repeat it.

This is worth recording as a class, not an incident: a gate that passed on five courses and failed on the sixth is a gate doing its job. The five that passed did so by accident of subject matter — property and tenants pull His name in naturally; **partnership prose can be written entirely in the register of prudence** and never mention Him. That is exactly the drift DR-0210 exists to catch, and it was invisible to every other dimension.
