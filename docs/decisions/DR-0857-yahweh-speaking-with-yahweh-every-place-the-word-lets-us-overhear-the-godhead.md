**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** accepted
**Area:** Living Lessons (L233), the Godhead in the Word
**Principle:** Spoken Teachings Are Build Input (Layer 0), The Source of Answers (the Godhead), DR-0331, DR-0076, DR-0098 (stay with what is revealed; where the Word does not explain, say so), DR-0215 and DR-0855

## Context

Darrell, 2026-10-09: *"Yahweh talking to Yahweh in all places in biblical truth and scriptures... lesson."* Rendered for meaning: gather every place the Word lets us hear the one Yahweh speaking within Himself, and teach what the conversations show.

The floor that nothing moves is Deuteronomy 6:4, in every band. The Word shows without explaining how, and the lesson says so (Deuteronomy 29:29). Seven movements: one LORD heard speaking within Himself (Genesis 1:2-3; John 1:1-2); the counsel before time (Genesis 1:26, 3:22, 11:7; Isaiah 6:8; Titus 1:2; 1 Peter 1:20; John 17:24); the Father to the Son (Psalms 2:7 with Acts 13:33; Psalms 110:1 and 110:4, which Jesus pressed in Matthew 22:43-44; Hebrews 1:8, 1:10; Isaiah 42:1; Matthew 3:16-17; Mark 1:11; Matthew 17:5; Daniel 7:13; Genesis 19:24, with no comment added); the Son to the Father (Psalms 40:7-8 with Hebrews 10:5; Luke 10:21; John 11:41-42; John 12:28, where the Father answered aloud; John 17; Mark 14:36; the three prayers from the cross, with Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Hebrews 2:12); the Holy Spirit hearing and speaking (John 16:13-15; 1 Corinthians 2:10-11; Romans 8:26-27; Isaiah 48:16; Hebrews 9:14); the conversation still about us (Romans 8:34; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:1; John 14:16); and what it shows: never a quarrel (John 8:29, 5:20, 10:30) and an open door (Ephesians 2:18; Galatians 4:6; Matthew 28:19; Revelation 22:17).
