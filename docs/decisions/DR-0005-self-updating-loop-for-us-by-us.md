## Context
Darrell: "poetech.us and the PoeTech App being used to consistently update the app for us by us." The app is both product and dev/feedback surface — dogfooded.

## Decision
A self-referential continuous-improvement loop: family/community input (Suggest button, family-voice wf30/wf08) + first-party signals ([DR-0009]) + measured outcomes ([DR-0008]) → sovereign LLM team(s) → work produced behind the Cage → shipped back to the app + the three sites → next round. **"For us, by us" = sovereign + community-owned: our own data, identity, models, and analytics; no external/proprietary dependency in the loop, and the data is never sold.**

## Rationale
Because the loop both does the work ([DR-0010]) and produces the telemetry that re-bases its own estimates ([DR-0004]) and tunes its own funnel ([DR-0008]). The system measures itself and re-plans itself.

## Consequences
Binds the other decisions into one system: three entities = what is served; F/G/H = the work; I = the fuel; J = the doing; the brakes = the safety; D = the measurement.
