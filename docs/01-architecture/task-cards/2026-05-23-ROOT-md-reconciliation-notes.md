## Region 1 — End of "Abiding Is Worth It" section

### PDF version (weaker, simpler)

> The connection between *"He restores my soul"* and the renewing of neural pathways is a faithful synthesis, not a direct scriptural claim. Scripture says the soul is restored; modern understanding of neuroplasticity rhymes with that claim. SKOS holds the synthesis as illustration, while the plumb line remains the text: the soul is restored beside the still waters.

### Existing repo version (stronger, longer)

> **On the Word reshaping the whole temple.** It would be too small a claim to say "Scripture speaks of the soul; neuroscience speaks of the brain; the two merely rhyme." Scripture says more than that. *"For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart"* (Hebrews 4:12, ESV). The Word operates on **both** registers at once — soul *and* spirit, joints *and* marrow, the immaterial *and* the material — as one act, down to the thoughts. Scripture is not merely *compatible* with the idea that Yahweh's Word physically reshapes the temple; it *claims* it. The renewing of the mind (Romans 12:2) and the Word piercing to joints and marrow (Hebrews 4:12) together assert that His Word works on the whole integrated system. Where modern observation of neural change lines up with this, it is *watching a mechanism* of what Scripture already declared — it is observation, not the source. The plumb line is Hebrews 4:12 and Romans 12:2. The science observes; Scripture declares.
