DROP THE SOUND ENGINEER'S TEACHING VIDEOS IN A FOLDER NAMED:

    intake/sound-engineer/

Then run, from /volume1/PoeTech/sme-pipeline:

    ./sound-engineer-to-lessons.sh ./intake/sound-engineer/<your-video>.mp4

What works: any common video or audio file (mp4, mov, m4a, wav, mp3...).
The engineer just talks through how they run the board -- one topic per video is
ideal (gain staging, EQ a vocal, ring out the monitors, mike the choir, the service
routine). Short, focused clips extract cleaner lessons than one long ramble.

Sovereign + local: nothing leaves the NAS. Manual-run only -- no auto-trigger.
Faithful: the pipeline captures ONLY what they actually say (it will not invent a
gain value or a frequency). Review output/sound-engineer/<run>/knowledge.md first.

See intake-sound-engineer-CONSENT.md for scope + consent.
