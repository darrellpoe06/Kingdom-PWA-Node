Hey choir, it's Christian. Let me walk through a couple of the songs for Sunday so
everybody's on the same page on the keys.

So "Way Maker," we're doing it in B. I know the original's in E but B sits better for
the altos and it lets the sopranos open up on the bridge without straining. I keep
the intro real simple, just a pad on the one chord, let it breathe for like four
bars, then the choir comes in soft on "You are here." On the bridge, "Even when I
don't see it you're working," I push the dynamics up and I do a quick run up to the
four before we hit the big moment, and that's where I want the choir to swell. Watch
me on that, I'll lift my left hand and that's your cue to come up.

Then "Goodness of God." That one we keep in Ab. It's a worship anthem so I lay down
more sustained chords, not a lot of movement, let the words carry it. The tenors have
that harmony line on the chorus, "all my life you have been faithful," and I'll give
you the note right before. Big thing on this one is don't rush the turnaround going
back into the verse, hang on the five an extra beat, I'll nod when we move.

One more, "Praise" by Elevation, that's an up-tempo one. We do it in C. It's busy on
the keys so I'm mostly comping on the rhythm and I need the choir tight on the stops,
the "praise" hits gotta be crisp and together, everybody off on the same beat. We'll
drill those stops at rehearsal.

General thing for everybody, watch my hands for dynamics, breathe together at the
ends of phrases, and when in doubt sing softer not louder so we blend. That's it,
see y'all Thursday.
